About Spinlet

Spinlet is a compact round-bodied spider striped boldly in orange and blue, with eight legs that tap-tap against the Dark Forest floor as it bounces along on springy steps. Its body is plump and glossy, its eyes multiple and bright. It spins small trial webs - getting better each time, but still prone to spinning them across walking paths at face-height and then being very surprised when something walks through. It is learning. The Forest's older spiders are patient teachers.

Field note: Spinlet reuses every strand of silk it accidentally ruins - nothing is wasted, everything becomes the next attempt.

About Webarch

Webarch is a stocky mid-sized spider with a vivid orange and blue-banded body and thick legs designed for precision work. It weaves webs of remarkable geometric complexity in the Dark Forest's mid-canopy - patterns so intricate and regular that researchers argue about whether they are functional or decorative, and Webarch does not clarify. It positions itself at the exact center of each web and remains perfectly still until something touches a single strand, at which point it moves at a speed that seems to break the rule about how large things move.

Field note: Webarch's webs use exactly 47 anchor points every time - different shapes, always exactly 47.

About Arachnova

Arachnova is a massive spider with an abdomen the size of a lantern, bold orange and blue plates across its body, and legs that span a full arm's length in each direction. Its webs now span significant sections of the Dark Forest canopy, and it maintains all of them simultaneously through psychic extension - it does not need to touch a strand to feel what lands on it. The guardians who first mapped the Forest found Arachnova's web described in the very oldest records, already vast even then. It has been weaving the same pattern for two hundred years.

Field note: Arachnova's master web contains one deliberately incomplete spiral - left open to remind itself that no web is ever truly finished.

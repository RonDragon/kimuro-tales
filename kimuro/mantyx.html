About Mantyx

A small purple-violet ray with a rounded body and spotted dorsal surface, Mantyx skims the surface of the poison marsh's still pools with its wide flat wings beating in slow, lazy circles. Its spots glow faintly blue-violet at dusk, drawing insects down to the water. The marsh is full of words the guardians hid in old bottles - Mantyx circles each one before it sinks deeper, as though reading what's inside without opening the cork.

Field note: Mantyx always circles a sunken object exactly seven times before swimming away - never more, never fewer.

About Mantaray

Mantaray has grown into a wide, graceful shape - deep violet with glowing blue-white markings along its trailing edges. Its long whip-tail streams behind it as it glides between the marsh's hanging vines, weaving without touching a single one. It has begun to show its psychic nature: objects in its path shift aside a heartbeat before it arrives, as if the marsh itself makes way. Guardians who travel the poison marsh say Mantaray appears whenever someone is lost and guides them to safe ground.

Field note: Mantaray has never been seen navigating the same route twice - it always finds a new path through the marsh.

About Mantysm

Mantysm is rarely seen in full - usually only its glowing wingtips pass through the deepest channels of the poison marsh, leaving trails of violet light across the black water. Its body has grown sleek and powerful, its wingspan immense, its tail extending into two trailing ribbons. Its psychic sense reaches the surface even from fifty metres below - marsh fishers say the ripples it sends up form patterns that spell out warnings. The guardians believe Mantysm keeps Abalont's oldest shadows trapped beneath the marsh floor.

Field note: Three days before a storm, the surface of Mantysm's deep channel shows concentric rings - the marsh folk call it 'Mantysm's writing'.

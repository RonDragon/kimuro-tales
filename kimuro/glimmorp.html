About Glimmorp

Glimmorp is a bulbous, bug-eyed blue fish with a tiny thin antenna sprouting from its forehead that ends in a dim glowing bulb. Its enormous round eyes take up most of its face, and its wide gaping mouth - packed with tiny pointed teeth - always hangs slightly open, as though it has just thought of something alarming. It floats in the murky pools of ghost hollow with surprising stillness, and the lure-bulb pulses at intervals that grow closer together when prey is nearby. It has been caught staring at its own lure's reflection for up to forty minutes.

Field note: Glimmorp has been timed staring at its own lure-light reflection for forty minutes - it is unclear whether it is confused or proud.

About Deepmaw

Deepmaw has grown larger and more menacing - its dark blue-purple scales are jagged along the sides, its teeth have extended into proper fangs visible even with its mouth closed, and its lure-antenna now curves forward with a brighter crescent-glow. It prowls the deepest pools of ghost hollow in absolute silence, using the lure not just to attract prey but to read subtle ripples in the water to map the hollow's secrets. Its great oversized eyes give it night-vision that extends beyond sight - it is said to see three days into the past as well as twenty meters into the dark.

Field note: Deepmaw's eyes reportedly see the most recent three days of activity in any space it enters - no secret has ever lasted in a room it occupies.

About Abysslure

Abysslure is a massive, terrifying deep-sea beast - its body is broad and armored in overlapping dark plates with spined ridges, its jaws stretch nearly the width of its body and reveal rows of recurved teeth, and its lure-antenna glows at a blue-white intensity that lights the entire pool. It is the ghost hollow's deepest-water guardian, and the pools it inhabits are avoided by everything else. When it opens its jaws fully the pressure wave makes the water surface ripple thirty meters away. The guardians tasked it with holding the hollow's most dangerous forgotten words - the ones that caused harm before they were unlearned.

Field note: Abysslure's jaw-pressure wave has been measured at the pool surface thirty meters away - it never opens its mouth fully without reason.

About Veilmin

Veilmin appears as a small purple teardrop of pure psychic flame with a single bright eye at its forward point and a flowing fiery tail that streams behind it. No legs, no arms - it drifts on wind currents at the Gale Ridge's base, drawn toward lightning sparks and the smell of ozone. It steers by tilting its teardrop body and can turn in a space no wider than a hand. Young scholars at the Ridge often lose track of it mid-lesson because it silently slips into a drafty corner the moment it grows bored.

Field note: Veilmin can hover inside a lamp flame without being harmed; it uses candle light to read the shadows of passing sounds on cave walls.

About Veilwing

Veilwing has grown a proper body - a sleek purple avian form with a rounded head, two broad tapered wings edged with violet flame-feathers, and a long plume tail that crackles with static. Its single bright eye has been joined by a second, giving it full front-facing binocular vision. It rides the rotating updrafts around the Gale Ridge with total ease, folding its wings to plunge and spreading them again to brake hard. When it speaks, the nearby air pressure drops just slightly and loose dust spirals upward.

Field note: A sudden fall in air pressure during calm weather at the Gale Ridge almost always means a Veilwing has just passed overhead.

About Veilphantom

The Guardians say it carries the voices of everyone the Ridge has lost in the spaces between its wingbeats.

Field note: When Veilphantom spreads its crest fully, the wind within a hundred metres changes direction to match where it is looking.

About Scaldite

Scaldite is a small, thick-bodied eel-like creature already covered in dark brick-red scales edged with orange, its wide-hinged jaw full of small jagged teeth it snaps open and shut when curious. It coils in pockets of warm lava-rock at the edge of the Lava Crater, absorbing heat through its belly scales. Despite its fierce expression, it follows bright objects with the focused delight of something that has never seen anything it did not find interesting. Jack, the crater's guardian, found the first Scaldite coiled around a lantern, convinced the flame was something that needed protecting.

Field note: Scaldite coils around heat sources it trusts, pressing its belly against them even when the source is completely cold - it seems to remember warmth.

About Scaldvern

Scaldvern has thickened considerably, its brick-red and orange scales now heavily overlapping like cooling lava-crust. Its jaw is wider and its teeth are proper fangs, and the spines along its back have erupted into full saw-toothed ridges. It swims through shallow lava channels with sinuous ease, surfacing only to snap at the fiery words that drift like embers in the Lava Crater's air. Jack found that Scaldvern hoards these memory-embers in a hollow under its favorite rock - it collects them the way others collect treasures, as if it knows their value.

Field note: Scaldvern hoards memory-embers in a hollow under its rock, arranged by brightness - the most luminous are always at the center.

About Inferscald

Inferscald is a great coiling lava-serpent, its dark red-orange scales now enormous and armored, its saw-toothed dorsal crest running the full length of its body. Its jaws can open almost a hundred and eighty degrees, and when it breathes out, the air ahead superheats. It coils around the deepest vents of the Lava Crater, hoarding the fierce, fiery words of the deep that Jack guards. Only a child whose courage burns brighter than the magma can coax it to share them - because Inferscald tests every visitor first by looking directly into their eyes and waiting for the flinch that never comes.

Field note: Inferscald tests every visitor's courage with a ten-second unbroken stare - children who hold its gaze get a word no one else receives.

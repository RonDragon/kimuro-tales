About Calkind

Calkind is a warm brown bull calf with a round, soft face and big curious eyes that make it look far too gentle for its Steel Fortress home. Its small horns are just beginning to curve, and it trots along the fortress's outer ramparts with the same cheerful obliviousness it shows everything else. Young Calkind practice charging at walls, training the muscle-memory that will eventually let them shatter steel plate. For now they mostly bounce off and look confused, which the fortress guards find quietly charming.

Field note: Calkind is the only creature in the Steel Fortress known to headbutt the same wall one hundred times in one session, then walk away satisfied.

About Hornbeast

Hornbeast is a pitch-black bull with sweeping curved horns as thick as a person's arm and a body built like compressed darkness. Its eyes gleam a faint red and it carries a cloud of something - dust, or shadow, or both - that trails behind it when it moves. It stands in the Steel Fortress's central yard with the confidence of a creature that has tested every wall and found them all insufficient. When it charges, the ground vibrates three seconds before it arrives. The fortress gates were built with a Hornbeast-width gap in the design spec.

Field note: Hornbeast charges at exactly 47 km/h - measured twice - and has never once deviated from that speed during a full run.

About Titanhorner

Titanhorner wears the Steel Fortress like a badge - its hide has hardened into overlapping plates of dark, forge-tempered earth, and its massive horns are wrapped in dark energy that crackles at the tips. Its face carries the stillness of something that has already won every fight it ever needed to have. The fortress was not built around Titanhorner - the legends say Titanhorner built the fortress, selecting the stone, testing each wall by walking through it, and leaving behind what was still standing as the foundation. The oldest walls bear faint horn-grooves.

Field note: The two oldest walls of the Steel Fortress each bear exactly three parallel horn-grooves at precisely the same height - Titanhorner's height.

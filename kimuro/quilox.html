About Quilox

Quilox is a chubby little porcupine-shaped creature blanketed in lush green fur with clusters of leafy quills rising from its back, each tipped in a warm orange-red like a tiny ember. Its round face carries a cheerful fierce expression, dark eyes glinting above a tuft of green around its cheeks. In the Dark Forest it burrows under leaf piles and waits quietly until words drift close enough to chase. The guardians first noticed this line when the forest glowed faintly amber at night - little Quiloxes sleeping with their warm quills lit up like lanterns.

Field note: On cold nights Quilox glows amber through leaf piles; forest travelers use the warm patches as impromptu campfire spots.

About Spinoquill

Spinoquill has grown thick and low, its body covered in overlapping green-tinged leaf-quills edged in blazing orange-red. It lumbers through the Dark Forest pushing through undergrowth with its broad snout, leaving a trail of gently scorched ferns. Its temperament is feisty - it stamps one forefoot when annoyed and fans its quills wide, releasing a warm waft of smoke. Travelers say the forest actually smells better where Spinoquill roams, because the small controlled singeing keeps the undergrowth from going too wild and lets unfamiliar names take root in cleared soil.

Field note: Spinoquill leaves a trail of half-scorched leaves; forest children follow the path to find new clearings where rare plants grow fastest.

About Pyrequill

Pyrequill is massive and low-slung, its back erupting in a great crown of long leafy quills that burn continuously at their tips, casting shifting orange light across the forest floor. Its head is broad, its jaw powerful, and its body ripples with overlapping bark-tough hide. It moves slowly but never stops. In the Dark Forest it is said that Abalont once tried to freeze the region with endless shadow - Pyrequill walked a slow circuit through the deepest grove, and wherever its burning quills passed, the shadow thinned. It is the fire that lives inside the forest's green heart.

Field note: Pyrequill's quill-flames never set the living forest alight - they burn only what is already dead or turned to shadow.

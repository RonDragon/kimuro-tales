About Piranule

Piranule is a small, chubby round-bodied fish with a bright green body and a single enormous eye that dominates its face. Its tiny mouth curves upward in what looks like an innocent smile, but a faint row of teeth hint at what's coming. It floats in the murky still pools beneath Ghost Hollow's twisted trees, and bubbles drift up around it constantly. When frightened, it puffs up to nearly twice its size like a tiny balloon and drifts to the bottom. Ghost Hollow travelers always warn each other: if you see a small green fish smiling at you from the dark water, keep your fingers up.

Field note: Piranule's single large eye can see in complete darkness, and it watches its trainer even while appearing to look the other way.

About Piranash

Piranash has lost any pretense of innocence. Its teal-green body is now angular and powerful, with a wide gaping mouth of triangular teeth that it rarely fully closes. Three sharp dorsal spines jut from its back, and water-bubble clusters appear around it as a threat display. Its large eye has gained a cold menacing quality, taking in everything around it without betraying any reaction. It circles the hollow's deeper pools in slow hungry loops, occasionally thrashing the water's surface with its tail to make prey flinch. Ghost Hollow fishers say that Piranash never bites the same spot twice - it tests patience before it strikes.

Field note: Piranash always surfaces exactly three times before attacking - watchers who count to three and step back are always safe.

About Pirannox

Pirannox is a terrifying sight - a massive teal-green fish armored with jagged ice-blue crystal spines along its back and a gaping maw of interlocking serrated teeth as wide as a child is tall. Its body is thick and muscular, and the cold dark water of Ghost Hollow steams faintly where it moves. The crystal spines conduct dark energy that radiates outward like cold fog. Ancient maps of Ghost Hollow mark one pool simply as 'Pirannox - do not fish.' The guardians say a Pirannox once held its territory unchanged for three hundred years, and that marks carved into its territory-stones stay sharp forever, preserved by its chill.

Field note: The temperature of Ghost Hollow's water drops exactly two degrees every time a Pirannox bares its teeth fully.

About Pebblyx

Pebblyx is a compact little round creature of sandy brown with a single teal crystal spike growing from its forehead like a proud antenna. Two large blue eyes give it an expression of perpetual wonder, and it scampers through the Crystal Mines' lowest tunnels poking its nose into every glittering crack. The crystal on its head is sensitive - it hums faintly near rich ore veins, making Pebblyx an accidental treasure-finder that miners quietly follow through the tunnels without letting on.

Field note: A Pebblyx's crystal hums at exactly the pitch of the ore vein it senses - higher for crystal, lower for raw stone.

About Cragyx

Cragyx has developed a broad armored body studded with multiple sharp rock spikes across its back and head, with two thick forward-facing horns flanking its face. Its hide is a mottled grey-brown, textured like natural stone, and it moves surprisingly quickly through the mine corridors with a low, wide-legged gait. When threatened it lowers its head and presents the full array of spikes in a flat defensive shield. Crystal Mines workers leave offerings of smooth pebbles at Cragyx's favorite resting spots - insults taken otherwise.

Field note: Cragyx can reverse direction instantaneously without slowing - miners still argue over how its legs accomplish this.

About Cragolith

Cragolith stands like a jagged peak given legs, its body wrapped in dense overlapping stone plates and its head crowned with a jagged ring of pale crystal spikes. The crystal crown is not decorative - it channels seismic vibration from the mine walls directly into its skull, giving Cragolith awareness of every movement within three hundred meters. It communicates with other Cragolith through low-frequency pulses that travel through the stone itself, imperceptible to any creature without rock in its bones. The mines feel quieter when a Cragolith is watching.

Field note: Cragolith can pinpoint a single footstep through forty meters of solid crystal mine wall using only vibration sense.

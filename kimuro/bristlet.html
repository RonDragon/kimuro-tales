About Bristlet

Bristlet is a small dark-brown boar-pup covered in shaggy layered fur that seems perpetually damp from the fog of Ghost Hollow. Its eyes are small, bright, and intensely watchful, missing nothing in the dim undergrowth. Bristlet communicates primarily by listening - it goes utterly still when anything interesting is happening nearby, processing the sounds and scents before deciding whether to approach or melt back into the shadows. It leaves no tracks on Ghost Hollow's soft floor; its passage is known only by the faint scent of damp earth that lingers at shin-height.

Field note: Bristlet leaves no tracks on soft ground - the only sign it was present is a faint cold-earth scent at ankle height.

About Bristor

Bristor has grown into something out of a hollow-tale - a long, low, bristle-covered beast with a wide jaw of over-large teeth and a permanent snarl that it wears as a default expression. Its dark spines are thick and stiff, catching on branches and making a distinctive rattle as it moves through Ghost Hollow's thickets, though somehow it is still surprisingly hard to spot until it is very close. Bristor does not chase prey - it positions itself and waits, with the unhurried confidence of something that knows the Hollow better than anything else alive.

Field note: Bristor waits in the same three ambush positions every night - the Ghost Hollow's sentinels have mapped them and use them as safe-path waypoints.

About Thorvusk

Thorvusk is the Ghost Hollow's darkest secret - a massive armored beast with a back forested in huge dark spines that creak in the fog like old timber, and enormous interlocking tusks that it clacks together before it charges. It is so perfectly adapted to the Hollow's eternal dim that it casts no visible shadow of its own, and the fog seems to part around it rather than cling. Ancient records describe Thorvusk as the Hollow's Gate: a creature that stands between the living world and whatever lies deeper in the dark, allowing nothing Abalont sent through to pass the other way.

Field note: Thorvusk casts no visible shadow in Ghost Hollow's fog - the fog simply moves to fill the space it occupies.

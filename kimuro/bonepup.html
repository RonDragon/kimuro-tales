About Bonepup

Bonepup is a small puppy-shaped skeleton with cream-yellow bones and wide hollow eye-sockets that somehow still manage to look endearingly bright. It patters across Amber Desert on bony paws that click lightly on the sandstone, wagging a tail of connected vertebrae that rattles cheerfully behind it. It is inexplicably friendly for something with no soft tissue, and it has discovered that sand makes a perfectly acceptable bed if you dig deep enough not to feel the heat. It checks on its digs frequently, having repeatedly forgotten where it left them.

Field note: Bonepup wags its tail in exactly eight vertebral segments - it once dislocated the ninth and has never quite forgiven itself.

About Skelewolf

Skelewolf is a lean skeleton wolf that lopes across Amber Desert with the efficient ease of something that never gets tired, hungry, or hot. Its ribs cast bar-shadows on the sand at midday and it stalks the desert routes at dusk, following travellers at a safe distance with what veterans have come to recognise as protective intent - it keeps other things away, not people. Its hollow eye-sockets catch the last of the sunset light and glow amber for a few minutes each evening.

Field note: Skelewolf always positions itself exactly fifteen paces behind any traveller it has chosen to follow - it measures this with impressive consistency.

About Deathmaw

Deathmaw is a massive armored skeletal beast with broad dorsal bone-plates, enormous jaws, and four stocky legs built to hold against desert storms. Its skeleton is dense and ancient, the bones stained amber from centuries of desert sand, and its jaw hangs open in a permanent grin that is less menacing once you realize it cannot close. It patrols the deep desert where no living creature goes willingly, marking the boundaries of Abalont's old influence and ensuring nothing crosses back out. Whatever Deathmaw chases turns around.

Field note: Deathmaw's jaw has been open for 200 years - historians believe it swallowed something it is still digesting.

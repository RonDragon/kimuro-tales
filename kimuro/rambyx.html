About Rambyx

Rambyx is a fluffy golden lamb with a single small curved horn just beginning to coil from its forehead. A bright green star-shaped mark glows on its brow, and its thick mane is the color of summer grass. It bounces through the Bug Thicket on four sturdy legs, nosing at every flower it passes and sneezing when insects tickle its muzzle. The thicket's giant beetles avoid it - somehow Rambyx always knows which leaf hides the most dangerous ones and steers its friends safely around. Even the wind seems to shift direction to keep pollen out of its face.

Field note: The green star on Rambyx's brow brightens just before a gust arrives - local children use it as a three-second wind warning.

About Oviking

Oviking stands taller now, its golden mane flowing behind it in a permanent invisible wind even on still days. Two full curved horns spiral from its head, etched with faint green vein-lines. It has grown alert and purposeful, patrolling the tall-grass corridors of the Bug Thicket with its nose high and ears swiveling. When it runs, its hoofbeats are almost silent - the wind beneath its mane cushions each step. Travelers who get lost in the thicket's tangled maze say a flash of gold between the stems and a rush of warm air usually means Oviking has already found the way out.

Field note: Oviking's hoofbeats are nearly silent at full gallop - the first sign it is near is the scent of cut grass on the air.

About Arcuron

Arcuron is a magnificent golden ram whose twin horns spiral into great arching loops wreathed in green light and woven with flowering vines. Its thick mane ripples with living grass, and emerald leaf-armor covers its flanks. When it walks, a gentle wind lifts from its hooves and stirs the canopy overhead. In the Bug Thicket it is both guardian and compass - creatures orient themselves by the direction of Arcuron's wind, and the thicket's ancient song-trees grow tallest in the ring where it rests. Abalont's shadows cannot enter a space where Arcuron breathes.

Field note: The flowers woven into Arcuron's horns are never the same two days running - it chooses fresh ones every dawn without pulling a single stem.

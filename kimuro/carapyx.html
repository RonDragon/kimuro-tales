About Carapyx

Carapyx is a segmented armoured larva - grey-silver plating covering its oval body in neat overlapping sections, with a single glowing purple eye at its front. It moves with a slow, deliberate rolling motion through Dark Forest's leaf litter, pausing often to feel vibrations with its underside. It cannot sting yet but its psychic eye reads surface emotions with unsettling accuracy. Children in the Dark Forest say if Carapyx stops and stares at you, it already knows what you are about to say.

Field note: Carapyx can predict the exact direction a nearby creature will move in by reading micro-vibrations with its belly-plates - it sidesteps threats before they form.

About Chrysapyx

Chrysapyx has sealed itself into a upright shell - a smooth rounded carapace of deep grey with a single focused purple eye that never blinks. It does not move from the spot it chose, but the air around it hums. In this sealed state it is absorbing every vibration, sound, and emotion passing through the Dark Forest, cataloguing them in a silence that others find unsettling. Dark Forest creatures give it a wide berth, sensing they are being studied.

Field note: Chrysapyx in its sealed state has been observed rotating very slowly - always toward the largest nearby psychic disturbance, like a living compass.

About Vespiarch

Vespiarch has emerged - a sleek, metallic dark wasp with four clear wings, a tapered stinger, and compound purple eyes that glow with an unsettling intelligence. Its black-banded body is elegant and precise, and its wingbeats are almost inaudible, making its approach startlingly sudden. It does not simply sting; it reads exactly what a target fears most and acts on that knowledge with surgical calm. In the Dark Forest, Vespiarch is spoken of as the reason the trees remember where you have been.

Field note: Vespiarch leaves a faint psychic trace on everything it stings - researchers call it a 'memory tag'; subjects reliably remember the exact moment of contact for years.

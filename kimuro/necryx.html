About Necryx

Necryx is a small, bleached-bone armadillo whose plated body is the colour of dry old ivory. Its joints are slightly translucent where ghost energy seeps through. It shuffles through the valley's root-layer with its nose to the ground, sniffing for something that may not be physical - the dark element draws it toward hidden secrets. Despite its unnerving pallor, Necryx is shy. It curls into a tight ring when startled, and the ghost energy at its joints briefly illuminates the inside of the curl - so a frightened Necryx looks like a tiny lantern.

Field note: Necryx curls faster than any other armadillo-form creature measured - complete curl from open posture in 0.4 seconds, every time.

About Ossyx

Ossyx has grown into a disturbing and strangely beautiful form - elongated, low-bellied, with armour plates that have darkened to deep purple-grey and begun to crack at the edges where ghost energy leaks out as faint smoke. The exposed skeleton-visible sections at its joints glow purple. It moves through the valley's shadow zones at dusk, leaving slight frost-marks where it steps. Verdant Valley's older guardians say Ossyx marks the places where old dark memories have been buried in the valley's soil - it can't help being drawn there.

Field note: Ossyx's footprint frost-marks always appear on soil that was a battle site within the last 300 years - the dark element reads history in the ground.

About Plaguarch

Plaguarch is the most unsettling creature in Verdant Valley - a massive, plate-armored dark armadillo whose body is half bone, half ghost-energy, with visible skeleton structures glowing purple at every seam. Dark smoke rises steadily from its back. Its jaw is wide and lined with bone-plates. Where Plaguarch walks, the valley's chime-flowers wilt for a day and then regrow stronger - as if its dark presence reminds the valley what it could lose. Guardians believe Plaguarch was created by Abalont's first shadow as a warning, but it chose to stay in the valley rather than serve him.

Field note: The chime-flowers around Plaguarch's resting spots always bloom twice as densely the week after it moves on - as if the valley is celebrating its departure.

About Kindling

Kindling is a bright orange-red fox kit with flame-colored fur that genuinely flickers at the tips of its large ears and its fluffy tail. Its eyes sparkle with excitement, and it hops between the cooled lava rocks of the crater rim, leaving warm paw-prints that glow briefly before fading. Every new voice it hears makes its tail-flame spurt an inch taller. The crater foxes were the first creatures brave enough to nest near the lava flow itself.

Field note: Kindling's tail-flame is always exactly the same height as whatever it considers the most exciting thing nearby.

About Pyrekin

Pyrekin stands lean and confident with three fire-bright tails fanning behind it in a spray of orange and red flame. Its fur bristles with heat across the back and shoulders, and its mane has grown wilder. It patrols the lava crater's mid-slopes where the rock is too hot for most creatures and too cold for the lava itself. Each tail tip holds a different-temperature flame that it uses to signal across the crater - warm for greeting, hot for warning.

Field note: Pyrekin signals distance by which tail it raises: left tail means near, right tail means far, center tail means danger.

About Pyrevixen

Pyrevixen is a great fire-fox with a full blazing body-wrap of flame and a thick multi-tail fan that spreads wide as a bonfire. Its fur appears fully ignited along the limbs and chest, and it runs low and fast across lava plains leaving a trail of scorched stone. The guardians say it first mapped the safe paths through the Lava Crater for all those who came after - every solid bridge of cooled lava bears the scorched imprint of an Pyrevixen paw.

Field note: Pyrevixen never steps on the same cooled-lava bridge twice - it tests a new path every crossing.

About Peblin

Peblin is a tiny round grey pebble creature with large cheerful eyes and a single blue ice-crystal spike growing from the top of its head like a frozen horn. It bounces rather than walks, each hop leaving a tiny frost ring on the stone surface it lands on. Peblin lives on the exposed rock faces of Frozen Peaks where the wind is strongest and temperatures drop below freezing before midnight. It seems to enjoy the cold entirely too much - turning its tiny crystal spike to face into the wind and holding still with what can only be described as bliss.

Field note: Peblin's crystal spike grows three millimeters for every degree below freezing - at the spire's peak, researchers have recorded spikes forty centimeters long.

About Geolith

Geolith has transformed entirely - from a round hopping pebble into an upright bipedal warrior with broad stone-plated fists, a chest covered in jagged ice-crystal armor, and a crown of large blue ice spikes jutting from its head and shoulders. It is not graceful, but it is nearly indestructible. Geolith patrols the mid-level ledges of Frozen Peaks, where lightning strikes the stone often enough to melt and refreeze the ice crystals on its body in constant renewal. It braces against the wind with its wide flat feet and never loses its footing.

Field note: Geolith has been struck by lightning forty-six times in observed history - each strike appears to fuse more crystal onto its body rather than damaging it.

About Crystallax

Crystallax has transcended its rocky origins - its body is now encased in gleaming golden-bronze armor with a massive crown of enormous blue ice-crystal spires radiating from its shoulders and head like a frozen sun. Each ice crystal in the crown is a different shade of blue, from pale powder to deep glacial, and they catch lightning and scatter it harmlessly into the storm clouds. At the Frozen Peaks' absolute peak, Crystallax stands facing the wind without flinching, arms spread, during the most violent storms - explorers have watched it for hours from the shelter of the lower rocks below.

Field note: When Crystallax extends its arms fully during a storm, the lightning always strikes the highest crystal spike and travels down through its body into the bedrock below, never entering the spire itself.

About Chillpod

Chillpod is a pale blue segmented grub, plump and round, with a soft ridged body that always feels cool to the touch like a stone from a mountain stream. Its six tiny legs barely reach the ground as it rolls and wriggles through the undergrowth of Verdant Valley, searching for the coldest, shadiest mossy hollows. Though small and slow, Chillpod leaves a faint icy shimmer on every leaf it crawls across. Explorers in the valley say finding one nestled in your boot means an unusually cold winter is coming.

Field note: Chillpod only moves when no one is looking - place it on a leaf and look away, and it will have vanished by the time you glance back.

About Chrysofrost

Chrysofrost has sealed itself inside a smooth teardrop-shaped shell of pale blue crystal, its interior form visible as a shadowy outline through the translucent walls. Faint leaf-vein patterns glow softly inside, pulsing once every few minutes like a slow heartbeat. It hangs from branches in the coldest parts of Verdant Valley, sometimes for weeks at a time. The guardians believed that stories whispered near a Chrysofrost would be absorbed into its forming mind, so they would tell the old tales beside the hanging chrysalises each morning.

Field note: If you tap a Chrysofrost shell and hold your ear close, you can hear a faint humming of whatever word was last spoken near it.

About Glacewing

Glacewing emerges a fearsome sight: a sleek pale blue wasp with large multifaceted eyes, four jagged legs tipped with ice hooks, and two pairs of wings printed with perfect snowflake patterns. Its wingspan creates a sharp buzzing hum that drops the air temperature by two degrees wherever it hovers. Each wingbeat scatters tiny ice crystals that drift down like silent snow. It is rarely aggressive, preferring to patrol the high tree canopy of Verdant Valley, but any creature that threatens its territory finds itself flash-frozen mid-motion.

Field note: A Glacewing's snowflake wing patterns are unique to each individual - no two have ever been found identical.

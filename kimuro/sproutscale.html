About Sproutscale

Sproutscale is a bright green iguana hatchling with a broad flat head crowned by a leafy crest, wide golden eyes, and a compact body carried low on four sturdy legs. Its tail is nearly as long as its body and it holds it curved upward. It is most comfortable in the sun-warmed clearings of Verdant Valley where it basks on flat rocks in the early morning, eyes half-closed, absorbing both warmth and the soft sounds of the valley's singing winds. It tests every rock it finds by tapping it twice - it is searching for one that sounds exactly right, for reasons it cannot explain.

Field note: Sproutscale has been observed tapping over two hundred rocks in a single morning; it discards each one with the same decisive head-shake.

About Leafwyrm

Leafwyrm stands taller now, its body broadened and its back ridge of leaf-green spines now sharp and pronounced. Its golden eyes have steadied into a calm, measuring gaze and its broad flat head carries a more developed leafy crest. It patrols the climbing paths of Verdant Valley with a slow deliberate rhythm, testing each traveler it meets by blocking the trail and waiting. It is not hostile - it simply will not move aside until it judges whether the person before it is worth the valley's trust. It has never been wrong.

Field note: In fourteen recorded encounters, Leafwyrm has moved aside for every trustworthy traveler and blocked every untrustworthy one without any known error.

About Verdrakian

Verdrakian is a great spiked dragon-lizard whose back is a fortress of tall leaf-green spines and whose heavy body moves with precise, unhurried authority. Its leafy crest has grown into a full crown and its golden eyes burn steady and deep. It guards a sun-warmed clearing deep in Verdant Valley that it has held since before Akilla fell - and the grass in that clearing grows greener and taller than anywhere else. Every traveler it has tested and found worthy has left with a leaf-scale from its crest; it grows them back in a week, unbothered.

Field note: The clearing Verdrakian guards is the only place in all of Verdant Valley where three specific song-trees grow side by side - they have never been found elsewhere.

About Phantkit

A tiny white-furred kit with alert blue eyes and two neat upright ears, Phantkit trots the poison marsh edges with surprising confidence for something so small. Its fur is almost luminous against the dark reeds, and a small bushy white tail flicks behind it. Newly hatched in the murk of the poison marsh, it does not yet understand the dangers around it - instead it pounces on reflections in the dark water, convinced something lives just beneath the surface.

Field note: Phantkit's paw-prints glow faintly blue-white for several minutes after it walks on damp mud.

About Spectryx

Spectryx has grown into a lean, long-legged fox with silver-grey fur and a thick, sweeping tail that carries a faint purple sheen at its tips. Its eyes are still vivid blue but now hold a calm, calculating look. It moves between the rotting trees of the poison marsh without stirring a single dead leaf, and other creatures part the water to let it pass. Older marsh folk say Spectryx carries small riddles in its mind and will share one if you sit still long enough.

Field note: Spectryx can hold perfectly still for up to two hours, so motionless that marsh birds sometimes land on its back.

About Phantomyx

Phantomyx stands as tall as a doorway, its brilliant white fur now threaded through with crackling purple-dark energy that forms a wild mane and blazing tail plume. Several trailing tails sweep behind it like slow flames, each tipped in deep violet. It strides the poison marsh as though the toxins are merely damp grass, and its gaze alone can still the chittering of marsh insects for a hundred metres. Those who have looked into its eyes speak of seeing a map of everywhere they have ever been.

Field note: Phantomyx sheds one glowing fur every full moon; marsh villagers frame these in birchwood for good fortune.

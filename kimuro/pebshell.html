About Pebshell

Pebshell is a small round tortoise with a smooth grey-brown domed shell marked by a single small blue gemstone embedded in the top. Its four stubby legs shuffle slowly through the Amber Desert's hot sand, and it blinks with a look of calm concentration that makes it seem older than it is. The blue gem is thought to be a piece of ancient amber desert crystal it absorbed at hatching. Pebshell sleeps buried to its shell-rim in warm sand and emerges only when the temperature drops at dusk.

Field note: Pebshell can stay buried in sand for up to four days without surfacing, surviving entirely on stored warmth.

About Rockarmor

Rockarmor is a stocky, broad-shouldered tortoise whose shell has roughened into a thick layered carapace of grey and amber stone. Its face has settled into a permanent squint - whether from the desert glare or deep thought, nobody is certain. When it plants its feet and sets its shell flat, even strong winds rolling across the desert floor can't budge it. Caravans have learned to camp in the wind shadow of a resting Rockarmor, which seems unbothered by company as long as nobody touches its shell.

Field note: The grooves in Rockarmor's shell map perfectly to the ridge patterns found in the oldest amber rock formations of the desert.

About Crystallyx

Crystallyx carries an eruption of pale ice-blue crystal spikes across its back like a mountain range, each spike as long as a grown person is tall. Its shell has become geological - thick as a fortress wall, with visible mineral strata running through it. Despite its mass, it moves with deliberate calm, each footstep leaving a crater in the desert floor. The crystals on its back are said to have taken five hundred years to form, and they ring like bells when the desert wind passes through them at exactly the right angle.

Field note: When desert winds hit the crystals at the right angle just before dawn, the sound carries for thirty kilometers.

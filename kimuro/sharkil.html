About Sharkil

Sharkil is a stocky slate-blue shark that navigates the flooded lower tunnels of the Shadow Caves, its wide blunt snout pressing into crevices in search of the small phosphorescent creatures that live there. Its eyes are proportionally large for reading in near-darkness and its dorsal fin is slightly flattened to let it move through low passages. Children who visit the cave pools find Sharkil circling their lantern light with great interest, drawn by the brightness it almost never sees.

Field note: Sharkil is the only cave-dwelling shark that actively seeks out light rather than avoiding it.

About Sharclaw

Sharclaw has developed hind legs tipped with curved claws it uses to anchor itself against the cave-tunnel currents. Its blue body is now dark as deep water and its jaw has widened, rows of teeth visible even when its mouth is closed. It rules the larger underground pools of the Shadow Caves, and the bioluminescent fish give it a wide berth. The guardians recorded it caching small objects - bone fragments, polished stones, lost tools - near the cave's oldest old carvings, as if protecting them.

Field note: Every cache Sharclaw keeps contains exactly one polished stone among the rest - no one knows how it picks which stone.

About Megashark

Megashark fills the main underground river of the Shadow Caves from bank to bank, its enormous body a deep blue-black so dark it looks like a section of the cave itself has come alive. Its jaw opens to a width that could swallow a doorway and its small eyes carry the cold patience of something that has outlasted every light source this cave has ever held. When Abalont's shadow swept through the region, Megashark submerged so deep even the darkness could not find it - and the words it was guarding with it.

Field note: The current in the Shadow Caves' main river changes direction each time Megashark turns around - it is large enough to redirect the flow.

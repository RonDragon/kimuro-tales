About Vexmite

Vexmite (beetle line) is a squat, round-bodied grub in a smooth purple shell, with a squinting green face peeking from beneath the carapace and two small stubby legs. It has a perpetually sleepy look, blinking slowly at anything new. In the Shadow Caves it rolls down sloped passages to travel, bouncing cheerfully off walls. Despite its easygoing manner, it secretes a faint purple irritant from its shell joints that keeps larger creatures from picking it up. Early-era cave explorers called it the 'grumbling pebble.'

Field note: Vexmite (beetle) can curl into a near-perfect sphere and roll more than thirty metres on a sloped cave floor without losing speed.

About Vexshell

Vexshell (beetle line) has shed its grub roundness into a proper upright beetle-form with a segmented purple carapace, six strong legs, and two large curved purple horns sweeping upward from its head like a rhinoceros beetle's crown. Compound purple eyes now scan the Shadow Caves with sharp alertness. The purple sheen on its shell is mildly toxic to the touch, discouraging any who try to handle it without respect. It is territorial about its stretch of cave wall and will charge sideways-on at intruders, leading with one horn.

Field note: The twin horns of Vexshell can be heard grinding together when it is agitated - a sound that echoes strangely in cave tunnels and carries far further than expected.

About Vexarch

Vexarch sprawls wide and heavy, a cathedral of dark purple armored segments bristling with blunt spines across its entire dorsal surface. The smooth green undercarriage is now plated and its six thick legs leave deep prints in cave clay. Purple venom seeps from every joint seam, pooling in shallow pits it claims as its den. Explorers mapping the Shadow Caves found they could navigate by avoiding the pools - wherever they led, Vexarch waited at the center. It is both keystone predator and reluctant protector of the cave ecosystem, and nothing crosses its domain without its knowledge.

Field note: Vexarch secretes enough venom passively over one week to fill a small clay jar - cave herbalists harvest the outer-ring drips (at great personal risk) for antidote research.

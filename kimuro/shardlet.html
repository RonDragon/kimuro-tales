About Shardlet

Shardlet is a compact dark-grey armadillo with rows of raised stone-plate scales across its back and small round eyes that peer out from a triangular wedge head. Its four sturdy legs splay wide for balance on the Crystal Mines' uneven floor. When frightened it tucks its legs and rolls into a smooth stone ball. Children working the upper tunnels use Shardlets as living test weights - if one will sleep in a corner, the rock there is stable enough to dig.

Field note: A curled Shardlet is indistinguishable from a loose stone - miners have accidentally put them in their pockets.

About Shardbeast

Shardbeast has grown into a broad-chested quadruped with a broad blunt head and thick stone-plate armour covering its shoulders and haunches. Crystal fragments jut from its back like rough-cut gems and its horn has lengthened into a single forward spike it uses to wedge apart seams in the rock face. It moves with slow deliberate purpose through the Crystal Mines, its footsteps leaving shallow pits in the stone. The guardians valued it as a natural excavator - every path Shardbeast opens reveals words the old rocks have been holding.

Field note: Every slab Shardbeast moves exposes mineral writing from the ancient earth - geologists argue whether it's pattern or language.

About Shardking

Shardking fills a tunnel from wall to wall, its body entirely encased in fused crystal-and-stone plate that has grown over centuries of the Crystal Mines pressing around it. Its massive horned head hangs low and forward, and its tail sweeps slowly, grinding sparks from the floor. The mines themselves seem to have adopted it - new crystal formations appear wherever Shardking rests, as though the earth is building a throne. Ancient lore says Shardking can feel the weight of every mark carved into stone throughout the entire region.

Field note: When Shardking exhales, a fine quartz dust settles on every surface nearby - miners call it the king's blessing.

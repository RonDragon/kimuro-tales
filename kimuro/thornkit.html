About Thornkit

A fluffy sandy-tan puppy-sized creature with oversized ears, a little round face, and a quick curious temperament, Thornkit scurries across the Shadow Caves' sloped rock floors in short investigative bursts. Its fur is pale brown with faint darker markings, and the tips of its ears are dark. It has an excellent nose - it can find a freshwater seep through twenty metres of solid rock by smell. Thornkit travels in loose groups of four or five, and when they settle for the night they pile into a heap, each one with its nose tucked against another's fur for warmth.

Field note: Thornkit can locate a hidden water source through solid rock up to twenty metres away purely by smell - cave explorers bring them as guides.

About Thorixon

Thorixon has grown lean and long-legged, its pale coat shifting to a warm amber-brown with dark saddle-markings along its back, and its mane developing into a loose ruff of longer guard-hairs. It carries itself with a wiry alertness in the Shadow Caves, never fully committing its weight before testing the ground beneath. It uses low rock ledges to survey routes before committing, and it can change direction at full sprint without any visible preparation. When Thorixon raises its mane ruff, other cave-dwelling creatures know to clear the passage.

Field note: Thorixon can change direction at a dead sprint without any body-rotation warning - researchers cannot reliably predict which way it will turn.

About Spinalord

Spinalord is a broad, shaggy predator that fills the widest Shadow Cave corridors with its presence - a tawny amber-brown beast with a massive ragged mane that catches cave-drafts and makes it appear even larger than it is. Long guard-hairs sweep back from its spine like a dark pennant. It has lived in the Shadow Caves long enough to memorize every echo and knows exactly which tunnel acoustics project its voice deepest. Spinalord does not chase prey; it calls once, and the cave itself seems to respond. Ancient cave-writing says it taught the shadows to carry a warning.

Field note: Spinalord's territorial howl creates standing waves in the Shadow Caves that can be heard through solid rock faces a hundred metres from the source.

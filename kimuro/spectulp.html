About Spectulp

A tiny lavender jellyfish no wider than a hand, Spectulp drifts through the shadow caves with a single wide eye dominating its pale bell. Four wispy translucent tentacles trail beneath it like pale ribbons, and a faint spiral pattern swirls along its cap. It is nearly weightless, carried wherever cave drafts wish. The guardians called it a wandering thought - something that had not yet decided what it wanted to become. It lingers near glowing crystal walls, staring at its own reflection for hours.

Field note: Spectulp will follow any light source for up to thirty minutes before drifting off again.

About Wispulse

Growing to roughly knee height, Wispulse has a rounded purple-tinged bell etched with swirling markings and eight long tentacles that fan outward and downward. Its single large eye has deepened from pale to violet, and a faint bioluminescent glow pulses along each tentacle tip. It moves with surprising deliberateness - choosing its path rather than drifting - and has been seen pressing a tentacle tip against cave walls as though reading something written there long ago.

Field note: Each tentacle tip glows a different shade when Wispulse is concentrating; explorers use the colour to guess its mood.

About Thanatopus

Thanatopus has shed the jellyfish grace of its youth and expanded into a looming skull-faced form: a broad grey-blue domed head carved with hollow eye sockets, a wide grimacing mouth, and eight thick muscular tentacles that writhe and grip cave stone with ease. Its appearance is solemn rather than threatening - old explorers say it guards the deepest shadow cave passages where Abalont's whispers still echo, and that it lets only those who know a name it has never heard pass unimpeded.

Field note: The shadow caves fall completely silent when Thanatopus extends all eight tentacles flat - a warning the deep passage is sealed.

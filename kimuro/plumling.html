About Plumling

Plumling is a tiny round fluffball of soft pink feathers with two enormous round eyes that take up most of its face. A small yellow beak peeks out from the fluff, and its stubby wings are barely visible under all the downy plumage. It lives in the lowest spire crannies of the Dragon Spire, puffed up against the wind. When the spire's updrafts kick in at dawn, Plumling tucks its wings in and lets itself tumble upward like a pink dandelion seed - shrieking happily the whole way. Dragon Spire climbers carry Plumlings in their pockets because the little owlets sneeze out a burst of warm air that rekindles frost-bitten fingers.

Field note: A Plumling sneezes precisely twelve times every hour - and the thirteenth sneeze always smells faintly of lightning.

About Plumwing

Plumwing stands upright on two taloned feet, its pink-and-gold feathers now arranged in neat layered tufts. Ear-tufts have grown from the top of its head like small horns, and its amber-gold eyes are sharp and focused. The wings have filled out into broad, capable fans edged with flight feathers. It perches on the Dragon Spire's mid-level ledges and watches the skies for hours, rotating its head nearly all the way around when something catches its interest. When Plumwing opens its wings fully and catches a spire updraft, it can sail upward for half a minute without a single wingbeat, eyes fixed on some distant point only it can see.

Field note: Plumwing can identify any word spoken in the wind from half a mile away, rotating one ear-tuft toward the sound like an antenna.

About Plumarch

Plumarch is a magnificent wing-spread owl creature whose pink-and-gold plumage blazes with bioluminescent patterns in full flight. Its wings are enormous, each primary feather edged with a warm golden glow that illuminates the spire's fog. The ear-tufts have grown long and swept back, giving it a crowned look, and its golden eyes see through both dark and storm. It is the Dragon Spire's living lantern - explorers lost in the high crags need only look for the moving glow of a Plumarch's wings to find their way back to the paths. The guardians say Plumarch is the only creature that has ever sat on the spire's highest point and looked across all four regions at once.

Field note: The glow of Plumarch's wing-edges brightens whenever a name it has never heard is spoken in the spire's ancient guardian language.

About Synyxling

Synyxling is a soft purple blob-creature with two long wobbly eye-stalks and a wide gentle smile below a freckled body. It drifts through the Shadow Caves on currents of air too faint for anything else to feel, bumping softly off the walls and turning with lazy contentment. It has no fixed shape and no clear idea of where it is going, which suits it perfectly. Those who see Synyxling in the cave say it radiates a faint warmth - not heat exactly, but a feeling like remembering something pleasant you had forgotten.

Field note: Being within arm's reach of a Synyxling for five minutes consistently causes people to recall a happy memory they had not thought of in years.

About Synxoid

Synxoid has grown more purposeful - its purple body has extended several short writhing tendrils around its base and its eye-stalks have multiplied into a small cluster at the top of its rounded form. Purple speckles cover its surface and it emits a faint glow from no clearly identifiable source. It clings to the ceiling of the Shadow Caves during the day and descends at night to drift through the cave floor pools, trailing its tendrils through the still water as if feeling for something lost beneath the surface. What it seeks, nobody has established.

Field note: Synxoid always hovers at exactly the same height above whatever surface it is moving across - never touching, never straying.

About Cosmynx

Cosmynx has become something formless and vast - a wide dark-purple mantle of shifting matter covered in glowing psychic nodes, with tendrils that dissolve at their tips into shadow rather than ending. It rests on the floor of the deepest Shadow Cave chamber, its body spreading wider than any room seems able to contain. The air above Cosmynx is always completely still, and the cave stones beneath it are faintly warm. Ancient cave legends describe Cosmynx as the thing that remembers everything the shadow forgot, storing the echo of every voice ever raised underground.

Field note: The glowing nodes on Cosmynx's body rearrange into different patterns every night - no pattern has ever repeated.

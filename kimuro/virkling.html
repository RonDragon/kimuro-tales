About Virkling

Virkling is a plump, bright-eyed green leopard cub with vivid lime-and-grass green fur spotted in bold darker green rosettes. Two small curved ears sit wide on its head and its tail curls up in a cheerful hook. It bounds through the Dark Forest with enormous energy, pouncing on fallen leaves and investigating every mushroom ring it finds. Akilla's forest was its home before the shadow fell, and even now Virkling returns to the old guardian-paths, sniffing the moss for traces of the old protectors. It trusts slowly - but once it does, its loyalty is absolute.

Field note: Virkling follows every new path in the Dark Forest twice - once to test it, once to memorize it - and never uses the same route twice in a row.

About Viridaunt

Viridaunt moves with quiet, self-assured grace through the Dark Forest, its emerald leopard coat now full and rich with rounded rosettes. It patrols the boundary between the safe inner forest and the shadow-tainted outer fringes, marking trees with claw-rakes at exactly child-head height as a warning to others. It keeps Akilla's old patrol routes alive through pure habit and memory. Children who pass through its territory and speak honestly find it falling into step beside them - a green guardian for the old paths.

Field note: Viridaunt claws a fresh mark into a guardian-tree every morning at dawn; foresters who study these marks can predict its daily route with near-perfect accuracy.

About Virantheon

Virantheon stands upright on powerful haunches, broad-shouldered and commanding, its vivid green body bright even in the deepest Dark Forest shadow. Huge rosette-spots glow with forest energy and its heavy tail sweeps slow and deliberate. Where it walks, the shadow retreats. It carries the memory of every guardian word Akilla ever spoke in this forest, and repeats them in a low rumble at the roots of the oldest trees as though feeding them meaning. Children who earn its trust say it places a single large paw on their shoulder, transferring a warmth that lasts for days.

Field note: The rosettes on Virantheon's coat rearrange slightly over decades - old illustrations show different patterns, and scholars track the changes like a living constellation map.

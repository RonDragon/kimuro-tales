About Serplet

Serplet is a small bright-green snake coiled into a neat spiral, with a round cheerful face and warm amber eyes. Tiny overlapping scales glint in the faint cave light as it watches newcomers with open curiosity. It makes its home in mossy cracks near the cave entrance where faint daylight still filters in, feeding on leaves that drift down from above. Children in the Shadow Caves learn their first tracking by matching prints to the patterns Serplet traces in the soft mud.

Field note: Serplet always coils the same direction - change it, and it will huff and recoil immediately back the other way.

About Vipray

Vipray has grown into a thick-bodied serpent with bold yellow-green banding across its scales and a flattened head bearing two curved fangs it shows when threatened. It moves with deliberate S-curves through the cave tunnels, pressing its belly against cool stone. The poison it carries has a faintly floral scent - the guardians discovered it marks territory by pressing its chin to a surface and exhaling, leaving an invisible trail of herbal musk that other creatures avoid for days.

Field note: The herbal musk Vipray leaves on a rock lasts exactly three days - locals use it as an informal cave calendar.

About Hydravex

Enormous and ancient, Hydravex coils in the deepest chambers of the Shadow Caves where the roots of surface trees press through the ceiling. Its scales are a deep jade-green marked by thick gold bands, and its broad head carries back-swept horns above a jaw that can spread wide enough to swallow a boulder. Venom seeps constantly from its hollow fangs, dripping into a pool below that glows faintly with bioluminescence. The guardians say it once sealed the cave mouth to protect the words stored inside from Abalont's hunger.

Field note: The glowing pool beneath Hydravex's resting coils has never been fully mapped - explorers always turn back before they reach the far end.

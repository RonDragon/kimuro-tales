About Corafish

Corafish is a tiny, exquisitely pretty fish - pink-tinged, round-bodied, with an oversized shimmering tail-fin and one enormous deep-blue eye that takes up a third of its face. It swims in slow, wobbling spirals through Poison Marsh's murky channels, trailing a faint bioluminescent wake. The glow is just bright enough to read by for five seconds, and marsh-walkers claim that if they watch carefully they can sometimes see a word written in the light-trail before it fades.

Field note: Corafish's light-trail persists for precisely five seconds - long enough to follow it to the next reef if you are watching for it.

About Coralux

Coralux has grown into a graceful mid-sized fish with pearl-pink and lavender scales, a crown of small coral-branches sprouting between its eyes, and a flowing double tail-fin edged in gold. A string of tiny luminous pearls rings its midsection like a belt of lights. In Poison Marsh it navigates the most toxic channels with ease, its pearl-lights never flickering - they only change colour when it has found a word the marsh has tried to dissolve. It guards them by circling them three times before swimming on.

Field note: Coralux's pearl-lights shift from gold to blue the instant it detects a word dissolving in the marsh toxins.

About Coralumina

Coralumina is magnificent - a fully bloomed fish-sovereign trailing vast ribbons of flowing finnage in every colour of reef coral, its body iridescent and soft-lit from within, its coral-crown now a full tiara of interlocking branch-antlers. Even in the darkest reach of Poison Marsh, Coralumina carries its own dawn with it, and the water around it turns transparent for a body-length in every direction. The guardians placed it here specifically: a light-keeper for the marsh's hidden words, ensuring they never fully disappear into the dark.

Field note: The water around Coralumina turns completely clear for one body-length - the only patch of readable water in the entire Poison Marsh.

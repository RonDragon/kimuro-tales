About Luxling

Luxling is a small golden-scaled dragon hatchling that glows faintly from within, brightest when it's happy and barely visible when it naps. It hatches nestled in the brightest clearings of the Light Grove, and grove-keepers say a Luxling's glow is often the first sign a clearing is truly safe. It is curious about everything and startles easily, dimming to almost nothing at sudden sounds.

Field note: Luxling's glow syncs faintly with sunrise and sunset even indoors - no light source required, just an internal clock no one has explained.

About Luxdrake

Luxdrake has grown a crest of golden feather-scales that catch and scatter grove light like stained glass, and its steady glow now lights a small clearing on its own after dusk. It patrols the Light Grove's inner paths at dawn and dusk, the two times guardians say the grove is most vulnerable, and shadows are said to visibly shrink back from its crest-light without it ever needing to act.

Field note: Luxdrake's crest-scales have been used, dried and pressed, as genuine reading light by grove scholars working past sundown.

About Luxwyrm

Luxwyrm is a radiant, fully-grown dragon whose entire body glows like captured sunlight, wings casting warm gold light across the whole grove canopy when spread. It is the Light Grove's own guardian in miniature, and the Abalont texts say it was the first creature to prove that light itself could choose to protect something, rather than simply exist. It chooses bonded partners slowly, but never lets one go once chosen.

Field note: Luxwyrm's full-spread glow has been documented reaching every corner of the Light Grove's central clearing at once, with no dark patches at all.

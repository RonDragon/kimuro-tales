About Voxnip

Voxnip's dark purple shell is dusted with tiny star-shaped pale specks, as if a piece of the night sky settled and hardened on its back. It moves sideways through the shallow pools of Poison Marsh with multiple slender legs, and its rounded eyes hold a dim bioluminescent glow that never quite goes out. It communicates not by sound but by brief psychic impressions - the feeling of a word rather than the sound of one. Trainers who spend time with Voxnip begin dreaming in unfamiliar words that they recognize as real when they wake.

Field note: Trainers who bond with Voxnip report waking up knowing the way through channels they never walked - the crab passes on what it knows through sleep.

About Voxclaw

Voxclaw has grown pronounced crescent-moon-shaped claws that arc inward like brackets around empty air, and psychic mist curls from their tips in thin spirals. Its shell is wider and darker now, and the star-speck pattern has sharpened into distinct constellation shapes. It walks the waterline of Poison Marsh's deeper pools, and its claws can reach across the boundary between physical and psychic space - it will sometimes pluck a thought directly from the water's surface reflection. The marsh locals say Voxclaw can hear what you were about to say before you decide to say it.

Field note: Voxclaw's crescent claws can grip objects that do not exist yet - researchers have photographed it holding shapes that formed minutes later.

About Voxmaelstrom

Voxmaelstrom is a nightmare architecture of claw, shell, and tendrils - its body has become almost entirely covered in radiating spikes and blade-like appendages, and the psychic mist that once curled from its claws now pours from it in torrents. Around it, reality softens: reflections appear in surfaces that are not mirrors, spoken words linger in the air as visible shapes for several seconds. In the deepest reaches of Poison Marsh, Voxmaelstrom keeps a perfectly still pool that no wind touches - there it reads the words that pass through the marsh in the thoughts of every creature that enters.

Field note: Voices raised within a kilometer of Voxmaelstrom appear briefly as glowing shapes above the still pool it guards - the pool's surface is the marsh's memory.

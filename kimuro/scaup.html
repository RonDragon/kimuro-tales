About Scaup

Scaup is a small, vividly purple chameleon-type creature with big round golden eyes and a coiled tail it wraps around branches like a corkscrew. Its body is covered in small raised scales that gleam with a faint toxic sheen. It moves along the lower stems of the Poison Marsh's plants with precise, measured steps - never rushing, always watching. The yellow eyes seem to process information much faster than anything its small body suggests; it has been seen solving two separate problems simultaneously, one with each eye, while holding perfectly still. It is strange, small, and deeply observant.

Field note: Scaup solves two separate visual problems at once by directing each eye independently - one tracks prey, the other watches for threats.

About Scaldor

Scaldor has grown longer and more imposing, its purple scales now deeper and faintly iridescent, its coiling tail wide and powerful. The toxic sheen on its scales is now clearly visible as a faint slick, and it uses this to mark the boundaries of its territory in the Poison Marsh - any creature that follows Scaldor's scent trail without invitation will regret it. It has developed partial camouflage that works especially well in the marsh's purple-tinged shadow-water, where it rests half-submerged, only the golden eyes visible. Those eyes never blink.

Field note: Scaldor has maintained the same ten-meter territorial boundary markers for three years without variation - it refreshes them every forty-eight hours exactly.

About Scalerath

Scalerath is a magnificent and dangerous creature - a deep purple dragon-chameleon, its body now muscular and armored with great fin-like back-spines and a magnificent spiraling tail. Its golden eyes burn with intelligence, and the toxic aura it radiates has turned the water in a ten-meter radius around its resting pool a permanently iridescent purple. It is the apex guardian of the Poison Marsh, the creature that other venomous things treat as law. Abalont tried to co-opt the marsh by poisoning its water-songs; Scalerath swallowed each one, neutralized the shadow-toxin, and exhaled the clean word back into the air.

Field note: Scalerath is immune to every poison found in its region - it identifies new toxins by tasting them once and has never been affected twice.

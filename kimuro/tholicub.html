About Tholicub

A rotund, steel-grey rhino cub with a nubby single horn and a scattering of round brass-gold rivets studding its flanks, Tholicub bumbles around the Steel Fortress looking for things to headbutt. It is compact and low to the ground, and its little rivets clank against the fortress stone when it rolls over. Though it seems clumsy, Tholicub has never been seen to accidentally topple anything it truly cared about - there is a precise sense of force behind every bump. The fortress smiths say Tholicub tests every new installation by butting it once, and they rely on its judgement.

Field note: Tholicub has never headbutted a wall that was structurally unsound - fortress engineers use it as a stress-test inspector.

About Tholoderm

Tholoderm has grown into a thick-muscled rhino with broadened horn and smooth steel-grey hide interrupted by lines of embedded gold-toned rivets that run along its shoulders and neck like armour seams. It lowers its head and charges with controlled, devastating precision - it has never charged anything it did not intend to hit, and it has never missed. At the Steel Fortress, Tholoderm patrols the outer walls on a precise schedule, and fortress guards set their watches by the sound of its footfalls rounding the eastern corner each dawn.

Field note: Tholoderm has completed its wall-patrol circuit at the same time within two minutes every morning for as long as fortress records have been kept.

About Tholoknight

Tholoknight is a wall-breaker wearing gold and stone like a knight wears plate - every surface of its massive rhino body is studded with thick brass-gold rivets and reinforced with broad rock-plate panels, and its twin curling horns sweep outward like fortress battlements made flesh. When it plants its four legs and braces, nothing short of a directed explosion has ever moved it. Its eyes are calm and steady. The Steel Fortress's oldest banners depict Tholoknight facing the gate - not as a decoration, but as a promise to any would-be invader: this door does not open.

Field note: A Tholoknight in full brace-stance has been measured by fortress engineers to exert more shear resistance than a four-metre stone buttress.

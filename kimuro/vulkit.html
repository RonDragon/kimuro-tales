About Vulkit

A tiny orange fox with enormous ears and a white-spotted coat, Vulkit trots through the Sand Dunes on dainty paws, its single fluffy tail always wagging. Two small flame-tipped wisps flicker from its ear tips when it hears a brave new voice. It was spotted dancing in the dune embers the night the first guardian was lost, and locals say it guards each buried bone like a precious secret.

Field note: Vulkit taps its front paw three times before pouncing - a habit no one can explain.

About Vulcara

Grown sleek and lean, Vulcara bears a vivid green leafy ruff around its neck and three full-flame tails that stream behind it in the desert wind. Its orange coat deepens to ember-red at the shoulders, and sparks trail from every pawstep. It circles the hottest dunes at noon, memorising the memory-stones half-buried in the sand - the ones the dunes would swallow if no one kept watch.

Field note: Vulcara's three tails burn at different heights, and sand-walkers read them like a fire-signal.

About Vulkarion

Vulkarion is no longer a fox but a great coiling fire-dragon: thick red-and-orange scales ripple down its serpent body, green flame-edged fins flare along its spine, and four powerful clawed legs grip the ground while blazing wings unfurl overhead. It circles the Sand Dunes at dusk, its roar shaking loose buried memory-stones, reading them aloud into the fire before the dunes reclaim them. Where the dragon breathes, unfamiliar names bloom in the ash.

Field note: Every scale on Vulkarion holds a word of the old language - together they form the first complete story ever told.

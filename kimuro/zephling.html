About Zephling

Zephling is a compact grey-blue parrot-like bird with a wedge of purple-gold wing-flash visible when it spreads and fans its feathers in challenge. Its round eyes are bright with the particular alertness of a creature raised in high winds, and it grips any perch with both feet and refuses to let go when gusts hit. On the Steel Fortress battlements it learns the garrison's warning-calls by mimicry - and it has them all correct by the second try.

Field note: Zephling always repeats a sound back slightly faster than it was made - scholars call this acceleration 'enthusiasm inflation.'

About Zephsoar

Zephsoar has grown into a fierce raptor with steel-blue and deep purple plumage, a crest of narrow upswept feathers, and bright eyes that track everything. Pale golden feathers catch light along the wing's leading edge. It rides the thermal columns above the Steel Fortress, scanning for weak words - the kind that crumble under pressure - and dives on anything that tries to pass without a good one. Riku the thunder-horse once raced these updrafts, and Zephsoar has not forgotten.

Field note: Zephsoar adjusts its crest-feathers in response to the question being asked - experienced trainers can read the angle and predict whether a student will pass.

About Zephyros

Zephyros is a regal and enormous eagle, its blue-purple body lit with a full halo of electric-gold feathers that frame its head and chest like a war-banner. Its wingspan fills the sky over the Steel Fortress when fully spread, and the fortress banners flatten in the downdraft every time it passes. It carries messages no one else can reach - words that need to cross the winds between regions - and it still searches the high skies for the last free guardian, the one Riku was waiting for.

Field note: Every message Zephyros has ever carried arrives at exactly the right moment - never early, never late, regardless of distance.

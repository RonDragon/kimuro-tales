About Scarabite

Scarabite stands upright on stubby legs, its small round brown body topped with a single softly glowing green horn - a tendril of ghost energy it absorbed from Ghost Hollow's misty air. Its wide eyes are always blinking, startled by everything, and it taps the tip of its horn against objects to "taste" whether they hold a word or a memory. The hollow's wandering spirits seem fond of it, drifting near as if drawn by its gentle glow.

Field note: A Scarabite's green horn light flickers faster the closer a ghost is - hollows residents use them as spirit-weather vanes.

About Scarabug

Scarabug has grown wide and heavy, its body now a deep violet-mauve, and its single horn has thickened into a blunt ridge that it uses to push through Ghost Hollow's clinging fog banks. Four legs carry it low to the ground with a determined hunch, and it marks territory by pressing its horn into soft earth and leaving glowing impressions. Old trainers say Scarabug's shadow is always slightly larger than its body, as though something dark rides behind it.

Field note: Scarabug presses its horn into mud to leave glowing prints; Ghost Hollow paths are often lit faintly by rows of these marks.

About Scarabyss

Scarabyss towers over the hollow, a dark purple rhinoceros beast whose horns have multiplied across its wide brow and shoulders like a crown of shadows. Its legs are thick as old roots and its back is broad enough that moss has begun to grow in the crevices of its plates. When Scarabyss roars, the ghosts of Ghost Hollow scatter for half an hour, and the fog lifts entirely. The guardians believe it holds the boundary between the living and the lost, and that it chose that duty willingly.

Field note: When Scarabyss stands still in fog, its outline looks enough like a ruined tower that travellers have mistaken it for ruins and sheltered against its flank.

About Stellaby

Stellaby is a bright blue-and-gold fawn with a spiky electric mane, wide lime-green eyes, and a small five-pointed golden star glowing at the tip of its tail. Tiny lightning sparks dance off its pointed ears as it bounds across Storm Spire's high plateaus. It is relentlessly energetic and cannot stand still - each time it stops it paws the ground twice and immediately starts again. Its sparks leave tiny scorch-trails in the shape of constellations, and on clear nights Storm Spire's highest plateau looks like a star chart drawn in miniature.

Field note: The scorch-trail constellation patterns Stellaby leaves match twelve actual star formations visible from Storm Spire - researchers debate whether it knows this.

About Cosmyx

Cosmyx has grown into a powerful deep-blue quadruped with a flowing golden electric mane that crackles with constant voltage. The golden star at its tail has grown larger and brighter, casting light in a wide arc when it runs. Its hooves strike gold sparks from Storm Spire's stone with each stride. It gallops the storm-ridge circuits at speeds fast enough to generate their own updraft, and small stormclouds form briefly in its wake. Trainers who bond with Cosmyx find their own thinking speeds up noticeably - a psychic spillover from its lightning-fast mind.

Field note: Cosmyx's full-speed run generates a localized electrical field strong enough to power a small lamp for ten minutes afterward.

About Celesthorn

Celesthorn is a breathtaking gold-and-blue celestial beast with a massive flowing electric mane of lightning-white and gold, a blazing five-pointed star anchored to its tail, and golden horns that fork like lightning bolts from its brow. Its body radiates a warm electrical glow visible from the valley floor on storm nights. Ancient Storm Spire legends name Celesthorn as the creature that called the first lightning down from the sky to teach the guardians the power of words spoken boldly - the thunder was its answer.

Field note: The lightning above Storm Spire strikes the same four points every storm; those four points mark exactly where Celesthorn stood when it last called down the sky.

About Sparkitail

Sparkitail is a small dark-red fox kit with one fluffy tail tipped in shadow-black and a coat the color of deep ember-red. It moves in short dashes, always close to the cave wall, pausing to sniff the dark carefully before darting to the next shadow. Its tail-tip doesn't glow - it absorbs light, making the space around it fractionally darker, which Sparkitail uses to hide in plain sight on dark cave floors. The guardians of the shadow caves leave small lights at their path junctions specifically because Sparkitail keeps snuffing them out to practice hiding.

Field note: Sparkitail's tail-tip shadow-spot is exactly five centimeters across; experienced shadow-cave guides call it 'one tail-step' in the dark.

About Infernatail

Infernatail has grown into a lean dark-red wolf-fox with multiple spiked dark tails that radiate shadow around it, making it look larger than it is. Embers flicker in its fur when it runs, trailing brief sparks that extinguish immediately in the cave air. It hunts the shadow caves' mid-passages with unsettling expertise, vanishing into darkness between one step and the next and reappearing from a completely different shadow. The guardians say it never sleeps in the same shadow twice, cataloguing every dark corner of the caves in a memory that appears to be perfect.

Field note: Infernatail can find every entrance and exit of any cave it has entered once, without fail, even years later.

About Novatail

Novatail is a massive dark-red multi-tailed beast with a dramatic ruff of dark-spiked fur around its neck and multiple great tails spread like a dark fan, each one tipped with living shadow. Ember-light pulses through its coat continuously, making it look like a creature built from cooling magma draped in night. In the shadow caves it is the apex presence - even the cave's own darkness seems to organize itself around Novatail, shadows pooling in its wake. The guardians call it the Keeper of What Is Hidden, believing it guards the secret language of the caves from those who would misuse it.

Field note: Novatail's tails never all move at once; they rotate in a slow wave-pattern, each shadow momentarily lighting the next before going dark again.

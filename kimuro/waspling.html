About Waspling

Waspling is a small, round wasp nymph with a yellow-and-black striped abdomen, big red compound eyes, and two tiny translucent wings that already hum faintly with static charge. Its stubby antennae twitch whenever a machine nearby switches on, and it drifts toward electrical panels in the Steel Fortress like a compass needle finding north. Apprentice engineers learned to watch for Buzzites nesting in open circuit boxes - a tiny shock to the fingers tells them one has arrived. It chittered busily as it explored every new path the guardians showed it.

Field note: A Waspling can drain a fully charged lantern battery in under two minutes just by resting on it.

About Vespwing

Vespwing has grown into a sleek, menacing wasp with a vivid yellow-and-black striped thorax, two pairs of buzzing wings that crackle visibly with arcing electricity, and a curved stinger that glows orange at the tip. Its red eyes scan the Steel Fortress catwalks with sharp intelligence. It patrols circuits of the Fortress that no guard bothers to walk, shocking loose bolts back into their sockets and driving off rust-beetles with targeted zaps. Trainers say Vespwing never stings twice in the same spot.

Field note: Each wing produces a slightly different frequency; the interfering buzz keeps smaller pests at bay for twenty paces in every direction.

About Apidrone

Apidrone commands the air above the Steel Fortress like a living stormcloud, its broad armored thorax banded in deep gold and black, four powerful wings trailing continuous arcs of electricity, and a reinforced stinger the size of a short sword. Twin orange compound eyes can track a moving target through dense industrial smoke. The Fortress's eldest engineers claim the first Apidrone arrived with the storm that fused the Fortress's original walls - that it and the steel were shaped by the same lightning bolt. Its roar shuts down unshielded machines within thirty paces.

Field note: The continuous charge field Apidrone generates is strong enough to magnetize iron filings into perfect circular patterns on the workshop floor.

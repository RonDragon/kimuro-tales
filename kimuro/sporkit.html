About Sporkit

Sporkit is a tiny round turtle with a smooth mushroom-cap growing from the top of its shell, spotted in warm tan and brown. Its four stubby limbs barely lift it off the ground, and it spends most of its time sitting perfectly still on rocky outcroppings of Granite Heights, absorbing warmth and moisture with equal contentment. It can pull its head and all four legs in at once, leaving only the mushroom cap visible - which makes it look remarkably like an ordinary toadstool until it decides to move.

Field note: Sporkit's mushroom cap changes color slightly with weather - it darkens before rain, even in the usually-dry heights.

About Fungrok

Fungrok's mushroom cap shell has grown large enough to shelter its entire body, spotted tan and brown with a frill of pale spores along the edge. It sits upright now with a broad flat face and watchful eyes, moving only when necessary - and never faster than a stroll. The granite outcroppings of the Heights are dotted with resting Fungroks that look indistinguishable from boulders to the untrained eye. When multiple Fungroks gather in the same area, their cap spores combine to create a gentle sleep-haze that settles over the rocks like mist.

Field note: Fungrok has been recorded sitting motionless in the same spot for eleven consecutive days - it emerged only when the sun finally moved fully off its rock.

About Fungshell

Fungshell has grown into a massive squat tortoise nearly as wide as it is tall, its mushroom-cap shell expanded into a wide flat canopy that acts as a true roof when it hunkers down. Its skin has thickened into warty plated hide, and a thick ring of root-like tendrils hangs from the cap's edge, anchoring it to the ground when it sleeps. Granite Heights locals have built rest shelters near Fungshell nesting sites because the creature's presence keeps dangerous rockslides from reaching paths below - its weight and roots stabilize the slopes.

Field note: When a Fungshell sneezes - which happens roughly once a month - the spore cloud it releases can fog an entire mountainside for half a day.

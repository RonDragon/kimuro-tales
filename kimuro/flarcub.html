About Flarcub

Flarcub stands on four clawed legs with an armored red-and-black body plated in overlapping scales like a knight's armor. A tuft of bright orange fire blazes at the tip of its tail, and its eyes are wide and golden. Even at this young stage its scale plates are fully formed, giving it the look of a miniature warrior. It runs along sand dune crests at dusk, leaving tiny claw marks and scorch spots where its flame-tail sweeps too close. Explorers who camp near the sand dunes often wake to find a Flarcub has singed the edges of their tents while inspecting the campfire.

Field note: Flarcub can hold a single ember in its closed claws for up to six hours without burning itself.

About Pyrdrake

Pyrdrake has grown into a fierce four-legged drake with a heavily armored red-black body and a broad, flat-skulled head crowned with spiky frills. The flame at its tail has expanded into a roaring blaze. Its claws are wider and sharper, and the scale plates on its chest and shoulders have fused into a nearly impenetrable breastplate. It hunts alone across the sand dunes, using the shimmer of heat above the sand to hide its silhouette. When it stops moving, it looks exactly like a red-streaked dune rock - only the steady flicker of its tail flame gives it away.

Field note: Pyrdrake buries itself in hot sand up to its neck each midday to supercharge its flame, then erupts out in a shower of sparks.

About Infernolith

Infernolith looms over the sand dunes on four pillar-like legs, its body a fortress of black-edged crimson scale-armor. The flame crown blazes from the nape of its neck, and its broad head is flanked by swept-back horns and a chest so wide it blocks out the horizon when it turns broadside. Three separate flame plumes burn from its back like living torches. Its footsteps melt a thin glass layer into the sand. The desert clans say Infernolith was born from the first volcanic vent that burst through the sand dunes and has burned without rest ever since, guarding the dunes from those who would put out every last fire.

Field note: The glass prints Infernolith leaves in the sand contain tiny trapped air bubbles - each one is shaped like a mark from the ancient fire-sigils.

About Tortlet

Tortlet stands on two legs, clutching a smaller detached shell-disc in one stubby fist like a shield or a toy it found very interesting. Its main shell is a warm sandy-brown dome across its back, and its face is set in the focused expression of a creature already measuring everything for eventual combat potential. It practices blocking moves with its shell-disc on anything it encounters in the Amber Desert - rocks, cacti, other Shellmites - and has never once been observed sitting still longer than a minute.

Field note: Tortlet has memorized the exact shell-disc throw angle needed to ricochet off three desert boulders and return to its hand.

About Terrashell

Terrashell has grown into a broad-shouldered bipedal tortoise-warrior with thick shell plates covering its chest and arms in overlapping layers. Its face has grown fiercer and its posture more deliberately defensive - it stands with its weight shifted forward, always ready to brace. The desert sun has darkened its shell to a deep amber-brown and the wear marks from years of combat practice are clearly visible in the scuffed shell surface. It positions itself at the threshold of any area it considers its own and waits. And waits. And waits.

Field note: Terrashell has held a single defensive stance in the same desert canyon gap for sixteen days straight - pausing only to eat.

About Terradon Rex

Terradon Rex is built like a walking fortress - golden-amber shell plates cover every surface of its wide bipedal body in an overlapping architecture that leaves no gap for anything to reach the creature beneath. Its face is broad and stern beneath a heavy shell brow, and it carries itself with the unhurried authority of something that has never once needed to move quickly. Every shell plate is scarred with the marks of past confrontations that ended in Terradon Rex's favor. Amber Desert legends say it is the last line of defense before the desert's oldest ruins - and that nothing has passed it in three hundred years.

Field note: No single impact in recorded history has cracked any of Terradon Rex's shell plates - the oldest ones are over four centuries old.

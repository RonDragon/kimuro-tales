About Solarix

Solarix is a compact golden-blue fawn with upright pointed ears, a swept-back mane of electric-yellow fur, and large bright eyes that carry a small star-spark in each one. Its coat crackles faintly with static when it trots across the high stone paths of the Storm Spire. It picks up speed quickly and turns sharply, using the wind to assist rather than resist its movement. The guardians' young scouts used to race Solarix across the spire's flat-top platforms - it always ran directly into the prevailing wind, leaning forward with complete commitment.

Field note: Solarix's mane stands fully upright exactly four seconds before a lightning strike - storm-spire children use it as a warning bell.

About Asteron

Asteron has grown into a proud and powerful stag with a full mane of deep blue-gold electric fur and a single lightning-bolt shaped growth at the tip of its tail. Its eyes hold steady twin star-sparks and its coat now discharges small visible sparks during rapid acceleration. It gallops the Storm Spire ridgelines at full speed, mane streaming, and the air behind it carries the sharp clean smell of ozone for minutes after. The guardians timed Asteron's ridge runs and found it always travels faster on days when a name it has never heard is spoken for the first time in the region.

Field note: The ozone trail Asteron leaves on a ridge run lasts forty minutes - early-rising travellers use it to tell which routes were safe at dawn.

About Heliarch

Heliarch stands at the Storm Spire summit in a permanent corona of blue-gold electric light, its magnificent full mane billowing with contained lightning. A large golden star-gem blazes at its chest and four great electric feather-wings of storm-light fan from its shoulders. Its eyes are twin suns. When it rears up and cries out, the storm above the Spire responds - clouds rotate, lightning follows the arcs of its movement. The guardians called it the Voice of the Spire - whatever Heliarch announces to the sky, the sky confirms.

Field note: When Heliarch rears and calls at the summit, a visible lightning arc follows the exact arc of its raised foreleg - every time, without exception.

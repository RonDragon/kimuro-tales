About Frostmite

Frostmite is a small, round-bodied ice-blue spider with enormous soft eyes that take up most of its face and six delicate legs it holds close to its round body. It clings to the undersides of the cool-moisture leaves in Verdant Valley, spinning tiny hexagonal webs between the stems. It collects morning dew, freezes it into tiny ice beads, and arranges them along its web in patterns that nobody has found a reason for except that they catch the light beautifully. The valley's flower-foragers follow Frostmite trails to find the best dew-heavy flower patches.

Field note: Every ice-bead array Frostmite creates is identical - a hexagon of 18 beads arranged in the exact same pattern each time.

About Cryamid

Cryamid is an athletic, long-legged ice spider with a broad armoured body covered in blue crystal spines and sharp forward-facing eyes. It has abandoned the small hexagonal webs for elaborate geometric trip-wire structures across the valley floor that are nearly invisible when the morning light is right. It waits at the geometric centre - always perfectly centred - and when something blunders into a wire it is there in a fraction of a second. Verdant Valley's insect population has become extremely careful about where they step.

Field note: Cryamid's trip-wire webs are consistently built at the exact height of the most common prey passing through the area.

About Cryoweb

This Cryoweb is the crystal matriarch of the frost-spiders - a large, round-bodied spider with heavy crystal plating on its abdomen and head, long powerful legs, and a constant slow turning motion as it surveys its domain. Its web now covers the entire north-facing slope of Verdant Valley, a permanent frozen silk architecture anchored to rock and tree alike. The valley's ice-creepers and frost-flowers grow densest inside its web perimeter, as if the web itself acts as a cold greenhouse. It has been tending this structure for so long that the valley's summer warmth no longer reaches inside it.

Field note: The temperature inside Cryoweb's web territory is four degrees colder year-round than the surrounding valley - a reliable natural cold-store.

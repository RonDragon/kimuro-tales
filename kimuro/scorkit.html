About Scorkit

Scorkit is a small dark-red quadruped dragon with two rounded wing-buds folded tight against its back and a blunt horn-ridge above its brow. Its scales are rough like dried lava and warm to the touch even on the coldest Dragon Spire mornings. It is perpetually curious and tends to follow larger creatures at a distance, watching what they do before attempting it itself. The first thing most Scorkits learn to do is scratch sparks from the spire's flint outcrops with one claw - a small act, but one that clearly delights them.

Field note: Scorkit's wing-buds, though too small to fly, already vibrate at altitude - locals use this as a signal that a spire wind-shift is coming.

About Scormaw

Scormaw has grown fully into its dark red scales and bat wings now spread wide enough to catch the updrafts that rise along Dragon Spire's cliffs. Its horns have multiplied across its brow in a low crown, and its tail whips in slow arcs when it perches. Fire comes easily now - a short exhale produces a sustained ribbon of flame that it uses to warm its resting ledge before settling. The spire's older dragons tolerate Scormaw on their ridges, which is considered a mark of real promise.

Field note: Scormaw's wingbeats smell distinctly of warm sulfur - spire guides use the scent to know when one is circling above cloud cover.

About Inferscor

Inferscor is a full great dragon - dark burgundy scales that have hardened to the consistency of forge-cooled iron, wide bat wings that block the sun when it soars between spire peaks. Its multiple brow horns sweep back like a crown, and its eyes burn with sustained flame. Each exhale is a controlled column of fire it can hold or release at will, and when it chooses to roar, the sound rolls down from Dragon Spire's summit to the valley below in waves. The guardians' oldest texts describe a creature matching Inferscor as the one who first taught the dragon words of power.

Field note: Inferscor's fire column can burn for nine continuous seconds before it must pause to breathe - the longest documented sustained flame of any Dragon Spire resident.

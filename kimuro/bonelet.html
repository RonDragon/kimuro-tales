About Bonelet

Bonelet is a small spider with a round skull-white carapace above a small pale body, six visible legs, and hollow dark eye-sockets that give it the look of a tiny lost ghost wearing spider shoes. It picks its way through Bug Thicket's leaf litter with careful steps, pausing frequently as if it just forgot where it was going. It is not lost - it is simply processing an enormous amount of ghost-echo information from the soil beneath it, which contains centuries of Bug Thicket's layered spirit-history.

Field note: Bonelet can tell how old a patch of leaf litter is purely by standing on it - it gives a small tremor when the layer beneath is very old.

About Skelspine

Skelspine is a larger pale arachnid with a spined carapace studded with bony protrusions and a rounder, heavier frame than its predecessor. Its hollow eye-sockets now carry a faint ghost glow, and it has learned to use the spines on its back to anchor itself in the undergrowth and become completely still - practically invisible in the thicket's dappled shadows. It eats ghost-energy that gathers in old insect husks, and is thus very useful to have around anywhere the Thicket's restless past tends to accumulate.

Field note: Skelspine can hold perfectly motionless for six hours at a time - the record, logged in the Thicket research register, is nine hours and forty minutes.

About Necrcrawl

Necrcrawl is an enormous pale-white spider with a massive pocked carapace like weathered bone and eight heavy legs that barely make sound as it crosses the Thicket floor. Its body is scarred and ancient, dotted with hollow pits that emit faint ghost-light from whatever it has absorbed over its long centuries in Bug Thicket. It does not spin webs anymore - it simply exists, and the Thicket organizes itself around it with the deference of a forest for its oldest tree. Smaller creatures move out of its path in absolute silence.

Field note: Necrcrawl has crossed Bug Thicket in the same direction on the same night every year for as long as the oldest records reach - no one has determined where it is going.

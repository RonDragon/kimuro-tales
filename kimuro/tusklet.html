About Tusklet

Tusklet is a plump, sandy-beige elephant calf with oversized ears, a stumpy trunk it hasn't quite learned to control yet, and small nubs of tusks beginning to push through its lower jaw. Rocky ridges have started to form across its back like a natural saddle - the first signs that this creature is built for something heavier than just wobbling around Sand Dunes. It uses its trunk to flick sand into the air and watch it fall, a habit that drives dust into everything nearby but delights Tusklet endlessly.

Field note: Tusklet's trunk can't reach its own forehead yet, so it scratches its head on whatever rock happens to be the right height.

About Tuskmaw

Tuskmaw is a solid, four-legged elephant now fully armoured across its back with rows of stone-like plates that have fused with its hide. Its tusks have grown long and curved, streaked with mineral deposits from the sand it constantly roots through. A calm, methodical creature, it paces the same dune ridge every morning - the vibration of its footsteps tells it which dunes are hollow and which are solid. Where it walks, the ground compacts into a reliable path other creatures follow.

Field note: Tuskmaw tests every dune before stepping on it - it has not fallen through a hollow dune in its entire adult life.

About Mammolith

Mammolith is immense - a walking ridge of stone and ancient bone, armoured head-to-tail in thick rocky plates that have grown too massive to remove. Its tusks are long, broad, and scarred by hundreds of battles, curving outward like the gates of a fortress. Every step leaves a deep impression in the desert that fills with sand over the years, forming ridges cartographers have mistaken for natural features. In Sand Dunes, Mammolith is the bedrock given legs - the guardians say it predates the dunes themselves.

Field note: Mammolith leaves footprints two hands deep in packed sand - surveyors have mapped its favourite route by following the indentations alone.

About Puppoxling

A tiny carved wooden puppet no bigger than a lantern, Puppoxling dangles from two red strings that rise into the shadow-cave darkness - nobody has ever seen what holds them. Its round beaked face blinks with wide curious eyes, and it knocks its stubby wood-block limbs together when startled, making a hollow tock-tock sound that echoes through the tunnels. The guardians once believed these strings were the vines of a sleeping cave plant reaching down, weaving its first shape from root and shadow.

Field note: Puppoxling's strings always point toward the deepest part of any cave, no matter how it turns.

About Puppoxid

Puppoxid's body has grown into a fully articulated humanoid marionette - jointed elbows, knees, and a neck that swivels silently. Its strings now number four and vanish upward into the cave ceiling like black roots in reverse. It moves with an unnerving smoothness, each limb swinging just a half-beat after the rest, as though something above is learning how to walk. In the shadow caves it patrols root-choked passages, drawn to the sound of spoken words, tilting its beaked head to listen.

Field note: Puppoxid can stand perfectly still for six hours; trainers only notice it has moved when its shadow is in a different place.

About Marionox

Marionox is encased in burnished gold armor etched with vine-knot patterns, and a spired golden crown rises from its angular head. A sweeping purple cape hangs from its broad shoulders, catching the cave drafts like a sail. Its strings - now thick as rope - fan out in seven directions and hum a low note when it walks, as though the cave itself is the instrument. Ancient texts in the shadow caves call it the Hollow Sovereign, a puppet that grew large enough to pull its own strings.

Field note: When Marionox spreads its cape fully, the shadows in the room rearrange themselves into shapes only children can see.

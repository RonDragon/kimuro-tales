About Bubkin

Bubkin is a round, small, purple creature with stubby fins and a large glowing pink bubble on top of its head that bobs when it moves, as though independently alive. It inhabits the Shadow Caves' quieter pools and tunnels, moving in a gentle slow-drift that belies how quickly it can vanish into the dark when startled. The bubble on its head pulses with a faint psychic light that other shadow-cave creatures find oddly soothing - Bubkin seems to produce it involuntarily as a by-product of thinking hard. It thinks very hard, very often.

Field note: Bubkin's head-bubble pulses in time with its thoughts - the faster it pulses, the harder it's concentrating on something only it can see.

About Toadburst

Toadburst has expanded considerably - a wide, bumpy purple toad with several smaller glowing pink bubble-growths covering its back that pop and reform throughout the day. Each pop releases a brief psychic shimmer visible in the dark caves, and a faint sweet-and-wrong smell that is technically toxic but at cave-toad concentrations only produces an unusual sense of déjà vu. Toadburst sits in the same spot for days at a time, watching the cave's drip-patterns with absolute focus. The shadow creatures of the cave have learned to let it be - disturbing it mid-observation produces a triple-pop that leaves everyone confused about what day it is.

Field note: Disturbing Toadburst mid-observation produces a triple-bubble-pop that causes a brief psychic disorientation lasting roughly one minute.

About Toxotaur

Toxotaur fills a cave chamber the way a thundercloud fills a sky - its massive broad body covered in large luminous pink bubble-nodules that glow and pulse with a rhythm that seems to sync with the heartbeat of anyone in the room. The psychic shimmer it broadcasts is now strong enough to cast visible shadows behind objects. In Ghost Hollow lore it is called the Dream-Lock: a creature that traps Abalont's shadow-agents in looping visions of words they never learned, keeping them confused and harmless in the deepest cave passages until the guardians can deal with them.

Field note: Anything that enters Toxotaur's glow-radius without specific intent will spend the next hour convinced it has forgotten a very important word.

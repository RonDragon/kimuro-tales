About Shellail

Shellail is the smallest creature of the Poison Marsh - a tiny pale blue snail with a perfectly round smooth shell and a soft round body that retracts instantly at the faintest vibration. It leaves a trail of faintly chilled water behind it as it glides through the shallows, and wherever that trail passes, the marsh water clears and the algae thins. Despite being tiny enough to sit on a large leaf without bending it, Shellail is found in some of the marsh's most dangerous spots, seemingly unbothered by the toxins around it. Marsh children collect empty Shellail shells as good-luck tokens.

Field note: Shellail's trail clears an exact thirty-centimeter-wide stripe of algae - so uniform that marsh children use Shellail paths to navigate to the safe swimming spots.

About Frostshell

Frostshell has grown noticeably larger with a more elaborate shell - its smooth spiral now bears a beautiful snowflake pattern in pale blue and white that shimmers when it moves. Its body has taken on a deeper blue, and the trail it leaves is actually cold enough now to feel on bare skin. When threatened, Frostshell withdraws entirely into its shell and releases a ring of freezing water vapor that crystallizes on nearby surfaces in seconds, creating a small frost barrier. Marsh travelers have learned to spot Frostshell's distinctive snowflake-patterned shells from a distance and adjust their routes to use the cleared water it leaves.

Field note: Frostshell always travels in a perfect spiral - start at any point in its trail and follow it inward and you will find where it spent the night.

About Crystail

Crystail is a magnificent sight in the grey-green marsh - a large snail with a crystallized spiral shell whose surface has frozen into angular facets, topped by a small crown of ice crystals that has grown from the shell's apex like a tiny glacier. Its body is a rich deep blue, and it moves with slow majestic purpose, never hurrying, never flinching. The marsh's clear-water channels all connect back to paths that Crystail has traveled repeatedly over centuries. It is believed that the Poison Marsh does not poison its own water simply because Crystail has been filtering it since before the marsh was named.

Field note: The ice crystal crown on Crystail's shell has been photographed from above and below - from directly above, it forms the oldest known sigil, though scholars disagree on what it means.

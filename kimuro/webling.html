About Webling

Webling is a compact black spider with a red hourglass mark on its abdomen and glowing red eyes that flicker like tiny embers. Near Dragon Spire's volcanic vents it lives in crevices where the rock stays warm year-round, using the heat as a hatchery for its eggs. It spins fire-resistant silk around the vent openings, and the silk glows faintly orange in the dark - a little warning lantern that dares other creatures to come close. Despite its intimidating coloring, young Spiderlings are shy and retreat from anything bigger than a pebble.

Field note: Webling silk resists temperatures that would melt tin - Dragon Spire's forge-workers sometimes use it to reinforce heat-seals.

About Araneox

Araneox has grown into a sleek black spider with a larger, more prominent red hourglass marking and a web of glowing orange-red fire-silk that it maintains across Dragon Spire's lava-ledge overhangs. Its eyes burn a steady hot red. In a fight it snaps its legs together to generate friction sparks, igniting a quick fire-burst from its spinnerets. Forge-workers at Dragon Spire leave offerings of iron filings near Araneox's web - legend says it blesses the metal of anyone who respects its territory.

Field note: Araneox fire-silk stays lit for up to three days without fuel - Dragon Spire's forge district uses it as emergency lighting during ash storms.

About Infernocrawl

Infernocrawl is an enormous black-armored spider wreathed in glowing orange lava-web strands that bridge its legs and spread outward like a living fire-trap. Its abdomen carries a blazing hourglass marking that pulses with heat, and its eyes burn like twin forge-coals. The web it casts across Dragon Spire's summit cracks acts as both territory marker and heat-barrier - lava flows have been recorded deflecting around Infernocrawl's anchor-web points. Ancient Spire lore says Infernocrawl was born the day the first volcano erupted, and will only sleep when the last one goes cold.

Field note: Infernocrawl's anchor-silk is woven into Dragon Spire's main bridge supports - the bridge has stood without mortar for four hundred years.

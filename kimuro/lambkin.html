About Lambkin

Lambkin is a bright golden-yellow lamb with a fluffy round body and small curved horns just beginning to form. Two green-tipped sprigs of leaves grow behind its ears like living earrings, and its eyes are wide and bright. It prances through the Crystal Mines' upper passages where filtered light catches on its wool and sends little gold flickers dancing on the cave walls. Young miners leave Lambkin first to explore a new tunnel - its coat glows brighter where the crystal veins are richest.

Field note: Lambkin's wool reflects crystal-light brighter than any lamp - miners use its silhouette as a reading light in passages too narrow for lanterns.

About Caproid

Caproid stands tall and luminous - a golden ram with long curved horns and a broad, confident stance. Green vine-patterns trace its legs like natural armour, and its fleece now holds a steady warm glow rather than just a reflection. It climbs where others hesitate, finding footholds on sheer crystal faces with almost casual precision. In Crystal Mines lore, Caproid marks safe passages through unstable tunnels by pressing its horns to the stone and reading the vibrations - a gift of light showing the living route through darkness.

Field note: Caproid can stand on a crystal ledge the width of a single hoof without wavering, for as long as needed - climbers have recorded it motionless for over an hour.

About Aurelion

Aurelion is a being of living gold - its great branching horns spiral upward like the crown of an ancient tree, and green vines wind through its fleece as though the Crystal Mines' plants have chosen it as a trellis. Its body radiates a steady warm light that reaches every shadow in a wide radius. According to the oldest Crystal Mines legends, Aurelion once stood at the entrance to a collapsed tunnel for three days until every living creature inside found the light it cast and walked to safety.

Field note: The light Aurelion casts promotes crystal growth - geologists have documented formations appearing within days of Aurelion's extended presence in a passage.

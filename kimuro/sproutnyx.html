About Sproutnyx

A small, lush-green kitten with oversized tufted ears and a single bright green leaf sprouting straight up from between them, Sproutnyx pads through the Shadow Caves on silent paws, investigating every fungal bloom with its nose. The leaf on its head is mildly toxic to touch but Sproutnyx seems unaware of this, pressing it cheerfully against things for emphasis. It is clever enough to open sealed containers by working at the seams with its paws, and cave foragers have learned not to leave food low to the ground. The Shadow Caves' elder texts describe Sproutnyx as the creature that discovered which cave fungi are edible by eating all of them and surviving.

Field note: Sproutnyx has consumed seventeen cave fungal species toxic to other creatures without apparent ill effect - researchers are still cataloguing its internal adaptations.

About Thornlynx

Thornlynx has grown into a long-bodied, muscular lynx with a full coat of overlapping green-and-brown leaf-scales, sweeping leaf-plume tail, and vivid orange-green eyes that glow slightly in the Shadow Caves' darkness. Thin toxic vine-tendrils grow from its spine and trail behind it as it moves, leaving faint chemical trails that other Shadow Cave creatures learn to avoid. It hunts by ambush, pressing its leaf-pattern coat against the cave walls until it is invisible, then striking with extraordinary speed. It has been in the Shadow Caves long enough to have memorised the scent-signature of every creature that shares its territory.

Field note: Thornlynx's vine-trails remain chemically active on cave rock for up to six hours after it has passed - it effectively keeps a dynamic chemical map of its territory.

About Venomarch

Venomarch is a terrifying apex presence in the Shadow Caves - a massive red-scaled predator bristling from nose to tail with toxic blade-horns, its back a ridge of venomous spines, its jaw a wide-set weapon capable of crushing stone. The vivid red colouration is not camouflage: it is a warning the Shadow Caves eventually taught everything else to obey. Venomarch secretes a territory-marking toxin from its spine-tips that lingers for days; nothing enters its range without that signal. Ancient Shadow Caves lore says Venomarch was created by Abalont's shadow as a guardian of the deepest passages - and it accepted the role.

Field note: Venomarch's spine-toxin has been detected in Shadow Cave water samples six hundred metres from any known Venomarch sighting - it marks territory more deeply than any visible boundary.

About Cetaby

Cetaby is a plump, snow-white beluga calf with a round melon forehead, tiny pectoral fins, and the most earnest blue eyes in Coral Bay. It bobs at the surface humming to itself, and the vibrations shimmer the water around it in small rings. Young explorers who wade near often say they suddenly remember a word they'd forgotten - Cetaby nudges those memories loose with a gentle pulse from its forehead, then flips away before it can be thanked.

Field note: Standing knee-deep near a resting Cetaby for five minutes is said to restore one forgotten word to mind.

About Cetarise

Cetarise has grown into a smooth, powerful beluga with a large rounded melon and a knowing expression. Its ivory skin ripples faintly with a pale interior glow when it echolocates, and its clicks carry so far through Coral Bay that the reef fish school differently in response. It patrols the outer edge of the bay with calm authority, threading clicks between the coral pillars as though checking each one for words that still need to be learned.

Field note: Cetarise can echolocate a single heartbeat hidden among reef crevices from 200 paces away.

About Cetamystic

Cetamystic is a luminous, powerfully built whale with fanned tail flukes and iridescent blue-tipped fins that catch the current like slow wings. Its broad melon pulses with soft light when it speaks, and its song carries across the full breadth of Coral Bay in under a minute. It is said the guardians consulted Cetamystic when they needed to remember the original words of the world - its body holds the echo of everything ever spoken near the bay.

Field note: On calm nights, Cetamystic's song reaches the far shore of Coral Bay and back, bouncing once before fading.

About Cubfrost

Cubfrost is a perfectly round polar bear cub, cream-white all over with one bright blue eye visible and a permanently open grin showing tiny teeth. It has arrived in the Sand Dunes from somewhere colder and appears delighted by sand, which it rolls in constantly and in great quantities. Its paw-pads cool whatever sand they touch and tiny frost-flowers appear around its footprints. The dune-skink families have adopted it as a neighbourhood curiosity - it does not eat skinks, it sits on them affectionately, which they tolerate with dignity.

Field note: Cubfrost's paw-prints leave frost-flowers that survive in the shade of dune crests for up to 72 hours despite the heat.

About Ursalid

Ursalid is a broad, powerful young polar bear with thick cream-white fur that now carries a fine grit of sand embedded in its outer coat, giving it a rougher texture and faint gold-brown shading. Its gait is steady and unhurried across the dunes. When it lies down on a slope it creates a natural cold-shade that other dune creatures seek out in the hottest hours. The dune-runners have noticed that oases near Ursalid territory stay cooler longer into summer - the cold it brings seems to linger in the sand even after it moves on.

Field note: The sand in a dune-hollow where Ursalid slept retains its coolness for up to five days after it departs.

About Glacieroth

Glacieroth is enormous and magnificent - a colossal ice-bear whose body has partly transformed into glacial rock-ice, great crystal spurs erupting from its haunches, shoulders, and crown. Its eyes burn cold blue and its breath turns to ice-fog even in the desert heat. It walks the deepest dune-beds with perfect slow confidence, and wherever it walks an ice-ridge forms in the sand behind it that lasts for weeks. The Sand Dunes caravans navigate by these ridges - they call them the Cold Roads and they have never once misled a traveller.

Field note: The ice-ridges left by Glacieroth align perfectly with the cardinal directions; navigators say they are more accurate than any compass.

About Hootix

Hootix is a round puffball of soft cream-brown feathers barely larger than two fists together, with enormous amber eyes that dominate its tiny face. Its stubby wings press tight to its sides and its feet are hidden under the fluff, giving it the look of a feathered ball balanced on nothing at all. It lives in the hollow trunks of Verdant Valley, where the breezes are warm and carry the scent of new leaves. When something startles it, it puffs up twice its size and lets out a squeaky hoot before toppling sideways.

Field note: Hootix cannot fly yet, but it spins in a full circle when excited - trainers call this the Hootix Wobble.

About Owlatch

Owlatch has grown into a trim, alert owl with warm brown-and-cream plumage, sharp yellow-orange talons, and keen golden eyes that can track a falling leaf in a gale. Its wings have filled out into proper flight feathers, and it beats them with a quiet precision that barely disturbs the canopy above. It patrols the ridgeline paths of Verdant Valley, counting voices on the wind - trainers swear it remembers everyone who ever passed and marks a stranger with a pointed stare. It perches on the same high branch each dawn.

Field note: Owlatch chooses one branch as its watching-perch and returns to it every single morning, even after a journey of many days.

About Vigilarch

Vigilarch spreads its broad brown-and-ivory wings wide enough to shadow a garden plot, and when it launches from a high branch the rush of air bends the grass flat below. Its fierce amber eyes have deepened to a burning gold, and the feather tufts above its brow give it the look of a judge who has heard every excuse. It circles Verdant Valley silently on thermals for hours, and those who learn well beneath the open sky feel a warm updraft brush their shoulders - Vigilarch's silent encouragement. The guardians trusted it with their oldest secrets.

Field note: Vigilarch's silent wingbeats generate a warm updraft felt up to thirty meters below, used by smaller birds as a free ride.

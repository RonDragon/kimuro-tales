About Starlamb

Starlamb is a small, fluffy white llama with a shimmering blue star-cloth draped over its back and a single spiralling horn glinting from its forehead. Its wide eyes hold tiny constellations, and when it blinks, they rearrange. It prances through the Psychic Temple leaving faint starlight hoofprints that linger for a few minutes before fading. Young creatures follow those trails the way children follow paths drawn in chalk - knowing something interesting must be at the end.

Field note: Starlamb's hoofprints glow for exactly ninety seconds and always point toward the nearest source of natural light in the temple.

About Celestlama

Celestlama stands tall and luminous, its neck long and proud, its mane a flowing river of blue-white star-light. The spiralled horn has grown long and elegant, and when it tilts its head, faint aurora-colour ripples pulse through its coat. It walks the Psychic Temple's outer terraces in slow deliberate arcs, and where it passes, the temple garden orient themselves toward it as they do toward the sun - a reflex so consistent that botanists can map its daily route by the flower-facing angle alone.

Field note: Every plant within six metres of Celestlama faces it regardless of sun position - the effect lasts for up to an hour after it has moved on.

About Cosmadeer

Cosmadeer is a being of pure living light wrapped in the form of a great horned llama - its coat a cascade of rainbow aurora, its horn a pillar of spiral crystal that catches and refracts the temple's glow into a permanent halo. Its long graceful neck carries its head high, and from a distance it looks like a moving lighthouse. Temple lore tells that Cosmadeer appeared at the exact moment the guardians first taught a creature to speak - and it has never left, as though it came specifically to witness what language would become.

Field note: Cosmadeer's presence in a temple courtyard doubles how long young creatures are willing to stay and watch something difficult - no explanation has been found.

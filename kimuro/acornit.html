About Acornit

Acornit is an upright acorn-bodied creature with a large ribbed cap, two bright round eyes, and a pair of small leafy fists held at its sides - each fist is a green-leafed pod it shakes when excited. It stands on two stubby legs and toddles along Granite Heights' lower paths at a determined pace, always counter-clockwise, as if memorizing the circuit. Axon the fish-guardian once used these same trails, and Acornit still follows that ancient route, marking the path for any child who wishes to learn it.

Field note: Acornit always walks Granite Heights' lower circuit counter-clockwise, completing the loop in exactly 22 minutes regardless of obstacles.

About Conkarl

Conkarl has grown into a stocky upright fighter - its acorn-cap crown is now thick and ridged, its body is broad and banded in warm chestnut, and its fists have become knotted acorn-pod knuckles it can pound together to make a sound that carries the length of Granite Heights' ridge. It still walks the patrol route every morning, but now faster and wider, covering the mid-elevation paths that Axon once climbed. When it finds an old mark carved into the granite it traces it with one knuckle three times before continuing.

Field note: Conkarl traces any old mark it finds carved in granite exactly three times with its knuckle, as if confirming the shape each pass.

About Terakhan

Terakhan is immense and ancient - an acorn-cap warrior grown to titan scale, its body wrapped in carved wooden armor and its great round crown cracked with age but unbroken. Its massive fists strike the granite path with such force that fresh sigils appear in the stone face. High in Granite Heights, where Axon the guardian first carved the learning-paths, Terakhan stands at the summit each dawn facing east, waiting with the patience of heartwood for the lost guardians to return and read the stone it has guarded since the shadow fell.

Field note: Each strike of Terakhan's fist on summit granite leaves a fresh sigil - cartographers have mapped 300 of them attributed to it.

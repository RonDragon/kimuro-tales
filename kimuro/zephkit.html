About Zephkit

Zephkit is a small, fluffy sky-blue husky pup with a spiral swirl pattern on its flank and bright pale eyes full of cheerful recklessness. Its tail curls up and wags constantly, and small static sparks fly off its fur when it shakes dry. At the base of the Storm Spire it races the lower wind-gusts, falling over its own paws, then immediately racing again. When it howls, the sound carries much farther than a pup its size should produce - a fact that delights it.

Field note: Zephkit howls in a specific direction before every storm, and that direction has never pointed the wrong way in recorded history.

About Zephound

Zephound is a lean electric wolf, its white-blue coat charged with crackling blue energy, a single spiraled horn rising from its brow that catches and focuses wind into a directed beam. Its howl at full stride has been clocked at three times the audible range for its size, and it leaves a faint ozone trail that lingers for twenty minutes. It races the lightning around the Storm Spire, tracking the paths Jack once walked, howling the guardian's name into every gale it passes through.

Field note: Zephound's horn acts as a lightning rod exactly once per storm - at the precise moment the storm peaks - and it always survives.

About Tempestyx

Tempestyx is a glacier-wolf of immense presence: thick icy-blue fur erupts in a mane of frozen crystal spines around its shoulders and haunches, and the air within five meters crackles and swirls in a permanent micro-storm of its making. It howls at the very summit of the Storm Spire where Jack was taken into the gale, and the thunder answers - not as coincidence but as confirmation. Every climber who reaches the top and speaks the right word finds Tempestyx waiting, ready to carry them through the deepest cloud.

Field note: The eye of every Storm Spire tempest is always positioned directly above wherever Tempestyx is standing - meteorologists have checked eighteen storms.

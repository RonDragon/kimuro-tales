About Vespling

Vespling is a small purple-grey bat with round body, stubby pointed wings, and a single bright violet eye at the centre of its face. It does not land - it hovers at all times, rotating slowly on the spot when stationary. It inhabits the Shadow Caves' upper chambers where thin shafts of moonlight occasionally penetrate, and it chases these light beams the way other creatures chase warmth, circling them until they fade. Its ghost-nature means it passes through cave walls with no effort and often reappears on the other side mid-chirp, startling the creatures it was talking to.

Field note: Vespling passes through solid rock with a faint pop that can be heard up to three metres away - experienced Shadow Cave spelunkers count the pops to track how many are nearby.

About Vespral

Vespral is a sleek dark purple bat with large leathery wings, long pointed ears, and wide glowing crimson eyes that catch no light source - they make their own. It flies through the Shadow Caves in absolute silence, wings producing no sound at all, which is physically impossible for its wing-loading - the guardians documented it as its ghost nature suppressing the sound of its own movement. It appears in places ahead of where it was last seen with no intermediate flight time observable.

Field note: In seventeen timed observations, Vespral crossed a thirty-metre cave chamber in under one second - far beyond any biological flight speed.

About Vestigon

Vestigon is a vast dark creature with enormous black leathery wings, a heavy angular body trailing tendrils of shadow-smoke, and two blazing red eyes that cast reverse shadows - pools of deeper darkness where light should be. It rules the deepest chambers of the Shadow Caves where no natural light has ever penetrated and no map has ever been completed. The guardians recorded it as the thing that decides which memories the Shadow Caves keep and which ones dissolve - and it has been deciding for longer than the caves themselves have had a name.

Field note: The pool of deeper darkness cast by Vestigon's eyes grows by one square centimetre per year - Shadow Cave keepers measure this annually with quiet reverence.

About Spinule

Spinule is a plump little orange-gold fish with a trio of purple-tipped psychic spines jutting from its head like a tiny antennae array. Its single wide purple eye catches Light Grove's canopy glow and reflects it back doubled. It lives in the shallow stream-pools beneath the light grove's glowing trees, and its spines pulse whenever it hears a name it has never heard spoken nearby. The grove's light seems to flow into it - it is slightly brighter on sunny days than on cloudy ones.

Field note: Spinule's spines vibrate at a frequency only young children can hear; they often find it before adults do.

About Spinara

Spinara has broadened into a proper lionfish silhouette but dressed in green-gold with sweeping fan fins that catch dappled Light Grove sunlight filtering through the canopy. Its body stripes are vivid against bright fins, and its pectoral fans spread horizontally wide. A gentle psychic resonance follows it through the water - Light Grove creatures seem to grow calmer when it swims nearby, and children who splash in the streams where it lives tend to remember unfamiliar names more easily.

Field note: Spinara memorises the surface patterns of every leaf that falls into its pool - over time it knows if the grove above is healthy.

About Spinamorph

Spinamorph is spectacular: a large deep-bodied fish surrounded by a full corona of long trailing spines and fins in magenta, purple, and gold, its broad pectoral fins sweeping like a flowing robe. Its eye carries a deep violet glow, and the water around it shimmers faintly as though light is bending. It rises from Light Grove's deepest spring pool only when the grove is in need - and every time it surfaces, the trees nearest the water grow an inch that same day.

Field note: The pool Spinamorph lives in is twelve degrees warmer than all surrounding water - warmed, the grove folk believe, by concentrated thoughts.

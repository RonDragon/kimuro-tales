About Cerulip

Cerulip is a round, glossy-blue pill bug no bigger than a fist, with tiny stubby legs tucked beneath a smooth domed shell. Two bright eyes peer out from a gap where the shell opens slightly at the front. It rolls into a perfect ball when startled, and likes to float along Verdant Valley streams tucked up tight. Small children in the valley say finding one downstream means rain is coming within the hour.

Field note: When Cerulip tucks into a ball, it spins slowly downstream like a tiny blue marble.

About Cerulex

Cerulex has grown a pronounced horn above its snout and developed a second segmented ring of blue-black plates around its midsection. Its underside bristles with strong gripping legs and it moves in short, deliberate rushes. In Verdant Valley's shallow creeks it wedges itself under stones at dawn, scrubbing algae from the rocks with the stiff bristles under its chin. The guardians once watched it clear a silted singing spring overnight.

Field note: Cerulex wedges so firmly under stream rocks that the current bends around it rather than moving it.

About Cerulord

Cerulord is a low, wide armored titan - a mass of overlapping blue-black plates edged in deep cobalt, with three forward-curving horns and a fringe of heavy clawed limbs that grip the riverbed like an anchor. Where it walks, the streambed reshapes itself. Valley lore holds that Cerulord's path carves new singing rivers when the old channels run dry, and it still haunts the deep pools beneath the guardian bridges, waiting for the waters to flow pure again.

Field note: A Cerulord passage reshapes the streambed; fishers say new pools appear wherever it has rested overnight.

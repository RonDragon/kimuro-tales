About Scarynip

Scarynip is a small dark-plated crab with a single oversized mechanical-looking claw and a round glowing eye mounted on the top of its shell like a periscope. It scuttles sideways through the Dark Forest undergrowth, and its one big claw makes a clipping sound against exposed roots as it clips off tiny pieces of iron-rich bark to swallow. The metal it absorbs strengthens its shell plate by plate. It is notably curious - it will approach a campfire's glow, claw raised, to investigate.

Field note: Scarynip's single large claw is strong enough to snap a finger-thick green branch cleanly at three weeks old.

About Scarbeam

Scarbeam crouches low and wide, its dark grey shell now reinforced with steel-hard plates that overlap like scale mail. Both claws are large and yellow-tipped, capable of peeling bark off a mature tree in strips. It moves in total silence through the Dark Forest's shadows, using its low profile to slip under fallen logs. When threatened, it spreads both claws wide and clicks them together in a rapid staccato meant to sound like something much larger. Other forest creatures give it a wide berth.

Field note: Scarbeam can remain completely motionless for up to two hours, claw pressed flat to the ground, before springing into action.

About Scaryx

Scaryx is a heavily armored dark crab whose entire shell is covered with upward-pointing spikes, each tip hardened to a needle point. Multiple enormous claws extend from its sides, some used for grip and others kept raised for striking. The shadow absorbed from the Dark Forest has darkened its plates to near-black, and the tips of its spikes gleam with a faint metallic sheen. When it presses all its claws to the ground at once, it can feel vibrations from over fifty metres away, making it nearly impossible to approach undetected.

Field note: Scaryx has eleven distinct claw tips and can control each independently - observers have counted it sharpening six at once against the same boulder.

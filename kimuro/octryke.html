About Octryke

Octryke is a small pale octopus with a single enormous glowing eye in the center of its head and a short purple horn that crackles faintly with psychic energy. It bobs in the shallower pools of Poison Marsh, extending one tentacle at a time to probe the murky water for thoughts - because it cannot fully tell the difference between objects and minds yet, and touches both with the same wondering curiosity. Its single eye narrows into a suspicious squint at anything that doesn't give it information fast enough.

Field note: Octryke touches every new object with exactly three tentacle-tips before deciding if it is interesting - anything that requires a fourth is very interesting indeed.

About Octrapus

Octrapus has grown substantially, its eight tentacles trailing long through the marsh water and its pale body now marked with purple ghost-energy patterns that pulse in rhythm with its thoughts. Its twin horns have sharpened, and it has gained the ability to become partially translucent - not quite invisible, but hard to focus on, like a word that keeps slipping out of memory. It lurks beneath Poison Marsh's floating weed-mats and occasionally surfaces to stare at travellers with unsettling comprehension.

Field note: Octrapus goes semi-transparent precisely when it is thinking most intensely - the more invisible, the deeper the thought.

About Octyrax

Octyrax is a vast purple octopus that barely fits in any single pool of Poison Marsh, its tentacles reaching the far banks while its body rests somewhere in the center, glowing with deep ghost-purple light. The single eye has multiplied into an eye cluster that sees on multiple psychic planes simultaneously. Old marsh-walkers say it is the Marsh's memory - every creature that has lived and died there exists as a pattern in Octyrax's enormous, still mind. It does not act unless the balance is threatened; when it does, the whole marsh knows.

Field note: Octyrax has one tentacle permanently extended into each of the marsh's seven deepest pools at all times - all simultaneously.

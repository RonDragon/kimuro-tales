About Zephykit

Zephykit is an adorable small creature that splits the difference between axolotl and tiny dragon: a golden-yellow face framed by blue feathered gill-frills, a plump cream body, and a fluffy fox-like tail that seems slightly too large. It paddles through Coral Bay's shallows, gill-frills fanning in the current, and pauses to lip-read the words that visiting children trace in the sand. When delighted it does a tiny spinning splash that soaks everyone nearby without malice.

Field note: Zephykit always faces into the current, no matter how you move around it - bay fishers call it the Living Compass.

About Zephydrake

Zephydrake has grown into a sleek bipedal water-dragon: deep blue scales cover a lean athletic body, swept-back water-fin crests adorn its head and back, and its clawed wings spread wide in a fighter's display when challenged. Its eyes gleam with focused intelligence, and it can launch itself from the water with enough speed to clear a tall fishing boat. In Coral Bay it guards the hand-carved coral formations that Axon made before Abalont arrived, herding curious fish away from the carvings.

Field note: Zephydrake carves a new notch into the coral each week - researchers have counted forward and backward and found the notches encode a repeating sequence.

About Zephyrath

Zephyrath is a magnificent tidal dragon, its long golden-and-blue body flowing like a living wave across the surface of Coral Bay. Enormous wing-sails catch both wind and current, and streaming water-tendrils trail from every fin like a royal cloak in motion. Its presence shifts the tide slightly - not enough to flood, but enough to lift every boat in the bay an inch higher in the water, as if it is carrying them home. It is Coral Bay's oldest guardian, and it remembers every voice raised there since the bay first filled.

Field note: The exact moment Zephyrath surfaces each morning determines the day's tide schedule - the harbor master uses only it, no instruments needed.

About Quilkit

Quilkit is a small, round hedgehog-like creature with tawny-brown fur and a back covered in short, stubby quills still capped with earthy grey tips. Its face is round and soft with tiny dark eyes and a button nose always dusted with grit. It roots through rubble piles in the Steel Fortress, pushing stones aside with surprising strength for its size. Guardians often find the tidiest rubble corners cleared overnight - Quilkit has organized them by weight, because it can tell the difference just by touch.

Field note: Quilkit sorts stones by weight using only its paws, stacking lighter ones on top with near-perfect accuracy every time.

About Quillyx

Quillyx has grown stout and low-slung, its body now covered in long, densely packed grey-brown quills that overlap like roof slates. It moves with a quiet, rolling confidence, belly close to the ground. In the Steel Fortress it follows the scent of molten ore through ventilation shafts too narrow for larger guards. When it shakes itself, the quills click together in a rhythmic pattern that other Quillyxes recognize from great distances inside the fortress's echoing corridors. Even enemies hesitate before striking something that rattles like a bag of nails.

Field note: Quillyx's quill-rattle changes tempo by mood - a slow beat means calm, a fast staccato means run.

About Ironquill

Ironquill is broad and low, its back a forest of heavy dark-steel quills with bright glowing tips that pulse red in low light. Its four legs are armored with layered plates, and its red eyes never blink. It patrols the deepest chambers of the Steel Fortress where memory-vaults are sealed, pressing its side against the doors to feel for vibrations. Nothing enters without its knowledge. Abalont once sent a dozen shadows at once to breach the vault; Ironquill spun in a circle so fast the quills became a spinning barrier of blades and held the line until dawn.

Field note: When Ironquill spins its quills at full speed, the air between them screams like metal under a grinding wheel.

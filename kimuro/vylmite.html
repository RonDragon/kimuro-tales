About Vylmite

A round purple orb of a body with a single large spiral-patterned eye, Vylmite skitters through the Dark Forest floor on four stubby legs. Its shell gleams like polished amethyst, and the spiral in its eye seems to slowly rotate - a trick that makes predators hesitate just long enough. It hides under dark leaves by day and taps a rhythmic signal to its siblings after sundown, counting the words it heard before the forest went quiet.

Field note: Vylmite's spiral eye rotates once clockwise and once counter for every creature that passes - locals use it as a living tally.

About Vyloxid

Vyloxid has grown a hard purple carapace decorated with neat rows of dark bumps, and two sharp curved pincers jut from its head. Its green compound eyes glow coldly in the forest dark. Six strong legs grip bark and stone equally well, and it moves with startling speed when cornered. In the Dark Forest it patrols the oldest trees, pressing its pincers into the bark to taste which words were spoken there before the guardian fell silent.

Field note: Vyloxid can detect which words have been spoken near a tree by pressing its pincers to the roots - it has never been observed getting it wrong.

About Vyloxarch

Vast and low-slung, Vyloxarch is armored in overlapping purple-black plates that slope like a fortress roof, ringed with sharp thorn-nodes along every edge. Its green eyes shine like lanterns in the deepest dark, and its six legs are thick as tree roots. It no longer hurries - everything eventually comes to Vyloxarch. In the Dark Forest it is the final guardian of the oldest sigils scratched into the stone before Abalont's shadow reached the trees, and it will not let them be erased.

Field note: The thorn-nodes along Vyloxarch's back each correspond to a mark in the ancient sigil-craft - scholars have been copying them for years.

About Sparkid

Sparkid is a round glassy spider with a large pale translucent globe body, six thin legs, and wisps of blue-white electric smoke drifting off its sides. Its wide eyes blink with tiny arc-flashes. In Steel Fortress's circuit-conduit passages it bounces between metal surfaces, building up charge with each contact. It communicates with other Sparkids by sending psychic pulses along the fortress's steel girders - the engineers discovered this when equipment started responding to thoughts rather than controls.

Field note: Sparkid can transmit a full memory-image through any metal surface it touches - the target receives it as a vivid complete flashback.

About Voltarachne

Voltarachne is a sleek pale-violet spider with long articulate legs that arc blue electricity between their joints as it walks. Its segmented body carries a soft violet psychic glow. In Steel Fortress it maintains its own private network of conductive threads stretched across the fortress's upper infrastructure, processing and storing the psychic residue of every thought the fortress's inhabitants have ever had. Engineers sometimes leave hard problems near its web junction and find solutions sketched in condensed frost on the steel next morning.

Field note: Voltarachne has solved seventeen previously unsolvable Steel Fortress engineering problems - always overnight, always without anyone watching.

About Cerebrawl

Cerebrawl is a magnificent large purple spider wearing what appears to be a glowing crown of pure psychic energy - a luminous ring that hovers just above its head. Its thick body is deep violet with neural-pattern markings, and its eight powerful legs leave faint arc-burns on any metal surface they touch. It sits at Steel Fortress's central control junction, where every conduit in the fortress connects, and processes all of it simultaneously. Visiting scholars describe standing near Cerebrawl as feeling every answer you ever needed suddenly sitting right at the edge of your thoughts.

Field note: Cerebrawl's psychic crown contains a compressed map of Steel Fortress's entire neural-conduit network - it can reroute any circuit in the fortress from where it sits.

About Flickkit

Flickkit is a small orange-red fox kit with a wild spiky mane and a bushy flame-tipped tail. Its fur stands on end at the tips, giving it a perpetually startled, eager look. It races across the crest of the Sand Dunes at dawn when the wind is fastest, tumbling end-over-end and bouncing back up without pause. It collects scraps of wind-tossed cloth and leaf in a nest of warm sand it rebuilds each day.

Field note: Flickkit's mane-spikes point exactly into the wind - dune travelers watch them for wind-direction changes.

About Flamewing

Flamewing has grown tall with a layered spiky mane and dense fire-feathered flanks that catch the desert wind like sails. Its tail fans into multiple spiky plumes that trail fire-streamers in a stiff breeze. It rides sand-laden wind gusts with all four paws off the ground, covering fifty meters in a single glide. Sand Dune nomads say hearing Flamewing's wind-borne cry is a sign the dry season is ending.

Field note: Flamewing always faces precisely downwind - if it turns around, the desert wind is about to reverse.

About Pyrocrest

Pyrocrest blazes across the dune-sea with a full fiery crest fanning from head to haunches in layered flame-spikes. Its body burns brightest at speed - the faster it runs, the brighter the fire trails behind it. Ancient dune-walkers carved its silhouette on their navigation stones as a symbol of the journey's end: the creature that leads you home when the dunes all look the same. Its wind-roar can be heard from three dunes away.

Field note: Pyrocrest's fire burns brighter at the tip of each spine than anywhere else on its body - the spines act as heat-relays.

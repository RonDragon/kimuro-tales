About Voxling

Voxling is a compact green serpent with alert amber eyes and vivid green scales that catch the mineral light of Dragon Spire. It moves in tight S-curves through the spire's lower ledge-cracks, testing every surface with the tip of its tongue before proceeding. A faint trace of toxin coats its scales - not enough to harm, just enough to discourage casual handling. In the ancient records of Dragon Spire's keepers, Voxling is described as the Spire's Reader: a young one placed in a library will have memorized every title on every shelf by morning.

Field note: Voxling maps every room it enters in under two minutes - it has never been observed retreating through the same path twice.

About Voxern

Voxern rears up with confidence now, its muscled upper body rising to face-height and its wide-flaring tongue tasting the air with a speed too fast to follow. Its green scales have deepened toward emerald with a dragon's iridescence, and a crest of small dorsal ridges has appeared along its neck - the first hint of the full spire dragon it is becoming. It hunts the mid-levels of Dragon Spire by memory alone, having mapped every tunnel so thoroughly that it does not need its eyes in the dark.

Field note: Voxern navigates Dragon Spire's entire tunnel network from memory without ever placing a wrong turn - it has memorized approximately 400 distinct route-choices.

About Hydrovox

Hydrovox is a massive coiled serpentine dragon, its broad heavy body thick as a tree trunk, covered in overlapping emerald-and-gold-edged scales and topped with a full crest of sharp spines from snout to tail-tip. Its enormous jaws drip with concentrated venom that pools in the carved channels at Dragon Spire's base - channels that the spire's ancient architects built specifically to contain it. It is not hostile by nature: Hydrovox tests knowledge. Those who approach with correct words pass; those who approach with wrong ones learn a different lesson.

Field note: The carved stone channels at Dragon Spire's base exist solely to redirect Hydrovox's venom drip away from the memory-stone archive below it.

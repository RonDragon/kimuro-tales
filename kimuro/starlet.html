About Starlet

A plump, glossy pink star no wider than a child's hand, Starlet beams a permanent happy smile and carries two small round bumps on its topmost arms like tiny rosy cheeks. It drifts through the Crystal Mines' glittering shafts of light, absorbing their glow until it pulses like a lantern. Miners say a Starlet hovering near your lamp means good luck is buried nearby. It flickers its arms in a slow wave whenever it hears a name it has never heard spoken aloud.

Field note: A Starlet's glow brightens noticeably the moment someone nearby learns something new.

About Stellix

Rounder and rosier now, Stellix sports a wide face at its center and five sturdy arms dimpled with glowing bumps. It rolls slowly through the mine's crystal corridors like a ball of soft light, leaving faint pink trails on the quartz walls. Local children believe each glowing bump on its arms holds a memory - and Stellix does seem to pulse whenever it passes a spot it has visited before. It guards the mid-level tunnels where the lightstones grow.

Field note: Stellix retraces the exact same patrol loop through its tunnel every single night without fail.

About Cosmara

Cosmara no longer rolls - it floats, its thick arms writhing slowly like the limbs of something between a star and a creature far older than either. Its face has deepened into a stern gaze beneath a brilliant luminous core, and its curled arm-tips trail glowing wisps the color of distant starlight. Every crystal in the deepest mines resonates when Cosmara hums. Those who have seen it say its light doesn't illuminate the dark - it simply makes the dark feel less important.

Field note: The deepest crystal veins in the mines grow fastest along paths Cosmara drifts through on its night rounds.

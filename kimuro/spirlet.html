About Spirlet

Spirlet is a round-bellied small monkey with warm brown fur, big blue eyes full of wonder, and a thick coiled tail with a golden glow at its tip that spins slowly like a living spiral. It clings to the lower steel fortress walls, curious about every bolt and rivet. Small blue electric sparks flick from its fur when it gets excited - which is often. It was one of the first creatures drawn to the steel fortress not by the cold metal but by the warm hum of electricity in the walls.

Field note: Spirlet taps metal surfaces with its knuckle three times before touching them with its palm - always checking the charge first.

About Spirax

Spirax has grown lean and tall for a monkey, with long arms and hard-knuckled fists that crackle with blue electric charge. Its brown fur is shot through with lightning-pale highlights, and blue arcs jump between its hands when it holds them close. It trains alone in the steel fortress courtyards, throwing punch combinations at nothing - its knuckles strike invisible targets with enough static to singe scorchmarks into the metal walls. Other fortress creatures know to clear the yard when Spirax starts its morning routine.

Field note: The steel fortress floor near Spirax's training spot has permanent scorch rings from ten thousand static punches.

About Spirarch

Spirarch is built like a fortress itself: a massive broad-shouldered ape with thick gold-armoured arms ringed in deep electric coils, a wide chest, and two enormous gold-banded fists perpetually wreathed in arcing lightning. Its amber eyes carry absolute focus. It stands at the steel fortress's highest rampart in all weather - lightning rods in the towers were placed there because the builders noticed that storms always centred on Spirarch first. When it roars, the steel fortress rings like a bell.

Field note: Spirarch can charge a drained power cell to full just by wrapping both hands around it for thirty seconds.

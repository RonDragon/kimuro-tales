About Flarekit

Flarekit is a small spotted ocelot cub with a warm golden-tan coat patterned in dark stripes and spots. Its green eyes are wide and inquisitive, and its stubby tail twitches when it hears a name it has never heard. It scrambles up the lower granite boulders of the Heights, leaving tiny claw marks that trainers learn to follow. The guardians who first climbed Granite Heights found Flarekit families already nesting in the warm crevices beneath the stone.

Field note: Flarekit counts its claw-marks on boulders each morning - if the number is odd, it considers the day lucky.

About Pyralicat

Pyralicat has lengthened into a lean, powerful ocelot with spiky ridge-fur running from the neck down the back like stone teeth. Its golden-spotted coat has deepened in hue, and its shoulders carry a layered armor of thick spiky fur. It hunts the upper granite trails with patient, low steps, leaping between ridgelines in near silence. Trainers who earn its trust say it leads them to marks carved by the ancient guardians in the cliffside.

Field note: Pyralicat's spiky back-ridge stands fully upright only when it senses a truly new and difficult word.

About Infernocat

Infernocat stands as a large, heavily-built wild ocelot whose entire back is armored with a crest of jagged spike-fur. Its haunches are thick and powerful enough to spring ten meters straight up a vertical cliff face, and its eyes glow faintly green with grass-energy. At the highest point of Granite Heights it was the guardians' sworn ally, holding the passes so the children of the valley could climb safely. When it roars, loose stone skitters down the mountain.

Field note: A single Infernocat roar dislodges exactly enough loose stone to reveal the hidden passes beneath.

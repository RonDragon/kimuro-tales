About Coilbug

Coilbug is a small golden-shelled creature, round as a bun with a spiral shell marking on its back and six stubby legs tucked underneath. Its shell catches every scrap of verdant valley sunlight and sends it back as a warm glow. It moves with slow, rolling determination - always in a straight line until it bumps something, then picks a new direction without any visible frustration. The guardians of verdant valley called it the valley's coin - something small, bright, and worth finding.

Field note: Coilbug's shell produces a faint golden light at night, just enough to show what's directly beneath it.

About Hornbeetle

Hornbeetle has grown into a proper large beetle with a gleaming gold-amber carapace, six strong legs, and a single curved horn rising from its head. The spiral on its shell has been integrated into the carapace plating as a raised ridge. It walks with a low centre of gravity and a swinging, sure-footed gait. It carries dung-ball sized objects for other verdant valley creatures when asked - nobody quite knows why, but it seems to find the work satisfying. Its horn catches morning light and throws a small sunrise onto the grass each dawn.

Field note: Hornbeetle pushes a perfect sphere of golden leaf-pollen wherever it walks, growing larger until it decides to bury it.

About Spirolith

Spirolith is enormous - a towering golden armoured beetle standing upright on four thick legs with two powerful forelimbs held forward. Its carapace is engraved with deep spiral runic patterns that pulse with warm light when it takes a slow breath. A massive curved horn crowns its head. It anchors itself at the centre of verdant valley during solstices, and every creature in the valley instinctively gathers near it - not out of fear but because its golden light makes unfamiliar names feel possible.

Field note: When Spirolith beats its wings - once a year, at midsummer - the entire valley hears a single deep resonant chime that lasts thirty seconds.

About Scuttlix

Scuttlix wears a cone-shaped pale-grey metallic shell like a knight's helmet that is slightly too big for it, tilting forward over round eyes that peer out with cautious optimism. Its orange-red legs are nimble and quick, and it can squeeze into crystal crevices no wider than a coin. In the Crystal Mines it collects flecks of ore, pressing them to the outside of its shell until the cone glitters. It greets every new learner by holding up one small claw in salute.

Field note: Scuttlix adds exactly one ore fleck to its shell each day - researchers can tell its age by counting the layers.

About Clawmor

Clawmor has traded its cone shell for a broad hammered-steel carapace with riveted plates and a forward crest, and its once-orange legs are now a deep rust-red with reinforced joints. Its claws have grown wide and flat - perfect for prying ore veins open or bracing a tunnel entrance against collapse. The Crystal Mine's deep workers call it the Tunnel Warden; Clawmor knows every passage by echo, and moves through pitch-dark shafts without slowing.

Field note: Clawmor navigates by tapping its claws and timing the echo - it has never walked into a wall.

About Titanyx

Titanyx stands upright like a fortress given legs, its entire body encased in massive orange-and-steel plating, with towering spine-crests along its back and hooked battle-claws that scrape sparks from the mine floor. Its eyes glow a steady amber behind heavy brow plates. The Crystal Mines were built around its territory - miners leave it an offering of raw ore each season, and in return it has never once collapsed a worked tunnel. It is both guardian and gatekeeper of what lies below.

Field note: The footfall of a walking Titanyx registers on seismic instruments forty kilometers away.

About Gemule

Gemule is a plump round purple slug with a smooth lavender body, two tiny blue-tipped antenna stalks that quiver when it senses nearby thoughts, and a perpetually serene expression. It glides slowly across the warm sands of the Dunes, leaving a faint glittering trail that attracts small sand beetles. Gemule is drawn to places where people have sat quietly and thought hard - it seems to absorb residual psychic impressions from the ground and collect them the way other creatures collect pebbles. Finding one asleep on a meditation stone is considered very good fortune.

Field note: Gemule always crawls toward the spot where the most words were spoken that day - camp storytellers wake to find it on their pillow.

About Gemaxon

Gemaxon has undergone a dramatic change - its smooth lavender body is now erupting with jagged crystal spikes of magenta and yellow-gold that have pushed through its hide like emerging teeth. The spikes are hard as quartz and vibrate at a frequency that can be felt rather than heard. Despite its spiny appearance, Gemaxon is not aggressive; the crystals are psychic resonators, not weapons, allowing it to broadcast its thoughts to creatures up to fifty meters away. Wandering traders in the Sand Dunes have learned to follow Gemaxon, as it always knows where the next oasis lies.

Field note: Gemaxon's crystal spikes hum in perfect harmony with each other - but the frequency changes every full moon, and no one has ever predicted what key it will choose.

About Crystallux

Crystallux has become nearly more crystal than creature - its body is so densely packed with enormous sharp spines of magenta, yellow, and deep purple quartz that the original slug form beneath is almost invisible. It moves slowly, dragging its weight of crystal with perfect dignity, and when it is still it is genuinely difficult to distinguish from a natural crystal formation. Its psychic field now extends far enough to touch the minds of creatures it cannot see, and it sometimes broadcasts entire memories from centuries of accumulated impressions stored in its crystal body.

Field note: The crystals on Crystallux's body contain visible internal inclusions - each one is a fragment of a memory it has absorbed and crystallized permanently.

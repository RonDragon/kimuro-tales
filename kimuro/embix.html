About Embix

Embix is a compact, round-bodied fire-bird with wide flapping orange-red wings and fierce round eyes. Its crest of flame-feathers blazes from its crown, and it hops along the lip of the Lava Crater, wings spread wide, practicing its balance against the updrafts. It cannot yet sustain flight for more than a minute but compensates by hurling tiny fire-sparks from its beak with startling accuracy. The crater's heat is its cradle - it hatches from lava-warmed rock.

Field note: Embix hops exactly twelve times before it attempts flight - trainers have counted, and it never varies.

About Pyreion

Pyreion soars above the Lava Crater on wide swept wings of deep orange and gold, its tail-feathers streaming fire behind it. Its entire body radiates heat like a second sun, and passing through its wake leaves a warm tingling on skin. It circles the crater in long lazy loops, watching for cooling lava bridges to land on. Its cry at dusk tells the creatures below that the crater is safe for another night - silence from a Pyreion is a warning worth heeding.

Field note: Pyreion's tail-flame streams longer behind it when it is carrying good news - explorers gauge fortune by the length.

About Solpyre

Solpyre is a towering fire-dragon-phoenix, its great wings wrapped in roaring orange flame and its coiled body armored with thick fire-scales. Its neck curves with serpentine power, and its talons grip stone as if born from it. When it spreads its wings above the Lava Crater at sunrise, the reflected fire lights the entire surrounding highlands orange-gold. The guardians recorded this creature at the crater's founding: the original flame that will never be extinguished, reborn from every eruption.

Field note: Solpyre sheds one flame-feather each year at midsummer - the feather burns for exactly one year, no more.

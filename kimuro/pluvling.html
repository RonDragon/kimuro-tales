About Pluvling

Pluvling is an endearing small axolotl with a round white-lavender body and a frill of purple-blue feathery gill-fans spread wide around its head. Four tiny legs and a flat tail help it waddle along the poison marsh's shallower edges. Its large eyes are pale and attentive, taking in everything. The gill-fans pulse gently with a faint psychic glow when it concentrates, which it does often - usually while staring at reflections in the water. Despite living in the poison marsh, its own body stays pristine because a thin psychic field filters out all marsh toxins before they reach its skin. The marsh's other creatures leave it alone, recognizing that aura.

Field note: Pluvling's gill-fans change from purple to blue whenever it detects a lie being told within five meters of the water.

About Pluvian

Pluvian's body has grown longer and more graceful, its pale lavender-white sides patterned with subtle blue-purple striping. The feathery gill-fans are now wider and more elaborate, flowing back along its neck in sweeping fronds. Four stronger legs let it move easily between water and damp marsh-earth. It moves with an unhurried patience through the poison marsh, using its psychic senses to map the currents flowing under the surface. Pluvian leaves faint glowing trails in the marsh water behind it - marsh-keepers say these trails last six hours and mark safe passage routes through the toxic waterways.

Field note: The glowing trail a Pluvian leaves in marsh water can be followed safely for exactly six hours before fading - not a minute more.

About Pluvarch

Pluvarch is a beautiful and alien presence in the poison marsh - its long axolotl body is covered in intricate purple-and-white feather-fin fronds that fan out all along its back and sides, each tipped with a soft glow. Its four legs are elegant and sure-footed on both land and shallow water. Pale psychic light radiates from its entire body, pushing the marsh's toxic haze back in a gentle sphere around it. It rarely speaks, but when it does, its voice carries across the whole marsh as a clear crystalline vibration that resolves into meaning rather than sound. The guardians of the poison marsh believe Pluvarch knows the true name of everything in the marsh, and that saying those names aloud could purify the entire region.

Field note: A Pluvarch that has lived thirty years will have turned every toxic plant within a ten-meter radius into a medicine herb.

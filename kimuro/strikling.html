About Strikling

Strikling is a round, fluffy grey-white bird with a fierce expression that seems too large for its small frame. Its chest feathers are a soft pale grey, and two white tufts jut from its head like tiny ears or eyebrows raised in permanent challenge. It perches on the upper ledges of Gale Ridge and stares down at everything below with intense yellow eyes. Fledgling Striklings practice dive-runs in the updrafts, missing their target more often than not but never seeming to notice.

Field note: Strikling practices the same dive run eighty times a day until it lands it perfectly - then moves to a harder angle.

About Strixon

Strixon has grown into a sharp-featured raptor with grey-tipped white wings spread wide and powerful dark-taloned feet. Its chest feathers have darkened to shadow-grey at their edges, and its gaze has sharpened from challenge to calculation. It hunts the mid-level thermals of Gale Ridge, riding invisible updrafts without a single wingbeat for minutes at a time. When it finally stoops into a dive, the sound of its passage through the air arrives before it does - a low keening whistle that sends everything on the rocks scattering.

Field note: Strixon can reduce its cross-section to half its normal width mid-dive, making it nearly invisible from directly below.

About Strixarch

Strixarch spreads wings so wide that its shadow covers the entire upper platform of Gale Ridge. Its feathers have darkened from grey to deep charcoal, with black-tipped primaries that trail like storm pennants in flight. The white head feathers remain, giving it a two-toned severity - pale head, dark body - like a storm with a calm eye. When Strixarch calls from the Ridge's peak, the call carries across three regions and every bird within hearing range goes still. It is the oldest intelligence in the storm zone, and it watches everything.

Field note: Strixarch has not been seen to land anywhere but the Ridge's single highest rock in living memory - not once.

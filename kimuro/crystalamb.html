About Crystalamb

Crystalamb is a small pale-blue lamb with a pair of small rounded crystal horns on its head and a fluffy coat patterned with deep-blue diamond facets. Its eyes are wide and violet-tinted, glimmering with awareness that seems a size too large for its little body. In the Poison Marsh it steps carefully between the toxic pools, and each hoof-print fills with clean water that lasts for hours. Young learners following it through the marsh call it a living bridge of safe ground.

Field note: Each hoof-print a Crystalamb leaves fills with fresh water that remains pure for up to six hours.

About Amethhorn

Amethhorn stands tall and confident, its body now a deep aqua-teal with a brilliant large diamond-faceted gem mounted between wide-sweeping crystal horns. Its fleece has thinned to reveal smooth crystal-patterned skin beneath, and its gaze carries a distant quality - like it is watching something a few seconds ahead of the present moment. In the marsh it stands at junctions and seems to choose the safest route before any scout does. Trainers rely on it completely in fog.

Field note: Amethhorn always grazes facing the direction of tomorrow's weather - it has been right 847 days running.

About Prismarch

Prismarch is majestic - a large crystal-horned ram with a coat that shifts between aqua and violet as it moves, refracting light into slow-moving prismatic ripples across the marsh surface. Its great curved horns are layered crystal, and the gem at its brow pulses gently with each breath. It is said to know every voice raised in the Poison Marsh for the past three hundred years, stored as light-patterns inside its central crystal. It never repeats them - but it remembers all.

Field note: The gem between Prismarch's horns glows brighter when someone nearby learns a name it has never heard for the first time.

About Embkil

Embkil is a vivid red-orange fox kit with a flame-bright tail that is the only light in the deepest Shadow Caves. Its orange eyes glow warm in the dark, and it pads softly between the rock formations, dragging its bright tail along the walls to leave a fading glow that others can follow. Young cave creatures follow Embkil's tail-light as a comfort, and the guardians say it was the first fire ever carried into those lightless passages.

Field note: Embkil's tail-glow lasts exactly forty seconds after it moves on - just enough time to follow safely.

About Embraze

Embraze has grown into a lean, wild-maned fox with three flame-bright tails spreading behind it like living torches. Its deep-red fur takes on a darker, richer hue in the Shadow Caves' filtered light, and its body moves with liquid certainty through narrow passages that would stop a larger creature. It navigates by the temperature difference of air currents, and the glow of its three tails maps a full cave chamber in a single sweep. Every light-trail it leaves lasts three times as long as Embkil's.

Field note: Embraze's three tail-flames are never the same brightness - the brightest one always points toward the nearest exit.

About Inferus

Inferus fills the widest passages of the Shadow Caves with the glow of nine blazing tails that fan out behind it like a bonfire given form. Its massive maned body crouches low when moving through the caves, and its dark-red fur absorbs shadow just as readily as it throws fire-light. When it stands in a cavern chamber and spreads all nine tails, the entire cave is illuminated wall to wall. The shadow creatures scatter and the stone itself seems to lean away - it is the fire that answers the dark.

Field note: Inferus can light a cavern a hundred meters wide using only three of its nine tails - it saves the rest for emergencies.

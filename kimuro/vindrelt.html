About Vindrelt

Vindrelt is a small, wide-eyed blue-grey leopard cub with dark rosette spots and a bright, hopeful expression that belies the heavy steel chain already looped around its neck like a collar. The chain drags along the ground as it trots, but Vindrelt doesn't seem to notice its weight. It is at home padding quietly through the Ghost Hollow's mist, pausing to sniff headstones and hollow logs. Visiting children sometimes see only the glowing eyes moving in fog before Vindrelt pops out to investigate them with a curious head-tilt.

Field note: Vindrelt's chain never rusts, no matter how long it drags through damp hollow mud - the links stay mirror-bright.

About Vindraclaw

Vindraclaw moves low and purposeful through the Ghost Hollow, its deep blue-black body slung close to the ground on powerful legs. The leopard spots have darkened to near-invisible on its coat, and the chain around its neck has grown thick and studded, draped across its chest in overlapping loops. It leaves no sound when it walks - not even the chain clinks. Hollow-keepers say Vindraclaw patrols the border between the living grove and the deepest ghost-mist, allowing neither to spill too far into the other's ground.

Field note: Vindraclaw can suppress the sound of its chain entirely at will; the only sign it is near is the faint scent of cold iron on the air.

About Vindramaw

Vindramaw crouches wide and massive, all dark muscle and overlapping steel-plate chains that now cover its shoulders and haunches like battle-armour. The Ghost Hollow's mist parts around it. Its eyes carry centuries of watch-keeping - burning with a cold violet focus that makes even the hollow's ghosts hesitate. It is guardian and judge of the Ghost Hollow both, and its presence is said to keep Abalont's wandering shadows from settling among the hollow's ancient trees. A Vindramaw that approves of a trainer's true heart will lay its chain-draped head in their hands.

Field note: The steel chains Vindramaw wears are forged from the same ore as the Ghost Hollow's oldest boundary markers, cast when the guardians first sealed the hollow.

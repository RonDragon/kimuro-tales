About Geodite

Geodite is a small black-shelled turtle covered in raised golden studs that serve as electrical contact points. It moves with quiet confidence through the Steel Fortress, drawn to active machinery by the faint electric fields it detects through its stud-covered shell. Its dark carapace absorbs no light - at night, only the golden studs are visible, giving it the look of a miniature starscape. It has learned to recharge itself by pressing its shell against the fortress's generator housings, and maintenance crews have found that Geodite-rested generators need less servicing.

Field note: Each golden stud on Geodite's shell is a separate electrical node - they can fire independently, which means it can release charge from any angle simultaneously.

About Gedonyx

Gedonyx has grown into a formidable dark-armored tortoise with golden spikes now rising from its shell like lightning rods, and a single prominent horn extending forward from its head. When it senses an approaching storm, every golden spike on its body begins to glow with accumulated charge. The Steel Fortress relies on Gedonyx herds in the outer wall corridors as living surge protectors - during lightning strikes, the creatures absorb excess charge and distribute it safely through their contact with the stone floor. They are fed and housed by the Fortress in return.

Field note: Gedonyx can absorb up to four consecutive lightning strikes without discharging - on the fifth strike, it releases everything at once in a single directed bolt.

About Gedomancer

Gedomancer is the Steel Fortress's most ancient resident, a creature so laden with stored electrical charge that arc lightning jumps between its shell spikes continuously in dry weather. Its dark armored body is broad as a siege vehicle, its golden spikes now the size of large spears, and the horn on its head has grown long enough to serve as a deliberate lightning rod when it wishes to draw a bolt down from the sky. On the night of the great storm three centuries ago, a Gedomancer stood at the fortress's highest point and absorbed the entire storm, leaving the fortress untouched.

Field note: Gedomancer has never once been struck by lightning - it has always been what lightning strikes instead.

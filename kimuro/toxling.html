About Toxling

Toxling is a cheerful little purple-and-green snake, no longer than a boot, with bright round eyes and a wide, friendly smile. Its short body curves in a loose S-shape, and two small green stripes run down its sides like racing marks. Despite its gentle look, the tiny venom sacs behind its jaw are already developing, and it drools a faint purple mist when overexcited. It slithers through the Poison Marsh's flooded reeds, tasting the water for unfamiliar names to learn.

Field note: Toxling always smiles, even in battle - the grin is inherited; the venom sacs beneath it are entirely real.

About Venomcoil

Venomcoil rears up tall, its hood flared wide and bristling with sharp purple spines along the rim. Its green-and-purple scales have deepened to a richer, more dangerous pattern, and its eyes have narrowed to fierce amber slits. It can hold a hooded threat display for ten minutes without wavering, daring anything nearby to make a wrong move. In the Poison Marsh it controls the deep channels, rearing up from murky water with hood spread to steer creatures away from its den.

Field note: Venomcoil's hood-spread is so precise it can direct its venom spray to hit a target the size of a coin at three metres.

About Hydratox

Hydratox rises from the Poison Marsh as five cobra heads on thick, writhing necks - each head independent, each wearing its own hooded frill and bearing its own set of deadly fangs. Its coiled lower body is enormously thick, keeping it anchored in the deepest marsh mud. All five mouths can spray venom in different directions simultaneously, filling the air with a toxic purple fog. Ancient Abalont lore warns that any word spoken carelessly near a sleeping Hydratox is stolen by one of its five hungry minds.

Field note: Each of Hydratox's five heads hisses in a different pitch - together they form a dissonant chord that can freeze prey mid-step.

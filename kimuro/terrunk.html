About Terrunk

Terrunk is a small grey elephant calf with several small rock and crystal protrusions growing naturally from its head and upper back - not armor, but formations that grew there the way crystals grow from a cave wall, given time. It explores the Crystal Mines with curiosity, using its trunk to tap the walls and listen for hollow resonant spaces. The crystal growths on its back ring faintly when Terrunk stands in a chamber with particularly rich ore deposits, acting as an inadvertent mining detector that tunnel workers quietly follow.

Field note: The crystal growths on Terrunk's back ring at a different pitch for each ore type - miners have learned all twelve tones.

About Terravant

Terravant has developed heavy stone plating across its back and a visible amber crack-pattern running through its hide - lines where the rock has stretched to accommodate the growth beneath. Its trunk is now powerful enough to break mine columns, though it uses this strength primarily to clear rockfall from tunnel paths. The crystal formations on its back have grown taller and more numerous. Other creatures working the mines follow Terravant's patrol routes because its weight compresses loose stone, making passages safer after it passes.

Field note: Every tunnel Terravant regularly patrols has a collapse rate exactly sixty percent lower than tunnels it does not visit.

About Terramalith

Terramalith carries a mountain on its back - great rounded boulders have accumulated across its entire upper body, held in place by the accumulated stone-mass of its own hide. Its mammoth tusks curve broadly outward and its face carries the deep patience of something that has watched the Crystal Mines form and expand over centuries. Its movement creates minor seismic events that can be felt on the surface above the mines. Ancient mine records mark the oldest tunnels as 'Terramalith-opened' - passages cleared by a creature that needed more room to turn around.

Field note: The oldest passages in the Crystal Mines are exactly two Terramalith-body-widths wide - the minimum clearance it needs to turn.

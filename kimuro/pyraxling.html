About Pyraxling

Pyraxling is a round, soft-looking purple dragon hatchling with big expressive eyes, stubby wings it flaps too fast to ever lift off, and a tail it curls around itself when nervous. Its small horns have barely sprouted from its forehead, and it squeaks when surprised. Found on the lower ledges of storm spire where the wind is gentlest, it hatches during thunderstorms and is so drawn to the sound of rushing air that it will walk directly into a gust with its wings open, delighted, no matter how many times the wind knocks it over.

Field note: Pyraxling can predict storms twenty minutes early; its wingtips tremble slightly and it starts spinning in small circles.

About Pyraxon

Pyraxon's wings are now broad enough to catch real storm-currents, and it launches from spire ledges in long gliding sweeps, reading the updrafts with its pointed snout. Its purple scales have deepened to a violet-blue and its horns sweep elegantly back from its head. It can hover in place against a strong headwind without once flapping, adjusting only the tip angle of each wing. The storm spire's wind-channels are its training ground, and experienced Pyraxon can fly an entire circuit of the spire backwards to show off.

Field note: Pyraxon lands on precisely the same perch every evening; it has worn a dragon-shaped groove into the stone over the years.

About Impedrax

Impedrax is a massive violet dragon that rides the uppermost spire thermals where no bird flies, its great bat-wings holding it still as a flag in a gale. When it dives, the resulting windshear flattens anything within thirty meters. Its deep-purple scales bear silver-grey wind-scars from centuries of flying through storm cores, and its eyes have turned pale silver from looking into lightning. The guardians gave it a name that means Ruler of High Winds, and every storm that passes over the spire is said to wait for Impedrax's permission.

Field note: Impedrax's wingspan is exactly as wide as the widest gap in storm spire's upper arches, which is not a coincidence.

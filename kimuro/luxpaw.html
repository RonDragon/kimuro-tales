About Luxpaw

Luxpaw is a bright orange dragon pup with a crest of black horns and a soft cream ruff. It loves to practice little punches in the morning sun, and its fists glow faintly when it gets excited.

About Lumiclaw

Lumiclaw has grown tall and quick, with wide orange wings and dark claws. It trains from sunrise to sunset, and the black horn on its head has started to grow into the shape of a helmet.

About Luxstrike

Luxstrike is a proud light warrior. A black thorn spear grows from one arm and a horn shield from the other, both edged with golden light, and black scales guard its chest like armor. It fights only to protect its friends.

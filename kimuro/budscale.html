About Budscale

Budscale is a pale cream-and-green lizard with a vivid row of flower-petal-like spines cresting its back in blue, green, and yellow - a startling bloom of color on an otherwise quiet creature. It prowls the cooled lava margins of the crater where green things first push through cracked stone, eating the tender sprouts before they fully open. Budscale is remarkably hard to spot on volcanic rock until it flares its spine-crest in alarm, at which point it becomes impossible to miss. Young ones in a clutch always flare in sequence, creating a traveling wave of color along the whole group.

Field note: When a group of Budscale flare their crests in sequence, the wave travels at exactly the speed of a walking human - no one knows why.

About Florasaur

Florasaur has grown upright and angular, its spine-crest now a full ridge of sharp purple and green thorns that extend and vibrate when it roars. The pale scales have deepened to a mottled green-yellow, and it moves with the sudden-start pace of a creature that is very still and then very fast. Florasaur claims a stretch of the Lava Crater's green margin as its territory, enforcing it with a spine-flash and a sharp hiss that most creatures respect without further negotiation. It is fond of rearranging its territory's rocks into rough rings - no one has determined why.

Field note: Florasaur's rock rings are perfectly round within a centimeter - crater geologists have been quietly measuring them for years.

About Thorndrake

Thorndrake is a towering dark-scaled dragon whose enormous purple spike-mane rises from head to mid-back like a fortress palisade. The pale colouring is entirely gone - it has become all shadow-green and black with deep purple thorns that flex and clack when it breathes. Thorndrake is the Lava Crater's most unlikely guardian: a creature born of growing things that chose to root itself in fire, covering the green margins against Abalont's shadow by growing too fierce to be approached. It is said that where Thorndrake holds its ground, the first flowers through the lava never stop blooming.

Field note: The patch of green below Thorndrake's regular resting spot is the most fertile ground in the entire Lava Crater - nothing there has ever died.

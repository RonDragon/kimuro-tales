About Solkit

Solkit is a bright golden-yellow rabbit with long upright ears that catch the storm-spire wind and large vivid red eyes that hold the steady warmth of a carried sun. It holds its forepaws together in a ready posture and watches everything with focused attention. At high altitude its golden coat shines noticeably brighter, and travellers on the Storm Spire claim seeing it in the distance helps them find their direction even in cloud. It races the high winds Jack the raven once watched from above, carrying a little sun into every storm it enters.

Field note: Solkit's ears glow faintly in thick cloud - mountain rescuers in the Storm Spire follow those two amber lights when visibility fails.

About Solhare

Solhare stands upright on strong hind legs with a muscular build and golden-amber fur that catches the light like polished metal. Its long ears are tipped in bright gold and its red eyes have deepened to a determined gleam. The claws on its forepaws have sharpened and it holds them in a ready fighting stance. It trains in the highest wind-lashed platforms of the Storm Spire, using the resistance of the gale to strengthen its strikes. The guardians wrote that Solhare arrives whenever someone faces something too large for them alone.

Field note: Solhare always arrives from the upwind direction - storm navigators say you can predict its approach by the way the clouds suddenly part.

About Solarch

Solarch is a blazing figure - a tall upright golden-armoured hare with a full brilliant mane of golden light trailing behind its ears like a comet tail, one arm raised in a strike pose that burns bright enough to cast shadows behind you. Its entire body radiates steady warm light that cannot be put out. On the Storm Spire summit, Solarch's glow is visible from the valley below as a fixed golden star on the peak - the one light that Abalont's shadow never managed to reach. It carries the memory of every brave word spoken by every child who climbed up after the guardians.

Field note: The light from Solarch at the summit is listed on storm-spire navigation charts as a permanent landmark - it has not gone out in recorded history.

About Orbpod

Orbpod is a translucent lavender jellyfish-orb with a single bright blue eye at its center and a handful of tiny trailing tentacles that drift like smoke. It floats through Shadow Caves' air pockets with total silence, navigating by the psychic impressions left on cave surfaces by creatures that passed long ago. When it finds a particularly rich impression - a word spoken with great emotion - it hovers in place and pulses slowly, absorbing. Its translucent body means you can see its single eye from any angle, watching everything without moving.

Field note: Orbpod never moves faster than drifting speed - but it is never not exactly where it needs to be.

About Tentavox

Tentavox stands upright on four of its eight arms as legs, with the remaining four held forward and upward as sensory limbs that probe the air and walls of Shadow Caves continuously. Its purple body holds a faint bioluminescent glow, and its large single-eye stare carries an eerie intelligence that makes new visitors uncomfortable. It is in fact gentle - it is simply reading every feeling you carry from the psychic residue in your memories, sorting them by importance before you have finished saying hello. It lives in the lower cave passages where the psychic echo is strongest.

Field note: Tentavox can sort everything a new person is carrying by emotional weight within thirty seconds of meeting them - it is rarely wrong about which one matters most.

About Voxelord

Voxelord is a vast purple-dark octopus that moves through Shadow Caves like a slow tide, its eight thick arms spread wide enough to touch both walls of most passages simultaneously. Its central eye is a pure black void that seems to absorb light rather than reflect it, and the psychic field it projects is strong enough to be felt as physical pressure in a room. It does not speak or signal - it simply holds the full history of every voice ever raised in Shadow Caves in its memory, and those who can bear its gaze may access any of them.

Field note: The psychic pressure of Voxelord's presence can be felt as a subtle headache from a hundred meters away - researchers always know when it is approaching.

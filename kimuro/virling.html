About Virling

Virling is a small, fluffy cream-white lamb with a round wooly body, large luminous green eyes, and a few vine-tendrils already threading through its fleece like natural decoration. Small green leaves sprout from the top of its head where a fringe flops forward. It grazes the Light Grove's meadow clearings, trailing loose vine-ends that catch on blossoms as it walks. Its psychic sensitivity shows early: it wanders toward trees before the wind picks up, positioning itself in shelter before anyone else has noticed the weather change.

Field note: Virling's vine-fleece grows in the same direction as the prevailing wind; grove rangers use a sleeping Virling as a compass without disturbing it.

About Viroak

Viroak has grown into a plump, sturdy sheep with a thick cream fleece swirled through with spiral vine-green patterns that seem to shift and drift if watched from the corner of the eye. Curved vine-horns have grown from its brow in smooth spirals. Its eyes have deepened to a vivid psychic green. It wanders the Light Grove in wide meditative circles, and grove-keepers say it is mapping every song-tree's emotional state - not just their health, but whether they remember being tended. Those that have been forgotten, Viroak lingers beside longest.

Field note: The spiral patterns in Viroak's fleece subtly match the growth-rings of whatever tree it stood closest to in the past season.

About Viroakian

Viroakian is a great, heavy-fleeced ram whose spiraling vine-horns have grown vast and elaborate, curling outward and upward in complex double-helix sweeps thick with blossoms. Its fleece cascades to the grove floor in living curtains of vine and leaf, and its eyes carry a permanent distant focus - it is reading threads of thought that no one else can perceive. The Light Grove's oldest trees are known to bloom first in whatever clearing Viroakian has passed through most recently, as though its psychic presence waters them at the roots of their memory.

Field note: Viroakian has been recorded producing a psychic resonance that causes nearby sleepers to dream about the places they walked that day, settling the memory overnight.

About Lunbun

A perfectly white rabbit with long pink-tipped ears and red-pink eyes, this Lunbun is pure and round, its fur cloud-soft. It hops through Crystal Mines' upper galleries where refracted light dances on the walls, and it seems to navigate by the patterns - stopping at each new light-shape and studying it before hopping onward. Crystal miners say a Lunbun in the tunnels means the vein nearby is rich, for it always sits nearest the most brilliant crystal without ever being told where it is.

Field note: Lunbun leaves no footprints in crystal dust - its hops are so precise it always lands on solid stone, never powder.

About Lunare

Lunare has grown fluffier and more assured, its white fur fuller, its eyes now carrying a steady pink glow. It still lives among Crystal Mines' deeper galleries, but now it moves with quiet purpose - pausing at crystal formations and pressing its nose lightly to the surface as if listening. Light bends around it in small prismatic arcs. Guardians who explore the mines with a Lunare discover tunnels they would otherwise have missed, for it always pauses longest at the openings that lead somewhere new.

Field note: Lunare's ears rotate independently and can locate an echo's source point within an arm's length - useful in labyrinthine crystal tunnels.

About Selenyx

Selenyx bounds through Crystal Mines in a blaze of light and streaming pastel ribbons - its larger form trails long luminous scarves of pink and white light from its ears and tail that flutter like torn silk. Every crystal it passes rings softly in response. Its fur catches the mine's refracted light and scatters it further, so that wherever it runs the walls light up. Crystal Mine elders say Selenyx appears only when the mines need a guide - and that following it always leads out safely, even from passages that no longer exist on any map.

Field note: The light-ribbons trailing from Selenyx's ears can tie themselves in knots when it leaps - and each knot-shape is a word in an old language.

About Corvit

Corvit is a perfectly round, soft purple bird-chick with one huge golden eye and tiny wing-buds that have not yet spread. It sits on Gale Ridge's lowest ledges gripping the wind-worn rock with both feet and faces directly into the gale, feathers rippling. Its one visible eye never blinks in the wind. It can feel the psychic resonance in the air-currents, and it sorts them by pitch - keeping the voices it recognizes, releasing the ones it doesn't yet understand back into the wind to circle the spire again.

Field note: Corvit sorts every wind-call it receives: recognized ones are swallowed, unrecognized ones are breathed back out to circle the spire again.

About Ravolt

Ravolt is a lean, angular purple raven with a narrow head-crest, strong wings that trail slightly longer than its body, and golden eyes that take on a faint metallic sheen during storms. It lives in Gale Ridge's mid-elevation wind-bands, riding the circular gales without landing for up to eight hours at a time. When a new sound arrives on the wind it catches it in its beak - literally, as a small glowing note - and carries it to the nearest learning-stone before releasing it.

Field note: Ravolt has been observed carrying a wind-call in its beak for up to eight hours without dropping it, even through full storm conditions.

About Vexrune

At Gale Ridge's summit it has etched rune-marks into the rock with its talons over decades - a spiralling record of everything the wind has carried to the Ridge since Abalont's shadow fell.

Field note: Vexrune's summit rune-archive spirals outward from the center: the innermost ring is the oldest known written record on Gale Ridge.

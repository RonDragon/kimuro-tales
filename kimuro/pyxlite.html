About Pyxlite

Pyxlite is a tiny round purple jellyfish drifting silently above the poison marsh's still surfaces, trailing four short tentacles and bearing a glowing lavender orb on its crown. It produces no sound and barely stirs the air it moves through. The marshland's toxic vapors pass right through it - ghost and fire both render the poison harmless to Pyxlite. Children of the poison marsh region are told to follow a Pyxlite through fog, as it always drifts toward clean air. Its crown-orb glows brighter near words spoken with kindness, dimmer near harsh ones.

Field note: Pyxlite's tentacles never touch the marsh water; they hover exactly one centimeter above the surface at all times.

About Pyxaura

Pyxaura has grown into a larger jellyfish with a clearly visible flame burning inside its translucent dome body - a warm orange-gold fire that illuminates the bell from within like a paper lantern. Its tentacles have multiplied and lengthened, trailing through the marsh air. The inner flame casts a moving glow across the murky water below and the poison-ivy walls of the marsh. Marsh-travelers say Pyxaura's flame goes out if it passes over a place where a lie was told, and comes back brighter when it drifts over where a truth was spoken.

Field note: Pyxaura's inner flame burns at exactly the same intensity day and night; the marsh appears brighter during the day only because Pyxaura is easier to see against dark sky.

About Pyxalith

Pyxalith has transformed into a breathtaking form - a golden-framed lantern encasing a brilliant fire, mounted within a spectral dome and ringed by heavy purple-gold tentacle-arms as thick as rope. Its golden frame bears inscribed symbols the guardians cannot fully translate, suggesting it predates the known history of the poison marsh. It drifts through the deepest marsh channels where no solid ground exists, and the flame at its center burns with a color that cannot be named - somewhere between orange and gold and something purely its own. The marsh itself grows quieter in its presence.

Field note: The symbols on Pyxalith's golden frame change arrangement once per lunar cycle; scholars have been recording the sequence for ninety years without finding a repeat.

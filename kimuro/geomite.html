About Geomite

Geomite is a small green turtle with wide curious eyes and a triangular-faceted shell made of purple and blue crystal rather than conventional bone. The crystal shell catches the dappled light of Light Grove and casts tiny prismatic rainbows on the moss below as it walks. When it withdraws into its shell, it can feel vibrations from far-off footsteps through the crystal's resonance, giving it an early warning system much larger creatures envy. The grove's guardian spirits reportedly taught Geomite to arrange small crystal shards in patterns that mark safe paths through the thickest undergrowth.

Field note: The crystal shell's internal facets form an ancient sigil that no living person has fully unravelled - only Geomite knows what it means.

About Geoshell

Geoshell has grown considerably, and its purple crystal shell has expanded and sprouted sharp angular spikes that jut outward from each facet. Its green-scaled neck and head are broader now, and it carries itself with unhurried authority through Light Grove's ancient groves. The crystal shell resonates at psychic frequencies that calm agitated creatures within a thirty-meter radius - birds that would normally not land near predators settle on the forest floor near a resting Geoshell. Explorers claim that sleeping near one reliably produces the most vivid and educational dreams they have ever had.

Field note: Every spike on Geoshell's crystal shell is aligned toward a different star - on clear nights its shell functions as an astronomical navigation tool.

About Geotyrant

Geotyrant is immense and ancient beyond guessing - a paired double-bodied giant whose two crystal-laden tortoise forms move in perfect synchrony, connected by a shared crystalline ridge that has fused the two shells into a single vast structure. When it moves, the sound of crystal plates grinding against each other is the sound the Light Grove uses to mark the deep hours of the night. The psychic resonance of its double shell is so powerful that it can project full visual memories into the minds of every creature in the grove simultaneously - the guardians called these broadcasts the "All-Lessons."

Field note: Geotyrant's double body has two heartbeats that alternate - never synchronize, never diverge - a rhythm that Light Grove healers use to calibrate their own pulse when they lose count.

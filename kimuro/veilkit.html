About Veilkit

Veilkit looks like a smooth, grey-purple oval with two wide, glowing lavender eyes and a single bright psychic gem embedded in its belly. Tiny stubby limbs press close to its sides, and the whole body has a pearly sheen as though lit from inside. It sits perfectly still in shafts of light that filter through the Psychic Temple canopy, soaking up the luminous energy of light-trees. It communicates entirely by pulsing its belly gem - one pulse for yes, two for no - before it has yet grown a voice.

Field note: Veilkit's belly gem pulses exactly once per second; a double-pulse means it has sensed an unfamiliar sound nearby.

About Veilsect

Veilsect has encased itself in a segmented purple-grey cocoon of chitin, three horizontal bands of ridged armour wrapping its torso. Its head emerges with large compound eyes glowing violet and a pair of small antennae that quiver to pick up stray thoughts. The gem has moved to a deep recess in its chest and pulses with a slow, rhythmic throb. Resting against light-tree trunks in the Psychic Temple, it absorbs the stored warmth stored in the bark and grows its mind quietly in the dark.

Field note: Press an ear to a light-tree where Veilsect rests and you will hear faint notes humming back - borrowed sounds it is memorising.

About Veilstyx

Veilstyx hovers among the highest light-trees of the Psychic Temple, patrolling corridors of still, humming air. The Guardians called it the Keeper of Promises - it remembers every voice it has ever stood near, and forgets none of them.

Field note: Veilstyx can replay any voice it has ever heard in perfect clarity, projecting it as a shimmering note audible only to the person who first spoke.

About Hornkin

Hornkin is a small, round-faced brown yak calf with two tiny nubby white horns and a coat of soft shaggy brown fur. Its blue eyes hold a combination of gentleness and stubbornness that is immediately endearing. It wanders Frozen Peaks' stony shoreline, using its small horns to nudge barnacles off rocks and lick the salt from underneath. Despite being an earth-and-ice type, Hornkin is completely at home near the water, and the cold ocean spray keeps its coat pleasantly cool. Fishers leave out scraps for the Hornkin that visit their docks.

Field note: Hornkin can identify fourteen different types of edible shore-moss by smell alone and will travel up to two kilometers for its favourite.

About Taurite

Taurite is a full-grown shaggy brown yak with swept-back pale horns and a heavy wool coat that hangs in thick curtains around its hooves. It stands near the waterline of Frozen Peaks without seeming to mind the waves breaking over its feet, watching the horizon with the patience of something that has stood there for a very long time. The cold currents keep its heavy coat comfortable and the salt air has worked its way into the wool, making its fur feel permanently slightly damp. Younger creatures shelter behind Taurite during storms without asking permission.

Field note: Taurite has stood at the same section of Frozen Peaks shoreline for at least eleven consecutive winters - it has worn a groove in the rock.

About Hornquake

Hornquake has been overtaken by ice - its coat is now a thick mantle of ice boulders and deep blue ice plates that cover its shoulders and back like natural armor, and its horns have grown into sweeping pale ice formations that arc forward and outward like a glacier's leading edge. Its body generates so much cold that a permanent frost ring extends two meters in every direction, and the rocks under its hooves crack from the temperature differential when it walks. Frozen Peaks' water is always coldest near wherever Hornquake last stood, even days afterward.

Field note: The water temperature in Frozen Peaks drops by two degrees within a radius of fifty meters whenever Hornquake wades in.

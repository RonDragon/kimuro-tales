About Sproutoad

Sproutoad is a round little toad the colour of wet soil, with a single white-budded sprout growing from between its eyes like a tiny candle. Its wide eyes blink slowly, and it sits almost perfectly still, watching. The sprout bobs when Sproutoad is excited and folds flat when it senses something creeping in the cave dark. Explorers in Shadow Caves use its flickering bud as a dim lantern, tiptoeing behind it through the blackest passages.

Field note: The bud on Sproutoad's head glows faintly for about three seconds whenever it hears a name it has never heard it recognises.

About Thornogre

Thornogre has grown into something half-toad, half-thicket - its warty green hide is draped in rough leaf-layers, and a wild crown of thorned vines spirals from its head. A curling vine-tail trails behind it as it lumbers through the cave undergrowth. Its mouth stretches almost ear-to-ear, and it uses its long tongue not just to catch prey but to taste the air for the bitter scent of dark-energy surges. Shadow Caves explorers call it the gatekeeper of the deep passages.

Field note: Thornogre never loses its way in the caves - it reads the dark with its tongue the way others read signs with their eyes.

About Verdewrath

Verdewrath is vast and low-slung, its body half-buried in its own wild moss and dark-vine growth. Glowing green vapour seeps from the dense tangle on its back, and its enormous flat head is nearly obscured by the living canopy it carries. It does not hunt - it simply opens its cavernous mouth and waits, patient as the cave itself, for the dark to bring things to it. Old tales say Verdewrath once swallowed a whole shadow-curse and slept for a century while the caves slowly brightened around it.

Field note: The green vapour rising from Verdewrath's back is mildly sleep-inducing - visitors often wake up somewhere safer than where they started.

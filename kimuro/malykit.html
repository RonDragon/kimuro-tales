About Malykit

Malykit stands upright on two powerful legs with wide clawed feet and long clawed arms raised to its sides - ready for anything from the moment it wakes. Its deep crimson-dark body is lean and angular, with glowing red eyes set in a flat spiky-headed face. It haunts Ghost Hollow's darker recesses, moving with sharp sudden bursts between long stillnesses, and has a habit of scratching territorial marks into trees at exactly eye height for a standing child. Guardians say it watches the guardians' old drills from the shadows and gets visibly agitated when a step is done wrong.

Field note: Malykit leaves claw marks in trees at exactly the same height every time - never an inch higher, never lower.

About Malyxen

Malyxen has grown taller and more defined - a lean bipedal imp-lizard with a broad chest and long serrated claws on hands and feet. Its skin is deep purple-red, traced with darker markings, and its glowing yellow eyes carry an intensity of focus. It patrols Ghost Hollow's middle levels with deliberate authority, stopping at each marked territory-point and adding a fresh scratch before moving on. When it fights it moves in sharp angular bursts - no wasted motion, no unnecessary flourish. Guardians say Malyxen is the most efficient creature in the Hollow.

Field note: Malyxen's claw-marks are catalogued by Ghost Hollow researchers - they form a precise territorial map updated every three days.

About Malyxord

Malyxord is Ghost Hollow's apex dark creature - tall and broad-shouldered, its body dense and powerful, every surface covered in overlapping dark armored scales that shine like obsidian. Long serrated claws curve at both hands and feet, and its wide chest bears a pattern of deeper crimson lines that pulse faintly. It stands at the Hollow's deepest interior boundary and nothing crosses without its decision. Ancient guardian writings describe Malyxord as the Hollow's last defense - the creature the darkness itself shaped to push back anything that would unmake the world further. Even Abalont's agents keep to the outer ring when Malyxord is present.

Field note: Malyxord's presence causes every ghost-light in a fifty-meter radius to burn steadier - as if the lights are trying to impress it.

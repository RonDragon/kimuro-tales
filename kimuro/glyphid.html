About Glyphid

Glyphid is a pale purple grub with a glossy segmented body, a round face with simple dot-eyes, and a neat purple orb on its head. It inches through the soil of the Dark Forest floor, eating absorbed shadow-matter that has soaked into the roots. As it feeds, very faint symbol-like marks appear briefly on its back and fade - the guardians who study the forest believe these are fragments of old words that Abalont's darkness has corrupted and buried. Glyphid eats them, and where it passes the soil is slightly lighter.

Field note: The marks that flicker on Glyphid's back are always legible for exactly one second before fading - never the same symbol twice.

About Glyphax

Glyphax has sealed itself inside a dark rounded cocoon-shell covered in dense living purple-black runes that shift and rewrite themselves continuously. It is not dormant - it is processing. Inside the cocoon it is decoding every corrupted word it absorbed as a grub, working through them one by one. Touch the cocoon at the wrong moment and the rune under your finger shows you a face you have forgotten - sometimes one you never knew. The Dark Forest grows quieter around a Glyphax, as if the shadows are listening.

Field note: Glyphax cocoons are always warm to the touch regardless of air temperature - and the warmth is highest where the active runes are.

About Glypheron

Glypheron emerges as a broad dark beetle with a polished obsidian-black shell covered in glowing purple rune-marks that shift across its back in flowing lines - every voice it ever absorbed as a grub now lives on its carapace in legible form. Its stubby legs hold it close to the ground and its two antennae wave slowly, reading the air for more corrupted language. The Dark Forest scholars discovered that standing near a Glypheron and tracing its markings restores faded guardian carvings - the beetle's shell is a living record of lost names.

Field note: Glypheron's rune-marks include seventeen words that scholars had declared permanently lost after Abalont's first shadow-wave.

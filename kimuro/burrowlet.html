About Burrowlet

Tuskora's first form is a small, dark-purple mole with two gleaming silver crystal-blade tusks proportionally enormous for its body, tiny star-shaped pink ears that rotate independently, and a round velvet snout. Its small forelimbs are already broad-pawed and built for digging. In the Light Grove it burrows up into glowing clearings at dusk, squinting at the brightness before deciding it is worth it, and then diving back down at the slightest loud noise.

Field note: Burrowlet moles use their star-shaped ears like radar - one full rotation in each direction before they surface anywhere new.

About Tuskora

Tuskora has grown wide and low, its dark body now thick with muscle and its crystal tusks gleaming silver-blue in any light. Its paws have widened into formidable digging tools edged with hardened steel-like nails. Small red star-shaped markings sit behind its shoulders - possibly sensory organs, possibly ornamental. It spends most of its time just below the surface of the Light Grove, creating a dense network of tunnels that routes moisture to the grove's oldest trees - a fact the trees seem to appreciate.

Field note: Tuskora digs its tunnel network to within a hand-width of every major tree root in its territory - never breaking through, just staying close.

About Tuskquake

The Light Grove's Tuskquake form is a wide-bodied, ridge-backed mole-titan whose entire dorsal surface is covered in overlapping armour plates that look forged rather than grown. Its multiple massive crystal-steel tusks radiate outward like a crown of blades. It moves underground mostly, but when it surfaces - which happens only for significant threats - the ground bulges and cracks before it emerges. The grove's oldest guardian trees are said to have grown up around the routes this creature carved, their roots tracing every tunnel like a map.

Field note: When Tuskquake surfaces in the Light Grove, the surrounding trees lean slightly toward it for three minutes afterward - as if greeting an old friend.

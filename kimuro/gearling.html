About Gearling

Gearling is a small mechanical-looking praying mantis with segmented dark metal plating over its thorax and abdomen, two slender antennae that double as signal receivers, and small grasping forelimbs it holds raised and ready at all times. It moves in precise, jerky increments, each movement measured rather than fluid, as if calculating the most efficient path before each step. The Steel Fortress hums at a frequency Gearling can feel in its antenna tips, and it follows the vibrations of machinery the way other insects follow flower scent.

Field note: Gearling can calculate the RPM of any spinning gear by feel alone - it places one foreleg against the housing and counts without looking.

About Mechant

Mechant stands tall on four legs with the regal posture of a machine that knows its own precision. Its forearms have lengthened into curved scythe-like blades with serrated gear-notch edges that spin freely when it strikes. Its body segments are now reinforced with interlocking metal plates that click into tighter configurations when it braces for impact. Inside the Steel Fortress, Mechant is treated as a maintenance creature - it instinctively tightens loose bolts with its foreleg notches and will spend hours re-routing a jammed gear track until it runs smoothly.

Field note: Mechant has never missed a strike in a recorded sparring session - engineers in the fortress have bet wages on it for years.

About Omnivex

Omnivex is the most sophisticated creature in the Steel Fortress - a towering mantis armored in heavy interlocking gear-plate, with broad gear-disc shoulders, six powerful legs, and twin sickle forearms that can slice through reinforced metal bulkheads. Its multiple compound eyes process information from sixteen directions simultaneously. The fortress's automated defense systems were reportedly designed with Omnivex as the model; it still walks the outer ramparts each night in the same pattern the automated turrets follow. Where Omnivex walks, nothing unauthorized survives.

Field note: Omnivex never attacks from the front - it has never once been documented initiating a strike from a position an opponent was facing.

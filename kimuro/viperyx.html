About Viperyx

Viperyx (poison line) is a small, cheerful-looking purple-lilac serpent with a single glowing white horn on its forehead that gives it the air of a tiny, serpentine unicorn. Wide eyes and a broad smiling face make it seem approachable - which is somewhat misleading, as its horn carries a weak numbing charge. It coils in the shallower channels of the Poison Marsh, occasionally rearing up to flash the horn at anything that interests it. New trainers consistently underestimate it. The guardians noted that its horn pulses faster when it hears unfamiliar names, as if trying to record them.

Field note: Viperyx's horn-glow brightens when it encounters a word it hasn't heard before - marsh guides say the brightness is a reliable measure of how new an experience is.

About Noxyx

Noxyx rears high in a wide cobra-hood spread, its body now deep blue-purple with banded steel-blue ridges and short back-spines. The single horn has grown prominent and sharp, and a thin venom-mist wisps from its fangs. It holds the deepest lily-channel stretches of the Poison Marsh as territory, and any creature approaching its marker stones receives one warning rattle of back-spines before being driven off. Despite its fierce territory-keeping, Noxyx has been observed using its horn to carefully lift trapped insects from sticky marsh-plants and deposit them on safe lily pads - a quirk that marsh scholars find fascinating.

Field note: Noxyx uses its horn to draw geometric patterns in the marsh mud each dawn; researchers haven't agreed on whether the patterns are territorial markings or something else entirely.

About Toxhydra

Toxhydra is massive, its deep blue-grey body coiled into ridged mountains of scale, and its broad hood edged in sharp ice-blue spines that make it look armored even at rest. The single horn has become a great swept ridge of crystalized venom. Poison mists roll continuously from its open jaw. It owns the deepest known chamber of the Poison Marsh, where no other creature dares rest. Ancient accounts describe Toxhydra as the reason the Poison Marsh was never fully claimed by Abalont - not because it opposes the shadow, but because it opposes all intrusion with identical ferocity. The shadow learned not to compete.

Field note: Toxhydra's shed skin sections have been found up to two kilometres from its territory - researchers believe other creatures carry them as deterrents against predators.

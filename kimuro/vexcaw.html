About Vexcaw

Vexcaw (raven line) is a tiny puff of black feathers with a single vivid purple eye that catches light like an amethyst. Its stubby wings and thread-like tentacle tail-feathers drag behind it as it hops through the Light Grove, strangely drawn to the brightest clearings despite its shadowy nature. It tilts its head at words as if memorizing them, storing everything it hears. The guardians of the Light Grove noticed these little dark birds nesting near the song-trees - opposites resting in the same branches.

Field note: Vexcaw (raven) mimics the last word it hears before nightfall and whispers it back in the dark hours before dawn.

About Vexraven

Vexraven spreads broad, ragged black wings edged with deep purple shimmer, its long wispy tentacle-like tail-feathers trailing threads of shadow. Two intense purple eyes carry a sharp, knowing gaze. It nests in the oldest trees of the Light Grove, where the glow of the grove-light throws its shadow into strange shapes on the bark. Those shapes are not random - rangers say Vexraven draws maps in shadow, marking paths only it can follow. It can read mood the way others read weather, and unsettles those with guilty hearts.

Field note: The shadow-shapes Vexraven casts on walls shift and change for up to ten minutes after it flies away, as if the shadows remember.

About Vexomancer

Vexomancer is a vast, magnificent raven-form wreathed in flowing shadow-tendrils that billow and twist like ink in water. Its wingspan fills the canopy clearing, and its body radiates dark and psychic energies that cause nearby lanterns to flicker and animals to fall silent. Three concentric rings of purple light orbit its chest. The Light Grove holds it in awe - the oldest texts say Vexomancer arrived the very day Abalont's shadow first touched the Grove's edge, and that its presence has slowed the darkness's spread. It speaks in voices it has collected over centuries.

Field note: Vexomancer is said to know every voice raised in the Light Grove for the past three hundred years - and chooses only one to say aloud per decade.

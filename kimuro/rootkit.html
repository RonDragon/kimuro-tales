About Rootkit

Rootkit is a round, chubby brown badger-like creature with a single broad leaf sprouting from the top of its head like an oversized hat it has not grown into yet. Its stubby legs and round body give it a rolling, tumbling gait, and it digs compulsively wherever it finds soft soil, piling up tiny earthen mounds in neat rows. In the dark forest's leaf-littered floor it spends its mornings nose-deep in the earth, cataloguing seeds by taste and then carefully replanting each one in a slightly different spot. It insists this is an improvement.

Field note: Rootkit replants every seed it finds in a new location - foresters claim it has single-handedly moved the dark forest south by several meters.

About Foliahorn

Foliahorn has grown upright and broad, with a thick collar of layered leaves fanning out around its neck and shoulders like a ruff of forest finery. Its strong clawed forepaws are built for digging, and the dark forest floor is riddled with its tunnel-networks below each patrol route. The leaf-collar seems to grow more leaves after a good day's walk, and Foliahorn has been observed collecting fallen leaves pressed flat between old stones - with great care and ceremony. It treats every piece of knowledge as something to be carefully buried for later.

Field note: Foliahorn buries knowledge like seeds - it digs up old 'planted' facts weeks later and presents them at exactly the right moment.

About Verdagarth

Verdagarth is a massive, four-legged creature of rock and leaf - its body is encrusted with mossy stone plates, and a full explosion of thick green foliage erupts from its back and shoulders like a living forest carried on its frame. Its legs are as wide as young tree trunks, and the ground shakes with slow authority as it walks the dark forest paths. It does not rush; it arrives. Creatures of the forest fall quiet when it passes, and the oldest trees bow their branches. It keeps the dark forest's deepest root-libraries - chambers of pressed leaf-songs no living creature besides itself has yet read.

Field note: Verdagarth knows the locations of all root-libraries in the dark forest and will guide any learner to the right one without being asked.

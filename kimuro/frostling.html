About Frostling

Frostling is a delicate small deer with a cream-and-tan coat traced with glowing blue swirl patterns, and a single slender ice-blue horn rising from its forehead. Its four thin legs end in tiny hooves that leave frost patterns on soft earth. It browses the lower paths of Frozen Peaks at dusk, and wherever it passes the wildflowers close gently as if bowing. It seems to absorb the snowfield's ambient psychic energy through its horn and radiate it back as cool calm light - young children fall effortlessly asleep within three paces of a resting Frostling.

Field note: A Frostling's swirl markings shift slowly when it is thinking deeply, tracing new patterns never seen twice.

About Glacstag

Glacstag has become a tall, elegant stag with an impressive rack of branching ice-blue antlers that shimmer with psychic light. Its long flowing tail of icy vapour drifts behind it as it moves through Frozen Peaks. The swirl markings across its flanks pulse in response to emotion - calm when the snowfield is safe, rapid when it senses Abalont's darkness nearby. It communicates with the snowfield's oldest trees by pressing its antlers to their bark, and they seem to respond, slowly turning their branches toward it.

Field note: Glacstag's antlers collect dew each morning and freeze it into tiny intricate sculptures by midday.

About Wyvernox

Wyvernox is a magnificent, blazing creature - a huge ice stag surrounded by cascading cold psychic fire that pours from its massive crystalline antlers and ripples down its spine. Its entire body glows ice-blue and the psychic energy it radiates reshapes the air around it into slowly turning geometric patterns. When it walks through Frozen Peaks the trees part ahead of it of their own accord and close behind like a door. The guardians named it the Dreamer-Stag, believing its presence keeps the waking world from blurring into Abalont's nightmare realm.

Field note: Standing beneath Wyvernox's antlers for one minute lets a traveller recall something they had long stopped thinking about.

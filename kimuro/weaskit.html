About Weaskit

Weaskit is a small, narrow-bodied creature - warm tan-brown, long-tailed, with a face that is pure sweet mischief. It moves in quick darting bursts, low to the ground, vanishing under roots before you can get a proper look. Ghost Hollow is full of old earth-mounds and hollow trunks, and Weaskit knows every one. It hides shiny objects from the hollow's ghost-folk in secret caches, then forgets half of them - which means the hollow is full of little buried treasures no one can find.

Field note: Weaskit caches average 23 items each - researchers have found only 4 per cache on average, which means 19 items per cache are never retrieved.

About Feroxen

Feroxen has grown lean and low-bellied, its long sinuous body built for threading through root systems and hollow-earth tunnels without slowing. Its eyes have deepened to amber-gold and carry a calculating gleam. The warm tan of kithood has shifted toward rich dark brown with shadows at the flanks. Feroxen moves through Ghost Hollow's tunnels with possessive confidence - it knows who enters, who leaves, and what they've brought. Ghost hollow guides say hiring a Feroxen's cooperation is the only safe way to navigate the deeper burrows.

Field note: Feroxen mentally maps every tunnel it enters and can retrace any path it has run, even once, years later.

About Savagrath

Savagrath is broad, powerful, and alarming - a massive dark-brown mustelid whose shoulders hunch with stored tension. Its jaw is deep and wide, built for crushing, and its claws leave furrows in packed earth. It rules the deepest roots of Ghost Hollow with absolute authority. The ghost-folk give it a wide berth; the hollow's shadows deepen noticeably wherever Savagrath has recently passed. Legends say Savagrath once bit through one of Abalont's shadow-tendrils when it strayed too close, and the shadow tasted so foul that Savagrath has been angry about it ever since.

Field note: Savagrath's territory markers are dug exactly 40 centimetres deep - the same depth as the hollow's oldest root system.

About Rosling

Rosling is an impossibly pink elephant calf, round and soft as a pillow, with tiny floppy ears and a curious trunk that it uses to prod everything within reach. It radiates a faint warmth that other creatures find immediately soothing - anxiety tends to dissolve in a two-meter radius around a Rosling. In the Light Grove it toddles between the luminous trees, patting roots and humming to itself in a frequency too low to hear but that makes flowers open early. Guardians say Rosling was the first creature to greet them when they arrived, and it offered comfort before anyone said a word.

Field note: Plants within two meters of a sleeping Rosling open their blooms out of season - florists in the grove call it the pink alarm clock.

About Rosmind

Rosmind has grown into a proper young elephant, still very pink but now with a swirling pink marking on its forehead that glows faintly when it concentrates. Its ears are larger now, always slightly raised as if listening to frequencies beyond normal hearing. It walks slowly through the Light Grove touching the luminous trees with its trunk-tip, and the trees seem to respond - brightening slightly, then fading as it moves on, as if a conversation is happening. Researchers believe Rosmind is actually reading stored memories from the trees, a skill no other creature has demonstrated.

Field note: Rosmind spends thirty minutes each morning touching each luminous tree in its grove - the trees it skips are always the ones that need attention.

About Rosmarch

Rosmarch is a grand, deep-rose mammoth with a full pair of gently curved tusks and psychic spiral markings winding along its flanks in bright pink light. Its bulk is enormous but it moves with extraordinary gentleness - not a single flower is crushed where it walks. In the Light Grove it is the memory-keeper, carrying the emotional record of every creature that has ever lived there within its vast, calm mind. Abalont could not reach the Light Grove's heart while Rosmarch stood before it; the creature's psychic field simply returned every shadow-thought to sender, peaceful and unchanged.

Field note: Rosmarch remembers every creature it has ever met, including their mood on every visit - it will mirror your own happiest expression back to you.

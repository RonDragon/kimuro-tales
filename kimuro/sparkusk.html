About Sparkusk

Sparkusk is a dark-furred pup bristling with energy - its fur stands in spiky tufts all over, and bold yellow lightning bolt markings streak its sides and haunches. Its sharp eyes dart between shadows constantly, and it instinctively circles any new object three times before approaching it. In Storm Spire, Sparkusk pups can be found in small clusters at the boundaries between the Hollow's inner dark and the faint light of the surrounding forest, neither fully in nor fully out - curious about both, committed to neither.

Field note: Sparkusk circles any new object exactly three times before deciding whether to approach or retreat - it has never been observed skipping this ritual.

About Thunderusk

Thunderusk has grown into a lean and powerful four-legged creature with vivid yellow lightning-bolt body markings that glow faintly even in daylight. Its dark fur has tightened against its frame, and its silhouette moving through the Storm Spire trees is more lightning-shape than animal-shape. It runs without sound until it chooses to - the spire goes perfectly silent for three beats before Thunderusk passes, and loud again three beats after. Old Storm Spire stories say that if you hear that silence, you are about to be tested on something you thought you had forgotten.

Field note: Storm Spire goes silent for exactly three seconds before Thunderusk passes through any given point on its route.

About Voltexar

Voltexar's body is a map of lightning - bold yellow bolts cover its dark coat from muzzle to tail in patterns that shift slightly each time it sheds, as if the electricity itself is redrawing its own signature. It stands with a weight and stillness that makes the ground feel different beneath its paws. When it discharges a full bolt it disappears entirely in the flash and reappears three body-lengths ahead - a blink-step that Storm Spire explorers insist must be cheating. The guardians recorded it as the Hollow's Arbiter: the creature that decides who may leave.

Field note: Voltexar's lightning-bolt markings shift slightly every six months as it sheds - each new pattern is different from any that came before.

About Posskit

Posskit is a small round-bodied opossum-like creature with soft grey-white fur, a pointed snout, and a thin pink tail that curls at the tip. Its big round ears frame a face full of alert curiosity. It clutches a small golden orb in its front paws - a psychic focus-stone it found in a hollow log and has never let go of since. In Verdant Valley, Posskit is found in the song-trees, hanging by its tail from low branches and listening to the leaves talk. Its gold orb glows faintly when it hears a word it has never heard before, which in its first weeks happens almost every hour.

Field note: Posskit's golden orb pulses once for every new voice it hears in a day - trainers count the pulses to track how far it has wandered.

About Possurge

Possurge has grown edgy and clever, its grey-brown fur now rougher and its posture upright and ready. The golden orb still hangs at its chest, but now it orbits in a slow arc around its body like a moon on a leash. Its long rat-like tail is fully prehensile, and it uses it to snatch objects from trainers mid-conversation. It sprints through the valley with low scuttling bursts followed by explosive leaps, zigzagging in ways that seem impossible until you realize it is reading the valley's wind currents with its psychic sense two seconds ahead of their arrival. Possurge never falls.

Field note: Possurge has never once stumbled or dropped a held object in its life - researchers believe it psychically calculates each footfall in advance.

About Possemancy

Possemancy stands upright like a seasoned champion, its dark grey fur wild and electric, and multiple golden orbs now orbit its body in slow intersecting circles that crackle with psychic energy. Its long tail coils and uncoils with a life of its own, and its sharp eyes hold the amused patience of a creature that has seen everything twice. It can pluck words from the air - literally: Verdant Valley children report that after Possemancy passes, the word they were about to say appears visible in the air in glowing letters for three seconds before fading. The guardians call it the Memory-Keeper: it holds Akilla's last spoken word hidden somewhere in one of its circling orbs.

Field note: The golden orbs orbiting Possemancy all stop moving simultaneously for exactly three seconds when it recalls a memory more than a century old.

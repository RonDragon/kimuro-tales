About Frosicalf

Frosicalf is a small, round-bodied calf with a short single horn of pale blue ice rising from its forehead and a coat that shimmers with frost-patterns in seafoam blue. Its round eyes and stubby legs give it a determined, if wobbly, look as it wades the tidal shallows of Frozen Peaks. The water around its hooves drops a few degrees when it steps in, forming a thin skin of ice that cracks pleasantly as it walks and serves as a fishing platform for the peaks' smaller creatures who hop on before it melts.

Field note: Frosicalf's ice-horn grows exactly one millimeter per day in winter and retreats the same amount in summer, acting as a precise seasonal calendar.

About Frostaur

Frostaur is a powerful, shaggy bull whose thick white-blue coat is permanently rimmed with frost crystals and whose two long horns curve outward and up. The water temperature of a mountain tarn drops noticeably within minutes of Frostaur wading in, and the cold-moss along its regular paths has adapted to those sudden chills, growing in a shade of blue found nowhere else in the range. Its massive neck and shoulders carry the frost-coat like armour, and rival creatures give it a wide berth - not out of fear, but because direct contact leaves a ten-second cold-burn.

Field note: Frostaur's hoofprints in the peak scree fill with clear cold meltwater that stays fresh for two days - a useful water source for small animals in the dry season.

About Glacihorn

Glacihorn is a titanic ice-encrusted creature whose entire body is now more glacier than animal, with great ice-formations growing along its spine and a crown of crystalline horns that chime in the mountain wind. Its breath comes out as dense blue-white vapour and its footsteps leave ice that lingers on bare rock for days. Frozen Peaks' oldest herders say Glacihorn appears at the start of every cold season and that the range's meltwater follows its path - where it wades, the cold currents arrive, and where the cold currents arrive, everything else follows.

Field note: A single horn-tip chip shed by Glacihorn keeps a full barrel of water cold for a week without ice - collectors leave offerings at the snowline.

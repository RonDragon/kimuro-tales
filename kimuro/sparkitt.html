About Sparkitt

Sparkitt is a golden kitten covered in large round amber spots, with round curious eyes and a tail held high and curling at the tip. Tiny yellow sparks fizz from its cheeks whenever it purrs, and a breeze seems to follow its every pounce, scattering leaves in cheerful spirals. In Storm Spire, Sparkitt kittens race between shafts of sunlight as though the light itself is a game to catch. They are drawn to sounds spoken clearly and loudly - to hear a new sound said with confidence makes a Sparkitt bounce on all four paws at once.

Field note: A Sparkitt bouncing on all four paws simultaneously is celebrating - it just heard a sound it had been waiting all day for.

About Strikat

Strikat has shed its round kitten spots for sharp gold-and-black tiger stripes, and a crest of pointed golden spines now runs from the crown of its head down to its shoulders. It moves with a stalking crouch and a low, focused gaze that misses nothing in its territory. When it springs into motion the wind bunches around its spine-crest and channels into a gust that bends the light through the spire. Strikat is fiercely loyal once it has bonded - it will not let another creature approach its trainer without a proper introduction.

Field note: Strikat's spine-crest acts as a wind vane - it always points in the direction danger is coming from before Strikat ever moves.

About Thunderfang

In Storm Spire the old teaching says: if you can stand still before Thunderfang and hold its gaze without flinching, it will lower its head and let you pass. Every child in the region knows the trick is simply not to run.

Field note: The instruction for passing Thunderfang is carved into the oldest stone arch at Storm Spire's entrance - the carving has never worn smooth.

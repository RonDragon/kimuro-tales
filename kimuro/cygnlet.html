About Cygnlet

Cygnlet is a round fluffy white duckling with a stubby unicorn horn on its forehead and the slightly bewildered, earnest expression of a creature that knows it will one day be magnificent and is patiently waiting for that day to arrive. It paddles the cleaner channels of the Poison Marsh and finds particular joy in floating in slow circles for no apparent reason. Locals say each circle it completes neutralizes a tiny patch of marsh toxin - and the math, remarkably, checks out.

Field note: Each clockwise circle Cygnlet swims neutralizes exactly 0.04 square meters of marsh toxin - measured and confirmed in field studies.

About Cygnaura

Cygnaura has grown into a graceful swan with a spiral horn now long and luminous, and wings that show faint prismatic iridescence at the feather edges - pale violet and teal shifting with each movement. Its neck arches with natural poise as it surveys the Poison Marsh, and its wake leaves clean water behind it. It navigates by reading the psychic resonance of the marsh's living things - it knows where the danger is before it appears and adjusts its route so seamlessly that followers never notice the close call.

Field note: Following a Cygnaura through the Poison Marsh reduces documented incidents of toxin exposure by 94% - the safest guide in the region.

About Cygnalor

Cygnalor is luminous beyond words - a vast brilliant swan with full prismatic wing-fans that scatter light across the entire marsh surface, a regal crown of light above its brow, and a spiral horn that pulses with gentle psychic radiance. Where it flies, the Poison Marsh's toxins part like clouds before the sun. Ancient texts of the Poison Marsh guardians say it was sent ahead of them by the light - arriving first so there would be clean water waiting when the children came.

Field note: The prismatic light cast by Cygnalor's wings has been used by lost travelers to navigate from up to eight kilometers away.

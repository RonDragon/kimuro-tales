About Strixlet

Strixlet is a small, fluffy grey-white owl-like bird with a rounded face, two white feather tufts on its head, and fierce bright eyes far too intense for something so soft-looking. It nests in the lower crevices of Dragon Spire where updrafts provide lift for its short practice glides. Though it cannot yet sustain flight against the spire's full wind, it spreads its stubby wings and tilts into every passing gust as if daring the wind to knock it over. The dragon-riders of the region consider Strixlet sightings a good omen.

Field note: Strixlet is known to stare into lantern flames for up to two hours at a time - blinking only once per minute.

About Strixion

Strixion has developed a full coat of spiky grey feathers that fan outward around its face like a ruff, giving it a much wider, more imposing silhouette. Its eyes have deepened in color and its talons are now strong enough to grip Dragon Spire's sheer rock faces in winds that would strip a person off the ledge. It hunts at the shoulder of the spire where the thermals converge, turning its wide-faced gaze on anything that moves below. The feathers around its face are so fine they detect air pressure changes a moment before the gust arrives.

Field note: Strixion always turns to face incoming wind exactly 1.5 seconds before anyone or anything else on the spire notices the gust.

About Strixigon

At its final form, Strixigon of the Dragon Spire has become wholly dark - its feathers are deep black-blue from wingtip to tail, and its spread wings fill the sky like a moving stormfront. Its face retains the wide owl shape, but the expression has shifted from fierce to ancient - a creature that has witnessed the full arc of Dragon Spire's history and found it worth watching still. The dragons of the spire give Strixigon distance; there is a shared understanding between them that neither will explain. Its call at night sends a chill through the whole region.

Field note: Even the oldest dragons of the Dragon Spire stop and hold still when Strixigon calls at night.

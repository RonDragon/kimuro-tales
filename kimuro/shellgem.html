About Shellgem

Shellgem peers out from a pale blue-purple spiral shell with wide eyes and two soft little legs, its whole body not much larger than a fist. At the Steel Fortress it is often found wedged into wall joints, watching the forge fires with fascinated calm. A single blue teardrop crystal mark sits on its forehead - the first sign of the mineral growth it will carry through life. It picks up scraps of ore and presses them experimentally to its shell, testing which ones stick.

Field note: The crystal mark on its forehead grows exactly one millimeter per month - field scouts use it as a calendar.

About Geoclaw

Geoclaw has burst out of its spiral shell entirely and grown a broad, bumpy carapace thick with jutting crystal spikes and raw mineral nodules. Its claws are now wide, rough, and edged with ore veins, and it scuttles sideways with surprising speed for something so armored. At the Steel Fortress it patrols the outer wall foundations, pressing its spiky back against the stone to shore up cracks - an instinct so reliable that fortress architects factor Geoclaw behavior into their structural plans.

Field note: Geoclaw sheds a crystal spike when it senses a structural weakness nearby - masons follow the shed spikes to find cracks.

About Titanoclast

Titanoclast is vast and terrifying in silhouette - a dark steel carapace bristling with thick mineral spines and glowing orange fissures at every joint, as if magma runs through its plating. Its legs are column-thick and its claws are broad bladed weapons that can shear through the Steel Fortress's own walls. Yet it never uses them against the fortress - it was there when the first stone was laid, and its own body is woven into the walls' legend as the creature that decided the fortress would stand.

Field note: The Steel Fortress was built around a sleeping Titanoclast; three outer walls follow the exact curve of its shell.

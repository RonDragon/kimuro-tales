About Jellotox

Jellotox is a tiny, round-capped jellyfish in purple and pink, bobbing gently with four thin, trailing tentacles that drip a faintly luminous venom. Its face - two wide eyes and a tiny open mouth - gives it a permanently startled expression, as if surprised by its own existence. It drifts through the Ghost Hollow, pulled by breezes no living creature can feel, and its tentacles leave short glowing streaks in the air. It seems to be looking for something it lost long ago.

Field note: Jellotox produces one tiny venom droplet per hour - if you bottle it, the liquid glows soft purple all night long.

About Toxjelly

Toxjelly has grown into a wide-capped jellyfish with prominent yellow-green spots across its dome and long, sinuous tentacles hanging below. Its body has a half-real quality - light passes through it at certain angles - and it moves through solid stone walls as readily as through open air. The spots pulse in sequence when it senses danger, broadcasting a warning that other Ghost Hollow spirits heed immediately. Where it drifts, the air tastes metallic and faintly sweet.

Field note: The yellow-green spots on Toxjelly's cap pulse in a pattern unique to each individual - experienced hollow-guides can tell them apart at a glance.

About Venomjel

Venomjel spans wide - its domed cap now as broad as a child is tall - and its many long tentacles spiral outward like the arms of a dark galaxy. Purple and deep green swirl across its surface in slow, hypnotic patterns that mimic the night sky above Ghost Hollow. Anything brushing a tentacle feels a profound chill before numbness sets in. Venomjel does not hunt; it simply drifts, and those who linger too near drift along with it, forgetting where they were going.

Field note: Venomjel has been observed drifting the exact same route through Ghost Hollow for three years - no one knows what it is circling.

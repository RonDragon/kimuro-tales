About Sproutwit

Sproutwit is the roundest, fluffiest thing in all of Gale Ridge - a ball of warm brown feathers with two alert eyes and a tiny branch-sprig growing from the top of its head like an antenna. It cannot yet manage a proper flight; it hops between the low moss-covered boulders and glides on updrafts for one or two meters before landing with a soft plop. Sproutwit listens with its whole body - turning to every new sound, tilting its twig-antenna in the direction of each unfamiliar sound until it has heard it at least five times and decided whether it likes it.

Field note: Sproutwit's head-twig tilts toward any new sound it hears - it has been seen tilting back and forth between two arguing spire-keepers for twenty minutes.

About Thornhoot

Thornhoot has grown from puffball to proper owl - sharp amber eyes under a furrowed brow and a small leafy crown sprouting from its head that fans out in strong winds. It perches on Gale Ridge's exposed rock fingers and rides the storm winds with patient, barely-moving wings, reading every gust the way a seasoned traveler reads a map. Thornhoot hoots twice before each weather change - the spire's bell-keepers ring the storm-warning bell only after they hear it. It collects bright-colored storm debris and arranges it near its roost in order of how interesting each piece is.

Field note: Thornhoot hoots exactly twice before every wind-direction change at the spire; no instrument has recorded the change faster.

About Verdanthoot

Verdanthoot spreads wings so wide that their shadow covers the full Gale Ridge lookout platform, the elaborate leaf-vine crown on its head now a full canopy of living growth that blooms in good weather and closes tight before a dangerous storm. Its deep powerful hoot carries across the spire and down to the valley below, shaping the wind it rides as much as riding it. The guardians wrote that Verdanthoot is the Gale Ridge's living weathervane and memory - it has witnessed every storm since the spire's first stone was laid, and remembers them all.

Field note: Verdanthoot's crown blooms or closes twelve hours before each weather change - more accurate than any forecast the spire's scholars have managed.

About Slityx

Slityx is a small, bright-banded serpent coiled in vivid blue and gold rings, its narrow head lifted with alert curiosity and two amber eyes gleaming. Its tongue flickers constantly, tasting the Dragon Spire air for old voices caught in updrafts. Despite its size it is already venomous - tiny droplets bead on its fangs when it opens its mouth wide to yawn, which it does nine times every sunrise as a kind of ritual counting exercise. The guardians called it the Spire's small interpreter.

Field note: Slityx yawns exactly nine times at sunrise without fail - trainers say each yawn corresponds to one word it plans to learn that day.

About Vipraid

Vipraid is a thicker, longer serpent with a flattened wedge-shaped head marked by deep blue and bright gold banding that intensifies near its neck. Its scales have developed a faint metallic sheen, and its hood flares when it rears up to full height. On Dragon Spire's heated ledges it practices strike-precision on carved stone old pillars, chipping one letter at a time with needle-fangs until the whole word is exposed. It has never struck anything it didn't mean to.

Field note: Vipraid can chip a single letter from a stone pillar without disturbing the letter beside it - verified by three different scholars.

About Hydracobra

Hydracobra is vast - a great cobra-dragon with a broad flared hood rimmed in blazing gold, a heavily scaled body banded in royal blue and deep amber, and four small draconic wings folded at its shoulders like a battle-cape. When it rises fully upright atop Dragon Spire's highest peak, its hood catches the wind and shimmers. Every creature on the Spire stops moving. It is the oldest voice in these lands, they say - its hiss alone carries more meaning than an hour of talk.

Field note: When Hydracobra spreads its hood at full height, the wind direction on the Spire changes to blow toward it from every angle at once.

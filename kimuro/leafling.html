About Leafling

Leafling is a tiny, bright-eyed deer fawn with large oval leaves fanning from behind both ears like a little green bonnet. Its light green coat is smooth and soft, and it picks its way through the ghost hollow on delicate hooves that barely stir the mist. It is curious but wary, pausing to sniff each new thing before proceeding. When startled, its leaf-ears fan wide and it freezes perfectly still - an instinct that makes it nearly invisible among the hollow's floor-plants. Children in the hollow say Leafling always knows the safest path, and following it never leads you wrong.

Field note: Leafling freezes motionless when startled, and its leaf-ears spread wide - from ten paces away it looks exactly like a leafy shrub.

About Leafyx

Leafyx has grown into a striking forest deer with a full spray of layered leaves fanning behind its neck and down its back like a living mane, and its eyes have deepened from bright to forest-deep green. Its slender legs carry it in long fluid strides through the ghost hollow's trees, and the leaf-mane rustles in patterns as it moves that seem almost like language - older hollow-dwellers claim to understand a few of the phrases. It marks its paths through the hollow with small leaf-circles pressed into the ground, and following them is said to lead anywhere you need to go.

Field note: Leafyx's leaf-mane rustling patterns have been partially mapped - seventeen distinct meanings are confirmed, and two scholars are still arguing about the eighteenth.

About Foliarch

Foliarch is a magnificent great stag whose towering antlers have become a living crown of interlocked branches, leaves, and flowering vine - an entire micro-forest carried on its head. Its body is broad-chested and deep-green in tone, dappled with golden leaf-light, and its steps press the ghost hollow mist apart like a ship's prow through water. When it stands still in a clearing and calls, the entire hollow falls silent and listens. The guardians of the hollow say Foliarch has walked these paths since before the first lesson was written down, and will walk them long after the last.

Field note: The flowering vines in Foliarch's antler-crown bloom in a different color each season, and each color corresponds to a different branch of knowledge.

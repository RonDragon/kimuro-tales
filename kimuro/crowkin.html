About Crowkin

Crowkin is a dark, fluffy crow chick with sooty blue-black feathers and round red eyes that glow like coals. Wisps of dark smoke curl from its wingtips even at rest. It lives near the entrance passages of the Shadow Caves, hopping through narrow crevices and pecking at crystallized darkness that clings to the walls. Its presence in the caves is ancient - the guardians found carvings of Crowkins in the oldest tunnel walls, suggesting these small birds have nested here since before Abalont's shadow first fell. It is startling but not hostile, and the smoke it trails smells faintly of charred wood.

Field note: Crowkin's smoke trail persists for several minutes after it has gone, marking its path like a living map through the cave tunnels.

About Malraven

Malraven has stretched into a lean, long-legged bird like a dark heron, with ragged trailing feathers at the wing-tips and a long curved beak. Its plumage is deep black-blue and its red eyes are brighter now. It picks its way through shallow cave pools with slow, deliberate steps, hunting the shadow-fish that swim in lightless water. Wispy tendrils of dark energy drift from its tail feathers. Guardians who venture into the caves' middle passages say Malraven always appears first - as if checking whether a traveler is worth the deeper paths. It is a gatekeeper that never speaks its terms.

Field note: Malraven stands perfectly still for up to twenty minutes in shallow water, not hunting - just listening to the dark.

About Vexwing

Vexwing fills the cave ceiling when it spreads its wings - a massive dark raptor whose plumage bleeds into deep red at the wingtips, the color of cinders cooling in the dark. Its body crackles with dark energy that trails in streamers behind every wingbeat. When it lands in the deep caverns, the temperature drops and every light dims for a moment. It was here before the Shadow Caves had a name; some say it IS the shadow the caves are named for. But the guardians know it chooses its companions carefully - it has carried more than one lost child to the cave exit on its back, silently, in the deep dark.

Field note: Once Vexwing decides a child deserves safety, it will cross the entire cave system in a single wingbeat to deliver them to light.

About Sproutlet

Sproutlet is a round, freshly-hatched chick still wearing the bottom half of its eggshell like a little skirt, its yellow-green down fluffy and bright. Two small leaf-like wing-nubs sprout from its sides and a neat tuft of green feathers tops its head. Its beak is short and bright orange and it opens wide whenever wind passes through - as if it is tasting the Gale Ridge's famous updrafts for the first time. It follows whatever is moving nearest to it and cheeps loudly when left alone.

Field note: Sproutlet cannot yet fly but bounces nearly twenty centimetres when it flaps hard enough - the eggshell skirt rattles like a tiny drum.

About Leafawk

Leafawk is a bright green parrot with a long elegant tail feather, a rounded white-crested head, and wide wings that it holds slightly open at all times as if ready to catch the next gust. Its eyes are large and golden and it tilts its head sharply to listen - it repeats back any sound it hears within three seconds, always perfectly pronounced. At the Gale Ridge it is prized by young learners because it refuses to let a mispronounced sound go unchallenged, chirping the correct form until they get it right.

Field note: Leafawk will repeat a sound back exactly seventeen times before it gives up - trainers have counted.

About Verdantyx

Verdantyx is a magnificent green griffin with a broad feline body, powerful feathered wings broad enough to block the sun, and a proud crested head with a fierce golden eye. Its plumage is vivid green and gold, and its primary feathers are as wide as ssounds. When it launches from the Gale Ridge's crags, the downblast flattens every blade of grass in a five-metre circle. The guardians treated it as a living storm-warning: when Verdantyx circles three times instead of two, a full tempest is coming within the hour.

Field note: Verdantyx circles clockwise in fair weather and counter-clockwise when a storm is forming - Gale Ridge keepers stake their safety on this.

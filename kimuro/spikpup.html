About Spikpup

A stocky tan-and-dark pup with a wide spiky mane of coarse fur that sticks up like desert scrub, Spikpup has the heavy shoulders and low stance of something that will one day be much harder to knock over. Its golden-brown eyes are wary and quick. It digs shallow dens in sand dune slopes at dawn, marking each one with a scratch pattern, then forgets which one it used. The other creatures of the sand dunes give it a wide berth - even at this size, its jaw pressure is startling.

Field note: Spikpup can bite through sandstone pebbles; trainers sometimes find small quarried rocks stacked near its sleeping spot.

About Spikfang

Spikfang has grown into a sleek, long-legged predator with dark tawny fur, a thick spiky mane spreading from neck to mid-back, and a sweeping tail. Its fangs are now prominent, slightly longer than its lip, and faintly darker than its teeth. It hunts at dusk when the sand dunes cast the longest shadows, using the crests of dunes to stay low and invisible until the last moment. It carries a pebble in each cheek when at rest - no one has ever figured out why.

Field note: Spikfang always approaches downwind and will circle a target three times before deciding to move closer.

About Spikmaul

Spikmaul stands at shoulder height to a tall adult, its enormous spiky mane now fanned wide and bristling with dark-tipped guard hairs that catch desert light like iron filings. Three heavy tails sweep behind it, and its deep-set golden eyes evaluate everything with the patience of someone who has never once lost. The sand dunes record its passing in a chain of deep prints - its weight sinks a paw-width into packed sand. The dune guardians say Spikmaul's roar settles the shifting sands when storms move them.

Field note: Spikmaul's footprints fill with rainwater that stays fresh for three days longer than any other puddle.

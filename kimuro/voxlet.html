About Voxlet

Voxlet is a small round-faced purple bat with large round ears, simple leathery wings, and a nose that twitches constantly at air currents too faint for other creatures to detect. It navigates Ghost Hollow by wind-taste alone - it knows the direction of every draft in the hollow, and drafts that carry spoken words are its favorite, carrying a sweet resonance it follows like a scent trail. It hangs in clusters with other Voxlets, and the air between them is always slightly cooler, as if their presence draws warmth from the world and replaces it with quietness.

Field note: A cluster of Voxlets sharing a roost makes the surrounding air measurably cooler - Ghost Hollow villagers use them as natural air conditioners.

About Voxian

Voxian has grown into a standing figure - upright on two clawed legs, with long elongated arms that end in wing membranes that fold and flare with expressive precision. Its purple-dark body is lean and angular, and its clawed wing-joints can grip surfaces that give it a disturbing stillness when perched on the side of a tree. It hunts by listening to the voices on the wind from great height, then diving without sound into the source of the voice it chose. In Ghost Hollow it is the voice-finder - it brings back echoes of words that were spoken too quietly to hear the first time.

Field note: Voxian dives toward any voice it finds interesting - finding one perched behind you in Ghost Hollow means you just said a word it had not heard before.

About Voxalith

Voxalith is a massive dark dragon-bat hybrid with horns curving back from its broad head, enormous wings mapped with purple-gold vein patterns, and a body armored in overlapping dark scales. When it spreads its wings in Ghost Hollow the air displacement is equivalent to a gale - trees bend, leaves scatter, and any sound in the hollow is carried away instantly. It is the Hollow's wind itself, some say - not a creature that lives in the wind but the embodiment of it, carrying every voice that was ever raised in Ghost Hollow on those vast dark wings.

Field note: When Voxalith spreads its wings fully, every sound in the surrounding kilometer is erased for three full seconds - the hollow goes absolutely silent.

About Frostpup

Frostpup is a round, fluffy white yeti child with huge blue eyes and a goofy open smile, standing on stubby legs with oversized hands dangling at its sides. Its entire body is a cloud of white fur and it waddles more than walks, often sitting down mid-stride to look at something interesting in the coral sand. Found playing in the tidal pools of Coral Bay, it builds tiny sand-and-ice sculptures and knocks them over with cheerful enthusiasm. The bay's shellfish have learned to hide when they see it coming.

Field note: Frostpup always builds the same tower - three ice-pebbles, one shell on top - then destroys it immediately, apparently satisfied.

About Glaciape

Glaciape has grown tall and powerfully muscled beneath its thick white-grey fur, with a broad scowling brow and knuckles that drag along the ground as it walks on all fours. Its blue eyes still hold a keen intelligence. It grips boulders at Coral Bay cliffs with startling ease, tossing stones into the sea to watch the splash - and measuring the distance with great seriousness. Trainers report it will refuse to battle until it has found a rock it considers fair to throw first.

Field note: Glaciape counts stone-skips on the bay surface and sulks visibly if it scores fewer than five bounces.

About Crystalbeast

Crystalbeast is enormous - a hulking white yeti whose arms and shoulders have erupted with jagged blue ice-crystal formations, cracking through the fur like armour grown from within. Its fists spark blue when it clenches them, and its eyes burn with fierce cold light. When it pounds the coral-stone floor the vibration is felt half a league out to sea and the tide temporarily reverses. The ocean creatures of Coral Bay swim to shore when they hear it approaching, not out of fear but to watch it break ice formations on the cliff-face for the joy of the sound.

Field note: The crystal spikes on Crystalbeast's arms regrow overnight - scholars have counted 27 new formations in a single night.

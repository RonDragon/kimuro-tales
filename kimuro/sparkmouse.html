About Sparkmouse

Sparkmouse (mouse line) is a small, round-eared mammal with tan-brown fur, wide bright eyes, a round belly, and a tiny glowing blue orb floating beside its tail like a personal lantern it never seems to notice. It hops through the Steel Fortress's maintenance catwalks with enormous confidence for its size, investigating every bolt and joint. The blue orb occasionally zaps the metal surfaces near it, leaving faint scorch-marks that workers follow like a trail of evidence to find where Sparkmouse has been. It is impossible not to like - it has the absolute certainty of something that has never been told it shouldn't be wherever it currently is.

Field note: The floating orb beside Sparkmouse's tail always hovers at the same relative distance regardless of how the creature moves - it has never been dislodged by any force yet attempted.

About Voltail

Voltail has grown into a stocky, upright draconic form with broad wings folded at its sides, tan-brown scales covering a wide chest, and a face set in permanent serious concentration. Its wings already carry electrical field markings in vivid blue and it stands on strong digitigrade legs. The playful chaos of Sparkmouse has organized into focused power - Voltail patrols assigned sections of the Steel Fortress with the dedication of a guard who takes its role seriously. Junior engineers hand-feed it copper wire, which it accumulates and delivers to forge-workers as apparent gifts.

Field note: Voltail's wings, even folded, generate enough field to keep the immediate area's instruments running on backup power during a full primary outage.

About Thunderwing

Thunderwing is an imposing dragon with a heavily muscled frame, vast scaled wings that crackle with persistent lightning along their leading edges, and a broad heavy head set with fierce golden eyes. When it spreads its wings inside the Steel Fortress's main hall, the lights dim and then surge. It has stationed itself near the Fortress's outer gate and treats every creature that passes as personally its responsibility to evaluate - allies receive a short nod, strangers receive the full wing-spread and electric display. It is the Fortress's unofficial gatekeeper and takes no days off.

Field note: Thunderwing's wing-spread can charge a fully depleted power core in under four minutes - Fortress engineers have documented this and built a failsafe protocol around it.

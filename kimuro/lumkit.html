About Lumkit

A small spotted kitten with warm amber-gold fur patterned in leopard rosettes, Lumkit has wide pink eyes and a cluster of tiny crystalline spines sprouting from its shoulders - barely as long as its ears. Sparks of gold and white float around it whenever it stretches. It pads through the Psychic Temple's sun-dappled clearings, batting at motes of floating light, and is known to fall asleep mid-leap, tumbling softly into the grass. Guardians keep its company when practicing hard new sounds, for its purring steadies the mind.

Field note: Lumkit's spines chime like a tiny wind chime when the morning sun first touches them - each cub's pitch is unique.

About Lumipard

Lumipard has grown into a sleek spotted leopard with a broad chest and legs built for bounding across the temples. Its shoulder spines have bloomed into full wings - wide, translucent, layered like stacked petals in pastel rainbow hues. Small glowing orbs float beside it as it runs, and the wings catch every beam of sunlight and scatter it as colored ribbons across the forest floor. In the Psychic Temple, Lumipard is said to mark the boundary of safe ground by circling it once at dawn, wings spread.

Field note: Each of Lumipard's wing-petals glows a different color depending on the angle of sunlight - none repeat in a single day.

About Chrysalix

Chrysalix stands tall, a great leopard-lion wrapped in rippling fire of gold and violet light. Its mane blazes outward like a living corona - not fur but light itself, streaming in waves. Enormous feathered wings fold at its shoulders, and when it walks, every rosette on its body pulses slowly. It is the sovereign guardian of the Psychic Temple, and those who face it in battle feel their own memories light up like lanterns. It is said Chrysalix once guided the lost guardians home by blazing so brightly the whole forest could see it from beyond the mountains.

Field note: Chrysalix's roar produces no sound - only a wave of warm golden light that passes through walls and wakes sleeping children.

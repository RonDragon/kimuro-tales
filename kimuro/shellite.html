About Shellite

Shellite is a tiny hermit crab carrying a smooth purple-and-white spiral shell that is nearly as large as its whole body. Two large round eyes sit atop short stalks that swivel independently, and small orange claws reach out to test every surface before it steps. It lives in the damp lower passages of the Shadow Caves, collecting shells discarded by other creatures and examining each one with serious attention before choosing a new home. Children who find one say it always has a shell that is slightly too large - it is saving room to grow.

Field note: Shellite always selects a shell one size too large - it says, in its way, that it already knows what it will become.

About Shelkrab

Shelkrab has outgrown its first shell and replaced it with a larger ridged spiral shell studded with rough spines. Its orange legs have lengthened and it now walks with a broad-legged confident stance, claws raised. It has begun secreting a mild venom from the tips of its claws that it uses to mark territories on the cave walls in patterns the guardians identified as crude maps of local water channels. Those who learn to read the markings can navigate the Shadow Caves without a lantern.

Field note: Shelkrab's claw-markings on cave walls form a working map of underground water - older ones show routes no longer accessible.

About Shelkiath

Shelkiath carries a massive ridged purple shell larger than a wheelbarrow, covered in twisted spines and old cave-calcite deposits from decades underground. Its six powerful legs grip the cave floor with such force it leaves claw-marks in solid stone. The venom from its claws has concentrated over the years into a potent compound - even a small drop permanently marks any surface it touches with a glowing pattern. The Shadow Caves' deepest chambers are covered floor to ceiling in Shelkiath's accumulated scratchings, a record of everything it has ever seen.

Field note: The glow from Shelkiath's venom markings in the deepest caves is bright enough to read by, even without any other light source.

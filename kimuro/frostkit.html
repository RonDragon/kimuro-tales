About Frostkit

Frostkit is a small fluffy white fox cub with oversized paws and a thick rounded tail that curves up over its back. A single pale blue gem sits on its forehead, glowing faintly when it is thinking hard. Its fur is impossibly soft - visitors at the Frozen Peaks often describe petting it as touching a fresh snowfall. Despite living at the base of the towering spire, Frostkit rarely strays far from the shelter of snow-covered boulders, poking out its round face only when it hears an unfamiliar sound that interests it.

Field note: Frostkit's forehead gem glows brightest just before it falls asleep - researchers believe it dreams in colors that have no names yet.

About Frostyne

Frostyne has grown lean and fast, its white fur now edged with pale blue, and two small feathery wings have emerged from its shoulders that are more for balance than flight. Cold wisps trail from its paws and tail as it sprints, leaving ghost-prints in its wake. It has discovered it can read the intentions of nearby creatures - not sounds, but a feeling of what they plan to do next. Frozen Peaks travelers say Frostyne always steps aside exactly one second before a falling icicle strikes where it stood.

Field note: Frostyne has never been hit by a falling icicle at the spire - locals claim it has never been surprised by anything at all.

About Glacifox

It no longer merely senses intentions; it can place a thought or a memory directly into a nearby mind with the precision of a needle. The Guardians named it the Voice That Doesn't Speak, and it served as a judge in disputes nobody else could settle.

Field note: Glacifox's nine tails move completely independently of each other - each tail is said to carry a different memory from the past nine winters.

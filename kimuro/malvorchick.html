About Malvorchick

Malvorchick is a small round-bodied dark bird with deep purple-black feathers and large wide eyes that glow with an inner violet light. Little wisps of purple wind-smoke drift from its wingtips. It lives in Shadow Caves' upper passages, perching on rock ledges with its wings slightly spread, letting the cave drafts ruffle its feathers. It is cheerfully oblivious to how ominous it looks - it follows travelers through the caves not to frighten them but because it likes company and the caves are dull alone.

Field note: Malvorchick always perches facing the direction of airflow - making it a reliable compass in tunnels where wind direction changes constantly.

About Malvorn

Malvorn spreads its wings with authority now - wide purple-black feathers that catch Shadow Caves' draft-currents perfectly, letting it glide the long corridors without a wingbeat. Its eyes shine with steady violet intensity. It no longer follows travelers through the caves; now it watches from above, tracking them by sound. When it decides someone is trustworthy it drops a small dark feather at their feet - Shadow Cave guides treasure these, as a feather from Malvorn means a passage is safe to use that day.

Field note: Malvorn sheds exactly one feather per day - always from the same spot on its left wing, always before dawn.

About Malvoraeth

Malvoraeth is Shadow Caves' apex aerial presence - a massive dark-winged raptor whose spread wings fill the cave corridors it hunts. Its plumage is layered in deep purple-black, and dark wind-energy visibly coils around its primary feathers like slow lightning. Its crest of feathers makes its head seem crowned. When it flies fast through the cave network, it creates pressure-waves that extinguish torches and rattle loose stones. Guardians say Malvoraeth has memorized the full interior of Shadow Caves and that when it flies its patrol it recites the layout - using wing-beats as punctuation.

Field note: Malvoraeth's shadow is always larger than its body should cast - and moves half a second ahead of it, as if leading the way.

About Fluttox

Fluttox is a plump sky-blue bird fledgling with soft round feathers and a single small iridescent crystal wing-fan sprouting beside its regular wing. Its eyes are wide and brilliantly blue, and it flaps both wings with cheerful uncoordinated enthusiasm. High in Gale Ridge's updraft chimneys, Fluttox tumbles on the wind currents, each tumble teaching it a little more about how air moves. Guardians of the Ridge leave seed perches on the upper platforms, knowing a tumbling Fluttox always finds its way back up.

Field note: Fluttox somersaults exactly three times before landing - never two, never four, always three.

About Windox

Windox has grown into a proud blue-and-white bird with layered feathers and large iridescent crystal wings that catch every draft like sails. Its posture is alert and bold, chest forward, tail fanned wide. It rides the constant gales around Gale Ridge without flapping - just tilts a crystal feather and the wind does the rest. Windox scouts ahead of storms, calling back weather-reads to trainers below in a series of precise whistled patterns.

Field note: Each whistled call from Windox encodes the wind direction, speed, and whether rain is coming - thirty notes, all distinct.

About Crystalwing

Crystalwing is a magnificent sea-green-and-teal eagle with enormous crystal-shard wings that catch the light like a stained-glass window mid-storm. Its wingspan blots out the sun when it passes overhead, and each crystal primary feather chimes against the others in a continuous moving melody. Its psychic perception now spans the full height of Gale Ridge - it reads thoughts the way a sailor reads clouds. Legends say Crystalwing once guided the guardians through Abalont's blindness-fog by broadcasting a psychic beacon from the tallest spire.

Field note: When Crystalwing circles three times over the same spot, a storm will arrive within the hour - the Ridge's guardians trust this completely.

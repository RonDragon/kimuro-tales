About Cubyx

Cubyx is a small, stocky honey-badger with a dark lower body, pale sandy upper face, and tiny bright eyes that convey one emotion: absolute determination. Its short claws are already hardening at the tips. In the Crystal Mines it investigates everything with its nose-first charge style - pointing itself at an interesting rock formation and walking straight into it, then looking indignant when it doesn't move. It eats whatever it finds and finds everything. Researchers note it has no off-switch.

Field note: Cubyx has been observed attempting to challenge creatures twelve times its own size - in every recorded case, the larger creature simply stepped aside.

About Urskar

Urskar is a longer, meaner honey-badger with a pale stripe across its broad head, a muscular neck, and thick claws darkened by constant rock-work. It moves through the Crystal Mines in a low, fast trot that never seems to tire. When it decides something needs to move - a boulder, a rival, a trainer who blocked a tunnel - it starts pushing and does not stop until it gets its way. Crystal Mine crews give it priority over all other foot traffic in narrow shafts, a policy established after the third incident.

Field note: Urskar has never backed away from a confrontation - researchers believe it lacks the neurological pathway to consider retreat as an option.

About Ironpaw

Ironpaw is a powerfully built armoured badger-bear with thick steel-plate shoulders and haunches, reinforced claws the length of a hand that leave scored tracks in crystal-embedded stone, and a scarred, grizzled snout that has never met an obstacle it respected. It controls the deepest shafts of the Crystal Mines, and the crystal formations there are in better shape than anywhere else - Ironpaw keeps them clear of debris and collapses simply by living there. It is deeply territorial and consistently effective, and the mine crews would never dream of asking it to leave.

Field note: Ironpaw's claw marks on Crystal Mine walls are used by surveyors to trace old passages - the marks stay sharp for decades in the hardest rock.

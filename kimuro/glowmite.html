About Glowmite

Glowmite is a small, cheerful blue jellyfish with a round bell and a tight cluster of short tentacles hanging beneath it, drifting through the shadow caves' underground pools with a soft blue glow. A thin antenna curves from its crown, ending in a dim warm bead of light that it can extinguish and relight at will. It is the first light most explorers see when they enter the cave system, and it has learned to associate approaching footsteps with the possibility of hearing a new voice - it brightens noticeably when someone recites something correctly. Local guides use it as an informal test.

Field note: Glowmite's antenna-light brightens measurably when it hears a correctly spoken word - cave-school teachers use it as a pass/fail indicator.

About Abyssal

Abyssal has grown a wider bell draped in long flowing tentacles that trail in elegant dark curtains, each tip glowing at intervals like stars in a night sky. Its lantern-antenna has extended and now carries a blue-white lamp bright enough to read by. In the shadow caves it drifts through the wider chambers in absolute silence, and its tentacle-glow-pattern shifts in response to sounds - quick bright pulses for voices, slow waves for music. The cave-scholars have been mapping the patterns for a generation and believe they form a complete underwater map that has never been copied.

Field note: Abyssal's tentacle-glow pattern shifts uniquely for every spoken word it hears - two scholars have mapped over four hundred patterns so far.

About Voidlume

Voidlume is a towering, armored entity that has transcended its jellyfish origins - its upper body has become a broad-shouldered dark form with wing-like tentacle fans sweeping wide, and it carries a great lantern-staff of cold blue-white light in one limb while the other bears a sweeping claw-extension. The light it carries throws everything around it into sharp dramatic relief, and the shadow caves restructure their shadows accordingly. It is the oldest entity in the cave system and speaks only in light-patterns now - a language that takes years to learn and cannot be unlearned once known. The guardians call it the Warden of What Was Forgotten.

Field note: Voidlume's light-language cannot be unlearned once understood - three guardians who mastered it have never spoken in words again.

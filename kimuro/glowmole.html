About Glowmole

Glowmole is a small, velvety black mole with a single glowing amber orb balanced on a thin antenna above its head like a living miner's lamp. Its wide pink nose twitches in constant radar-sweeps, and its enormous digging claws look impractically large for its body. It burrows beneath the steel fortress's foundation plates and taps the electrical conduits it finds there, charging the antenna-bulb to a brighter gold when it has a full feed. It navigates by electrical field rather than sight, and has been found inside sealed generator rooms that had no open entry point.

Field note: Glowmole's antenna-bulb brightness indicates the local electrical charge - engineers have learned to read it as a live current gauge.

About Aurrow

Aurrow has grown longer and sleeker, with a row of amber-gold electroplates running down its spine that glow and flicker in sequence as it processes electrical charges drawn from the soil. Its digging claws are formidable now, able to split a steel conduit with two strikes, and it uses this ability to reroute power flows in the fortress's substrata - often helpfully, occasionally catastrophically. The fortress engineers have learned to check whether the lights dimmed before concluding there is a fault in the wiring.

Field note: Aurrow's spine-plates pulse in a specific sequence before it taps a new electrical source - the engineers have decoded fifteen distinct patterns.

About Radiatusk

Radiatusk is a hulking, broad-shouldered mole with a full array of glowing gold electroplates across its back, a pair of massive armored tusks that crackle with built-up static charge, and a single oversized antenna-orb burning at constant white-gold intensity. Its passage through the earth leaves charged tunnels that glow faintly for hours. The steel fortress was built partly over Radiatusk's ancient tunnel-network, and its old routes still carry charge - fortress engineers call them 'live veins' and map around them. Radiatusk surfaces once each year at the fortress's main gate and places one tusk on the ground, grounding years of accumulated charge in a flash that resets all faulty circuits.

Field note: Radiatusk's annual grounding flash resets every clock in the steel fortress to midnight - the fortress has kept no other calendar since it was recorded.

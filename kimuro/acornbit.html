About Acornbit

Acornbit is a plump, honey-colored squirrel with a perfectly round acorn cap balanced on its head and wide bright eyes full of absolute conviction about where the next nut is buried. It bounds across the sandstone shelves of Amber Desert carrying treasures twice its head size, and buries them in precise locations it then forgets completely before it gets ten steps away. Despite this, it is one of the happiest creatures in the desert - every buried nut is a future surprise.

Field note: Acornbit buries approximately 60 nuts per day and remembers the location of exactly zero of them.

About Squirrelion

Squirrelion stands upright now on muscled legs, its coat a richer amber and its fluffy tail dramatically large for its body. It clutches a stone nut in one fist like a trophy and holds its ground on the desert's rocky escarpments with the energy of something that has finally decided it is in charge of this particular boulder. Its cheeks can expand to store three large stones at once, which it uses as projectiles at ranges that surprise everyone who assumed it was just looking for a snack.

Field note: Squirrelion's throw accuracy is perfect at 12 metres - it trains daily by bouncing stones off the same canyon wall.

About Crystalnut

Crystalnut stands tall with a crown of ice-blue crystals sprouting from its head and a puffed-up chest radiating a quiet authority that has cowed dust storms. Its enormous amber tail sweeps behind it like a living dune, and its fists are now solid knuckle-stone hardened by decades of desert wind. The guardians who cross Amber Desert leave offerings of smooth river stones at the ancient feeding rock Crystalnut has claimed - it has sat on the same boulder for forty years and has no intention of moving.

Field note: Crystalnut's crystal crown grows a new facet for each decade it lives - currently at four, and counting.

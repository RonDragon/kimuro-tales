About Rhomling

Rhomling is a soft-looking, rounded rhino calf with grey-brown hide and a small stubby horn nub on its broad nose. Its body is low and wide, built close to the ground as if it already knows its future weight. It shuffles through the Crystal Mines tapping the walls with its horn-nub, listening for hollow spaces the way miners listen for water. The crystals in its section of the mine grow faster than elsewhere - researchers believe Rhomling's footsteps compact the mineral-rich soil in exactly the right pattern for crystal formation.

Field note: Crystal clusters grow up to three times faster in tunnels where Rhomling regularly walks - miners follow its paths to find the richest veins.

About Rhinoam

Rhinoam has become a proper, powerfully built rhinoceros, its grey hide thickened and its single center horn now a proper solid spike. Its shoulders are high and round, its legs short but tremendously strong. It moves through the Crystal Mines with the confidence of something that knows it cannot be stopped - and it is right. The mine foremen discovered long ago that Rhinoam will only charge a wall section that is actually unstable; its geological judgment has saved more lives than the best surveyor in the region. It seems to know the mountain from the inside out.

Field note: Rhinoam has never charged a structurally sound wall - every breach it makes reveals a dangerous hollow or a flood cavity behind it.

About Rhinotitan

Rhinotitan is immense, a fortress of grey rock-hide studded along its back and flanks with rows of stone spines that have grown from within over long decades. Multiple horns of different sizes crown its nose and brow. When it walks, the mine shafts tremble in a two-hundred-meter radius. It has carved more of the Crystal Mines' tunnels with its body alone than generations of miners with their tools. The guardians sealed a chamber of the mine's oldest words inside a rock formation Rhinotitan shaped - no one else could have made walls that thick.

Field note: The largest tunnels in the Crystal Mines are perfectly smooth at shoulder-height - the result of Rhinotitan walking the same route for a hundred years.

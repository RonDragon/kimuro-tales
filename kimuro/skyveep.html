About Skyveep

Skyveep is a fluffy round blue chick with stubby wings it flaps with enormous enthusiasm and a tiny open beak it cheeps through constantly. Its round body is sky-blue and its small wing-tips are just beginning to show the white-edged feathers that will come later. It lives in the upper reaches of the Gale Ridge where the wind is almost always blowing, and it leans into gusts with clear delight, letting them ruffle every feather before snapping them flat again. Trainers say it is the noisiest small creature in the entire region.

Field note: Skyveep cheeps exactly once for every metre of altitude it gains - climbers use it to measure how high they have come.

About Skywing

Skywing is a proud hawk-form with white-and-blue plumage, broad fanning wings it holds spread to catch every thermal, and keen golden eyes that track movement two cliff-faces away. It rides the updrafts of the Gale Ridge in long slow circles, occasionally diving to inspect something below with a sharp scream. Its feathers have developed a water-resistant oil that lets it fly straight through rain clouds without losing any altitude. The guardians relied on its flight paths to predict which routes through the Ridge would be safe on storm days.

Field note: Skywing always turns into the wind before calling out - its cry travels three times further downwind than any other call in the Ridge.

About Skyverath

Skyverath is a magnificent eagle of storm and current, its broad white-and-blue wings spanning wide enough to block the sun from the Ridge's summit. Its golden eyes carry a calm authority and its talons are hooked and powerful. When it spreads its wings fully, the wind changes direction around it - a localized weather phenomenon that guardians mapped and named after it. It is said to know every sound carved on the Ridge's stone pillars simply from reading them at full speed during its daily circuits of the summit.

Field note: When Skyverath spreads its wings at the summit, the wind on all four sides of the Ridge shifts in sequence - like the turning of a page.

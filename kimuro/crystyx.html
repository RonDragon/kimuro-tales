About Crystyx

Crystyx is a small, bright blue serpent with violet eyes and a head that is more round and endearing than fearsome. Its scales shift from sky-blue to white along the belly, and tiny ice-crystal flecks are already forming along its neck ridges. It lives in the cold spray of Frozen Peaks' waterfall channels, coiling in the mist and tasting the ice particles on its tongue. It will approach any moving water and simply watch it for long stretches, utterly content.

Field note: Crystyx can tell whether a water source is fresh or salt in under one second - it has never been tricked by ice.

About Glaceryx

Glaceryx has grown confident and coiled, its body now a deeper blue with ice-crystal spines beginning to push out along its back ridges, and a hooded head that spreads in threat display with a sharp cold intelligence behind its eyes. It navigates the Peaks' flooded lower passages by pressing its chin to the current and reading the temperature gradients. In old Frozen Peaks sailors' logs, Glaceryx appears as a calm omen - when it surfaces before a voyage, the ice-lanes are passable.

Field note: Glaceryx's spine-crystals function as temperature sensors accurate to 0.1°C; it can map an entire underwater cave by feel.

About Hydrocryss

Hydrocryss is a serpent of enormous proportions, its body encrusted along the dorsal spine with jagged glacier-shard crests and drifting mist from the cold it emanates. It rests coiled on the submerged ledges of the great meltwater lake, only its crested back above the waterline, where it is consistently mistaken for a ridge of ice. The old survey charts mark that stretch as the Hydrocryss Shelf - a place where a serpent's stillness became geography, and its patience became land.

Field note: The Hydrocryss Shelf on Frozen Peaks charts is not ice - surveyors confirmed in the last pass that it breathes 0.3 times per hour.

About Pigmole

Pigmole is a round, golden-brown creature blending pig and mole: a wide snout, small forward eyes, and two tiny crystal-blue tusks flanking its nose. Its stubby body barely clears the ground, and its feet are wide-pawed for digging. In the Granite Heights it burrows into the thin soil between rocks, surfacing to sniff the cold mountain air and immediately going back down. It communicates with other Pigmoles by tapping its tusks against buried stones - a surprisingly complex language made entirely of knocks.

Field note: Each Pigmole has a personal tusk-tap rhythm - other Pigmoles recognise individuals by the beat alone, even through ten feet of granite.

About Groundtusk

Groundtusk has emerged as a powerful digging beast with a wide muscular back, heavy shoulders, and strong claws designed to break through compacted granite. It burrows headfirst into solid rock-face with its broad snout acting as a wedge, and its dark coat is permanently streaked with granite dust. Its eyes have grown even smaller as it relies on vibration-sensing through its wide paws to navigate. On the Granite Heights it creates hidden tunnels just wide enough for itself - and precisely that narrow.

Field note: Groundtusk emerges only at night and seals each tunnel entrance with a plug of earth it mixes with its own saliva - the plugs are waterproof.

About Terravorn

Terravorn erupts from the ground rather than climbs out of it - a massive, dark, soil-caked behemoth with wide crushing forelimbs, a scarred and heavy snout, and a back that is half-buried by the mountain dirt permanently clinging to its hide. Its tusks are broad and deeply scored. A dark aura clings to it, not evil, but profoundly ancient - the kind of darkness that belongs to deep places where light has never reached. The Granite Heights have recorded this creature's emergence sites for generations: the same three clearings, decade after decade.

Field note: Terravorn always surfaces at the same three granite clearings, ten years apart - the locals put out a flat stone as a landing pad, and it uses it every time.

About Ashpup

Ashpup is a compact dark-blue wolf pup with a small spiky orange flame-mane beginning to sprout and glowing amber eyes. It was born in Ghost Hollow's peripheral mists, where the fire and shadow blend. It gnaws on cooled ember-stones it collects from the Hollow's edge and spits small sparks when startled. The ghost-mist clings to its fur, giving it a faint blue shimmer at night. Despite its fiery nature, it moves almost silently through the hollow's leaves.

Field note: Ashpup's flame-mane sparks orange when it detects a ghost-wisp passing unseen nearby.

About Embron

Embron is a large wolf with a full spiky red-and-dark mane and a body of dark-navy fur layered with fire-touched bristles along the back and neck. Its wide red eyes cut through the ghost-mist effortlessly. It patrols the boundary between Ghost Hollow's living forest and its haunted heart, growling at anything - living or spectral - that crosses without permission. When it prowls, the mist curls away from its feet as if afraid.

Field note: Embron's growl reverberates differently in the ghost-mist than in clear air - residents say it sounds like two wolves calling.

About Infernbrak

Infernbrak is a massive dark-fire wolf whose back and shoulders are armored in jagged plates of hardened shadow-flame. Its broad body carries a full crest of fire-spikes from skull to tail-base, and its powerful jaws open to reveal glowing ember-red fangs. In Ghost Hollow, it is older than the mist itself - some say it was here when the first shadow fell. When it sets its jaw and holds ground, even Abalont's creeping darkness finds a different route.

Field note: Infernbrak has held the same post at Ghost Hollow's eastern gate for so long that the trees have grown around its usual sleeping shape.

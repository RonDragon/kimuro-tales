About Embkit

Embkit is a small golden-brown fawn with enormous ears, wide dark eyes, and a light spotted pattern that glows warm in firelight. Its legs are long for its body and it uses them with comical enthusiasm - bounding ahead before its brain has checked whether the landing spot is safe. Near the Lava Crater it grazes on volcanic-ash grasses that grow in the heat, converting the mineral-rich soil into bright energy. Every step it takes leaves a tiny tuft of new green pressing through the ashen ground behind it.

Field note: Wherever Embkit rests for more than an hour, a ring of new grass appears by morning - crater researchers call them Embkit Circles.

About Embolt

Embolt has grown into a full stag with a proper branching rack of antlers and a coat that glows amber-red at the flanks as if lit from within. Spots remain but have deepened to amber embers. It moves through the Lava Crater's grassland edges at a careful trot, antlers clearing the low ash-brush, leaving a warm trail of regenerated vegetation. The local fire-grass blooms at exactly the temperature its hooves maintain - a relationship so precise that some researchers believe Embolt grows the grass intentionally.

Field note: The fire-grass trail left by Embolt's passage blooms in exactly twelve hours, regardless of weather or ash cover.

About Embryxal

Embryxal is magnificent - a great stag with a crown of wide-spreading antlers that burn at their tips like living torches, casting warm amber light across the Lava Crater's rim. Its thick coat blazes with fire-pattern markings and the ember-glow has spread across its entire body. Where it stands, the barren crater edge erupts in sudden green - moss, fire-grass, shoots pressing through lava rock. The guardians' founding text calls it the Renewal - the proof that fire does not only end things, but begins them.

Field note: The antler-flame of a standing Embryxal has guided travelers home from the Lava Crater in zero-visibility ash storms.

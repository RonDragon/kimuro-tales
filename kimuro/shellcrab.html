About Shellcrab

Shellcrab is a cheerful orange crab with a large translucent blue bubble-dome for a shell, through which its tiny face peers out with round bright eyes and a permanent grin. Its two front claws are bright orange and held wide in a gesture that could mean hello or challenge - often both at once. It skitters sideways across Coral Bay's tidal shallows, collecting small shiny objects and depositing them in its bubble-shell as though decorating a room. No two Shellcrab shells contain the same collection.

Field note: Shellcrab collects exactly seven small objects before stopping for the day - if one is taken, it will search until the set is complete again.

About Ironpinch

Ironpinch is a formidable blue-grey crab with a heavily plated carapace studded with short spines and two large, asymmetric claws - the right distinctly bigger than the left, built for gripping, the left sharper for cutting. It moves quickly across the sea floor with a confident side-step and claims the largest tide pools in Coral Bay through sheer presence. Smaller creatures do not attempt to challenge it; they simply wait until it finishes eating and leave it whatever it did not take.

Field note: Ironpinch always places its larger claw on the ground first when claiming a tide pool - marine biologists have confirmed it is a deliberate territorial signal, not accident.

About Titanomaw

Titanomaw is enormous and angular - its steel-blue carapace is plated in massive ridged sections, its claws broad and spiked, and its orange-rimmed eyes hold an intelligence that suggests it has outlasted several generations of everything around it. It moves with deliberate slowness in shallow water but is remarkably fast in the deep; Coral Bay fishers say if Titanomaw is hunting, the safest place is directly behind it. Its claws can cut through stone, but it reserves that capability - it has never been observed using it carelessly.

Field note: Titanomaw has been documented at the same underwater rock formation every winter for at least forty recorded years - it is believed to be the same individual each time.

About Sparkail

Sparkail (eel line) is a small aquatic serpent with a smooth bright-blue body curled in a tight spiral, decorated with soft blue swirl-patterns that pulse faintly with electrical energy. It lives in the Steel Fortress's coolant channels and drainage conduits, tucked into spiral coils against the pipe walls where the current keeps it company. Its psychic sensitivity shows early: it tracks the emotional state of engineers above it through the metal floors, and workers learned that a Sparkail leaving its usual pipe section predicts an equipment fault within the hour.

Field note: Sparkail's spiral coil orientation mirrors the current direction of whichever pipe it inhabits; reversing the pump flow causes it to spin in confused circles for several minutes.

About Coilwarp

Coilwarp has grown into a sleek, powerful eel-form with vivid blue-white scales banded in electric-blue circles. Continuous arcs of lightning play between the ridges on its back and its long body coils and uncoils with fluid speed. It has moved out of the coolant pipes into the main power-channel network of the Steel Fortress, traveling at speed through electrical conduits. Control panels briefly light up in random patterns when Coilwarp passes beneath them. Engineers who understand the Fortress's power-grid use Coilwarp's location - tracked by flickering panels - as a diagnostic tool.

Field note: Coilwarp can travel through a powered electrical conduit at the same speed as the current itself - it has been timed crossing the Fortress's full length in under twelve seconds.

About Thundervortex

Thundervortex rears from the Fortress's primary power core in a storm of crackling lightning and psychic shimmer, its vast scaled body looped around the central generator like a living engine. Blue spines crown its elevated head and continuous lightning arcs between them. The Fortress's instruments run more smoothly when Thundervortex is near - its psychic field stabilizes the chaotic energy fluctuations that cause equipment failures. Legends in the Fortress say it arrived with the first lightning strike that powered the Fortress's original core, and that it has been the true engine of the building ever since.

Field note: The psychic field Thundervortex maintains around the Fortress's core increases power output by a measured eighteen percent - Fortress engineers have formally documented this and stopped trying to remove it.

About Embole

Embole is a compact round creature covered in overlapping lava-rock plates, dark brown with vivid orange cracks between every scale where inner heat glows. It hugs the ground at all times, stubby legs barely visible beneath its heavy shell, and rolls slowly across the granite heights with a sound like a bag of warm rocks. Despite looking completely armored, it is shy and tucks its head into its shell at any sudden noise. The granite heights' miners consider Embole a good-luck sign - finding one near a dig site means the rock face is warm and stable.

Field note: Embole's shell-plates shift slightly every dawn, expanding as it warms, producing a faint tick-tick sound granite-heights children call the morning clock.

About Magmole

Magmole has grown into a proper pangolin-lizard with a spine-ridge of upright armor plates running from neck to tail-tip, and its lava-crack glow has intensified to a near-continuous orange shimmer across its sides. Four sturdy clawed legs carry it with surprising speed along the granite ridges, and it can dig through rock faces that would blunt a pickaxe in a single hour. The guardians discovered that Magmole tunnels always run true-north without any instrument - the creature uses the granite heights' geothermal gradient as a compass.

Field note: Magmole's claws glow brightest just before it strikes rock, as though calling heat forward to soften the stone ahead.

About Infernole

Infernole is a massive low-slung armored beast, broad as a boulder and covered in thick volcanic plating that blazes with orange-lava cracks from head to tail. Its weight alone cracks the granite underfoot, and each step leaves a warm impression in the rock. When it lowers its head and charges the resulting tremor registers in the granite heights' crystal-monitors half a mountain away. Ancient tunnels in the heights were made by Infernole ancestors, and the current generation uses those same tunnels, slowly widening them year by year.

Field note: Infernole's underside is cooler than its top; the stone it rests on stays warm for three days after it moves on.

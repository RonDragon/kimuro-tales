About Pinchlet

Pinchlet is a bright sky-blue crab with two oversized rounded claws it holds aloft like tiny fists raised for a fight it hasn't started. Small yellow lightning bolt markings crackle across its shell, and two little antennae bob with every step. It lives in the rain-pools near Storm Spire's base, where it sparks harmlessly at passing raindrops - trying, by all appearances, to count them. It has never succeeded but never stopped trying either.

Field note: When Pinchlet zaps a raindrop it lets out a little satisfied click; observers have counted up to 200 clicks per storm.

About Tideclaw

Tideclaw has grown lanky and forward-leaning, its shell deeper blue with electric arcs now pulsing visibly along its leg joints. The claws have lengthened into proper weapons that crackle with charge, and its shell carries a faint hum audible in rain. It crawls the lower cliffs of the Spire during squalls, using the storm surge to amplify its own electric field. Trainers describe battling it as fighting a live wire wrapped in seawater.

Field note: Tideclaw's claws discharge at peak storm intensity; it has been measured generating two brief arcs per second.

About Maelstrand

Maelstrand is massive - deep cobalt and crackling continuously with white-blue arcs that leap between its spiked legs and raised claws. Its shell has grown angular and fortified, and its eyes glow with electric intensity from beneath heavy brow ridges. It was born in the eye of a storm and has not known calm water since. At the top of Storm Spire it is the circuit between lightning above and sea below - a living conductor of the sky's wildest words.

Field note: A Maelstrand generates a local electromagnetic field that scrambles compasses within thirty meters.

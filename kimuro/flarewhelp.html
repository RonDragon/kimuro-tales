About Flarewhelp

Flarewhelp is a small orange-scaled dragon whelp with stubby wings still too soft to lift its own weight, and a tiny flame that flickers at the tip of its tail whenever it gets excited. It scrambles over the cooled black rock at the Lava Crater's rim, chasing embers that drift up from the vents below. It is fearless around heat and deeply afraid of loud noises - an odd pairing for a creature born in a crater.

Field note: Flarewhelp's tail-flame brightens with excitement and dims to almost nothing when it's startled - the opposite of what most trainers expect.

About Flarewyrm

Flarewyrm has grown a proper set of wings and a crest of hardened, ember-orange horn running down its neck, and its tail-flame has become a steady torch rather than a flicker. It flies low circuits above the Lava Crater's rim, and crater-dwelling traders use its silhouette against the smoke as a rough compass. It is territorial about airspace but shares its warmth freely with anything grounded and cold.

Field note: Flarewyrm's wing membranes are warm to the touch year-round - crater travelers have been known to warm their hands on a passing shadow.

About Infernodrake

Infernodrake is an enormous crested dragon whose full wingspan casts a shadow across the entire crater rim, scales glowing faint orange along every ridge as if lit from within. It is the undisputed sovereign of the Lava Crater's high air, and the Abalont texts describe it as the reason the crater's vents have never fully gone cold - its passage overhead is said to stir the deep heat awake again each morning.

Field note: Infernodrake's roar has been recorded triggering small, harmless ember-showers from vents nearly a kilometer away.

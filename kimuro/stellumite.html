About Stellumite

A cheerful pink starfish with a glowing spiral marking on its forehead and two bright little eyes, Stellumite bobs on the surface of the Poison Marsh's still pools with surprising confidence for something so small. Its spiral mark pulses slowly when it senses the thoughts of creatures nearby - it seems especially drawn to confusion, clustering around creatures that have just heard something they don't understand. Marsh travelers use it as an odd compass: where Stellumite points, something is thinking.

Field note: Stellumite's spiral glows brightest whenever someone nearby is puzzling over a word they just heard for the first time.

About Stellaxis

Stellaxis has grown into a full upright starfish with five arms spread wide and a glowing purple orb nestled in its center - a concentrated knot of psychic energy it has spent years compressing. It walks the marsh shallows on the tips of its lower arms, keeping its orb elevated. The orb broadcasts a faint hum that other marsh creatures steer clear of, but Stellaxis itself seems oblivious to the effect. It patrols the boundary between the murky deep and the reedy shallows with slow authority.

Field note: The glow from Stellaxis's central orb is visible through up to two meters of murky marsh water on a dark night.

About Stellavortex

No longer a star at all in appearance, Stellavortex has become a broad, hulking creature with eight thick writhing arms ringed in glowing purple nodes and a blazing psychic core at the center of its flat body. It rests on the deepest floor of the Poison Marsh, and the water above it swirls in a slow permanent eddy that never settles. Those who enter the marsh at night report hearing whispered words from beneath the surface - not random words, but the exact words they struggled to learn that day. No one knows how Stellavortex knows.

Field note: Every name whispered back from the marsh depths at night has been confirmed as one the traveler genuinely could not remember.

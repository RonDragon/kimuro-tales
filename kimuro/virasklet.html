About Virasklet

Virasklet is a small, upright serpent with bright aquamarine-green scales and a row of vivid sky-blue dorsal spines that flash in the dim Shadow Caves light. Its large round eyes carry a bold, dauntless expression unusual in a creature so young. It sits up alert on its tail-coil, peering at anything that moves. Despite being a cave-dweller, it carries a brightness about it - trainers who found one describe feeling briefly warmed as if in sunlight. Its spines rattle when excited, a sound that rings clearly through the cave tunnels.

Field note: Virasklet's blue dorsal spines produce a faint bioluminescent glow in total darkness - just enough to read by if you hold it still.

About Viraxen

Viraxen rears into a wide cobra-spread, its scales now deeper teal mottled with tan, and its open fanged jaw dripping purple-tinged psychic venom. Wisps of violet energy curl from the corners of its mouth. It has shed the brightness of its youth for focused menace, holding perfectly still before each strike. Shadow Cave spelunkers mark its territory by purple-stained rock faces where it has venom-misted a wide arc. Despite the threat it presents, it sleeps in the same hollow each night and knows every mark carved into the cave walls.

Field note: Viraxen can hold its hood-spread pose for up to four hours without muscle fatigue, waiting for prey to make the first movement.

About Toxihydra

Toxihydra fills the widest chamber of the Shadow Caves, its scaled bulk thick and armored, its enormous hood spreading to block entire passages. Green-yellow venom clouds drift from its gaping fanged maw and purple-rot energy crackles along its flanks. It has grown beyond serpent into something almost geological - a living feature of the cave system that explorers route around on their maps. It keeps the deepest parts of the Shadow Caves impassable to Abalont's creeping dark, not because it opposes the shadow, but because it tolerates nothing in its territory. Nothing at all.

Field note: The cave floor beneath Toxihydra's usual resting spot has been dissolved by venom drip into a shallow basin that now holds a permanent pool - cave explorers call it the Toxic Font.

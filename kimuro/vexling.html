About Vexling

Vexling is a small jet-black ant with a rounded glossy body, two short antennae, and a single glowing violet eye at the centre of its head. Its six legs are disproportionately long for its body size and it moves with a quick, jerky energy - never in a straight line, always at a slight angle to its destination. It inhabits the edges of the Ghost Hollow where the shadows are thickest and the soil is cold. It has no nest of its own - it walks into the nearest occupied nest, eats one thing, and leaves. Every colony in the Hollow has a Vexling file in its threat records.

Field note: Every ant colony in Ghost Hollow maintains a specific patrol route around the one entrance Vexling prefers - they've never managed to block it effectively.

About Vexant

Vexant is a powerful dark ant with a spiked elongated body, long jagged mandibles, and narrow violet eyes gleaming from beneath a heavily armoured head. Its thorax is broader and its legs carry it at speed, moving in the same oblique direction as Vexling but now with obvious purpose - it is hunting. It patrols the Ghost Hollow's mid-canopy level, gripping tree bark upside down as easily as right-side up, and drops on targets from above. The hollow's ghost-type residents give it notable distance despite being incorporeal, which suggests Vexant can affect things that have no physical form.

Field note: Three Ghost Hollow apparition-type sightings have been interrupted by a Vexant arrival - in each case, the apparition did not reappear for days.

About Vexalord

Vexalord is a dramatic and terrible insect - a great black ant with wide dark wings spreading from its thorax, enormous curved mandibles, and a long blade-like abdomen trailing behind it. Its single violet eye is now large enough to see from some distance and it radiates a low pulse of dark energy that causes nearby shadow-patches to shift unnaturally. It rules the Ghost Hollow as a whole, not through any claim but through the simple fact that everything else moves aside when it passes. The guardians considered it one of the Hollow's oldest problems - and secretly one of its most reliable constants.

Field note: Shadow-patches within five metres of a resting Vexalord shift in the same direction it is facing - even when no light source changes.

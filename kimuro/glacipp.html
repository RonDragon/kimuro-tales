About Glacipp

Glacipp is a plump blue-and-white penguin chick with a single spiralling ice horn on its head, large luminous eyes, and a swirl mark on its belly that pulses faintly with psychic light. It cannot fly, swim, or fight - but it can predict where the next warm sunbeam in Frozen Peaks will fall to the minute, and it sits there waiting. The other snowfield creatures follow it to find warmth and it appears completely unaware of this, wandering from sunbeam to sunbeam without looking back.

Field note: Glacipp stands in a future sunbeam spot an average of 11 minutes before the light actually arrives there.

About Glacude

Glacude stands tall and crystal-armoured - a sleek penguin with blade-like ice panels growing from its foreflippers and cold blue eyes that glow with focused psychic awareness. The swirl patterns on its chest have become complex geometric lines. It moves through Frozen Peaks like a scholar on a mission, stopping to press its forehead against trees as if reading them. The snowfield's oldest guardians say it is compiling something - a memory they cannot access. The psychic glow it radiates keeps the snowfield's shadows at bay more effectively than torchlight.

Field note: Glacude's psychic field erases small anxious thoughts from those who stand near it for more than two minutes.

About Glaciking

The Guardians believe it sees all futures at once, chooses the one in which the fewest are left behind, and walks toward it every single day.

Field note: Glaciking has stood on the same stone in Frozen Peaks for three hundred years; the stone has not worn down.

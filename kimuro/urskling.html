About Urskling

Urskling is a plump, warm-brown bear cub with a single small steel-blue spike rising from the top of its head like a tiny helmet-crest. Its eyes are wide and warm, and it walks the Amber Desert with its nose low to the sand, sniffing every grain as if searching for something Hunter the watcher lost at sunset. Its paws are large for its body - already built for strength it hasn't grown into yet. It repeats words quietly to itself as it patrols, practicing the way a good guardian always keeps skills sharp.

Field note: Urskling cubs walk the same patrol route every evening within minutes of the same time - they seem to have absorbed Hunter's schedule through the desert sand itself.

About Urskalon

Urskalon is a broad-shouldered bear now sporting a full ridge of steel-grey spines running from the back of its skull down its spine. Its thick desert-tan coat is streaked with dust it never fully shakes. It patrols with a long, measured stride, pausing at specific landmarks to sniff the air - the same landmarks Hunter once checked. Other Amber Desert creatures give it a wide berth, not out of fear, but out of something closer to respect. Its strength is quiet, purposeful, and patient in the way that only long practice makes.

Field note: Urskalon stops at the exact same three desert rocks every patrol - each one, when pressed, is warm from a different underground heat source it has memorized.

About Urskalith

Urskalith is fully armoured - steel-grey plate fused across shoulders, chest, and flanks, a spiked crown of blade-like dorsal spines, and massive paws that leave craters in hardpacked desert sand. Its face carries decades of dust in every crease, and its eyes hold a patience that has outlasted every storm the Amber Desert has offered. It does not fight unless forced; it simply stands where it stands, and the desert moves around it. The guardians say Urskalith carries Hunter's oath in its bones - strength is for protecting words, never for stealing them.

Field note: Urskalith's armour plates carry the magnetic alignment of the Amber Desert's bedrock - navigators claim a compass held near it points not north but toward the nearest word that needs protecting.

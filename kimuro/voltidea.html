About Voltidea

Voltidea bobs upright in the warm waters of Coral Bay, its soft body a gentle sky-blue with a luminous orb glowing on its belly like a captured bubble of sunlight. Its crown ridge is smooth and delicate, and its curling tail wraps around coral stems for balance as it drifts. The glowing belly orb brightens and dims in time with Voltidea's heartbeat, acting as a living lantern to attract the tiny plankton it feeds on. The seahorse children of the reef learn to follow its light when the current runs dark.

Field note: Voltidea's belly orb dims when it is frightened and blazes when it recognizes a familiar voice.

About Voltimer

Voltimer has grown prominent spined fins along its neck and back that fan open during a threat display, sparking at their tips like a lit fuse. Its color has deepened to rich cobalt, and its belly crest now channels electricity in thin crackling lines that run up to its crown. It no longer drifts - it swims with direction, steering by the electric fields laid down by every other living thing in the Bay. When two Voltimerexchange recognition, they touch crown-to-crown and share a brief shared pulse.

Field note: Voltimer can identify every creature in a hundred-meter radius by the unique electric signature of its heartbeat.

About Volthydra

Volthydra is a majestic blue seahorse grown to the size of a warhorse, its crown now a sweeping corona of electric-edged fins and its body armored in overlapping scales that chime against each other in the current. A pair of broad side-fins carry it through the water with unhurried grace, and the electric aura around its body illuminates the deep reef trenches for ten meters in every direction. Ancient Coral Bay lore says Volthydra is the Reef's Memory - it knows every coral formation by name and the name of every creature that ever sheltered there.

Field note: When Volthydra sheds its outer scale layer once a decade, coral reefs within a mile experience a growth surge from the mineral shower.

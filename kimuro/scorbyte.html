About Scorbyte

Scorbyte is a compact orange-brown crab-like bug with one outsized pincer that it holds raised, showing it off at every opportunity. Heat shimmers around it at midday because its shell absorbs sunlight and stores it as warmth in the Bug Thicket canopy. It will grab a hot ember from a campfire with its big claw and carry it around - not to harm anyone, just to feel the heat in its grip. Young Scorbyte are sometimes spotted standing in beams of sunlight with eyes closed, soaking warmth like a tiny sunbather.

Field note: A Scorbyte's big claw runs consistently warmer than its body - you can cook a small bug-berry against it in under two minutes.

About Scorpaw

Scorpaw has armored itself in overlapping burnt-orange plates that act as heat vents, letting steam escape between each layer. Its claws are now dual and enormous - one for grip, one for smashing - and the fire trapped inside them turns the striking surfaces cherry-red during combat. It marks its territory in Bug Thicket by scorching claw-prints into the bark of large trees at head height, and those marks never fade. Other bugs avoid its grove unless invited.

Field note: Scorpaw's striking claw reaches surface temperatures high enough to leave a clear brand in oak bark with a single tap.

About Infernacle

Infernacle is a massive brick-red armored crab covered in interlocked spikes that radiate heat like a forge. Every plate is thick as a roof tile and carries a faint ember-glow in its grooves. When it fully opens its claws, waves of heat ripple outward in visible bands, and nearby dry brush begins to smolder within five seconds. It walks slowly, since anything that hurries nearby catches fire, and Bug Thicket keeps a wide charred ring around wherever Infernacle has chosen to rest that season.

Field note: The charred ring around an Infernacle's resting spot grows by roughly one metre per week; old rings can be up to forty metres wide.

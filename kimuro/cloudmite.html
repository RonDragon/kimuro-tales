About Cloudmite

Cloudmite looks exactly like a tiny white cloud that decided to grow a face - two bright eyes and a gentle smile peek out from its fluffy body, with wispy tendrils trailing behind it like tiny legs it never quite uses to walk. It drifts through the lower gales of Storm Spire, humming softly and absorbing the mist rising from lightning-heated rain. Guardians who first climbed the Spire thought these floating puffs were simple weather formations, until one of them winked back.

Field note: A Cloudmite can hold exactly 40 mL of rainwater inside its body before it must sneeze it all out.

About Stormjelai

Stormjelai has shed its cloudlike shape and grown into a true jellyfish form - a pale blue translucent bell with long trailing tentacles that crackle with stored lightning. Its gentle smile has sharpened into something more focused, and the eyes now glow when a storm front rolls in. It rides the mid-altitude winds of the Spire like a living kite, letting its tentacles act as lightning rods that feed it. Touch one of those threads and you'll feel why it earned its name.

Field note: Stormjelai recharges itself by dangling its tentacles into active lightning bolts - it takes exactly three strikes to fill up.

About Tempestus

Tempestus fills the upper storm layers of Storm Spire like a living storm cell - its broad dark-blue bell crackles with continuous lightning and its long tentacles hang down through the clouds below, invisible until they brush the mountain's highest crags. Its eyes, now heavy-lidded and ancient, watch the valley far beneath with the patience of weather itself. The old guardians believed Tempestus was responsible for every storm that swept the Spire, and they were not entirely wrong.

Field note: When Tempestus drifts east, locals say to bring in the laundry - the storm it carries is never more than ten minutes behind it.

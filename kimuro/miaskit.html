About Miaskit

Miaskit is a pale blue-white armadillo-form with articulated bony plates that clank softly when it rolls. Its round eyes are almost too large for its head, adapted to the shadow caves' utter darkness. A faint spectral shimmer traces the edges of each plate, as if the ghost element is seeping out through the cracks in the earth-armour. It rolls into a perfect sphere when startled, and the ball glows like a faint lantern in the dark - confusing predators who expected it to vanish.

Field note: When Miaskit rolls into a ball, it glows bright enough to read by for exactly forty-three seconds before fading.

About Miasmole

Miasmole has grown into a heavier, more angular form. Its segmented plates have darkened to slate-blue-grey and carry a faint ghost-light glow at the seams. It moves through the shadow caves with the unhurried confidence of a creature that can walk through solid rock if it chooses to - which it occasionally does, passing half of its body through a cave wall before pulling back out to look confused at itself. Its claws are built for tunneling and leave glowing scratch-trails behind.

Field note: Miasmole's glowing claw-trails persist on cave rock for up to an hour - shadow-cave explorers follow them as makeshift trail-markers.

About Miasmorph

Miasmorph barely seems to be fully present - its body ripples at the edges, plates phasing partly in and out of the cave rock, trailing wisps of blue-white ghost-light. It is the oldest thing in the shadow caves, and the caves themselves seem shaped around it, as if the tunnels were carved by its presence rather than by water. Its breath produces a low mist that settles into curling shapes before dissolving - the shadow-cave scholars have been copying them for decades and believe the shapes form a complete map.

Field note: Miasmorph's mist-breath shapes cycle through the same sequence every forty-seven breaths - scholars call it the 'shadow round'.

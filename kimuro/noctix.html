About Noctix

Noctix is a small round-bodied purple bat with wide glowing eyes and stubby wings it flaps with great determination. A faint violet glow hovers at its chest - the first hint of the heroic energy that runs through its lineage. It roosts in the highest towers of Steel Fortress, dangling upside down from iron beams, and launches itself at any noise in the dark with fearless curiosity. New recruits sometimes mistake it for a piece of equipment until it squeaks and falls on their heads.

Field note: Noctix always launches itself headfirst toward the loudest noise it can hear, landing on it before looking first.

About Noctilix

Noctilix stands upright now on powerful clawed legs, its wings spread wide with crackling purple lightning tracing their veins. The glow at its chest has become a constant crackling light, and its eyes carry a focused intensity that makes smaller creatures step aside. It patrols the Steel Fortress ramparts at night, and the guardians learned long ago to leave lanterns unlit when it is on watch - Noctilix reads the dark better than any torch.

Field note: Noctilix can detect a single unlit candle from 200 metres away purely by the heat it would have made.

About Noctivex

Noctivex is a great broad-winged bat with two blazing purple rings of heroic energy framing its chest - the sigil that marks it as one of the Steel Fortress's ancient guardians. Its wings, now fully spread, blot out the moon when it circles the Fortress walls on patrol. Those rings pulse with a light that wards off both shadow-threats and doubt. The youngest recruits are told: when you see those twin purple moons moving overhead, the Fortress is safe.

Field note: The two glowing rings Noctivex carries are said to be promises it made to the first guardian - and it has kept both.

About Pryxling

Pryxling is a chunky, angular violet-grey insect with a compact rounded body plated in thick stone-textured shell segments. Its four limbs end in strong gripping claws, and its small compound eyes are deep violet. It scuttles along the light grove's stone borders, wedging itself into rock crevices and blending in almost perfectly with the grey-purple lichen. When something approaches, it freezes solid - not just still, but truly rigid, like a rock. Grove visitors have sat down on a Pryxling thinking it was a comfortable boulder, only to have it suddenly skitter away. This extreme stillness is a genuine psychic trick: its nervous system actually pauses to avoid detection.

Field note: Pryxling can hold perfect stone-still for up to four hours - grove-keepers mark known Pryxling 'rest spots' with chalk so visitors don't sit on them.

About Praxion

Praxion stands tall and bipedal, its purple-grey carapace now sharp-edged and crystal-faceted. A wide flat head and enormous compound eyes give it a commanding presence, and its two bladed forearms are edged with natural stone-crystal that can chip granite. It carries a large kite-shield of solid stone as its left arm-guard. Praxion patrols the light grove's stone outcroppings with methodical sweeps, leaving parallel scratch marks in the rock as territory signs. It is a dutiful creature: if it adopts a trainer, it will stand between them and any threat without hesitation, shield raised, and refuse to move until the danger is gone.

Field note: Praxion's stone shield has never been successfully broken - researchers estimate it has blocked over three hundred strikes without a crack.

About Pryrarch

Pryrarch is an imposing bipedal insect-warrior whose entire body has crystallized into angular purple-violet stone plates. Four swept-back wing-blades extend from its back like frozen lightning-strikes, purely decorative now but razor-edged. Its head is a broad armored plate with deep-set glowing violet eyes, and its limbs end in heavy crushing claws. It stands at the stone gates of the light grove like a living wall, motionless for weeks at a time, then moves with sudden frightening speed when needed. The guardians say Pryrarch was the first creature to defend the light grove against Abalont's initial advance, and that the deep gouges in the grove's stone gate-posts are its claw-marks from that battle.

Field note: The claw-gouges Pryrarch carved into the grove's stone gate-posts have not eroded at all in the centuries since the first battle.

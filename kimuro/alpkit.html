About Alpkit

Alpkit is an adorably round cream-white llama foal with a fluffy woolly coat, large blue eyes, and tiny stumpy legs that make it wobble slightly when it walks. Its wool catches the faint light of Crystal Mines and throws back soft pastel glints. It nuzzles anything that holds still long enough, pressing its forehead against crystals to absorb their stored memories. Young Alpkits born deep in the mines reportedly hum back fragments of ancient guardian songs they learned from the crystal walls.

Field note: Alpkit absorbs one new memory from any crystal it nuzzles - and will nuzzle every crystal in range before resting.

About Alpacay

Alpacay has grown into a graceful full-sized llama with a dense white woolly coat and long elegant legs. Its fluffy mane frames an intelligent, knowing face, and subtle iridescent shimmer runs through its coat in sunlight. It navigates the deep tunnels of Crystal Mines by sensing psychic echoes stored in the ore veins - a walking map of everywhere it has ever been. Crystal miners leave small gifts of salt at tunnel crossings, knowing Alpacay will nuzzle the walls nearby and keep the veins calm.

Field note: Alpacay remembers every tunnel junction it has ever passed through; lost miners follow it out of the dark by watching its ear tilt.

About Majestiaca

Majestiaca is a breathtaking llama adorned in flowing rainbow-pastel wool that shifts from rose to gold to violet as it moves, hung with ornate golden bells and crystal pendants at its chest and neck. Its mane and tail are braided with light itself. When it walks, its hoofbeats chime softly and the air around it smells of warm quartz. It is said Majestiaca carries the complete memory of every voice ever raised in Crystal Mines - and will share any of them with a child who asks politely.

Field note: The golden bells on Majestiaca's chest ring a different note for each region of the mines - experienced miners navigate by sound alone.

About Rimepup

Rimepup is a stocky white-furred wolf pup with frost crystals permanently rimed along its paws and jaw, and a habit of headbutting anything that moves - including rocks, and occasionally itself. It tumbles through the Frozen Peaks' lower drifts practicing pounces on nothing in particular, building the strength that will define it later. It is stubborn, warm-hearted, and utterly undeterred by falling down.

Field note: Rimepup's frost-rimed paws never actually feel cold to the touch - the ice is decorative, not thermal, according to guardian researchers.

About Frostknuckle

Frostknuckle has become a broad, powerfully built wolf with ice-hardened knuckle-plates along its front paws that it uses to strike stone as easily as flesh. It spars against the Frozen Peaks' ice formations for hours at a stretch, and climbers have learned to follow the sound of its training as a rough trail-marker toward safer ground. It respects strength but reserves genuine warmth for whoever keeps training after they've lost.

Field note: Frostknuckle's ice-knuckle plates re-form within minutes if chipped in sparring - a self-repairing trait unique to this evolution stage.

About Glacierbrawler

Glacierbrawler is a massive ice-armored wolf whose entire forequarters are sheathed in glacier-blue plating, capable of cracking exposed stone with a single forepaw strike. It holds the highest, coldest reaches of the Frozen Peaks as its own proving ground, and guardian texts call it the peak's oldest standing challenge - a test that has never once needed to be repeated for anyone who genuinely earned its respect.

Field note: Glacierbrawler's strikes have been measured cracking stone at a force no other creature of its size in the Peaks can match.

About Squeamox

Squeamox is a small, round dark brown mouse with enormous amber eyes that take up most of its face, two oversized front teeth, and stubby powerful forepaws built for digging. Its ears are large and round and it holds its hands pressed together in front of its chest as it sits. It burrows into the Sand Dunes with impressive speed - a Squeamox can vanish into dry sand in less than two seconds flat - and it caches food pellets in small side-chambers off its main tunnel, then systematically forgets which chambers they are in.

Field note: A single Squeamox tunnel system has been found to contain forty-three separate food caches - none of them ever revisited after the initial digging.

About Gnawmox

Gnawmox is a lean, battle-scarred sand rat with long whiskers, narrow amber eyes, and a long bare tail trailing behind it. Its brown fur has the texture of worn leather and its forepaws have grown heavy and clawed from years of excavation work. It moves low and fast across the Sand Dunes at night, hugging the shadows between dune crests, and rarely appears in the same place twice. Its gnaw-marks on dune stone have been studied and found to contain patterns - whether language, map, or simple habit, no one can yet say.

Field note: Gnawmox gnaw-marks on dune stones always face north - researchers have proposed several theories and confirmed none of them.

About Pestilox

Pestilox is a massive, hunched armadillo-rat with a thick plated back of overlapping earth-brown armour scales, a wide flat head, enormous curved claws, and a long thick tail it swings as a club. Its small amber eyes glow dully in the dark of the deep dunes. It has abandoned the surface almost entirely, operating through a network of tunnels beneath the Sand Dunes so extensive that large sections of dune occasionally collapse into them. The Sand Dunes' few surface-dwellers treat the sudden appearance of a Pestilox as a serious warning that the ground ahead is unsafe.

Field note: A section of Sand Dune that has partially collapsed overhead is almost certainly sitting above a Pestilox tunnel junction - they always collapse at the intersections first.

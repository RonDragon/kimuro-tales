About Slowth

Slowth is a small round-faced sloth with light green fur patched in darker moss green, and a perpetually contented drowsy expression. Its long curved claws hold it to the bark of any surface it reaches and it seems perfectly happy wherever it ends up. It moves at a pace that makes watching grass grow feel urgent, but once it settles on a branch or a friendly shoulder it radiates a calm that the whole Bug Thicket seems to absorb. Children who spend time near Slowth consistently score better at recalling long words - nobody is certain why.

Field note: Slowth blinks exactly once every forty seconds - researchers use it as a reliable timer when their instruments are wet.

About Boughling

Boughling hangs from a branch with all four long limbs, its green-and-brown fur now thick with actual living moss that has colonised its back. Small leaf-sprigs poke from between its shoulders and its rounded face wears an expression of serene focus. It spends most of its time suspended upside-down high in the Bug Thicket canopy, slowly turning to face whichever direction the light is coming from. Trainers who travel with it report that their sleep improves significantly - something in the rhythmic slow breathing seems to quiet everything around it.

Field note: Boughling's breathing rate - one breath per two minutes - has been used by doctors in the Bug Thicket to calibrate calm.

About Canopyth

Canopyth stands on four vast earth-brown limbs, its entire body covered in a dense living canopy of moss, mushrooms, flowering vines, and small trees that have grown through its fur over centuries. Antler-like branches crown its broad head and blossoms bloom across its shoulders in season. It moves so rarely that the Bug Thicket's map treats its resting position as a fixed landmark. When it does move, the entire forest canopy shifts with it. The guardians believed it was the Bug Thicket itself given form - a creature that had simply decided to remember everything.

Field note: Three different species of bird nest exclusively in Canopyth's antlers and are never found anywhere else in the entire Bug Thicket.

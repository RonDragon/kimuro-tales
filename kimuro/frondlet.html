About Frondlet

Frondlet is a small bright-green creature that sits upright like a vigilant lizard, with a row of pointy green spikes running from its crown down its back. Its large golden eyes and slightly grumpy expression give it the look of a tiny creature taking itself very seriously. Found in the sunny clearings of Verdant Valley where the grass grows tall, it spends most of its time sitting perfectly still among the stems, listening. When it does move, every motion is precise and deliberate - this creature has never taken a careless step.

Field note: Frondlet's back-spikes each point a different direction when wind shifts, mapping gusts across the valley floor more accurately than any weather vane.

About Frondarch

Frondarch has grown into a powerfully built four-legged dragon with ridged green-gold plating across its back and a spiral leaf-marking etched into its flank like a natural sigil. Its spines have grown longer and harder, and the deep golden eyes now carry a spark of genuine fire. It walks the outer edges of Verdant Valley where the grass meets the wilder undergrowth, marking territory with scratches on stones. Other grass-creatures give it a respectful distance - not because Frondarch is aggressive, but because it radiates a quiet certainty that takes up space.

Field note: Frondarch can bite through a vine as thick as a wrist in one snap and immediately weaves the halves back together into a tighter knot than before.

About Frondyrant

Frondyrant is a massive quadruped dragon covered in thick overlapping plates of dark green armor, each plate edged in gold. Its spine is now a continuous ridge of great curved horns, and the swirling vine-sigil on its flank has expanded to cover most of its side in luminous green spiral patterns. It settles at the heart of Verdant Valley each season-change and the valley's plants seem to grow faster, taller, and greener in a circle around its resting spot. Guardians who knew it as a hatchling still bring it exactly three leaves of sweet clover as a greeting - it accepts them with great solemnity.

Field note: Every plant within ten meters of a resting Frondyrant grows at twice its normal rate, including weeds, which Frondyrant finds mildly undignified.

About Luxpup

Luxpup is a pale silver-white pup with faint violet tints in its thick fur and a single large golden-amber eye glowing steadily in its forehead. It trots through Steel Fortress's lower maintenance corridors on sturdy little legs, sniffing at every metal surface. Its fur crackles with static when it shakes itself, and small sparks jump between its ears when excited. Fortress engineers say Luxpup can detect a faulty wire three corridors away - it sits and stares at the wall above the problem and refuses to move until someone looks.

Field note: Luxpup's eye dims whenever it detects danger and brightens again once it is past - a reliable alarm that costs nothing to run.

About Luxfang

Luxfang is a lean white wolf with electric arcs threading through its mane and tail. Its single large eye is a steady amber light, and its stride carries confidence - head low, watching, always a half-second ahead of where it looks. In Steel Fortress it patrols the upper ramparts, and its presence makes the fortress lights brighten slightly. When it howls, every electronic system in earshot stutters once then runs cleaner afterward, as if the howl cleared interference from the air. Guardians trust it with perimeter watch above any other creature.

Field note: Luxfang's paws leave tiny scorch-arcs on metal floors - fortress workers have mapped its patrol route just by following the marks.

About Luxthorne

Luxthorne is a massive pale wolf whose thick mane erupts with violet-white electric light - it looks like a storm given legs. Its amber eye blazes, and every footstep on metal sends arcs across the floor. When it runs in Steel Fortress's outer grounds, the fortress lighting pulses in sync with its gait. It is the fortress's oldest guardian, and the walls are marked with the scorch-patterns of every battle it has fought. Abalont's shadow once dimmed its eye - it took twelve children working together to speak enough words to brighten it again.

Field note: Luxthorne's mane acts as a lightning rod - in storms it draws every bolt in range, releasing the energy as light instead of heat.

About Vaulkit

Vaulkit is a small but armoured creature: a stout, rounded body encased in a grey full-plate helmet and chest-piece, with a swirling green runic emblem glowing on its front - a mark of purpose rather than power. Its tiny limbs stick out from beneath the armour at optimistic angles. Despite its size, it carries itself with the bearing of something that has already decided it will not be moved. In the Bug Thicket, it patrols the thorny paths where no other creature bothers to go, checking every root and leaf with methodical care.

Field note: Vaulkit's rune-glow brightens when it encounters an unlearned word - the glow intensity appears to correlate to how important the word will later become.

About Vaultusk

Vaultusk has grown into a powerful, low-slung guardian with thick armour plates covering its entire upper body, a broad armoured face-plate, and two heavy curved tusks that glow faintly with the same green rune-light as its chest. Its legs are wide-set and planted, and it rarely runs - preferring to walk steadily at whatever it needs to deal with. In the Bug Thicket it has mapped every unsafe bridge, unstable trunk, and dangerous burrow, and it redirects creatures away from them with a firm shoulder-block, no words needed.

Field note: Vaultusk's tusks glow stronger when near danger - trainers say a tusk-brightness of three means turn around, four means run.

About Vaulwarden

Vaulwarden is a massive, battle-worn armoured badger-guardian covered head-to-haunch in heavy plate, with bright green runic glyphs blazing across its chest and bracers, and twin heavy tusks that arc wide and catch light from across the Bug Thicket. Its helm is dented and scratched from centuries of guarding, but the runes beneath the scratches still shine. It is the oldest warden in the Thicket - it does not know how many threats it has turned aside, only that it is still standing and so is the Thicket.

Field note: Vaulwarden's runes have been identified as an older form of the guardian sigils - scholars believe they predate all known guardian records by several centuries.

About Voxbat

Voxbat is a perfectly round-headed little bat-dragon whelp, ink-black from snout to wing-tip, with bright wide eyes that catch the lava glow of the crater and reflect it back as two orange points. Its leathery wings are proportionally huge for its body, folding around it like a cloak when it rests. It hangs from the cooler underside of lava-shelf overhangs in Lava Crater, sleeping by day and gliding through the heat plumes by night. It is attracted specifically to words spoken in the dark - as if the absence of light sharpens the sound for it.

Field note: Voxbat hangs from exactly the same overhang each day to sleep - and each morning the overhang is a fraction warmer from its body heat.

About Voxwing

Voxwing has grown from round whelp into a proper dark dragon with a muscled body, a spiked back ridge, and green-veined wing membranes that glow faintly when spread. Its eyes have narrowed to slitted amber intelligence, and it now hunts the thermal currents of Lava Crater's mid-zone, riding them for hours with minimal effort. Its dark scales absorb heat and radiate it back as a faint shimmer, making it nearly invisible against the crater's orange glow. When it lands, it grips with all four clawed feet simultaneously - a sound like four locks clicking shut at once.

Field note: Voxwing's four-footed landing is heard as four distinct clicks - crater explorers freeze when they hear it, knowing it has spotted them first.

About Voxdrake

Voxdrake is a fully realized dark dragon of Lava Crater - massive black-scaled body, vast wings with green-glowing veins that illuminate the underside of storm clouds, four powerful limbs ending in heavy claws that gouge the crater rim. When it breathes out, the air carries the taste of carbon and deep time. It does not fight for territory - it simply is the territory. The lava flows of Lava Crater follow channels carved by Voxdrake's passage over thousands of years, and the crater's ecology is calibrated entirely around where it chooses to rest.

Field note: Every major lava channel in Lava Crater was cut by Voxdrake walking the same path repeatedly over the centuries - geology confirms deliberate routing.

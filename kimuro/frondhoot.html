About Frondhoot

Frondhoot starts life as a small, sturdy-looking creature with a boar-like face and a dramatic crown of large green leaves erupting from the top of its head. Its round body and short legs give it a determined, slightly top-heavy appearance as it trots into the Gale Ridge's lower winds. It is fearless about weather in a way that impresses creatures twice its size, pressing its leaf-crown directly into gusts that would send lighter animals skidding. The leaves on its head act as natural sails, and it steers by angling them.

Field note: Frondhoot navigates by tilting its leaf-crown and letting the wind do the steering - it has never taken a wrong path in a headwind.

About Frondwise

Frondwise has transformed into a broad-winged owl with a full leaf-ruff framing its disc-face and long green-brown wing feathers that whisper in the storm winds. Its large yellow eyes seem to glow from within, and it can hold completely still on a ledge while a gale howls around it, feathers pressing flat while its leaf-ruff fans wide. Gale Ridge climbers say Frondwise always perches above the safe path and that following the direction its beak points has saved more lives than any guidebook.

Field note: Frondwise can rotate its head 270 degrees and has been seen scanning three separate storm-fronts simultaneously without moving its body.

About Frondophage

Frondophage is a great owl whose wingspan spans the width of the Gale Ridge's widest ledge, and whose leaf-patterned feathers have layered into a deep green-brown armor of overlapping fronds. When it opens its wings fully, the wind passing through its feathers produces a chord of tones audible from the valley floor. It hunts in the highest storm-belts, riding turbulence that would destroy any other flier, reading weather patterns the way other creatures read faces. The Gale Ridge's oldest legend says Frondophage was already nesting in the peak when the first storm was born.

Field note: Frondophage's flight is silent in calm air, but in a storm its wings produce a low harmonic that calms smaller creatures sheltering below.

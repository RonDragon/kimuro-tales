About Burrit

Burrit is a soft yellow furred creature with an outsized pointed snout and two enormous flat claws - almost bigger than its body - held out in front like a pair of tiny shovels. Its small round eyes blink owlishly in the light; it spends most of its life underground and finds the open air of Granite Heights startlingly bright. When it stumbles on the surface, it sits back on its haunches, claws spread wide, head tilting left and right as it tries to make sense of the world above.

Field note: Burrit can excavate a tunnel the length of a classroom in under an hour - and usually forgets which end it started from.

About Burrixe

Burrixe is a powerful, compact earth-digger with golden-brown fur and claws that have hardened into ridged stone-edged scoops. It hunches low, using those massive forelimbs to pull through granite with surprising ease, leaving perfectly round tunnels behind. Its snout has lengthened and now tapers to a point tipped with a tough keratin tip for probing rock seams. Miners in Granite Heights follow Burrixe tunnel networks - they reliably lead to crystal pockets.

Field note: Burrixe leaves a faint sulphur-and-stone scent at every tunnel fork that guides other Burrixe out of dead ends.

About Terravix

Terravix is armoured in overlapping plates of compressed earth and stone, its claws now truly massive - each one longer than a child is tall. It does not simply dig; it reshapes the rock of Granite Heights according to some slow intention, rerouting underground rivers and opening new caverns that no map has ever recorded. Its long snout rests close to the ground as it moves, reading the mountain's heartbeat through vibration. The heights themselves seem to breathe differently when Terravix is near.

Field note: Every tunnel Terravix makes is perfectly circular - geologists cannot explain how a living creature achieves such geometric precision.

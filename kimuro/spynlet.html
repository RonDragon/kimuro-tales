About Spynlet

Spynlet is a tiny purple sea slug moving through shadow caves on a slow muscular foot, its soft rounded body dotted with small pale spots and topped with two short sensory antennae-horns. Its colour glows faintly with toxic warning - nothing in the shadow caves eats it, which suits it perfectly. It leaves a thin luminescent trail of psychic slime that persists for hours, and small shadow insects have been observed following these trails to navigate the darkest passages.

Field note: Spynlet's trail glows strongest at the exact spot where it paused longest - always where the air is warmest.

About Spynose

Spynose has grown to the length of a forearm and developed a dramatic ridge of sharp golden-tipped spines running down its back in alternating rows. Its body is vivid purple with pale yellow spots scattered across the softer flanks. The spines carry a mild contact venom, but Spynose only raises them when crowded - otherwise they lie flat. It navigates shadow caves with remarkable accuracy despite the dark, reading the psychic imprints left in cave walls by everything that has ever touched them.

Field note: Spynose has been observed pressing its antennae to cave walls for up to twenty minutes - researchers believe it is reading old impressions.

About Spynarch

Spynarch is a wide, heavy-bodied sea slug filling the shadow cave floor from wall to wall when it fully extends. Its back is a forest of long, stiff violet-purple spines that spread sideways and catch the faint cave glow. The spots on its flanks have grown into iridescent eyes-patterns that seem to follow you. It moves at the pace of a slowly turning page, but covers ground steadily. The shadow caves' most venomous passages are also Spynarch's territory - other creatures navigate around its route, not through it.

Field note: Spynarch has mapped every shadow cave passage in living memory - it corrects its own path by a fraction of a degree to explore a new section every single year.

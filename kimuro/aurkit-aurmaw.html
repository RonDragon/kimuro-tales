About Aurkit

Aurkit is a vivid green kitten with bright yellow-green spots scattered across its round body, a plump curved tail, and wide innocent teal eyes. Its soft jungle-green fur smells faintly of fresh leaves after rain. In Shadow Caves it is an unlikely inhabitant - it wanders in from the forest edge and seems genuinely unbothered by the dark, since its spots faintly glow in the absence of light. Young trainers find it irresistible, and Aurkit returns the affection by bumping its head against anyone who crouches down to its level.

Field note: Aurkit's spots glow brighter when it learns a name it has never heard - a particularly bright flash means it understood something important.

About Aurfang

Aurfang has grown into a lithe and powerful green-and-gold leopard with large sunflower-pattern spots and bright sharp teal eyes that pierce the cave dark. Its muscles move smoothly under its sleek coat, and its claws click lightly on the cave floor. In Shadow Caves it has found its purpose - patrolling the boundary between the forest and the dark, guiding lost creatures back to the light. It carries the scent of jungle flowers into the deepest shadows, which is strangely comforting to creatures trapped in the dark.

Field note: Aurfang's scent-trail through Shadow Caves leads outward, never inward - any creature that follows its smell finds the forest edge within ten minutes.

About Aurmawlix

Aurmawlix is a magnificent large jungle cat with emerald-and-gold spotted fur, powerful wide forequarters, and a dramatic pair of lush green leaf-wings unfurling from its shoulders like a forest canopy taking flight. Its mane is a rich tangle of deep green vines and glowing teal markings trace the lines of its face and sides. In Shadow Caves it stands as a living bridge between darkness and light - its wings carry the scent of the deep forest into places where the forest has never been, and creatures come out of the shadows to follow it home.

Field note: When Aurmawlix opens its wings fully in Shadow Caves, photosynthesis occurs in the immediate vicinity - moss blooms on cave walls within minutes.

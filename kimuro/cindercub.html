About Cindercub

Cindercub is a small orange-and-red canine pup with a spiky flame-mane beginning to sprout along its neck. Its compact body is covered in fire-touched fur, and its tail flicks with tiny embers. It digs shallow burrows in the Sand Dunes at dawn before the heat grows unbearable, filling them with warm sand to sleep in. It chirps at the other dune creatures with its tiny voice, and the locals say you can smell its warm fur long before you spot it.

Field note: Cindercub's flame-mane points toward water beneath the sand - dune tribes follow it during droughts.

About Blazarc

Blazarc is a lean, long-legged fire-beast with a full spiky blaze-mane and a body of fire-orange layered fur. Its legs are built for long desert strides, and its narrow head turns quickly to scan the horizon. When the sandstorms roll in from the deep desert, Blazarc is the first to face them - it stands at the dune-crest and lets the wind fuel its mane until the blaze is visible for half a league. Nomad children follow its mane-light to safety.

Field note: Blazarc's mane-fire gives off no smoke and no heat beyond arm's length - safe enough to sleep beside in a storm.

About Infernarch

Infernarch stands vast and fierce with a full volcanic mane of jagged fire-spikes that crown its entire upper body. Its thick fire-fur armor covers broad shoulders, and its massive paws carry claws that score deep grooves in sandstone. When it raises its head and howls at the desert moon, its mane-fire reflects off the dunes in a ring of amber light visible from the desert's edge. It is the guardian flame of the Sand Dunes, the fire-road marker the ancient travelers prayed to.

Field note: Infernarch's roar travels exactly one league through desert air - the unit of measure was defined by its cry.

About Scytlip

Scytlip wears a round helmet-like shell over its head and carries two forward-facing claws it holds up in front of it at all times, like a tiny knight on permanent guard duty. Its eyes peer from a slit between its helmet and body with intense focus. It lives at the edges of Bug Thicket's clearings where it can watch everything at once, and it challenges anything larger than itself with a rapid claw-display - not to fight, but to demonstrate it has already thought through the fight and decided it wins. Young guardians found it inspiring.

Field note: Scytlip challenges creatures ten times its size with its full claw-display, and researchers have never once seen one back down first.

About Scythar

Scythar has grown into a proper knight's silhouette - silver plate armor across its thorax, a sword-like forward claw on one arm, and a shield-like plate claw on the other. It walks upright on four rear legs and keeps its weapon arms raised. In Bug Thicket's border disputes between creature territories, Scythar acts as a neutral adjudicator - it positions itself between conflicting creatures and holds both claws out until both parties retreat. The behavior is instinctual, not trained, and it works every single time.

Field note: Scythar has been observed peacefully resolving territorial disputes between creatures up to three times its own size - it has no record of ever needing to strike.

About Scytharos

Scytharos is a commanding armored crab-knight in full plate - helmet, pauldrons, greaves - each piece worked with golden trim that catches the thicket light like a moving shield. Its right arm carries a broad sword-claw capable of deflecting strikes meant for others, and its left holds a rounded shield-plate bearing a crest worn smooth by long use. When Scytharos enters a battle, both sides pause instinctively - something in its bearing tells every creature present that only one outcome is possible. The guardians called it the First Sworn, the creature that understood duty before duty had a word.

Field note: Scytharos's sword-claw has a distinct ring tone that carries 400 metres through Bug Thicket canopy - other creatures use the sound to navigate toward safety.

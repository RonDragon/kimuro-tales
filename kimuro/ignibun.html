About Ignibun

Ignibun is a compact red-orange rabbit with long upright ears tipped in fire-orange and bright eyes that crackle with orange-red energy. Its round body is covered in ember-warm fur, and when it hops, its ears leave tiny sparks in the air behind them. It lives in the Steel Fortress among the metal conduits and fire-vents, nesting between heated pipes where it charges its spark-ears overnight. It is the smallest and most excitable creature in the fortress.

Field note: Ignibun's ears generate enough spark to ignite a cooking fire - fortress workers carry one on cold mornings.

About Scorhare

Scorhare has grown into a powerful hare with wide flame-wing-like ears that crackle with fire and electrical discharge simultaneously. Its body courses with heat and static, and its fur stands on end in a ring around its neck like a ruff of charged bristles. It dashes through the Steel Fortress corridors at sixty kilometers per hour, leaving scorch lines on the metal floors and causing sparks to jump from every rail it passes. Fortress engineers have learned to rubber-insulate its sleeping quarters.

Field note: Scorhare's speed increases during electrical storms - fortress workers time their work to it.

About Pyreinferno

Pyreinferno is a large blazing hare whose vast flame-wings have become true fire-sails that arc and crackle with electrical discharge. Its entire body is wrapped in controlled fire, and its paws leave scorch-prints on hardened steel. When it stands at the center of the Steel Fortress and stretches its fire-wings wide, every light in the fortress flickers and the machinery hums a pitch higher. It is said to be the living battery of the fortress itself - if Pyreinferno rests, the fortress dims.

Field note: Pyreinferno rests exactly once a day for thirteen minutes - fortress workers synchronize their maintenance to that window.

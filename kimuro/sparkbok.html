About Sparkbok

Sparkbok is a small round hedgehog-type creature covered in warm orange-and-red fire-spines that crackle softly in the Granite Heights wind. Its bright blue eyes are alert and watchful, and it curls into a tight fire-ball when startled. It roots along the base of the granite boulders for heat-cracked seeds and mineral-rich grubs, using its spines to sense the warmth beneath the soil. Trainers say picking up a Sparkbok for the first time is a rite of passage - the spines spark, but never truly burn.

Field note: Sparkbok's spines crackle loudest just before sunrise - Granite Heights hikers use it as their alarm.

About Flambrok

Flambrok has grown into a robust, low-slung creature with a full coat of thick fire-and-earth-brown spines over a broad muscular body. Its four paws carry stone-hard calluses that let it walk across hot granite without slowing. In the upper passes of Granite Heights, Flambrok is the trail-cutter - it charges boulders that block the path and shatters them with a full-spine impact that sends sparks skittering across the rockface. Its passage always opens new routes.

Field note: Flambrok leaves a trail of small scorched stone-chips - guides use them to confirm the path hasn't changed since last season.

About Infernok

Infernok is a massive wide-bodied fire-earth beast whose entire upper body blazes with dense fire-spines that radiate intense heat. Its short powerful legs grip granite with crushing force, and when it lowers its head and charges, the impact is recorded as a minor tremor on Granite Heights' lower slopes. It was the guardian of the pass before the guardians themselves climbed - it cleared the route three hundred years ago and has been maintaining it ever since. The stone it clears smells of scorched flint for a full season.

Field note: Infernok charges the same boulder once every decade, wearing it down by exactly one hand-width each time.

About Shellox

Shellox is a round dome-shaped beetle the colour of baked desert sand, with a single large amber eye on its face and six stubby legs it uses to trundle purposefully across the dunes. Its shell has a smooth mounded surface that lets desert wind slide over it without catching. It buries itself to the neck during the hottest midday hours, leaving only the top of its dome visible - which looks exactly like a small sandy rock, fooling most passers-by. Desert children collect the neat round holes Shellox leaves when it emerges each dawn as trail markers.

Field note: The emergence-hole Shellox leaves in the sand is a perfectly round cylinder - desert travellers use a fresh one to estimate the morning hour.

About Carapox

Carapox has hardened its shell into segmented stone-plate armour with ridged edges that catch the wind and produce a low harmonic hum on breezy days. Its single eye has grown larger and gained a richer amber tone, and its legs are now thick and purposeful. It can roll itself into a complete sphere and tumble down dunes at remarkable speed to escape danger or to reach a cool valley faster. The guardians used the hum of its shell as a weather signal - a rising pitch meant a desert storm within the hour.

Field note: The pitch of Carapox's shell-hum rises noticeably before a sandstorm - desert villages listen for it before securing their shutters.

About Titanox

Titanox is enormous - its shell now covers the entire back of a creature the size of a small cart, with complex armour plates that have fused with actual mineral deposits from the Amber Desert floor. A ring of raised stone spines circles its shell rim and its single eye glows deep amber in the desert night. When it walks, its legs press into the sand deep enough to strike the cooler damp layer beneath, and small desert plants sprout in the footsteps it leaves behind. The guardians called it the Desert's Slow Memory - patient enough to outlast any sandstorm.

Field note: Plants grow in Titanox's footsteps within a week - desert communities plant seeds in its trail after it passes through.

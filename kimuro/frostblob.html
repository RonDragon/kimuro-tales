About Frostblob

Frostblob is a perfectly round pale-blue blobby creature with two enormous dark eyes and three tiny ice-spike tufts on top of its head. It bounces more than it walks, leaving small frost circles wherever it rests. At Frozen Peaks it clings to the wet rock faces, absorbing meltwater and reemitting it as faint prismatic breath-puffs. Children who encounter it try to catch it, but its spherical body makes it almost impossible to hold - it simply rolls free, delighted.

Field note: Frostblob's body temperature is exactly −4°C at all times, even in tropical climates - researchers cannot explain the mechanism.

About Glacierm

Glacierm has grown flatter and wider, its surface now studded with symmetrical snowflake-pattern ice spines and two large gentle eyes that glow faintly in the dark. Snowflake symbols decorate its frosted hide, and its ice spines chime when the storm wind blows. It drifts through the Peaks' lower halls on thin air currents, navigating by the direction of the storm rather than sight. Sailors sheltering in the Peaks' base caves have called it a living compass - it always points toward calm.

Field note: The ice spines on Glacierm's back chime at exactly the same pitch as the storm's eye - a tone no instrument can replicate.

About Crystallord

Crystallord sits like a monument - wide, flat-bottomed, and crowned with a perfect ring of upward ice spires that glow at their tips. Snowflake markings across its body shift and rearrange like a slow-turning kaleidoscope. Its eyes are a deep steady glow, ancient and unhurried. It arrived at Frozen Peaks before the first storm was recorded, and the Abalont texts say it does not cause storms - it stores them, holding the oldest gales inside its crown until the world is ready for them again.

Field note: Each ice spike of Crystallord's crown contains a preserved storm-current; breaking one releases a gust felt for thirty kilometers.

About Tinycara

Tinycara is a small, stocky beetle with a broad grey-green shell and six busy legs that carry it in enthusiastic arcs across the Crystal Mines floor. A small blunt horn nubs from its head - too small yet to be useful, but Tinycara uses it to poke at crystal formations anyway, apparently checking if anything rattles loose. Its eyes are wide and slightly too far apart, giving it a look of permanent mild surprise at everything the mines contain.

Field note: Tinycara tests every crystal it finds by tapping it once with its horn - if it rings, the beetle marks the spot with a small scratch and returns daily for a week.

About Thorapyx

Thorapyx has grown a proper horn - swept back and broad, almost as wide as its head, and notched from where it has hammered at mine walls. Its grey-green shell has thickened with ribbed armour segments, and six powerful legs carry it with surprising speed through the mine's lower tunnels. It digs for crystals not by breaking them but by undermining the surrounding stone in precise oval trenches - a method that leaves crystals whole and free. Crystal miners find cleaned specimens where Thorapyx has worked the night before.

Field note: Thorapyx excavates around crystals without touching them - every specimen recovered from its work sites is unscratched, regardless of crystal fragility.

About Colossarapyx

Colossarapyx is a hulking, ridge-backed titan whose entire body is a landscape of interlocking steel-green armour plates, glowing green crystal-inlays visible in the deepest plate grooves, and a sweeping multi-ridged horn that has punched through solid granite. It is the unquestioned authority of Crystal Mines' deepest passages - not because it fights, but because nothing has ever found a reason to challenge something so completely, obviously armoured. It keeps the deepest crystal chambers clear of collapse by reinforcing walls with the weight of its own body.

Field note: The crystal inlays in Colossarapyx's shell grow over its lifetime from the minerals it absorbs tunnelling - no two adults share the same crystal pattern.

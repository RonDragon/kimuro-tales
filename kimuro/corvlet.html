About Corvlet

Corvlet is a very small, extremely round crow-chick, jet-black and fluffy, with stubby wings tucked against its sides and two wide amber eyes that glow slightly in the dark. It has barely any neck - just a ball of dark feathers with a beak sticking out. In Ghost Hollow it perches on fallen memory-stones and stares at them for long stretches, as if reading something adults can no longer see. Every so often it pecks once, lightly, like placing a punctuation mark.

Field note: Corvlet pecks once on any surface it studies for more than two minutes - ghost-hollow scholars say it is marking a word it has decided to remember.

About Corvawk

Corvawk has grown into an alert, angular crow with sleek black feathers edged in a deep navy sheen, bright gold-amber eyes, and a crest of four stiff feathers at the back of its head. Its wings are fully developed and it rides the Ghost Hollow's cold wind-columns with minimal effort. It collects objects - buttons, pebbles, bits of old text - and arranges them in patterns outside its roost that, if you squint at the right angle, match the old guardian sigils.

Field note: Corvawk's ground-arrangements outside its roost have been copied by three scholars; each one matches a different guardian sigil.

About Corvulture

Corvulture is a massive, imposing dark raptor with a wide wingspan marked by amber lightning-bolt patterns on the underside of each wing. Its feathers are dense black and its neck-ruff is thick as a collar of shadow. At rest it hunches forward with closed wings, looking like a carved gargoyle. When it spreads its wings in Ghost Hollow the hollow wind pauses for a moment - every creature in it freezes - then begins again carrying its voice. It remembers every voice raised in the hollow since the guardians left.

Field note: When Corvulture spreads its wings, all wind in Ghost Hollow pauses for exactly three seconds before resuming.

About Sparkling

Sparkling is a chubby, wide-faced draconic axolotl with vivid blue scales, small but sturdy limbs, and a yellow mane-crest that crackles with faint static between its spines. Its wide-set round eyes carry a cheerful, unbothered expression. It shuffles through the Steel Fortress's water-cooling channels and drainage troughs, pausing frequently to zap small floating debris with its mane-static and watch it spin. Fortress technicians adore it unconditionally despite its habit of shocking anyone who reaches for it without warning.

Field note: Sparkling's mane-static recharges fully after a short swim in cold running water - it returns to the coolant channels after every zap like a creature who has found the perfect resource loop.

About Voltlotl

Voltlotl (axolotl line) has grown into a long-bodied draconic lizard with vivid blue scales, spined back-crest, and powerful four-legged stance. Its mane-crest has matured into full electric spines that arc between tips continuously. It moves fast along the Fortress's wall-rails and overhead struts, scaling them with suction-tipped claws, and has developed a sharp tactical intelligence - it has been observed redirecting power fault-currents away from sensitive equipment by placing itself as a bridge. Engineers credit it with preventing three major Fortress fires.

Field note: Voltlotl (axolotl line) can climb any metal surface at full running speed - the ceiling included - and has been photographed sleeping upside-down from the Fortress's main archway.

About Stormlotl

Stormlotl stands at full height as a formidable blue-scaled dragon, four powerful limbs braced wide, electric spines forming a continuous crown along neck and back. Lightning arcs between every spine-tip in a permanent crown of electricity visible across the entire Fortress. Its gaze is sharp and calculating. It controls the Fortress's upper-tier power-routing with apparent intelligence - operators discovered that the most efficient power distribution patterns were not what they had programmed, but what Stormlotl had rerouted. They have since stopped reprogramming and let it run the grid.

Field note: Stormlotl's spine-crown produces enough ambient charge to power the Fortress's secondary lighting system indefinitely without drawing from the primary grid.

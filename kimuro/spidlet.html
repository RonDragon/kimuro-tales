About Spidlet

Spidlet is a round sandy-tan spider with a broad domed abdomen, eight moderate legs, and three pairs of glowing deep-red eyes arranged across its face. Its earth-toned body blends perfectly with Ghost Hollow's dead-leaf floor. The multiple red eyes give it eerie all-angle vision - trainers say you can never quite catch it looking at you, because it's always already looking. It spins short dense webs low to the ground, using them as tripwire alarms that wake it when anything larger than a moth approaches.

Field note: Spidlet has six pairs of eyes that never all point the same direction; it has never been successfully startled in recorded history.

About Arachnode

Arachnode has grown into a wide low-slung brown spider with a large flat abdomen that presses close to the ground for maximum stealth. All six of its red eyes now glow with an active crimson intelligence. It constructs complex layered web networks across Ghost Hollow's root systems - not visible webs, but vibration-reading ones, buried just under the leaf litter. Creatures that walk through Ghost Hollow on Arachnode's route feel as if something is watching from every direction at once, because it is.

Field note: Arachnode's underground vibration-web can identify a creature by its footstep weight alone - it has never misidentified in documented tests.

About Toxaranyx

Toxaranyx is a massive dark-stone spider armored in rough rocky-textured chitin plates the color of dried mud and shadow. It carries six sharp crimson eyes and thick blade-tipped legs that leave deep grooves in Ghost Hollow's earth when it walks. Its web is so dense in the deepest hollow that even light bends around it slightly. Ancient Hollow records describe Toxaranyx as 'the hollow's memory' - it has lived so long in the dark that it has become part of it, patient as stone, as difficult to move.

Field note: Toxaranyx has not moved from its central hollow in an estimated eighty years - everything it needs simply comes to it, eventually.

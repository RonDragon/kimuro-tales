About Scalion

Scalion is a tiny silver-grey pangolin-like creature at its first stage, with a single oversized disc-scale on its head like a helmet visor and a curling tail. One bright blue eye peeks out from beneath the disc with intense curiosity. It moves cautiously but precisely through the Steel Fortress, using the disc on its head to deflect any debris that falls, and its curling tail to anchor itself in narrow passages. Guardians say every child who first enters the fortress sees a Scalion sitting near the gate - and that the Scalion always seems to recognize the ones who are going to make it.

Field note: Scalion's head-disc is already harder than tempered steel at birth - the fortress smiths tested one and broke three of their best tools on it.

About Platynox

Platynox has grown into a proper scaled lizard with a broad flat head and a body covered in overlapping steel-bright plates. Its single blue eye is alert and intelligent. It moves low and fast through the Fortress corridors, scouting ahead of whatever guardian it has chosen to follow that day. Its plates are not just armor - when Platynox is excited about a word it has just learned, the plates shift and reflect light in a rippling pattern that veteran trainers can read like a language. It is, they say, the most honest creature in the Fortress because it literally shows its feelings.

Field note: Platynox's plate-ripple pattern is different for every creature it meets - trainers have documented over two hundred distinct ripples.

About Aerodactox

Aerodactox stands upright, a gleaming armored lizard-knight with a spiraling tail, plated limbs, and the brilliant blue eye now blazing with accumulated intelligence. Its body is a fortress in itself - layered metal-bright scales over powerful muscle, built for both speed and impact. It guards the Steel Fortress's memory-vault with absolute dedication, and the memories it has gathered over its long life fill its armor like light fills glass. Forged-tough and loyal as the guardians themselves, it believes courage is built one word at a time - and it has been watching to see which children are building it seriously.

Field note: Aerodactox has memorized every voice ever raised within the Fortress walls - it can quote any conversation from any point in its lifetime, perfectly.

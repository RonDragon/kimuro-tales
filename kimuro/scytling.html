About Scytling

Scytling is a pale round crab with large liquid eyes and small neat claws it keeps close to its body. Its shell has a soft texture that catches the light like fine sand, and it moves with a gentle rocking walk that makes it look perpetually uncertain. It lives under the wide leaves of Verdant Valley's ground-cover plants, emerging to feed on fallen song-seeds. Its large eyes see in multiple directions at once, and it has the endearing habit of going completely still and staring at things that interest it for up to two minutes without blinking.

Field note: Scytling can remain perfectly motionless for two full minutes while staring - valley children hold contests to see who can make one blink first.

About Scytsting

Scytsting has grown a rough-textured golden-tan shell and developed substantial claws that it uses to dig deep burrows in Verdant Valley's softer earth. Its body has taken on an angular roughness that makes it look like a piece of valley stone that grew legs. It is strongly territorial about its burrow entrance, decorating it with a specific arrangement of pebbles it changes each season. Removing a pebble from the arrangement sends Scytsting into a visible agitated display - though it never leaves its entrance to chase the offender.

Field note: Scytsting rearranges the pebbles at its burrow entrance precisely 24 hours after any stone is removed or added - as if correcting a record.

About Scytarch

Scytarch is a large, wide golden crab whose flat shell stretches broad as a cart wheel. Its thick claws are built for leverage rather than speed, capable of shifting boulders with sustained pressure. It has lived long enough in Verdant Valley that its shell has developed mineral deposits matching the valley's own rock chemistry - making it genuinely difficult to tell from a large valley boulder at rest. Young valley creatures learn to navigate around the 'boulders that breathe,' and the first time they see one move is considered a rite of passage. The guardians placed Scytarch in the valley's foundation myths as the first creature to stake a claim.

Field note: Scytarch's shell mineral content is chemically identical to Verdant Valley bedrock - field tests cannot distinguish them without biological markers.

About Embhorn

Embhorn is a small, stocky dragon-antelope hatchling with deep brown-red scales, three short curved horn-nubs on its brow, and small bat-like wings folded tight to its back. It moves on all fours with a curious, rooting behavior - pressing its horned head against the volcanic rocks of the Dragon Spire as if listening for something. The faint warmth it radiates is comforting rather than dangerous. Guardians at the Spire say an Embhorn in the nesting chamber signals calm weather; when they scatter, check the volcano's pressure gauges.

Field note: Embhorn's horns glow faintly orange twenty minutes before a volcanic pressure event - the Spire's guardians use it as their primary early-warning system.

About Magmask

Magmask has grown into a proper powerful dragon-antelope hybrid, its four curved horns now fully formed and glowing orange at the tips, and its wings developed enough for short gliding leaps. Its dark brown-red scales have thickened and its face bears a mask-like darker patch around the eyes that gives it an intense, focused expression. It charges across the Dragon Spire's rocky terraces with tremendous speed, using its horns to test the terrain before committing weight. The magma channels of the Spire seem to open slightly when Magmask passes - as if the volcano recognizes its kind.

Field note: Magmask's wing-leaps carry it sixty meters without touching the ground - it uses the volcanic updrafts the way a sailor uses wind.

About Scaldhorn

Scaldhorn is a magnificent dragon of the Dragon Spire, its body deep brown-red and black with a full eruption of horn-spikes along the crown and spine, wings wide enough to shadow a courtyard, and streams of molten orange running from the base of each horn like lava tributaries. Its four legs are armored with layered fire-scale and its eyes burn steady amber. When it spreads its wings at the Spire's peak, every fire in the region brightens for a moment in response. The guardians say Scaldhorn was the first to answer when the Spire's ancient dragon memory-vault needed a warden - it arrived before it was asked.

Field note: When Scaldhorn spreads its wings at the Spire's summit, every flame in the region brightens simultaneously - the guardians call it the Dragon Salute.

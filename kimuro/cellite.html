About Cellite

Cellite is a small, translucent jellyfish with a pale blue dome and a single large deep-purple eye at its centre, through which it watches everything at once. Four delicate tentacles trail beneath it as it drifts through Poison Marsh's still waters, pulsing gently. It communicates by changing the bioluminescent patterns in its dome - quick flickers for surprise, slow pulses for contentment. Marsh travellers use Cellite light patterns as a weather guide; three long pulses means the water ahead is safe.

Field note: Cellite's eye never fully closes - even in deep rest, the pupil continues slow tracking movements, as though watching things the rest of us cannot see.

About Cellix

Cellix has grown into a fuller, rounder jellyfish with a glowing blue dome and a brilliant purple eye whose iris has widened to nearly fill it. Six tentacles spiral below it, trailing light in lazy curves as it drifts. It radiates a mild psychic warmth that nearby creatures find calming - arguing parties in Poison Marsh will often halt and look around, confused, when a Cellix drifts between them. It feeds on the dark energies dissolved in the marsh water and leaves behind something cleaner.

Field note: Patches of Poison Marsh within ten metres of a Cellix bloom have measurably lower toxin concentrations - the creature is apparently filtering as it drifts.

About Cellumarch

Cellumarch is magnificent and strange - a great spreading jellyfish whose luminous dome pulses with deep psychic violet, ringed with pink and blue aurora-frills, and whose many long tentacles trail in spiralling curtains of cold light. Its vast purple eye is the fixed centre of everything around it, and creatures nearby feel gently observed in the way one feels observed by something ancient and patient that has decided you are probably harmless. In Poison Marsh's deepest channels, Cellumarch drifts the old routes where the guardians once taught water-songs, and the marsh itself seems calmer for its presence.

Field note: Cellumarch's full bioluminescent display has been documented drawing creatures from over a kilometre away, all of which eventually leave again looking inexplicably at peace.

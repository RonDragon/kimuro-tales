About Noctidlet

Noctidlet is a small purple crab with a luminous crescent moon symbol on its back and wide, curious eyes that pick up the faintest traces of ghost energy drifting through Ghost Hollow. It scuttles sideways in the leaf-fall, pinching at shadows to test whether they are real or just illusions left behind by the Hollow's restless spirits. Its little claws are more curious than fierce - it pinches once to say hello and twice to say goodbye.

Field note: Noctidlet can tell a real shadow from a ghost-shadow by pinching it - real shadows do not pinch back.

About Noctidus

Noctidus has grown a broad dark shell spangled with glowing star-spots, and its horns have curved into twin crescents that frame its face like a crown. Its large star-patterned claws now carry real psychic weight - objects near Noctidus sometimes drift an inch off the ground when it concentrates. It prowls the central paths of Ghost Hollow at dusk, arranging fallen bones and trinkets into patterns that nobody has fully decoded yet.

Field note: The star-spots on Noctidus's shell rearrange themselves each night - no two mornings show the same pattern.

About Noctidemon

Noctidemon is encased in a massive star-flecked shell bristling with jagged golden spines, and its claws are enormous, crackling with psychic energy that bends the air around them. Its eyes - now a fierce glowing yellow - have seen every ghost that ever wandered Ghost Hollow, and it has spoken to all of them. It does not hunt; it simply sits at the Hollow's heart, holding the boundary between the living world and whatever lies beyond, as patient and immovable as the oldest grave.

Field note: Touching a Noctidemon's spine causes vivid flashbacks of memories you have never personally lived.

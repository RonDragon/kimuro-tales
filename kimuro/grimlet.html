About Grimlet

Grimlet is a round dark armadillo whose black-grey armored shell is segmented like stacked rings, and whose red eyes glow faintly in the dim corners it favors. It tucks into Granite Heights' shadowed overhangs, perfectly still, watching every approaching thing with unhurried calculation. It does not run from threats - it rolls into a tight ball, armor interlocking so precisely that nothing can find a gap. Guardians note that Grimlet always knows someone is watching even before the watcher has decided to watch.

Field note: Grimlet's shell-segments seal together so completely when rolled that even water cannot penetrate - researchers have tested this in streams.

About Magrum

Magrum is built low and wide - its armored body has expanded into thick overlapping dark plates with faint red seams, and wisps of dark shadow-smoke curl off its flanks. Its snout is flat and heavy, its red eyes now burning brighter. In Granite Heights it occupies the areas where rock overhangs and deep gullies create permanent shadow, and it moves through these with unexpected ease, its broad low profile fitting every gap. It is one of the few creatures that Abalont's agents have never managed to drive from its territory - it simply ignores them.

Field note: Magrum's shadow-smoke is denser on its left side than its right - no one has found out why, but it has been true in every specimen observed.

About Infermagrim

Infermagrim is a fortress that breathes. Its body is an architecture of massive black rock-plates layered in overlapping rows, each one shot through with deep crimson lines like cooling magma, and dark shadow-smoke pours from between the seams of every plate constantly. Its face is almost entirely obscured by forward-angled plate-armor, and its eyes are slits of red in the dark. Granite Heights' older guardian records call it the mountain's immune system - the thing the stone sends out when it decides something does not belong. It cannot be pushed. It cannot be lured. It simply arrives, and waits.

Field note: The plates on Infermagrim's back have never been seen separating - not even during documented battles. It may have permanently fused them.

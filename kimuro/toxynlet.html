About Toxynlet

Toxynlet is a gentle-looking purple-and-green snake with round eyes and an open, curious mouth - it raises its head high and sways left to right when something catches its attention. Two thin green stripes mark its body from head to tail, and its scales shimmer softly in the volcanic light of Dragon Spire. Though small, it has already developed a mild venom, and it practices its strike on fallen leaves, rehearsing the motion the way a young scholar rehearses unfamiliar names.

Field note: Toxynlet raises exactly one-third of its body off the ground to survey its surroundings - no more, no less, every single time.

About Venomyx

Venomyx rears up with a full hooded frill ringed by sharp purple spines, each one a secondary venom delivery point. Its scales have deepened to a rich teal-and-purple gradient, and its eyes have gone narrow and calculating. It patrols the Dragon Spire's basalt ledges with the patience of a sentry that has memorized every shadow. When it detects an intruder, it inflates its hood slowly - a display that takes a full ten seconds and is meant to give the intruder time to leave.

Field note: Venomyx always inflates its hood three times before striking - researchers say it is giving itself permission.

About Dracotoxus

Dracotoxus is a great coiled serpent draped in overlapping jade and violet scales, its immense body ringed by thick armour-like ridges. Its full hood bristles with long blade-like spines, and when fully spread it blots out the light behind it. Green-tinged venom seeps from the tips of its fangs constantly, pooling in the volcanic rock below. At Dragon Spire it is one of the oldest living presences - the guardians believe it was here before the Spire had a name, and it will be here long after.

Field note: The largest recorded Dracotoxus was measured at exactly the same length three separate times, five years apart - it had simply stopped growing.

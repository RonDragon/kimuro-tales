About Terraling

Terraling is a sleek golden-brown ferret-like creature with flowing swirl markings across its entire body - natural earth-runes that shimmer faintly in the right light. Its long body moves through the Dragon Spire's narrow rock passages with liquid ease, and its curious bright eyes miss nothing. The swirl marks are believed to be a kind of passive map - as Terraling explores, the marks shift subtly, recording the terrain. Dragon Spire residents have learned that a Terraling moving purposefully in a particular direction means there is something worth finding there.

Field note: The swirl patterns on Terraling's coat shift position slightly each time it visits a new tunnel it has not mapped before.

About Terrakind

Terrakind has grown upright into a thicker, more imposing form with a broad chest and a crown of gold-tipped earth spikes running from head to tail ridge. Its swirl markings have deepened and now glow a warm gold when it calls on its heroic energy. It patrols the Dragon Spire's mid-level ledges with the deliberate authority of a warden, stopping at key lookout points to survey the terrain below. Dragon hatchlings follow Terrakind along the spire paths - not because they are told to, but because Terrakind seems to know exactly where all the dangers are.

Field note: Dragon hatchlings raised near a Terrakind are statistically far less likely to fall from spire ledges - as if they absorbed its path-sense.

About Terrakolos

Terrakolos is a massive, lion-bodied creature with a full mane of flowing earth-brown ropes that drift like roots or slow lava, and a face of deeply scored swirl markings that have become more symbol than skin. It moves through the Dragon Spire's heights with the ground-anchored certainty of something that has claimed every square meter of it. Where it walks, small plants push up through rock cracks within days. The dragons of the spire defer to it - not out of fear, but recognition. Terrakolos was here before the dragons, and will be here after, and both parties understand this perfectly.

Field note: Plants begin sprouting through solid rock within three days wherever Terrakolos walks - even where nothing has grown before.

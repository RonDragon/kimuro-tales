About Sparkfang

Sparkfang is a compact dark-grey wolf pup with a red-tipped spiky mane beginning to form around the neck. Its eyes are bold red, and its short tail has a small flicker of dark flame at the tip. It was born deep in the Shadow Caves where no light reaches and grew up navigating by heat rather than sight. It nips at the heels of older wolves and barks short fire-sparks when startled by an echo.

Field note: Sparkfang marks the walls of the Shadow Caves with tiny scorch prints from its paws - its own trail map.

About Infernyx

Infernyx stands tall and lean with a full spiky red-and-black mane flaring from head to mid-back. Dark markings stripe its grey-black fur, and its eyes burn a hot red. It prowls the middle passages of the Shadow Caves, where fire-veins run through the rock and heat rises from the floor. Its roar splits into twin echoes that travel down both forks of every tunnel simultaneously, confusing everything except its own pack.

Field note: Infernyx can tell which cave tunnel leads upward purely by the smell of cooling stone - it has never taken the wrong fork.

About Blazeclaw

Blazeclaw is a massive wolf with a full mane of jagged fire-spikes running head to tail, its body built like a moving boulder. Broad chest, wide-set paws like iron, and a tail that sweeps dark fire in broad arcs behind it. The deepest chamber of the Shadow Caves holds its heat signature years after it passes through. Travelers who have seen it describe the sound: a deep tectonic snarl that vibrates the walls and shakes loose stalactites from the roof.

Field note: Blazeclaw's passage heats the stone walls of any tunnel for a full day - old explorers use this warmth to map safe routes.

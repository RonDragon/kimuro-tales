About Sparkemice

Sparkemice is a puff-round blue-white mouse whose fur carries a faint frost pattern that thickens on cold mornings. Its tiny round ears are almost too big for its head, and small blue musical-note sparks drift from its cheeks whenever it hums - which is often. In Frozen Peaks' tide pools it shelters under overhangs just above the waterline, attracted to the chill of sea spray and the static charge left behind by lightning-touched waves. It collects single sounds the way others collect pebbles: one at a time, treasured.

Field note: Sparkemice hums a two-note tune when content; trainers say each note changes pitch with each new sound it learns.

About Volteek

Volteek has grown a spiky crown of pale blue fur along its neck and shoulders, and a single bold blue lightning-bolt marking runs from its ear to its flank. Its round shape has become lean and alert, and its eyes carry a sharpness that misses nothing. When it stamps a back foot on a hard surface, a ring of frost spreads outward and tiny sparks leap from the tips of its spiky fur. In Frozen Peaks, Volteek stakes out the highest tide-pool ledge and guards it fiercely, letting no other creature pass without a sound.

Field note: Volteek's foot-stamp creates a frost ring exactly one body-length in radius - it stamps twice as a warning, three times to charge.

About Stormion

Stormion is a four-legged predator with the full heavy build of a storm-season wave - broad chest, thick legs, and a mane of crackling blue-white electric fur that stands out like a blizzard halo around its shoulders and neck. Ice crystals form on the ground wherever it walks, and the electric mane sheds sparks continuously with a sound like snapping glass. In Frozen Peaks it patrols the boundary between the warm shallows and the cold deep water, the one living landmark that separates the two - old enough to remember before the boundary was drawn.

Field note: The temperature drops measurably three steps before Stormion arrives - its electric mane flash-freezes the moisture in the air.

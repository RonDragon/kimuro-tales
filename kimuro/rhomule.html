About Rhomule

Rhomule is a compact, armored rhino cub whose body is already segmented like a suit of plate armor in cool grey-violet steel, with purple-tinted banding between the plates. A small blunt horn sits above its snout. It wobbles slightly when it walks - the armor is growing faster than the creature inside, and there are always gaps at the joints where dark energy leaks through in faint wisps. In the Steel Fortress it follows older Rhomites through the forges like a shadow, fascinated by heat but too sensible to touch anything until it is cool.

Field note: Rhomule's plate gaps widen when it is frightened, letting out small puffs of dark energy - fortress workers use it as an early intruder alarm.

About Rhomarch

Rhomarch has grown into a dangerous-looking beast, its segmented steel-grey armor now covering most of its body in overlapping plates edged with deep purple. Multiple horn-spikes protrude from its head and back. Dark energy bleeds from its joints in thin continuous trails. It patrols the fortress walls with a methodical, heavy tread, and the sound of its plates shifting has been used for generations as the unofficial timekeeping clock of the Steel Fortress - three plate-clicks means the watch has changed. Nothing has ever successfully broken through a wall that Rhomarch was actively patrolling.

Field note: The shifting of Rhomarch's plates makes a sound so precise that fortress engineers synchronize their clocks to its patrol rhythm.

About Rhomithor

Rhomithor is a leviathan of dark-forged steel, its body encased in massive overlapping armor slabs that creak as it moves, each one etched with battle records in the Fortress's own script. Three great horns crown its head. Dark energy pours continuously from every joint in slow heavy streams. The ground cracks where it stands still for too long. Abalont sent his strongest champion against the Steel Fortress gates once; Rhomithor lowered its head, took three steps, and the champion was not seen again. The guardians keep its stall door made of the strongest alloy in the region - more out of respect than necessity.

Field note: The cracks in the Steel Fortress's outer courtyard were all made by Rhomithor during a single practice charge - it has never charged the walls themselves.

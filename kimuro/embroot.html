About Embroot

Embroot is a round orange-red bulb sitting on a ring of dark volcanic rocks, with a crown of flaming orange petals-and-tendrils erupting from its top like a sunburst. It cannot move its body but it can rotate its flame-petal crown in a slow full circle, which it does whenever it senses something interesting approaching. The lava crater's youngest guides learn direction from Embroot clusters - the flame-petals always lean slightly toward the nearest active vent, no matter which way the surface wind blows.

Field note: Embroot petals always point toward the nearest underground lava flow, even through fifty meters of solid rock.

About Lavamaw

Lavamaw has unfurled into a taller volcanic creature, its orange-red body now mounted on a rocky base and sprouting several long lava-tendril arms that wave and reach independently of each other. Each tendril glows at its tip and can probe the rock for heat or cracks. In the crater's mid-zone, Lavamaw tendrils sometimes grow up through rock fissures and surprise travelers who step on what looks like solid ground. The guardians use Lavamaw's fissure-sense to map the safest walking routes through the crater's unstable surface.

Field note: Lavamaw extends its longest tendril in the direction of structural weakness, so miners follow its reach before striking with a pick.

About Infernoflora

Infernoflora has become a broad volcanic cone-creature sitting on a wide platform of hardened lava rocks, its multiple tendril-arms extended outward like the roots of a great fire-tree, each one thick as a beam. Its interior glows visibly through cracks in its rocky outer shell, and when it contracts its arms the lava inside pulses brighter. In the lava crater it is the anchor - the one creature that never moves, never needs to. The crater's entire ecology orbits it; even the youngest Embroot clusters lean toward it before they lean toward the vents.

Field note: Infernoflora produces a low sub-frequency hum once every twelve hours; every living thing in a hundred-meter radius pauses for exactly three seconds.

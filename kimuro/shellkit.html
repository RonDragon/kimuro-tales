About Shellkit

A little upright tortoise-shaped creature with a glossy burnt-orange shell marked in a classic hex-tile pattern, Shellkit marches through the Crystal Mines clutching its smooth round shell against its round belly like a prize. Its wide-set eyes glare with frowning determination and its stubby arms stick forward as it waddles, ready to bump anything that gets in its way. Shellkit is a crystal collector - it nudges loose gems into a corner and sits on them to keep them warm, apparently convinced they need hatching. Mine workers find this both endearing and maddening.

Field note: Shellkit has been observed sitting atop the same cluster of uncut rubies for fourteen consecutive days, convinced they are eggs.

About Terravern

Terravern has grown into a broad, powerful turtle-form with a heavily ridged and spurred shell that catches and scatters crystal-mine light in orange and brown glints. It moves on four solid legs now, head low and purposeful, excavating new crystal seams with steady sweeping claw-strokes. Mine workers follow Terravern's tunnel-trails because they reliably lead to the richest deposits - its sense for crystal beds is faultless. Old mining logs describe Terravern as the best geological instrument ever discovered: patient, accurate, and willing to work for shiny pebbles.

Field note: Terravern's tunnel-trails average twelve per cent higher crystal yield than any mine-surveyor's path across all recorded Crystal Mines seasons.

About Megalord

Megalord is a great ridged titan-tortoise, its enormous spiked shell standing taller than a grown guardian, scarred by centuries of crystal-mine tunnels and still gleaming beneath the scratches in warm ochre and dark sienna. Its great jaws can crush mineral veins that stump iron pickaxes. It moves with the patience of geology itself - it has tunnelled the same Crystal Mines route for so long that the tunnel walls on either side have curved to accommodate its exact shape. Ancient mine records say Megalord was there before the first guardian lit their first mining-lamp.

Field note: Megalord has worn a body-shaped groove into the widest Crystal Mines passage; new miners learn the layout by following the groove.

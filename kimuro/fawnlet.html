About Fawnlet

Fawnlet is a delicate, tawny-coated fawn with wide sky-blue eyes and two tiny nubs where antlers will someday grow. Its slender legs end in neat dark hooves, and a small tuft of lighter fur sits between its ears like a soft snowflake. In the Dark Forest it picks its way along ice-edged paths, and its blue gaze flickers when it senses a forgotten word nearby - it tilts its head and stamps once before moving toward it.

Field note: Fawnlet always stamps its right front hoof exactly once when it discovers something hidden under the snow.

About Cervain

Cervain carries a full rack of branched antlers dusted with frost and stands tall on strong legs striped with rich brown. Its coat has thickened into layered bands of tan and chestnut, and two pale patches glow faintly on its chest. In the Dark Forest it walks the border paths between shadow and cleared ground, using its antler tines to scratch runes into frozen bark - each rune a word it has guarded through the long winter.

Field note: Cervain carves one new rune into a frozen tree each full moon; scholars count the marks to read a winter's hidden history.

About Frostarch

Frostarch is a magnificent beast clad in deep blue-white ice-patterns etched across its flanks like ancient script, its enormous branching antlers trailing crystalline wisps of cold light. Where it stands, frost flowers bloom on the ground in rings, and the air hums with a low psychic resonance that quiets every creature within hearing. It is said to be the memory-keeper of the Dark Forest, holding every voice ever raised in its antlers - frozen, waiting to thaw.

Field note: A ring of frost flowers 30 paces wide marks any spot where Frostarch has stood still for more than an hour.

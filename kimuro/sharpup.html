About Sharpup

Sharpup is a chubby little shark with navy-blue and yellow colouring and an oversized grinning jaw it can barely close. Its pectoral fins stick out like stubby wings and its eyes are wide and bright with mischief. It bobs near the surface of Coral Bay stealing shiny pebbles and bits of coral from other creatures' hoards. Trainers describe it as the reef's least-threatening scoundrel - it wants to look fierce but mostly ends up looking pleased with itself.

Field note: Sharpup cannot resist a shiny object - it will abandon a battle mid-fight if something glints on the seafloor.

About Sharkaw

Sharkaw is now a full-sized powerful shark with a navy-and-gold body and a jaw lined with rows of sharp teeth it displays freely. Dorsal and pectoral fins cut the water cleanly and its dark eyes carry a calculating gleam. It circles the deeper reef channels of Coral Bay, tolerating no trespassing in its claimed territory. The guardians noted it memorizes the location of every reef creature it encounters - it returns to check on the same spot at the same hour each day, as if keeping attendance.

Field note: Sharkaw patrols exactly the same route at the same time daily - local divers set their watches by its passing.

About Abyssjaw

Abyssjaw has grown beyond shark into something closer to a dragon of the deep - massive legs have pushed through its flanks, its jaw has widened to an enormous rend of gold-and-dark teeth, and its yellow eyes burn with predatory intelligence. It moves along the bottom of Coral Bay in a slow prowl, stirring up columns of sand that rise like towers. Ancient charts drawn by the guardians show the entire bay shaped around where Abyssjaw chooses to rest, as though the reef grew to accommodate it.

Field note: The sand towers Abyssjaw raises as it walks sink back down in exactly the shape of letters - researchers are still decoding them.

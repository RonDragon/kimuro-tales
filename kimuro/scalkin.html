About Scalkin

Scalkin is a round, small pangolin-type creature with warm earthy brown scales and a short blunt snout pressed permanently against the ground. It noses at every surface it crosses, tasting the mineral content of stone and metal alike. In the Steel Fortress it gravitates to the oldest foundations, where the earth beneath the steel is deepest, and it has been known to dig out small pockets that later turn out to be stress points in the stonework. The fortress engineers learned quickly to follow Scalkin's digging - wherever it excavates on its own initiative, something needs reinforcing.

Field note: Every spot Scalkin has voluntarily excavated in the fortress has turned out to conceal a structural weakness - its record is perfect.

About Scalisaur

Scalisaur is a substantial pangolin-lizard with overlapping brown scales that have begun to grey and harden toward steel at the spine. Its body is long and low, moving with a steady rolling gait. It has developed a habit of pressing its entire flank against walls for minutes at a time - not resting, but listening. Other creatures in the Fortress have learned to watch where Scalisaur leans, because it always identifies the wall that needs attention next. Trainers say its bond with the earth beneath the steel is so deep that it processes soil composition the way others process language - instantly and intuitively.

Field note: Scalisaur can identify the mineral content of any wall in the fortress just by pressing against it for thirty seconds - and it is never wrong.

About Metallisk

Metallisk is a broad, ancient-looking pangolin whose scales have fully converted to true steel-grey metal, edged along every plate with sharp silver points. Its body is as wide as it is long, and its tail can be used as a counterweight when it braces for impact. The earth beneath its feet actually shapes around it - over years of walking the same fortress paths, it has worn grooves in the stone that other creatures use as drainage channels. When Abalont's forces tried to undermine the fortress foundations, Metallisk simply lay flat and held the earth together with its body weight for three days while repairs were made.

Field note: The grooves Metallisk has worn in the fortress floor over decades now function as the fortress's entire drainage system - no engineering required.

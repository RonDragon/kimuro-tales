About Slugmaw

Slugmaw is a plump pale slug with purple-and-white stripes running lengthwise along its soft body and two gentle eyestalks that bob as it moves. It glides on a silver foot-trail across the mossy floors of the Light Grove, leaving a faint shimmering path that catches the morning light. Its trail has mild psychic residue in it - small creatures that follow a Slugmaw path often arrive feeling unexpectedly cheerful. It is drawn to the sound of unfamiliar names being spoken, gathering silently under the trees where children practise their lessons.

Field note: The trail Slugmaw leaves glows faintly for one hour - children in the Light Grove use fresh trails as reading lamps for dawn lessons.

About Gastroplex

Gastroplex has grown a small ridged spiral shell on its back that it cannot retreat into but uses as an amplifier for the psychic pulses it now emits deliberately. The purple stripes on its body have deepened and tiny frilled edges have appeared along its sides that it fans to slow itself on downhill slopes. It moves through the Light Grove with gentle authority, its trail now broad enough to walk along comfortably. The grove's song-trees lean slightly toward any path it has recently crossed, as though the soil carries an afterglow.

Field note: Song-trees in the Light Grove lean measurably toward Gastroplex's old trail-paths - grove-keepers reroute them to shape the trees.

About Gastromorph

Gastromorph has transformed into something extraordinary - a large upright form trailing sweeping frilled tentacles that billow around it like a living skirt of purple and white. Its eyestalks have lengthened dramatically and carry a deep violet glow. The frills pulse with psychic light and the air around Gastromorph shimmers at the edges, bending light slightly the way heat does over hot sand. In the Light Grove it is regarded as a philosopher-creature - it moves to wherever an argument is being had and sits quietly until the participants reach agreement on their own.

Field note: Arguments near Gastromorph rarely last more than twelve minutes - the purple light seems to make sharp edges go soft.

About Noctylet

Noctylet is a small pale blue-white owl wreathed in electric blue ghost-flame that flickers whenever a gust passes through Gale Ridge's upper ledges. Its eyes glow a steady cool blue, and it carries itself with the slightly confused dignity of a creature that knows it is important but is still figuring out why. It perches on the highest antennae of the Ridge's old lightning conductors and calls into the storm, learning each thunderhead's name one at a time.

Field note: Noctylet's ghost-flame doubles in size in the thirty seconds before a lightning strike it cannot yet see.

About Noctavis

Noctavis has grown powerful wings edged in crackling blue energy, and the ghost-flame around its body has intensified into a constant corona that dims all other light within arm's reach. A bold thunderbolt symbol now blazes on its chest - earned, the Ridge's oldest records say, when Noctavis flew into the eye of the Ridge's greatest recorded storm and came out changed but calm. It rides storm thermals at speeds that make it look like a streak of blue lightning from below.

Field note: Noctavis can predict the exact second a lightning bolt will land - it positions itself one wingspan away, every time.

About Noctyryx

Noctyryx is a great blue raptor ringed in fierce electric fire, wings spread wide enough to shadow an entire market square from above. Its tail fans out in brilliant blue feathers that crackle with continuous psychic lightning, and when it screams, the sound arrives before the visual - it travels at the speed of thought. Gale Ridge's legends say Noctyryx appeared the first time Abalont's shadow reached the heights, and it has kept watch there ever since, too proud of its sky to share it with darkness.

Field note: Noctyryx's scream has been measured arriving 0.8 seconds before the creature itself does - something physics professors quietly file away.

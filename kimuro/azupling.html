About Azupling

Azupling is a round, wide-eyed blue chick with fluffy white belly-down and the oversized beak of a bird that fully intends to grow into it someday. Its tiny wing-stubs barely flutter when it tries to leap from one spire ledge to the next, yet it attempts the jump every morning without fail. The Dragon Spire's older creatures watch from above without interfering - they say each fall teaches it something a gentle push never could. Azupling is the first to peep in greeting when a new companion arrives at the spire's gates.

Field note: Azupling attempts the same impossible ledge jump every dawn; it has never once succeeded and has never once stopped trying.

About Azuwing

Azuwing has earned its full feathers - crisp white chest and blazing blue wings with a sharp golden beak that knows how to carry the wind. It soars the updrafts of the Dragon Spire with the confidence of a creature that has already conquered every ledge it once dreamed of reaching. Azuwing acts as messenger between the spire's highest roosts and the valley below, arriving so quietly on an updraft that those it visits often only notice it when it speaks. It hovers above struggling travelers as a windbreak, though it never acknowledges doing so.

Field note: Azuwing arrives so silently on an updraft that its shadow on the ground is the first sign it is there.

About Azuraptor

Azuraptor is a colossus of feather and gold-armored talon, its massive blue wings spanning the width of a storm. Chest plating the color of hammered brass catches the light as it descends, and its screech clears every cloud within sight. The Dragon Spire records state that Azuraptor once carried the Guardian Council's banner into the sky during Abalont's great assault, its wings generating a gale that turned the shadow tide back for a full day. It lands only on the highest spire point, and lower creatures instinctively hold still when its shadow passes.

Field note: Azuraptor's wing-strike as it lands creates a downdraft that extinguishes every torch on the spire's upper levels at once.

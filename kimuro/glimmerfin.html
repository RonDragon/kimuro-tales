About Glimmerfin

This Glimmerfin wears a softer look - its round pale-lavender body drifts with a single large blue eye and a gentle lure-bob on its brow, tail trailing like a comma. Shadow Caves' still pools are its home, and it spends its days nosing along the cave floor, turning pebbles over with its snout. Those who find one say it watches you without blinking for a long time before deciding you are safe. It does not fear the dark - it was born inside it.

Field note: Glimmerfin always presses itself against the cave wall at the sound of footsteps - then peeks back out exactly three seconds later.

About Luminaw

Luminaw trades the Glimmerfin's softness for hard edges. Its body is now solid and spined, jaw square and teeth visible, dorsal fin jagged as broken glass. Its lure-light burns cold white, swinging on a longer stalk now. It hunts Shadow Caves' mid-depth streams alone, and when two Luminaw meet in a narrow tunnel, they each hold perfectly still until one backs away. It is said they never fight - just stare until one decides the other's lure is brighter.

Field note: Luminaw's jaw clicks audibly when it detects moving water - researchers mapped a whole cave system just by listening to those clicks.

About Abysslight

Abysslight has grown into the apex of Shadow Caves - a massive wide-jawed angler with a blazing crown of ghost-flame sprouting where the lure once hung. Its body is broad and armored in matte-grey, and its eyes burn the pale blue of deep cavern ice. Blue flame-tendrils stream back from its sides like torn banners. It rarely moves, waiting in the deepest pool the caves hold, and any creature that approaches sees its own reflection distorted in those ancient eyes - showing a version of itself it hasn't become yet.

Field note: The shadow a passing Abysslight casts does not match its actual shape - it always shows the creature it will become.

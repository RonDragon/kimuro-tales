About Skelplet

Skelplet is the skeleton of a small upright theropod dinosaur with large round amber-glowing eye sockets and tiny forearms that clutch at the air. Its exposed ribs are narrow and its small tail-bones flick nervously. Despite being entirely skeletal, it moves with surprising energy - hopping between the warm volcanic stones of the Dragon Spire and letting heat radiate up through its bones like a radiator. Children encounter it first as a small clattering sound in the dark, followed by those two amber eyes peering around a rock.

Field note: Skelplet seeks out the warmest spot in any room and sits there for exactly an hour before moving on to the next warmest.

About Skelptor

Skelptor stands upright on powerful hind legs with the full ribcage and spine of a mid-sized theropod, eye sockets burning with deep amber fire. Its forearms are small but its jaws are substantial, lined with individual bone-teeth that clack impressively when it opens wide. It climbs the Dragon Spire's lava-rock shelves at night, apparently drawn to stored heat, pressing its entire skeleton against warm surfaces like it is recharging. The guardians found old etchings showing Skelptor guarding the Spire's flame-carved memory-stones after Akilla's fall.

Field note: Skelptor's jaw produces a specific clacking rhythm when it is content - locals transcribed it and found it matches an ancient drum-prayer.

About Skelphadon

Skelphadon is a magnificent terror - a full-sized skeletal theropod with a sweeping thick tail, a huge armoured skull, and fire burning in both eye sockets that casts orange light across the entire Dragon Spire. Its ribcage has broadened and new bone-plates have grown over the joints, giving it a plated war-armour look. When it opens its jaws, a gust of hot air carries ash from somewhere inside the chest cavity. The guardians say it was already ancient when the Spire was first settled - it has watched every guardian pass through and waited for each one to return.

Field note: The heat from Skelphadon's eye-fire can be felt three body-lengths away - Dragon Spire nights are always slightly warmer wherever it walks.

About Spiklet

Spiklet is a tight ball of ice-blue crystalline spines radiating in every direction, with two small glinting eyes peeking out from the dense cluster. It rolls along the frost-shattered scree where cold air pools against sun-warmed stone, and every spine is razor-edged and faintly luminous. It cannot move fast, but nothing in the peaks wants to nudge it either. The Guardians of Frozen Peaks once used these little balls as lanterns through the longest winter nights.

Field note: Spiklet can retract all its spines at once and drop into a snow hollow in under a second when it senses danger.

About Spiconid

Spiconid has flattened into a wide low body plated in dark grey-blue armour, its spines now longer and arranged in dense overlapping rows along its flanks and back. It moves slowly across the high scree, ploughing a clear furrow through the frost. The spines at its front are slightly longer and angled forward, used to wedge apart stones to reach the frost-algae it eats. Young snow-hares use it as a moving shelter, darting between its spines while Spiconid remains entirely unbothered.

Field note: A single Spiconid spine, once broken off, regrows to full length in exactly eleven days.

About Crystallurk

Crystallurk barely fits through most cave mouths in Frozen Peaks: a massive low-slung armoured body bristling with enormous ice-blue spines grown thick as stalactites, packed so densely that its body is almost invisible beneath them. Its eyes are slitted and cold, scanning the ice shelf with patient intelligence. When Abalont's influence tried to freeze the whole range solid, Crystallurk was already there - holding the cold in one place so the rest of the range stayed liveable.

Field note: Crystallurk's outer spine layer is hollow; on calm nights you can hear the mountain wind whistle through it.

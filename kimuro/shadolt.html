About Shadolt

Shadolt is a compact black foal with a short dark-purple mane and tail that lift and drift like smoke when the Lava Crater's heat rises beneath it. Its red eyes glow steadily in the volcanic dark. It trots across the cooled lava flats with surprising nimbleness, occasionally stopping to paw at the ground where heat vents hiss beneath the surface. Children who encounter it say it watches them steadily without blinking for longer than is comfortable, then suddenly trots off - and the direction it goes is always toward the safest path through the crater.

Field note: Shadolt always trots away toward the safest exit from the Lava Crater - lava-zone guides follow it without question.

About Nightmane

Nightmane is a powerful black stallion with a full purple-smoke mane that streams fire-edged behind it at full gallop. Its red eyes have deepened to a burning intensity and hooves strike the lava rock with enough force to leave scorched prints. It circles the active crater rim at night, and the violet fire in its mane lights the outer edges of the Lava Crater in a slow purple glow. The guardians recorded that Nightmane's presence near a flame vent increases its output - as if the dark fire in its blood calls to the fire below.

Field note: Flame vents near Nightmane's patrol path run 30% hotter than equivalent vents elsewhere in the Lava Crater.

About Phantorvex

Phantorvex is a magnificent terror of the Lava Crater - a great black stallion wreathed entirely in violet-purple flames that pour from its mane, tail, and hooves in long cascading streams. Its eyes blaze with ancient fire and its nostrils trail dark smoke. When it runs the full crater rim, the trail of purple fire it leaves lingers for hours, visible from the valley below as a ring of violet light. The guardians say Phantorvex was present at Abalont's first shadow-fall - it watched from the crater edge, then turned and set every fire-marker in the region burning to warn the others.

Field note: The fire-ring Phantorvex leaves running the full crater rim lasts six hours - Lava Crater communities use it as a nightly clock.

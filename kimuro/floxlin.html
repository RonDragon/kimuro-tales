About Floxlin

Floxlin is a perfectly round lamb whose cream-white fleece is so thick it seems more cloud than wool. Its small pink face peers out from the fluff with a wide, content expression, and when a breeze passes through the Light Grove, the fleece ripples and sheds tiny motes of brightness that hang in the air for a few seconds before fading. It follows anyone who moves slowly and calmly, nudging their ankles with the top of its woolly head. The guardians kept Floxlin in the grove's clearing as a gentle weather sign - fleece puffing out means a warm wind is coming.

Field note: Floxlin's fleece grows exactly one centimeter longer on the side the next day's wind will come from.

About Floxcur

Floxcur has grown stocky and sure-footed, with a pair of curling horns and a dense fleece that stands out from its body in windswept tufts. Its coat has lightened to near-white with a faint luminous sheen, and when it trots across a sunlit clearing the grass bends away from it as though parting in respect. It navigates the Light Grove's shifting air corridors with perfect instinct, and younger creatures follow its path through fog because Floxcur never walks into a dead-end.

Field note: Floxcur butts its curled horns against grove-stones three times each dawn; locals say the knocking sound tells the grove it is still being watched over.

About Floxarch

Floxarch is a great broad ram whose massive curled horns sweep wide and whose dense mane-fleece has turned a luminous silver-white. The grove's light threads through its coat permanently now, giving it a gentle halo visible even at midday. When it stamps a hoof, the air ripples outward in a visible pressure wave that bends tall grass for twenty meters. Ancient and deliberate, Floxarch spends long hours standing on the high ridge of the grove, facing the wind, as if reading something written in the air that no one else can see.

Field note: Floxarch never lies down during a storm; it simply lowers its massive head and holds still, and the wind parts around it like water around a stone.

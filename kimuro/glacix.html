About Glacix

Glacix is a scrappy, pointy-eared ice-grey wolf pup with a wild mane of spiky fur that sticks up in every direction and keen amber eyes. Small ice-crystal studs dot its scruff and flanks. It prowls the upper tunnels of the Crystal Mines, sniffing out new crystal deposits by following temperature changes in the rock. It has the habit of howling at fresh crystals when it finds one, which makes it easy to find but loud to travel with. The mine-guardians have learned to give it space - every crystal it announces is worth excavating.

Field note: Glacix can detect a new crystal deposit by smell through 15 centimetres of solid rock.

About Frostmane

Frostmane has grown into a powerful, athletic wolf with a broad chest, thick spiky silver-blue mane studded with crystal formations, and ice-plate armour growing along its spine. A crystal emblem glows on its sternum. It runs the deep shafts of the Crystal Mines in long fluid strides, checking every tunnel it mapped as a pup. When it finds a collapsed passage it pins the location with a howl pattern that the miners have learned to decode precisely - one long, two short means structural failure; three short means crystal bloom.

Field note: Frostmane's tunnel-warning calls are so specific that mine engineers have written a formal translation guide based on them.

About Cryofang

Cryofang is an imposing wolf-like titan - massive, dark blue-grey body armoured in interlocking crystal plates that erupt from every joint and shoulder, its enormous mane a cascade of crystal shards that ring softly with each step. Its eyes blaze with cold crystalline fire and its paws leave faint crystal flowers in the rock wherever it walks. The Crystal Mines themselves seem older where Cryofang has been - as if its presence ages the crystals, deepening their colour and clarity. Ancient mine texts call it the Lord of the Vein.

Field note: Any crystal that Cryofang has rested against grows 30% larger than surrounding crystals over the following year.

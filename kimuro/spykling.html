About Spykling

Spykling is a small, dark-crimson dragon-bat with a wide head, two large red eyes, a stocky body, and stubby wings it cannot yet use for sustained flight. Its feet are large and clawed for clinging to the lava crater's vertical walls, which it does for most of the day, watching everything below with unblinking red eyes. In short glides it launches itself from ledge to ledge, and each landing is precise. The other crater creatures do not push it off its ledge - it seems too certain for something so small.

Field note: Spykling licks the cooling lava rim each morning - researchers believe it is reading the temperature to decide when to move.

About Spyxon

Spyxon has grown bipedal and humanoid in outline - a lean, tall dark-crimson drake standing upright on two muscular legs with two broad arms ending in claws, and wide leathery wings folded at its back. Its red eyes glow in the lava crater's thermal haze. It can launch itself from the crater rim and circle the entire crater in a single thermal, riding the heat without a wingbeat. It does not chase things. It waits and glides, and arrives exactly when expected.

Field note: Spyxon's shadow is consistently thirty centimetres longer than its body size should produce - no one has found an explanation.

About Spyxalor

Spyxalor is a fully grown dragon with the proportions of a warlord - massive chest, broad wings that shadow a ten-metre circle when spread, a long heavy tail, and enormous clawed forelimbs that grip the lava crater rim with casual ease. Its deep crimson scales have darkened toward black at the wing joints, and its red eyes hold a low burning glow. It is old enough to remember when the lava crater was formed, and the guardians placed it there not as a threat but as a recorder - its body heat has mapped every eruption for a century.

Field note: Spyxalor exhales a smoke ring once each hour, on the hour - no one has ever observed it miss.

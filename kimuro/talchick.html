About Talchick

Talchick is a small, compact bird chick with dark blue-grey feathers, a sharp hooked beak, and a tuft of spiky head-feathers that stand on end when it is excited - which is nearly always. Its bright yellow eye gives it an expression of intense focus that belies its tiny frame. It hops along Gale Ridge's narrow ledges, practicing the precise weight-shifting that will one day let it ride full storm winds. When the gale picks up, Talchick spreads its short wings and leans into it, absolutely refusing to shelter.

Field note: Talchick refuses to shelter from any storm, no matter how severe - it has never once been blown off a ledge.

About Talonawk

Talonawk has grown lean and long-winged, its dark feathers now shot through with a single electric-yellow marking that runs from eye to wingtip like a lightning bolt. It hunts the Gale Ridge's middle heights in long, patient circles, reading the invisible architecture of rising air with better precision than any instrument. When it spots a target, it doesn't hesitate - the dive is sudden and absolute. The yellow marking is said to burn brighter during storms, turning Talonawk into a living lightning flash against the dark cloud.

Field note: Talonawk's yellow marking glows so brightly during a lightning storm that it has been mistaken for actual lightning by sailors below.

About Skytalon

Skytalon is a massive, wide-winged dark eagle whose wingspan blocks the Ridge's floodlight when it passes overhead. Its feathers are deep blue-black, and the gold-yellow accent has spread from the single eye-stripe to ring the edge of each primary feather with a faint luminous border. When it spreads its wings fully - which it does rarely, only in the highest updrafts above the Gale Ridge's peak - the wing-membrane catches the wind so completely that it sings. Skytalon does not need to hunt. It only needs to be seen, and the mountain adjusts accordingly.

Field note: Skytalon has never been observed to pursue prey twice - if it misses the first strike, it returns to altitude and continues circling.

About Hootlet

Hootlet is a small plump purple owl with enormous violet eyes and a crescent moon marking on its chest - it blinks very slowly, as if every thought takes longer than the one before it. A faint curl of lilac smoke trails from its wingtip when it tilts its head, which it does constantly. It nests on Dragon Spire's lowest stone ledges, and trainers say it is practicing: every slow blink is an attempt to read the mind of whatever it is staring at, with limited success.

Field note: Hootlet blinks once per question it thinks of - on a quiet day it has been seen blinking seventy times a minute.

About Prowlure

Prowlure has grown tall and sleek, its purple plumage darkened to near-violet at the wingtips and tendrils of lavender smoke now curling continuously from its head feathers. Its eyes have narrowed into a focused, knowing stare that unsettles even the dragon-kind that nest above it on the Spire. It no longer simply tries to read minds - it succeeds, sorting through nearby thoughts the way a scholar sorts letters, filing each one before hooting softly and moving on.

Field note: Prowlure will always hoot once at the exact moment you think of something you were trying not to think about.

About Starprowl

Starprowl unfurls wings so wide they catch the highest Dragon Spire thermals with ease, and the star-swept feathers along its face and crown mark it as a creature old enough to remember when the Spire had no dragons yet. Its eyes now glow with a soft, continuous light that can be seen from the valley floor on clear nights - the locals call it the third moon. It flies in slow spirals above the clouds, watching everything below with perfect recall and absolute calm.

Field note: Starprowl never forgets a face - it has recognized returning travellers after absences of more than thirty years.

About Malachick

Malachick is a round-bodied dark owlet-chick, its deep black feathers ruffled and unfinished, and wisps of purple ghost-smoke curling from its wingtips. Its round yellow eyes stare with a gravity unusual in something so small. It perches in Ghost Hollow's lower branches and watches in complete silence for hours. Occasionally it turns its head almost backward to track something no one else can see. Guardians camping nearby learn not to worry about this - Malachick has always been watching things that are not quite there.

Field note: Malachick blinks only once per hour - field researchers who tried to count found themselves losing track and suspecting it was counting them instead.

About Malacrow

Malacrow has grown into a proper raven - sleek black plumage with a single curving horn rising from its brow, and purple ghost-vapor trailing from its wingtips in flight. It moves through Ghost Hollow's upper canopy in quick deliberate hops, cocking its head and processing everything with bright calculation. It has learned to mimic the sound of footsteps to call other creatures toward empty paths. Guardians who learn its different calls gain an advantage: Malacrow's high sharp caw means a clear passage, its low burring croak means turn back now.

Field note: Malacrow never calls out the same sequence of sounds twice - its vocalizations are always novel arrangements of known sounds.

About Malachaos

Malachaos is enormous for a bird - a great raven with wings spread wide enough to shadow a house. Its plumage is a deep blue-black that seems to absorb light, and dark wind-currents spiral outward from it when it beats its wings. Its two curved horns frame a face of fierce intelligence. When it spreads its wings in Ghost Hollow, the ghost-smoke between the trees thickens and swirls. Lore says Malachaos was present when Abalont first began its assault, that it watched everything and remembered every detail, and that it will tell the full story to any creature who is patient enough to ask the right question.

Field note: Malachaos's wingspan is always described as larger than the observer expected - no two recorded estimates agree on the exact measurement.

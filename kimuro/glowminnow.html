About Glowminnow

Glowminnow is a chubby round fish no bigger than a palm, covered in teal-blue spots that pulse faintly in the dark. A single wispy tail-thread trails behind it, and a thin luminous lure hangs from its forehead on a delicate stalk. It drifts through Ghost Hollow's underground pools, following the drip-sound of water through stone. Children who wade in the shallows sometimes feel something nudge their toes - it is always a Glowminnow, curious about the warmth of a living foot.

Field note: Glowminnow's spots pulse faster when it hears a new sound - it once lit up a whole cave trying to count an echo.

About Phosphink

Phosphink has grown angular and spiky - jagged fins like broken stone jut from its back, and its lure-light burns brighter, casting blue-white flickers across wet rock walls. Its body is longer now, the rounded cub-shape replaced by a lean predator silhouette edged in violet ghost-flame. It prowls Ghost Hollow's deeper channels where no natural light reaches, using its lure to draw in curious creatures and study them before drifting away. Guardians say Phosphink never shows fear - it only shows curiosity.

Field note: Phosphink's fin-spines vibrate at the same frequency as Ghost Hollow's deepest dripping tunnels - it uses them to navigate in total darkness.

About Abyssalure

Abyssalure fills doorways. Its wide-jawed head is all gape and gleam - rows of pale teeth behind a great maw, and a blazing lure that burns blue-white like a drowned star. Ghost-flame ripples off its fins and trails behind it in long tendrils. It has abandoned Ghost Hollow's ceilings for the deepest lightless reservoirs beneath the roots. Ancient records say it first appeared the night Abalont's shadow first touched the water, and that it keeps memory of every creature that ever passed through the hollow - holding their echoes in its light.

Field note: Abyssalure has never been seen fully in one place - part of its body always remains in shadow, even in bright light.

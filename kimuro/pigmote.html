About Pigmote

Pigmote is a round brown boar-pup with a broad flat snout it uses to bulldoze through Sand Dune hillocks like a miniature plow. Rows of flat armored plates have already begun forming across its back, and two small rounded tusks poke out from a perpetually grumpy expression. It is surprisingly fast for its shape, and covers a lot of ground each day in a zigzag pattern as it roots for buried seeds and stone beetles. Pigmote does not share its food and will sit on anything edible it hasn't finished yet to stop others from reaching it.

Field note: Pigmote sits on its unfinished food to save it for later - the dunes are dotted with its small flat depressions.

About Tuskling

Tuskling's back is now fully covered in overlapping stone-colored armor plates that clatter faintly as it moves, and its tusks have lengthened into forward-curving weapons that leave clean arcs in the dune sand when it charges. It tests every new piece of ground by stamping once before committing its full weight - a habit that has saved it from hidden sinkhole traps on dozens of occasions. Tuskling does not back down, but it is patient about choosing when to go forward, studying obstacles with small piggy eyes until it has a clear plan.

Field note: Tuskling stamps once on every new surface before crossing - no Tuskling has ever fallen through a sand sinkhole.

About Boarthon

Boarthon is a walking stone fortress on four tree-trunk legs, its entire back covered in a fused shell of spiked rock plates and its tusks grown into great sweeping weapons that can carve a channel through a dune in a single pass. The ground shakes in rings when it walks, and approaching sand dunes visibly shift as the compression wave moves ahead of it. Boarthon is the Sand Dunes' unmovable anchor - when Abalont's shadow rolled across the dunes, Boarthon simply stood still in its path and did not move for nine days until the shadow went around it.

Field note: Boarthon has stood completely still in the same spot for recorded periods of up to nine days - the dune shifts around it, not through it.

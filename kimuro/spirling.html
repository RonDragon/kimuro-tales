About Spirling

Spirling is a plump purple caterpillar with spiral markings along its entire body, each segment ringed in a swirling circle that pulses faintly with psychic light. It moves in a slow, deliberate S-curve rather than a straight line, as though writing something with its path. Every leaf it passes on the verdant valley floor is turned face-up by a gentle unseen force. It is so calm and slow that other creatures often step over it without noticing, which suits Spirling perfectly - it is in no hurry.

Field note: Spirling leaves a faint invisible psychic trail that keeps the precise shape of its path for up to six hours.

About Cocolix

Cocolix is a large purple egg-shaped cocoon decorated with deep spiral swirls that faintly rotate in one direction when observed. It rests perfectly still in the verdant valley, balanced on nothing visible. Inside, something is reorganising - the spirals on its shell slowly shift position over days, and children who sit near it and speak find their thoughts come out clearer. The guardians say Cocolix is the moment between knowing a word and truly understanding it.

Field note: No one has ever seen a Cocolix fall over - even in high wind, even placed on a steep slope, it simply stays put.

About Spiralyx

Spiralyx emerges as a wide-winged purple butterfly with broad wings printed in deep spiral eye-patterns and soft curved edges. Its large vivid eyes hold a calm psychic glow. It drifts through verdant valley on slow wingbeats, and wherever it passes, the air shimmers faintly with an after-image of its wing pattern. Children in the valley say if you watch Spiralyx long enough and then close your eyes, you will remember the hardest word you ever learned - perfectly.

Field note: Spiralyx's wingbeats produce a low psychic frequency that is below hearing but causes nearby flowers to open slightly wider.

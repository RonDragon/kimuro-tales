About Primlet

Primlet is an orange-furred infant hanging from a vine with both small arms, big round eyes taking in the Psychic Temple below with wide-eyed wonder. Its arms are already disproportionately long compared to its small body, perfectly built for swinging. It makes soft cooing sounds when curious and clings to anything it can reach - trainers' sleeves, vine loops, the edges of star-charts. The light temple's psychic currents make its fur stand on end whenever it passes a particularly powerful light-tree, which it interprets as exciting rather than alarming. Primlet is the most trusting creature in the temple.

Field note: Primlet will swing toward any glowing light-tree without hesitation - no fear, only curiosity, which the temple-keepers say is its greatest gift.

About Primorph

Primorph is a fully grown orangutan-build ape with a broad face, long red-brown arms, and powerful shoulders. It moves on all fours with a deliberate calm, and its orange-red fur is thick and textured. It no longer swings freely; instead it moves steadily through the temple, stopping to press its large flat hands against light-trees and closing its eyes. The psychic connection it makes in those moments leaves a faint warm handprint glow on the bark that lasts for hours. Primorph is patient with young creatures but firm - it will gently move aside any creature blocking a light-tree so that it can read it properly.

Field note: Every handprint a Primorph leaves on a light-tree bark glows for exactly as many hours as the creature's age in years.

About Primachon

Primachon speaks rarely. When it does, temple-keepers write down what it said, because nothing Primachon has told anyone has ever turned out to be wrong.

Field note: The orb atop Primachon's staff glows brightest when a child tries something difficult for the very first time.

About Sporeling

Sporeling is a stumpy little mushroom-cap creature with a shiny brown cap, a wide smile, and round stubby feet it pats against the marsh floor in a kind of perpetual happy shuffle. Its ghost element is gentle at this stage, just a faint shimmer at the cap's edge when light catches it. It explores the edges of the poison marsh's pools with cheerful recklessness, tasting the water with a tiny extended tendril and then making a face. Despite tasting everything, nothing in the marsh has ever made a Sporeling sick.

Field note: Sporeling has been observed tasting 37 different marsh-pool water samples in a single day - apparently for fun.

About Fungstem

This Fungstem has grown into a stooped, vine-wrapped figure, its cap broader and more muted, its body trailing dark tendrils that probe the marsh soil. Its face carries a tired, knowing expression. Ghost energy leaks from the base of its cap as faint wisps that drift downwind. It stands at the edges of the deepest poison pools for hours, as if listening to something the water is saying. Marsh-keepers say Fungstem marks the boundary between safe marsh and truly dangerous marsh by simply refusing to cross it.

Field note: No Fungstem has ever been found on the other side of a genuinely toxic pool - their refusal to cross is a reliable safety marker.

About Spectralmush

Spectralmush towers over the poison marsh's reed-beds, its immense glowing cap lit from within by ghost-energy in cold green-white light. Its body has become partly translucent - the outline of internal root-structures visible through its trunk. Vines and marsh-growth drape it like a king's cloak. When Spectralmush exhales, ghost-light spores drift across the marsh surface, marking the safe paths through. Old marsh guides say Spectralmush appeared right after Abalont's shadow tried to poison the marsh permanently - and that its ghost-light has kept the water from going fully dark ever since.

Field note: Spectralmush's glowing spore-paths are visible from three metres altitude on dark nights - marsh travellers navigate by them like stars.

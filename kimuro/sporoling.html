About Sporoling

Sporoling is a squat purple toad whose head is dominated by a cluster of eye-stalks - each one a different height, peering in a different direction simultaneously, with a single large forward-facing eye at the center that never blinks. This gives it near-complete vision in all directions at once, which makes sneaking up on a Sporoling essentially impossible. It lives in the Bug Thicket's damp floors, where the soil exhales spore-clouds that it seems to navigate by tasting with its wide flat tongue. Other creatures in the thicket avoid crossing its line of sight - not because it is dangerous, but because being stared at from seven angles at once is disconcerting.

Field note: Sporoling has seven independent eye-stalks each tracking a different quadrant - it has never been documented failing to notice something moving near it.

About Bulbwart

Bulbwart is heavier and wider now, its purple skin raised in bumps and nodules that each contain a smaller secondary eye peering outward. Combined with its central eye-stalks, it maintains close to three hundred degrees of visual coverage simultaneously, and can process all of it. Bulbwart moves slowly and deliberately through the Bug Thicket, broadcasting a low psychic field that makes insects within ten meters instinctively stop moving - a foraging advantage so efficient it doesn't need to hurry. The thicket's more sensitive creatures say being near a Bulbwart feels like standing very close to an extremely interested audience.

Field note: The secondary eyes in Bulbwart's skin nodules can each be focused independently - researchers have documented it watching five separate insects simultaneously.

About Mycodrak

Mycodrak is a low-slung colossus of purple warty hide and clustered eye-growths, its body so wide it fills the Bug Thicket's broadest clearings. Every raised nodule on its back is a functioning eye; its psychic field has grown so strong that insects within thirty meters freeze entirely when it broadcasts, and larger creatures experience a strong involuntary impulse to hold still and consider whether they really needed to walk this way. The guardians' records describe Mycodrak as the Thicket's All-Seer: a creature that has observed every living thing in the Bug Thicket for centuries and holds the knowledge of each in its silent eyes.

Field note: Mycodrak's psychic still-field has been measured by researchers at up to thirty meters - no instrument has yet located its outer edge.

About Golet

Golet is a small, stocky baby gorilla with charcoal-grey fur and large round eyes that give it a solemn expression it has not yet earned. It sits very still in the lower ash-fields of the Lava Crater and watches the magma channels with intense focus, occasionally reaching out to pat a cooling lava-stone and immediately withdrawing its hand in surprise. It does this every few minutes. It never learns, and it never stops trying. The crater's older creatures find this endearing in a way they would never admit.

Field note: Golet has patted a cooling lava-stone approximately 900 times in observed records; it is surprised every single time.

About Gorask

Gorask stands upright on powerfully built legs with thick charcoal-black fur and veins of lava-orange glowing beneath its skin at the wrists and throat. It beats its chest in the Lava Crater more for the echo than for dominance - it seems to enjoy the sound. It wades through shallow lava channels with total indifference, trailing small fires in its wake. The crater guardians have noticed it always moves along the same patrol path each evening, clearing falling ash from the safe crossing-points, though no one asked it to.

Field note: Gorask's chest-beat creates a resonance in the crater walls that causes volcanic gas to vent harmlessly - unintentionally, but reliably.

About Goliathyx

Goliathyx is an overwhelming creature - a colossal white-furred gorilla titan wreathed in flame from every surface, lava-orange fire pouring from its mane, knuckles, and shoulders as it stands upright. Its eyes are red with fury and its stone-cracked skin glows like the crater floor. When Goliathyx pounds the Lava Crater walls, volcanic tremors ripple across the entire region and the lava lake stirs. Ancient carvers depicted it as the moment before an eruption - not a disaster, but a question the mountain is asking, and Goliathyx is the voice the question travels on.

Field note: Seismic records from the Lava Crater show that every major eruption in history has been preceded by Goliathyx's chest-beat by exactly forty minutes.

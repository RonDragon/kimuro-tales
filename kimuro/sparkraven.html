About Sparkraven

Sparkraven is a small, sooty charcoal-black bird chick with stubby wings it has not yet learned to use and bright orange feet it shuffles forward on with great determination. Its black plumage absorbs heat from the lava crater vents and radiates it back as a faint warmth that singes finger-tips on contact. Its tiny beak is already sharp, and it pecks at embers along the crater rim to test which words glow hottest. Explorers find it bold for its size - it will hop directly onto a boot to investigate.

Field note: Sparkraven collects small glowing embers in a pile each evening, counting them before sleeping - always the same number.

About Inferryx

Inferryx has erupted into a black-feathered bird with broad wings whose trailing edges blaze with living orange flame. Burning embers drift behind it wherever it flies, and its wingspan casts a firelit shadow on the crater walls. It screams as it dives, a sound like tearing metal, and the blast of wind from its wings fans the lava below into spinning fire-spouts. It is fiercely territorial over its updraft columns and will challenge anything larger than itself on principle. Young guardians call it the test of courage - whoever stands still when Inferryx stoops gets its grudging respect.

Field note: The embers that fall from Inferryx's wing-tips stay lit for three full minutes after landing, even in rain.

About Phoenixolt

Phoenixolt is a magnificent black bird with enormous wings wreathed entirely in billowing flame - orange and gold fire streams from every primary feather as it spreads wide and catches the thermal columns above the crater. A crown of burning plumes rises from its head, and its talons glow red-hot at the tips. When it lands on the crater's peak, the air itself shudders from the heat it carries. It is said that the ashes from its feathers, collected at the winter solstice, can restore a forgotten word to memory. It has never forgotten a single one.

Field note: Phoenixolt lands on the same crater peak at sunset every day, and the glow of its wings can be seen from every region on the map.

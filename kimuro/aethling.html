About Aethling

Aethling is a small round blue bird with a white belly and a distinctive teal swirl marking on its chest, like a tiny whirlpool. Bright water-drop sparkles float around it constantly. In Poison Marsh's misty channels it hops along floating lily pads, tilting its head at every ripple. Its feathers repel the marsh's toxic vapors like a tiny forcefield. Guardians first noticed Aethling leading safe paths through the marsh by hopping only across patches where the water sparkles blue rather than green.

Field note: Aethling's swirl marking spins visibly clockwise when fresh air is nearby and counter-clockwise near marsh gas - a perfect compass.

About Aethawk

Aethawk has grown into a bold blue jay-shaped bird with icy-blue plumage and tail feathers that stream behind it like jets of water. Its chest is pure white and its wings carry teal water-streaks. It sprints low across the marsh surface fast enough to leave a ripple trail in the murky water. In battle it whips its tail feathers to conjure short water-jet gusts that knock toxic spores away from allies. Trainers crossing Poison Marsh trust Aethawk to scout ahead and report back in coded tail-fan patterns.

Field note: Aethawk can sprint 40 meters across open water without getting its feet wet by riding its own air-cushion jet-wake.

About Aetherion

Aetherion is a powerful wide-winged raptor draped in deep royal blue, with gold talons and wings spread in a full commanding display. Bands of teal and ice-blue run across its wings, and a crest of swept-back blue feathers crowns its head. When it opens its wings in Poison Marsh, the toxic mist parts for thirty meters in every direction. Ancient marsh-keepers say Aetherion's call is the only sound that reaches across the whole marsh at once - a rolling wave of water and wind combined.

Field note: One sustained call from Aetherion clears toxic spores from a full marsh sector; the marsh falls silent for a full minute afterward.

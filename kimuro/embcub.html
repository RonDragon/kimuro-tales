About Embcub

Embcub is a pudgy, round-bellied tiger cub in bright fire-orange with bold dark stripes and enormous gentle eyes. It sits on the amber sands of the desert and watches heat-mirages with intense focus, tilting its head as if studying words no one else can read. It is the earliest known form of a dragon-kin tiger born in the desert heat, and even its yawn releases a brief wave of warm air that makes the sand shimmer. Every explorer's first fire here is lit with an Embcub's breath.

Field note: Embcub yawns exactly three times before a sandstorm - desert travelers count carefully.

About Embered

Embered has grown into a powerful low-slung tiger with a ridged back of raised fire-scales and deep orange-red stripes over its stocky frame. Its body has begun taking on dragon characteristics: the spine-ridges are harder than bone, and its eyes have gone from round to the narrow vertical-slit of a desert predator. It sprints across the Amber Desert at night, its ridges glowing faint orange in the darkness as a warning to anything in its path.

Field note: Embered's spine-ridges glow orange only after midnight - the same hours the desert's ancient trading roads were once traveled.

About Inferyx

Inferyx is a vast armored dragon-tiger whose body is plated with thick fire-scale ridges across the back, shoulders, and flanks. Its massive jaw opens to reveal rows of fire-orange fangs, and its wide lizard-like feet grip the sand-stone with iron certainty. When it presses its front feet to the amber sand and roars, the vibration carries all the way to the opposite side of the desert. The Amber Desert's heat belongs to it - it inherited the fire from both its tiger and dragon bloodlines.

Field note: Inferyx's scale-plates heat up before it speaks - if you feel warmth on the air, it has already chosen its words.

About Emblkit

Emblkit is a blue-grey kitten with warm amber eyes, stubby orange-tipped ears, and a small tail that curls upward. A shy creature of the Dark Forest, it hides behind gnarled roots and watches travelers through the gloom with those luminous eyes - never quite trusting, never quite leaving. It was noticed by the early guardians because it would follow them to the forest's edge, then turn back. Something in the Dark Forest calls it home, even as the hero-blood in its veins pulls it forward.

Field note: Emblkit's eyes reflect light in a unique two-color split - left eye gold, right eye silver - which never reverses.

About Emblicat

Emblicat has grown large and powerfully built, its blue-grey fur now deeply shadowed with dark markings and its body low-slung and muscular. Thick orange-tipped ears alert to every sound, and its amber eyes glow in the forest dark. It has taken on a guardian role in the Dark Forest, standing between the deepest shadows and the creatures that live on the light side. Its roar keeps the darkness at bay - the guardians say one Emblicat patrolling is worth three sentry posts.

Field note: Emblicat places its right paw on the ground at a trail's fork - the side it rests on is always the safer path.

About Emblion

Emblion is a vast, broad-shouldered dark lion whose mane blazes in deep navy-purple shadow-fire. Its body is pure muscle under dark-sapphire fur, and its wide amber eyes carry centuries of forest memory. When Abalont's shadow crept deepest into the Dark Forest, Emblion stood at the boundary and refused to move for seven days. The forest remembers. Every tree in the Dark Forest grows slightly bent toward wherever Emblion last walked - following its guardian.

Field note: Emblion always faces east at dawn - the direction it once watched for the guardians' return.

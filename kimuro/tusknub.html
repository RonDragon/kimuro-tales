About Tusknub

Tuskle's first form is a chunky little brown boar with a wide snout, small sharp eyes, and a ridge of bristly spines running from head to tail. Its two forward tusks are already prominent, curving slightly outward and catching amber light beautifully. It charges everything it finds interesting - which is everything. The sandy floor of the Amber Desert suits it perfectly; it roots through it constantly, flinging pebbles and crumbling sandstone chunks in enthusiastic fountains.

Field note: A Tusknub boar charges at a full run, stops precisely one body-length from its target, then sniffs it as if nothing unusual happened.

About Bristusk

Bristusk is a powerful, low-to-the-ground boar with a wide armoured forehead, heavy curving tusks streaked with mineral deposits, and a dense ridge of thorn-like spines along its spine that have hardened into rock-solid keratin. When challenged it drops its head and digs its hooves in - a warning it gives once. Amber Desert traders mark the locations of Bristusk dens on their maps; not out of fear, but because a Bristusk den always sits above a stable rock shelf - ideal for a safe campsite.

Field note: Bristusk marks its territory by dragging its tusks through sandstone - the gouges it leaves are perfectly level, as if measured.

About Terrausk

Terrausk is a massive, squat-bodied boar whose entire dorsal surface is covered in thick interlocking stone plates that have fused with its spine. Its great tusks are as long as a child's arm and as hard as granite. It moves at a slow, deliberate walk - but when it charges, nothing short of a solid wall stops it. The Amber Desert's western canyon was partly widened by centuries of Terrausk herds pushing through it each dry season. The guardians revere it as a creature that reshapes the land by simply going where it needs to go.

Field note: Terrausk's stone back-plates absorb so much desert heat that small lizards bask on sleeping individuals, undisturbed, all afternoon.

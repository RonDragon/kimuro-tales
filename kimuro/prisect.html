About Prisect

Prisect is a tiny bright-green mantis with outsized triangular eyes and a small shining blue crystal gem embedded in its chest like a jewel. Its six legs are slender and precise, and its two forearms are held in the classic folded-prayer mantis pose at all times. Small translucent wings lie flat along its back. The crystal in its chest pulses with a slow psychic light when it focuses, which it does frequently while standing completely motionless, watching a single point in the bug thicket for minutes. Young trainers say that Prisect is either thinking very deeply or has simply forgotten what it was doing - the effect is identical.

Field note: The crystal on Prisect's chest glows brightest when it is looking at something invisible to everyone else in the thicket.

About Vitralis

Vitralis has grown into a tall, angular green mantis with two pairs of transparent wings spread in a permanent display stance. Its forelegs are now long serrated blades that it holds at chest height, and the crystal gem has grown to cover a larger patch of its thorax. It moves with extreme deliberation - each step tested, each pose held - yet when it decides to strike, the movement is invisible to the naked eye. It occupies the middle canopy of the bug thicket, and smaller creatures instinctively give it wide berth. Vitralis uses its psychic senses to replay lessons it has observed and shares them with the thicket's young insects by projecting them as crystal-clear mental images.

Field note: Vitralis can project a full three-minute psychic lesson-replay into any creature that makes direct eye contact with it.

About Iridoptera

Iridoptera is a striking and alien-looking mantis of crystalline blue-green, its body now largely translucent with glowing internal light visible through its carapace. Four enormous iridescent wings fan out behind it, each layered with hundreds of prismatic cells that scatter light into color clouds. Its blade-forearms are longer and more precise than ever, held in a permanent ready stance. It stands still for hours at the thicket's highest reaches, then vanishes between wingbeats. The bug thicket's oral history says that Iridoptera was the first creature to decode the psychic language of the guardian song-trees, and that its wing-patterns are actually a written record of every voice ever raised in the thicket.

Field note: If you photograph Iridoptera's wing pattern and enlarge it, each cell contains a different word from the ancient guardian language.

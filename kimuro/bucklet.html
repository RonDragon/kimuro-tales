About Bucklet

A delicate warm-brown fawn with neat white spots, short button antler-buds, and two small dark-purple spines jutting from between its shoulder blades, Bucklet moves through Ghost Hollow's dappled undergrowth with cautious, precise steps. Its large liquid eyes miss nothing, and it has memorized every ghost-light path in its home territory. Bucklet collects fallen ghost-hollow leaves in a small pile near its resting spot each dawn - no one knows why, but the piles are always neatly arranged, largest leaves at the base. The guardians say a Bucklet sleeping in your camp means the hollow approves of you.

Field note: Bucklet sorts fallen leaves by size each morning; the habit takes approximately twelve minutes and is never skipped regardless of weather.

About Thornstag

Thornstag stands with striking presence - a warm amber-brown stag with a full branching rack of antlers and a row of dark purple spines spreading down its back from neck to hindquarters like a saddle of shadow. Its coat is deeper-hued than Bucklet's, shading to near-black at the legs. It moves through Ghost Hollow with the slow deliberate grace of something that owns the space, pausing at intervals to test the air. When Thornstag presses its antler-tips to the earth, the local ghost-lights shift colour - the hollow itself seems to listen. Hollow guides believe it maintains the ghost-light routes.

Field note: When Thornstag presses its antlers to the earth and holds still, every ghost-light within fifty metres dims blue - local guides call it 'reading the hollow'.

About Splinthor

Splinthor is one of Ghost Hollow's oldest presences - a great dark-furred stag whose body has deepened to rich chocolate-brown and dark amber, its back now fully armoured with overlapping dark spines that spread from nape to tail in a continuous ridge. The antlers have branched so many times they look like a leafless winter oak. Ghost-lights collect around Splinthor's antlers at night, and the creature seems to navigate by them rather than by sight. Hollow lore says Splinthor was present when Abalont's shadow first reached Ghost Hollow, and it chose to stay - not out of fear, but out of a refusal to abandon the paths it had learned.

Field note: Ghost-lights cluster in Splinthor's antlers during fog events - experienced hollow navigators follow it as a living landmark in zero-visibility conditions.

About Hornhoot

Hornhoot is a compact grey-and-white owlet with a row of tiny plate-like feathers beginning to harden along its breast, giving it the look of a little knight in training. Its round amber eyes peer out with intense curiosity, and its small hooked beak is already sharp enough to scratch steel. In the lower corridors of the steel fortress, Hornhoot perches on girders and watches the gears turn, memorizing the rhythm of every machine. When asked a question it can not yet answer, it ruffles its breast-plates and looks away with wounded dignity.

Field note: Hornhoot sharpens its beak on steel beams each morning - maintenance crews can tell the time by the ringing sound.

About Hornowl

Hornowl's chest is now covered in overlapping grey metallic feather-plates that clatter softly when it adjusts its posture, like coins settling in a purse. Its wings carry flight feathers with hardened silver-grey tips, and its golden eyes cut through forge-smoke without blinking. It has appointed itself guardian of the steel fortress's memory-archives, refusing entry to any visitor who can not correctly answer its three riddles. Its head swivels silently - a full turn in either direction - before it delivers its judgment with a slow deliberate blink.

Field note: Hornowl's metal feather-plates clatter in a unique rhythm for each individual bird - fortress guards use the sound to identify them.

About Hornsoar

Hornsoar is armored from crown to talon - full articulated steel-gray plate covers its breast, shoulders, and swept-back wing-edges, and its talons are forged to gleaming points that leave grooves in iron floors. Its great wings, when spread wide, ring with a low metallic hum that silences a room. It keeps watch from the highest spire of the steel fortress, scanning the horizon with golden eyes that never tire. Once, during the time of Abalont's assault, it shielded a class of young learners beneath a single wing for three days without moving.

Field note: Hornsoar's wing-hum, produced when it spreads both wings fully, can be heard from the fortress's outermost wall half a league away.

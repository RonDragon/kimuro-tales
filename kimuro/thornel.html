About Thornel

Thornel is a small, bright green pup made of living grass and curling vines, with a few orange thorns on its back and a curly vine tail. It hides in the tall grass of the Dark Forest and watches travelers with big dark eyes before deciding whether to play or run away. Younger forest creatures follow it everywhere, and it guards them bravely.

Field note: The swirling vines on Thornel's body grow a little longer every time it protects a friend.

About Thornfang

Thornfang has grown into a slim, clever fox. Its tall pointed ears have purple insides, a black mask surrounds its glowing amber eyes, and its body is half green leaves, half dark fur. Green vines wrap its black legs, sharp thorns curve back from its shoulders, and its huge tail is made of twisted thorny vines. It moves through the thickets without a sound and always knows the hidden path.

Field note: Thornfang sleeps curled inside its own tail, and the thorns keep it safe all night.

About Thornmaul

Thornmaul is a huge thorn wolf that guards the darkest groves of the Dark Forest. Its dark purple body is made of roots and bark wrapped in green vines, a wild mane of leaves and giant thorns covers its neck and shoulders, and its purple eyes glow in the shadows. When it roars, the whole forest goes quiet. It is fierce, but only to those who try to harm the forest.

Field note: The darker the forest grows around Thornmaul, the brighter its eyes shine.

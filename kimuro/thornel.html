About Thornel

Thornel is a small grey wolf pup whose fur is woven through with living thorned vines, thickest along its back and tail. It moves silently between the roots of Verdant Valley's deepest undergrowth, watching travelers from the dark before deciding whether to growl or simply vanish. Younger creatures of the forest treat it as an unofficial guardian - it snaps at anything that threatens them, then slinks away before it can be thanked.

Field note: Thornel's vine-fur snags fewer leaves than a bare-furred pup its size - the thorns are angled to slide through brush, not catch on it.

About Thornfang

Thornfang has grown into a lean, dark-furred wolf whose thorned mane now runs from skull to shoulder like a crown of brambles. It patrols the tangled thickets at the valley's edge, and the vines along its spine thicken visibly whenever it senses a threat to the smaller creatures in its territory. Trainers who earn its trust say it fights fiercely but never presses an advantage against something that has already yielded.

Field note: Thornfang's mane-thorns curl inward when it rests - the only time the vines relax their outward, defensive angle.

About Thornmaul

Thornmaul is a massive dark wolf whose entire body is armored in thick, ancient-looking bramble growth, vines thick as rope wrapping its legs and back like living plate. It patrols the oldest, darkest groves of Verdant Valley, and guardians' texts describe it as the reason the valley's deepest undergrowth has never been cleared by anything that wasn't invited. It chooses patient partners who do not pull at its vines or rush it into battle.

Field note: The oldest bramble growth on Thornmaul's back is older than the wolf itself - passed down, root and all, from its own parent at evolution.

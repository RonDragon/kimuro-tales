About Calfling

This small brown-and-white calf wanders Verdant Valley's open grasslands with the unhurried confidence of a creature that knows it will be large and does not feel the need to rush about it. Its round white patches sit against warm brown hide, and small nub horns push through the crown of its head like they are still deciding how curved to be. The calf stamps each new patch of grass to judge its quality, circling it twice before grazing - a method the valley's older farmers say produces the valley's most even lawns without any human effort whatsoever.

Field note: Every patch of grass a young Bull has circled twice grows back more evenly than the surrounding field - the valley farmers leave sections deliberately uncut for it.

About Bullock

Now fully grown to a stocky, muscle-packed bull with proper curved horns and a head it carries low and forward, this creature has become the pace-setter of Verdant Valley's herds. Its broad dark stripes run front to back and its hooves carve clean half-moons in the soil wherever it turns. The valley's wind patterns seem to bend slightly around the herd when this bull leads, deflecting gusts that would scatter the lighter grassland creatures. It has a habit of locking eyes with any creature that enters the valley's central plain - a long, considering look - before deciding whether to stay or go.

Field note: Wind measurements taken in the central plain consistently show a calm zone that precisely tracks this bull's position - meteorologists have no satisfying explanation.

About Taurolith

The ancient form of this creature is a dark massive ox-bull marked with deep etched X-patterns on its flanks - battle scars and growth-marks worn into its hide over lifetimes of standing in Verdant Valley's fields. Its great curved horns have thickened and curled, and a patch of vivid clover has taken root in the mud always caked between its hooves. The valley floor vibrates gently when it walks - a pulse the valley's creatures respond to by parting ahead of it and closing behind it like water around a stone. The guardians say it was walking this valley before the first name was ever spoken here, and will be walking it when the last one is forgotten.

Field note: The clover permanently growing between this creature's hooves is a variety found nowhere else in Verdant Valley - it blooms in all seasons.

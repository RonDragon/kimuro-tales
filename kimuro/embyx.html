About Embyx

Embyx stands upright on two strong hind legs, its charcoal-black body ringed with blazing orange heat patterns that glow brightest along its spine. Short, broad-based spines crown its head, and its stubby arms end in sharp amber claws. It hops between the thermal vents of the Amber Desert, soaking up heat with every breath. It stamps its feet when excited and leaves tiny scorch-prints in the sand.

Field note: Embyx's heat markings pulse brighter each time it hears a word it already knows - like a proud little ember.

About Ignaur

Taller and armored now, Ignaur wears interlocking plates of black-and-orange scales across its broad torso, and a ridge of sharpened spines rises from head to thick tail. Its eyes glow a fierce amber, and it walks on two powerful legs, arms raised and ready. In the Amber Desert it circles the ancient fire-pillars - heat-cracked columns where the guardian Kael used to hold council by firelight - and drives off anything that disrespects the heat.

Field note: Ignaur's spine-ridge vibrates at a low rumble when lava flows nearby, long before any instrument can detect it.

About Vulkyx Inferno

Vulkyx Inferno is a towering winged dragon: vast leathery black wings edged in orange fire fan from its shoulders, a ridge of blade-like spines runs from skull to tail-tip, and its entire body blazes in a pattern of smoldering ember-orange on deep volcanic black. Its four legs are thick as desert pillars, and when it opens its jaws, a torrent of white-hot breath pours out. The Amber Desert ignites with color in its wake - the sands turn glass where it treads.

Field note: The glass beads left by Vulkyx Inferno's footsteps are collected and strung as protective charms by desert children.

About Embrit

Embrit is a small orange-red fox kit with a spiky flame-crest on its head and warm green eyes that catch the filtered light of the Bug Thicket. It darts between the thick root-bundles and web-strands with quick agile leaps, never staying still long enough for the thicket's creatures to follow. It carries a natural warmth that dries the dew from the thicket floor wherever it walks, making a warm path for the others. Its hero-blood makes it braver than any creature its size should be.

Field note: Embrit charges directly at anything that frightens it - and has never once been caught by what it charged.

About Embriox

Embriox has grown into a powerful spotted cat with a full fire-mane and a lean body built for speed through the dense Bug Thicket. Its spotted coat blazes warm orange in the thicket's shadowed light, and its powerful haunches let it clear full root-walls in a single bound. It has taken on a patrol role, chasing the larger insects out of the thicket's central clearing where young creatures play. When it runs full speed, the ground it covers smells of warm cedar for hours after.

Field note: Embriox clears the same patrol loop through Bug Thicket in exactly four minutes - if it takes longer, something has moved.

About Pyrembros

Pyrembros is a great blazing lion-beast whose entire upper body is wreathed in fire-mane spreading from neck to flanks. Its spotted coat shimmers with hero-gold beneath the fire-color, and it carries itself with the bearing of a leader that has never lost. It stands at the heart of the Bug Thicket where the oldest trees form a natural cathedral, and every creature in the thicket knows its territory by the scorch-ring it walks once a week. The guardians called it the Ember-Champion.

Field note: Pyrembros walks the same scorch-ring perimeter every seventh day, the burn always perfectly precise to the centimeter.

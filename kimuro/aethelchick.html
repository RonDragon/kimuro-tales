About Aethelchick

Aethelchick is a round grey-white owlet with a single curved pearlescent horn rising from its forehead and wide sapphire eyes ringed with pale blue. Its breast feathers shimmer faintly as if dusted with starlight. In the Psychic Temple's canopy it perches on low branches and tilts its head at new sounds with intense concentration - that horn glows when it understands something for the first time. The temple's guardians say Aethelchick can hold perfectly still for six hours waiting for a single fascinating fact.

Field note: Aethelchick's horn tip glows blue whenever it hears a sound it has never encountered before.

About Aethelwing

Aethelwing stands proud as a hawk now, its plumage a deep swirling blue-grey that mirrors the night sky of the Psychic Temple. The single horn has lengthened and curved back like a crescent blade, and its talons are tipped with luminous gold. Its wide-spread wings reveal faint galaxy-like streaks when it lifts off at dusk. It hunts by reading the intentions of its quarry a full heartbeat before they flee - a psychic edge it uses only to protect its temple, never to harm.

Field note: Aethelwing can read the mood of an entire temple from a single overhead pass; trainers feel unusually calm in its shadow.

About Aethelion

Aethelion is a blazing golden eagle with wings spread wide in triumph, its entire body radiating warm amber-gold light with violet-purple secondary feathers trailing behind. Water droplets shimmer off its wingtips as it flies through cloud. Its horn has become a full golden crown-spike, and its eyes carry a calm all-knowing warmth. Ancient the Psychic Temple hymns say Aethelion is the temple's living lamp - when Abalont's darkness tries to creep in, Aethelion rises to the highest branch and simply opens its wings.

Field note: Three wingbeats from Aethelion are enough to clear fog from an entire valley - local farmers time their mornings by its flights.

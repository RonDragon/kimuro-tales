About Thornlet

A small, warm-brown rabbit-kit with bright dark eyes, a compact body, and short bristly spines beginning to poke from behind its ears and along its back, Thornlet hops through Ghost Hollow on silent, wide-padded feet. It freezes absolutely motionless when scared, blending with fallen bark and rotted leaf until whatever frightened it moves on. Thornlet is one of the few small creatures that actually follows the ghost-lights in Ghost Hollow - it has learned they lead to warm mineral springs, and it has bathed in every one it has found.

Field note: Thornlet can remain motionless for up to forty minutes - Ghost Hollow guides have stepped over them believing they were pebbles.

About Thornbuck

Thornbuck has shed its kit-round shape and grown into a lean, fast hare with long powerful hind legs and a back now bristling with long dark spines that fan backward when it runs, making it look like a streak of shadow with quills. It reads the Ghost Hollow's moods like a native, using the fog patches as cover and the hollow's tilted terrain to accelerate into explosive sprint-bursts that cover ground faster than they look. When cornered, it lowers its head and presents the spine-fan to its opponent - an elegant way of saying: reconsider.

Field note: Thornbuck leaves no tracks on soft soil; its wide paw-pads distribute weight so evenly it runs across mud without sinking.

About Thornarch

Thornarch has become something between a great hare and a nightmare - a long, muscular predator with a mane of stiff dark spines spreading from neck to haunches, and jaws that open wider than its skull seems to allow. It moves in Ghost Hollow's deepest sections, where even veteran guardians carry a light, and it has never once been surprised. Thornarch's spines flex and shiver when emotions shift, broadcasting its mood to every creature in the hollow - the few predators that tried to challenge it read the message and backed away. Ancient hollow-lore names Thornarch the Keeper of the Hollow's Silence.

Field note: The shadow-patches in Ghost Hollow that never hold a ghost-light are, without exception, spots where a Thornarch regularly rests.

About Buzzling

Buzzling is a pudgy green caterpillar with a round friendly face and two tiny antennae that bend toward any sound - especially spoken words. It inches along the storm-struck branches of Storm Spire's lower reaches, leaving a faint trail of tiny electric footprints on bark. When it hears a word it does not know, it stops completely and rocks its whole body side to side, as though the word is a flavor it is tasting for the first time. Buzzling eats electric-charged leaves exclusively, and grows slightly brighter green with each meal.

Field note: Buzzling's antennae point in the direction of the nearest electrical storm at all times - Storm Spire navigators use them as living compasses.

About Voltbug

Voltbug has grown thick yellow-and-green banded segments and nubby front legs that grip with surprising strength, and its antennae now crackle with continuous electric sparks. Its round eyes have sharpened into focused amber ovals that track movement precisely. It feeds openly now, climbing to the highest branches of Storm Spire during thunderstorms and absorbing the charge directly from the air. On calm days it is placid and slow; on storm days it is frenetic, racing from branch to branch with crackling speed.

Field note: Voltbug doubles its movement speed for each additional lightning strike it absorbs during a single storm - on peak storm nights, it is nearly invisible.

About Stormswarm

Stormswarm emerges from its storm-charged cocoon with broad blue-grey wings that crackle at every vein-line with visible electricity - a living circuit board spread against the sky. Its wingspan casts a flickering shadow that strobes as the electric charge pulses through it, and when it beats its wings the air pressure creates tiny local thunderclaps. In Storm Spire it serves as weather reader and herald - its wing-beat rhythm matches the incoming storm's timing so precisely that the spire's keepers could set a clock by it.

Field note: The wingbeat rhythm of a Stormswarm matches the exact interval between lightning strikes in any storm it has absorbed for longer than one hour.

About Crystkit

Crystkit is a fluffy white fox kit with a light-blue snowflake patch on its forehead and a thick curling tail tipped pale blue. Its four paws leave tiny star-shaped frost prints on the Frozen Peaks tidepools. It chases light refractions through the shallow water with a focus that looks almost scholarly - stopping dead whenever one lands perfectly on its snout. Locals say Crystkit is trying to bottle a sunbeam in ice, and one day it will succeed.

Field note: Crystkit's breath crystallizes into a tiny six-pointed snowflake exactly once per exhale - no two are the same.

About Crystrail

Crystrail has grown graceful and swift, its white coat now shimmering with pale-blue runic patterns and a long swirling ice-plume tail that streams behind it in motion. A single ice-blue horn rises from its forehead, and when sunlight hits it, prismatic light scatters across the Frozen Peaks like dropped jewels. It races the tide in at dawn and races it back out, never wet, never caught - the peaks fishers call it the morning omen of a good fair wind.

Field note: Crystrail runs at full speed across water surfaces without breaking the meniscus - scientists call it an unsolved pressure mystery.

About Crystalleigh

Crystalleigh is radiant - a large sleek fox with a full fan of crystalline tail-plumes that refract light into full spectrum arcs behind it, and glowing blue rune-markings etched across its body. Its eyes are deep sapphire, and when it stands still on the high ice shelf at sunrise, the light it throws is visible from the valley floor. Old Frozen Peaks tales say it was spun from the first ray of morning that ever touched the snow.

Field note: The light-fan of a resting Crystalleigh projects stable prismatic patterns across 200 square meters of snowfield.

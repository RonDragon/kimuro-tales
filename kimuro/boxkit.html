About Boxkit

Boxkit is a young kangaroo with a small pouch and two oversized paws wearing what can only be described as round orange boxing mitts that it was apparently born with. It bounces through Light Grove's open meadows practicing combinations on air, on trees, and on any companion patient enough to hold still. Boxkit has excellent balance - it can bob and weave before its hind legs even decide to commit to a direction. It keeps a small collection of interesting pebbles in its pouch and examines each one after every training session, rating them by feel.

Field note: Boxkit rates every pebble in its collection after training - the ones it keeps longest are always perfectly round.

About Boxbound

Boxbound has outgrown the orange mitts - its hands are now powerful bare knuckles with the kind of reach that makes every other creature in Light Grove keep a polite distance. It stands tall and lean, still bouncing from foot to foot in a way that suggests it is always ready and never quite still. Boxbound uses the grove's trees as sparring posts, and the ones it trains on have grown visibly thicker on the impact side. It is a surprisingly patient teacher for younger creatures, demonstrating combinations at half-speed with grave concentration before bounding off to try them at full power.

Field note: The trees Boxbound trains on have grown measurably thicker on the side it strikes - the grove's oldest trees record its entire training history.

About Pugalist

Pugalist stands wrapped in combat tape from knuckle to elbow like a veteran who has fought every worthy challenger in Light Grove and earned the right to stop doing the wrapping elegantly. Its massive tail is a rudder and a weapon both; a full swing can redirect a wind gust enough to clear the grove's canopy. The guardians' texts call Pugalist the Grove's First Fist - a creature who understood that the strongest defense of a peaceful place is one fighter who loves peace enough to be very good at fighting. It mentors the next generation by watching silently from above until it is needed.

Field note: Pugalist has never struck first in any recorded encounter - it always delivers exactly one warning hoot before responding.

About Pricklet

Pricklet is a tiny, round-bodied hedgehog wrapped in a coat of cream-colored fur with stubby steel-tipped quills just beginning to push through its back. Its wide black eyes sparkle with curiosity and its small snout twitches constantly, smelling out the tiniest scraps of iron ore. In the Steel Fortress it scurries between wall-joints, collecting flakes of rust that it somehow works into its growing quills. Guardians say Pricklet once led young explorers to a hidden ore-seam by following its nose alone.

Field note: Its tiny quills are still soft enough to bend - press one and it springs back like a spring wire.

About Shardine

Shardine's back is now bristling with long, sharp steel-grey quills that clatter softly when it walks. Its body has broadened and its legs are stout and sure-footed on rough stone. It patrols the lower ramparts of the Steel Fortress with a slow deliberate tread, pressing its broad snout against the walls to sense vibrations. When threatened it curls its head low and fans its quills outward in a rattling display. Engineers in the fortress have learned to follow Shardine when they hear its quills chime - it always finds the stressed beams first.

Field note: When Shardine fans its quills, they hum a faint chord together - each quill tuned to a different pitch.

About Ironspike

Ironspike is a hulking armored beast whose back sprouts enormous bladed spines of dark metal, each one edged bright with ice-blue light at the tips. Its four legs are thick as gate-posts, and its glowing red eyes scan the fortress walls with cold authority. The ground trembles faintly when it stamps. Abalont's forces never broke through a wall that Ironspike guarded; its spines can splinter stone if swung with full force. The guardians carved its shape above every forge in the region as a promise: some things are built to last.

Field note: Each of Ironspike's spines rings like a struck blade in cold air - the deeper the tone, the older the spine.

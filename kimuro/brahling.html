About Brahling

Brahling is a bright green caterpillar whose most striking feature is the glowing blue gem set into its forehead like a third eye that it cannot yet fully control. It blinks with all three eyes at different rates, creating a rhythm that the Crystal Mines' other creatures seem to find oddly calming. Brahling inches along the crystal veins in the mine walls, pressing its forehead gem against each crystal to listen - the gem briefly amplifies the crystal's natural hum to something audible. It is extraordinarily patient and will wait motionless for an hour for the right vibration.

Field note: Brahling's forehead gem glows brighter near high-grade crystal deposits - miners call it the living dowsing rod.

About Brahman

Brahman has transformed completely - from crawling green larva to a tall graceful deer whose hide shimmers in green-and-gold crystal patterns and whose antlers are formed entirely of glowing crystal branches. It moves through the Crystal Mines with soundless delicacy, its hooves barely ringing on the stone, and tilts its antlered head to catch the light refracting from the walls. Brahman can broadcast the mines' crystal-hum as a visible light, briefly illuminating entire caverns when it raises its head. The mine workers leave gifts of polished crystals at its trail crossing.

Field note: Brahman's antlers broadcast the mines' crystal-hum as light when raised - the deepest shafts have never needed lanterns where it walks.

About Divyahm

Divyahm is a magnificent towering stag whose full crown of golden crystal antlers reaches the ceiling of all but the Crystal Mines' grandest chambers, blazing with a warm amber-gold light that fills every crack and shadow in a hundred-meter radius. Gem-bright markings run from snout to tail in perfect symmetry, and when it breathes out, its breath carries a visible shimmer that settles into the mine walls as an iridescent residue. The guardians' oldest texts speak of Divyahm as the Mines' Memory - a creature who recorded every promise made in the crystal walls so nothing would be forgotten even if Abalont came.

Field note: The iridescent residue of Divyahm's breath on crystal walls holds a clear impression of every voice raised near it for the past century.

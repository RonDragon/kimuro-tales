About Sproukit

Sproukit is a small fox kit with brown fur and a pair of broad green leaves sprouting from behind its ears like extra ears of a better kind. Its bushy tail is tipped in leaf-green, and it bounces through the Light Grove with bright-eyed enthusiasm. Wherever Sproukit plays, small seedlings appear in its pawprints the following morning - not because it plants them intentionally, but because the seeds catch in its fur and drop wherever it rolls. Light Grove travelers follow the seedling trails to find where Sproukit sleeps.

Field note: A trail of seedlings in a Light Grove clearing marks exactly where Sproukit rolled in the grass the night before.

About Thornbark

Thornbark has grown lean and sharp, its brown fur now overlaid with a scattering of broad green leaves along its back and flanks, and its ears have taken on a pointed, leaf-edged shape. Its tail still bears the leaf-tip but has thickened into a bushy plume it holds high as it patrols the Light Grove's inner ring. The leaves on its back rustle distinctly differently from grove leaves - a sharp dry crinkle when it's annoyed, a soft flutter when it's content. Grove creatures have learned to read Thornbark's leaves before approaching.

Field note: Thornbark's back leaves rustle at a specific frequency that only its closest allies can distinguish from the grove's ambient sound.

About Sylvox

Sylvox is a large, broad-chested fox whose body is nearly half covered by wide overlapping leaves - back, flanks, shoulders, even around its muzzle - as if the grove itself has begun to claim it. Its thick tail sweeps leaf-litter with every step. Its eyes carry the patient depth of old-growth forest. When Sylvox sits and goes still in the Light Grove, it disappears entirely - the leaves on its body align with the canopy above and it becomes indistinguishable from a patch of undergrowth. Those who have the Grove's favor say Sylvox finds them, not the other way around.

Field note: Sylvox can remain completely motionless for six hours - grove creatures walk past within a body-length without noticing it.

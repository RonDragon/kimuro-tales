About Psibab

Psibab is a small psychic monkey wreathed in drifting violet wisps that brighten whenever it hears something it has never heard before, briefly holding a shape in the air before dissolving. The temple-keepers say every Psibab is born already certain of exactly one thing - and never the same thing twice.

Field note: Every Psibab arrives already certain of one thing - no two have ever been born certain of the same one.

About Psihowl

Psihowl is a powerfully built orange-red gorilla that moves through the light temple on its knuckles with confident weight. Its broad muscular body is plated with natural purple psychic-energy tattoo-like markings that glow when it concentrates. It pauses often, pressing both fists into the earth and closing its eyes - a meditation posture that lets it read the light temple's psychic root-network from below. It communicates with other creatures through projected feelings rather than sound, though when it does vocalize, the sound is felt in the chest. Psihowl has memorized every light-tree in the temple by the texture of the root it grows from.

Field note: Psihowl identifies every light-tree in the temple by feel alone - blindfolded, it can navigate to any specific tree by tracing its root through the soil.

About Psikanth

Psikanth sits in the deepest heart of the light temple with its back straight and its powerful fists resting on its knees, surrounded by swirling rings of violet psychic fire. Its white-silver fur radiates an electric calm, and purple flame burns steadily in each clenched fist without consuming them. It meditates constantly, but its eyes are open - it watches the temple even while its mind traverses its root-network. When it stands, the air pressure in the temple changes measurably. It is one of the few creatures that Abalont's shadow has never been able to approach: the psychic field emanating from Psikanth's meditation is a ward that has held without interruption since the guardians first fell.

Field note: The psychic fire in Psikanth's fists burns precisely as bright as the collective unspoken knowledge of every creature currently in the temple.

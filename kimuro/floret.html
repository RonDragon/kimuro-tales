About Floret

Floret is little more than a cheerful yellow body with three wide leaf-wings fanning out on each side, making it look more like a flying flower than an insect. Two short antennae bob as it hovers at low altitude, and its tiny round eyes study every passing leaf with great concentration. It lives in the Bug Thicket's sunlit glades, feeding on pollen dust that drifts between the canopy gaps. Floret moves in tight loops, tasting the air, and always finds its way home even when it wanders far.

Field note: Floret can hover motionless for up to four minutes while it decides whether a particular flower petal is worth landing on.

About Florant

Florant has grown a longer armored yellow-gold body and its leaf-wings have narrowed into sharper blades, buzzing at a frequency that makes nearby grass hum sympathetically. It now hunts fast, darting between trunks in the Bug Thicket like a living arrow, tracking the subtle scents left by ripe seed-clusters. Its antennae have extended and curve back along its body, and its mandibles have hardened enough to score bark. Experienced Thicket guides say a grove guarded by Florant never loses a tree to blight.

Field note: When Florant's wings beat at full speed, the vibration shakes loose exactly the seeds it wants to eat - the rest stay on the stem.

About Floravant

Floravant is a heavy-bodied gold-green insect with broad overlapping leaf-blade wings and thick segmented armor plating. Its clawed legs grip bark like a vice, and when it spreads its full wingspan, the canopy around it goes still - smaller insects stop flying and leaves flatten. It patrols the Bug Thicket's oldest trees, repairing damaged bark with resin it produces itself, and its buzzing presence drives off anything that would cut or burn the grove. Guardians left a small carving of Floravant at every old tree in the thicket as a marker of protection.

Field note: Floravant can seal a wound in a tree's bark within two hours using a resin it secretes from glands behind its mandibles.

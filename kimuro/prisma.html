About Prisma

Prisma is a chunky iridescent grub with a segmented body that shimmers with soft rainbow colors - purple, blue, pink - as it inches along. Its face is dominated by a single enormous round eye that reflects the whole world in its surface. It moves with a slow rolling undulation across leaves and bark, leaving a faintly shimmering trail. When it finds a spot it likes, it curls into a tight loop and pulses gently, broadcasting a soft psychic contentment that makes nearby creatures feel briefly peaceful. Verdant Valley children consider finding a Prisma grub in their garden to be a sign that good things are coming.

Field note: Wherever a Prisma rests for more than one night, the surrounding grass grows into a perfect circle of richer, brighter green.

About Prismoll

Prismoll has encased itself in a floating crystalline shell that hovers a few centimeters above any surface. The chrysalis is shaped like a rounded diamond, its faceted surface reflecting light into brief spectrum flashes. It has no visible eyes, legs, or mouth in this stage, and seems barely alive - yet it is wildly active internally. Psychic observers report that the interior of a Prismoll chrysalis is filled with thousands of luminous sound-fragments from everything Prisma ever absorbed, being rearranged and woven into something new. Verdant Valley explorers are told never to shake, tap, or shout near a Prismoll: it may be dormant, but it is listening to everything.

Field note: Prismoll's crystal shell plays back the last sound it heard in the valley whenever the valley goes completely silent.

About Prismagnis

Prismagnis has emerged from its chrysalis as something extraordinary - a sleek, angular insect with a smooth pale body and four enormous prismatic wings that scatter light into color storms wherever it flies. Its dark eyes are multifaceted and seeing, its six legs light and sure. It moves through Verdant Valley in low buzzing circuits, and where its wing-shadow passes over the hand-carved stones, the letters briefly lift and float in colored light before settling again. Children chasing its light-reflections often find they have followed it to the exact memory-stone they most needed to study. The guardians say Prismagnis remembers every voice ever raised in the valley and can return them to whoever lost them.

Field note: Prismagnis always lands on the memory-stone containing the word the closest child has been struggling most to learn.

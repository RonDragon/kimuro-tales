About Owlite

Owlite is a round, fluffy purple owl with a glowing golden star on its forehead and wide bright eyes that track movement with unnerving precision for something so small. It sits atop rocky outcroppings near Dragon Spire's base, ruffling its feathers in the updrafts and occasionally emitting a single soft hoot as if answering a question nobody else heard. Trainers who camp on the Spire overnight say Owlite watches them sleep and seems to take careful notes.

Field note: Owlite's star-mark brightens whenever it hears a word it has not learned yet - it brightens a lot.

About Twilight

Twilight has grown a sharp horn-like crest and feathers that shimmer between purple and blue. Its wingbeats are near-silent even in calm air - a useful quality near Dragon Spire's listening walls, where sound carries in strange directions. It hunts by reading the intent of prey before they move, and it is almost never surprised. The guardians who trained near the Spire used to ask Twilight for advice in difficult decisions, and it would answer with one deliberate blink: yes, or two: no.

Field note: Twilight's horn-crest crackles faintly every time it detects a conscious mind focusing intensely on something.

About Celestowl

Celestowl carries a crown of blue-white ice crystals that never melts, even in Dragon Spire's volcanic updrafts - a sign of how completely its psychic energy has crystallized inward. Its great wings spread wide in silver-blue, and the wind it commands is cold with purpose. It circles the highest peaks in absolute silence, and the dragons nest only where Celestowl permits them to. The old stories say it arrived before the Spire had its name and that it decided what the mountain was for.

Field note: Celestowl's crown of ice was formed in a single night - no one who watched it form could remember it afterward.

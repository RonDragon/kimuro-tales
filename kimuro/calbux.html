About Calbux

Calbux is a sturdy brown calf with a white-patched face and a gently determined look. Its small horns are just nubs, and white markings on its brow form an almost-cross shape. It trots through the Crystal Mines with surprising confidence for something so young, sniffing at crystal outcroppings and tapping them with one hoof to hear the ring. Miners say Calbux brings good luck to new excavations - it has an uncanny habit of standing precisely above the richest crystal veins.

Field note: Calbux taps any crystal three times before walking on - the third tap is always the one that rings clearest, and miners mark that spot.

About Taurox

Taurox is a powerful, deep-chested bull with a wide head, broad sweeping horns, and a dark brown striped hide that has begun to take on the warm shine of polished stone. The white cross-mark on its face has grown bolder. It lowers its head when thinking and raises it sharply when it has decided - a motion the Crystal Mines dwarfs have learned to read as clearly as speech. Once Taurox chooses a direction, no other creature in the mine tries to redirect it.

Field note: Taurox's horns vibrate at a barely audible frequency near large crystal deposits - cave explorers have learned to hold a hand near each horn to navigate.

About Minothor

Minothor stands like a living monument - a great bull whose rocky striped hide has fused with the crystal lattice of the mines, and whose enormous cross-marked face radiates a quiet, unshakeable authority. Its horns span wide enough to block a tunnel entrance, and its slow movements cause crystals on the walls to hum sympathetically. Old Crystal Mines legends say Minothor was the first guardian to find the light beneath the stone, and that it still walks the deepest passages, ensuring the mines never go entirely dark.

Field note: Every crystal within fifteen metres of Minothor brightens noticeably - miners have mapped the whole mine just by following its light-wake.

About Prismule

Prismule is a compact, fluffy white foal with a short purple-tipped horn spiraling from its forehead and a full purple mane and tail that are already longer than its legs. Its large blue eyes are wide with the unfiltered wonder of a creature that has just discovered the world. It trots through the crystal mine's upper galleries on small sure hooves, knocking its horn gently against gem-veins and giggling - a psychic chime sound - when they ring. The mine-keepers say Prismule's touch never dulls a crystal but always brightens it, as if the foal is returning something the stone forgot it had.

Field note: Every crystal Prismule touches with its horn glows ten percent brighter for the rest of its life.

About Prismalin

Prismalin stands gracefully in the crystal mines, a full-grown unicorn with a long flowing purple mane and tail, and a spiraling horn of pale crystal that casts rainbow light on the cave walls. Its white coat is luminous, lit from within. It moves with measured elegance, choosing its path deliberately through the narrow mine passages, its hooves ringing on the stone floor like struck bells. When it lowers its horn to a child's eye level, the light it casts temporarily illuminates words on any scroll or tablet nearby in their original intended meaning - clearer than any printed page. Mine-keepers say it visits the deepest memory-vaults once a month and reads everything inside.

Field note: Prismalin's horn illuminates words in the exact color corresponding to their emotional meaning - warmth words glow gold, difficult truths shine blue.

About Prismarcy

Prismarcy is a vision of pure radiance - a magnificent unicorn whose coat has deepened to brilliant white-gold, its mane and tail now flowing in every color of the spectrum simultaneously: red, gold, green, blue, violet, all shifting and blending as it moves. The horn is long, faceted like a cut diamond, and blazes with psychic light bright enough to read by a mile away. Its hooves leave glowing prints on the mine floor that fade after one hour, but while they remain, they mark the safest path through every crystal passage. Ancient mine-records describe Prismarcy as the guardian who first taught the crystal mines' creatures to speak, by shining its horn on the walls and turning gem-veins into letters.

Field note: The prints Prismarcy leaves in the mine dust spell out, letter by letter, the most important word any reader nearby needs that day.

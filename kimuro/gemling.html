About Gemling

Gemling is a small round badger kit with soft dark-brown fur and wide eyes that glow faintly pink when it is curious - which is nearly always. A tiny spark of rose-pink psychic light orbits its head when it is thinking hard, appearing and disappearing at random. Despite being a creature of the Crystal Mines, Gemling is remarkably social, pressing its whiskered snout against every surface it encounters and sending out gentle psychic pulses that amount to a friendly greeting. Miners have adopted them as unofficial companions, since Gemling always taps twice on a wall before it is going to crack.

Field note: Gemling taps twice on a mine wall exactly eight seconds before it fractures - miners have relied on this early warning for generations without understanding how it works.

About Geomaw

Geomaw has grown powerful and compact, with dense rounded shoulders and two long curved tusks that it uses to pry open crystal seams. Its back bristles with erupting pink-magenta crystal spikes that cast rose-colored light through the mine tunnels. Its psychic sensitivity has sharpened to the point that it can taste the composition of a crystal formation by pressing its nose against it. Geomaw marks the richest gem clusters in the Crystal Mines by scratching patterns around them with its tusks - gem prospectors follow these markings to make their biggest finds.

Field note: No Geomaw marking has ever been found at an empty vein - the strike rate for miners following Geomaw scratch patterns is recorded at one hundred percent.

About Crystalloth

Crystalloth is a formidable creature to meet in the deep mines - a large powerful badger with enormous tusks, a body armored in dense overlapping pink and magenta crystal formations, and a crown of massive crystal spires radiating from its neck and shoulders. Its psychic field is so powerful that it broadcasts strong impressions to every creature in the mine without effort - trainers call approaching Crystalloth "walking into a thought." The Crystal Mines' oldest gem seams are all located within a kilometer of a Crystalloth sleeping site, as if the creature's presence draws them near over time.

Field note: Crystalloth is the only creature recorded to have successfully moved a sealed mine wall by applying sustained psychic pressure - it took forty minutes of motionless concentration.

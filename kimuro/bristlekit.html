About Bristlekit

Bristlekit is a tiny brown kitten with huge round eyes, a small tuft of green leaf-sprout sitting neatly between its ears like a mohawk, and a fluffy tail that curls up behind it. It is the most common companion found at the entrance to the Light Grove, greeting every new visitor with an immediate head-butt to the shin. Despite its harmless appearance, Bristlekit's leaf-tuft senses vibrations through the ground, and it will have run to hide in a tree hollow a full minute before any large creature arrives. Guardians say it was the last creature to see Akilla's green light fade.

Field note: Bristlekit's leaf-tuft doubles in size when it is happy; grove visitors have learned to look for large tufts to find safe clearings.

About Thornfur

Thornfur has shed its kitten softness into a lean, sharp-eyed feline whose entire pelt now bristles with needle-sharp green thorns in dense clusters. It moves with wary, low-to-the-ground grace through the Light Grove's bramble zones, body perfectly adapted to squeezing through thorn-walls that stop anything larger. Its eyes have sharpened to intense amber focus. Thornfur marks its territory by pressing its flanks against tree bark and leaving neat rows of thorn-impressions - a message that reads clearly to anything with sense: this path is claimed. It continues to visit the grove entrance to supervise new arrivals.

Field note: Thornfur's thorn density is highest along its shoulders - a precise brush against that patch leaves an itching impression that lingers for three days.

About Viryx

Viryx is a magnificent, living-forest creature - its body encased in lush leafy foliage so dense it resembles a moving grove more than a feline. Broad leaf-plating covers its shoulders and haunches, and vine-tendrils trail from its mane as it walks. It stands with absolute confidence in the deepest Light Grove clearings, and every green thing nearby grows a little faster in its presence. The oldest grove-keeper accounts describe Viryx emerging from the trees to help trainers find lost paths - not with guidance but by simply walking the right direction and trusting them to follow.

Field note: The clearing Viryx uses as its permanent resting spot has the densest canopy in the entire Light Grove - trees there have never shed a single leaf in autumn.
